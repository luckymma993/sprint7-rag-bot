# Quinria

Quinria, referred to as Quinmund by some natives, was the desert home planet of the Quinrians. It hosted the League of Free Realms major battle automaton foundries and was the site of the First Battle of Quinria, the opening conflict of the Echo Wars, as well as the subsequent invasion by the Concord of Auren. During and after the war, the Void Core was constructed above the planet. Five years after the conflict's conclusion and transformation of the Concord of Auren, the Vastrian Dominion would garrison the planet heavily and attempt to mine the planet's asteroids for minerals. At least five years after the Echo Wars, the Dominion moved the Void Core to another construction site and the planet was sterilized by the Dominion as a security measure, an act which resulted in the virtual elimination of the planet's native population.

## Description

Situated in the Quinria system of the Vorrax sector in the Far Reaches, the planet of Quinria was 43,000 light years from the Sovtia Core, and less than a parsec away from the neighboring Far Reaches planet of Tevlan. It was the homeworld of the Quinrians, a species of sentient insectoids. It had a breathable atmosphere, but a hot and arid climate. Its irradiated surface was covered in harsh, rocky deserts, marked by mesas and buttes. Both its rocks and sky were tinted in shades of red. Surface water was scarce, amounting to only 5% of the entire total planetary surface. However, Quinria had frequent flash floods. From space, it could be seen that Quinria had awe-inspiring rocky rings. The planet was orbited by fifteen moons, four of which were major and included the icy moon of Nyzek.

## History

### Ancient history

At some point in the ancient history of Quinria, a comet crashed in one of the planet's many moons. The collision debris not only formed the famous rings of Quinria, but also formed a field of small asteroids which would sometimes rain down on the planet's surface.

### Under the Concord

Sovtia colonizers were attracted to Quinria when the Kawyn system was discovered due to its close proximity to Tevlan. When the Concord of Auren made first contact with the Quinrians, it was successful and beneficial to both groups. Due to this, the Quinrians became interested with Concord technology and became skilled in adapting and improving advanced technology. As such, Quinrians exported automatons and other technology to the rest of the galaxy, although this didn't last long. Once Tevlan was almost abandoned by the first wave of colonizers, Quinria suffered from its distance from the Inner Realms as visitors to the planet grew smaller and smaller until visitors stopped traveling to Quinria altogether. Quinrian exports were more easily met by other manufacturers closer to the Core.

Quinrians became split over the idea of working with offworlders, and with the planet's economy in ruins, wars erupted throughout the planet for millennia, while the rest of the galaxy largely forgot the planet and left it to its endless wars. A few hundred years prior to the Secessionist Crisis, the world was rediscovered by the Concord of Auren and manufacturing deals were made to sell and distribute their advanced technology and automatons. Allying with the Pyrlumeix Corporation of the Techno Union, the Quinrian Baelgan produced protocol automatons, battle automatons, and labor automatons in concert with Pyrlumeix for centuries.

### The Echo Wars

#### Meeting of the Secessionists

In the later years of the Concord of Auren, Quinria became a major base of operations for the League of Free Realms, a collection of star systems that spread across the galaxy. The enormous Quinrian automaton factories began to manufacture of tens of thousands of different types of battle automatons, thus maintaining their own Quinrian automaton army. During that time, Quinria built automaton armies for large corporations such as the Merchant Combine. To produce the Combine's automaton armies, Pyrlumeix Armor Workshop had approached the Quinrians on the Combine's behalf with the offer for Quinria to establish automaton foundries, create battle automatons, and produce other automata. Ten years after the election of Torsera Vorix as Supreme Chancellor of the Concord, the Markern of Quinria, Olnormund the Lesser, hosted a summit of the heads of the League. During that meeting, the trade barons pledged their military forces to Count Ophvael, a defrocked Salphos Master and head of the League. While the Secessionists were ready to wage war, their plans were overheard by Faelnor Halthossul, a Salphos Master.

The Quinrians took Halthossul captive, but he managed to dispatch a distress call to his superiors on Daelvash, the Salphos High Council. The Council then dispatched an assault team of Salphos to Quinria. Meanwhile, the Quinrians sentenced Master Halthossul to death in the Yththos Arena along with his apprentice Yarwyn Daelthos, and the Concord senator Nymund Daelphos. Eventually, the Salphos reinforcements arrived, and quickly dealt with the Quinrian warriors. However, the immense automaton army of the League was laying in wait, and many Salphos fell under enemy fire. When all seemed lost, even more reinforcements arrived, in the form of the newly created Grand Army of the Concord, which made use of the echo soldiers. Upon the arrival of the clone army, the Concord and the League engaged in the first battle of what was soon known as the Echo Wars.

#### Second invasion by the Concord

After Quinria was again under the control of the Secessionists, and following the engagement near Dovgan, the Concord sent Salphos Generals Irgoth, Faelnor Halthossul, Vorrel Lunkrix, and Yarwyn Daelthos to take Quinria with a massive invasion. As they made their descent, Halthossul's and his Echo Commander's, Lorvash, force boarded their gunships and flew to secure the vedris point. However their gunships came under heavy fire from the Quinrians and their defenses. Yarar and Commander Cyrzor's forces followed him but they were forced to land their QZ-ZXs away from the vedris due to the heavy fire. Daelthos's, Salphos Commander Tevzek Caenor's, and Echo Captain Tevtia's gunships were shot down and all their QZ-ZXs were destroyed. Despite that, they continued towards the vedris point and were met with heavy resistance from the Quinrians. Yarar's gunship was shot down and he was wounded. Lorvash was able to land his QZ-ZXs, but the landing area came under fire from the Quinrians and their Faelvash. Halthossul's gunship was then hit and crash landed away from the landing zone. Only Halthossul and Hyvashgan survived the crash and were then rescued by echo soldiers Ulvdris and Boil. Halthossul, Lorvash ,and their troopers then held the vedris awaiting Daelthos's and Yarar's forces.

As Yarar's forces made their way through the Grimria field to the vedris point, Cyrzor contacted Concord Admiral Cyrvash Grimthas for air support. The Admiral however, could not divert any air support with risks to other units. Cyrzor then left a squad with the QZ-ZX walkers and with Yarar and his clone flamelegionnaires continued to the vedris. Daelthos and his force then came to a fortress that blocked their path. Daelthos and Caenor then scaled the wall. They were able to destroy the battle automatons, but they were then confronted by Fenphos who they were able to defeat with Tevtia's help. They then placed their explosives inside the wall and their way was cleared. Daelthos's forces were then able to meet up with Yarar's forces. Daelthos then contacted Grimthas for air support and Grimthas was able to provide a squadron of Ithlis Brakwing starwing/bombers.

As Halthossul's forces were being overrun, the bombers then took out the Quinrians' forces surrounding the vedris. Daelthos's and Yarar's forces then joined Halthossul's to plan the last part of their attack. Daelthos would attack the defenses of the Sovdrenkern shield generator while QZ-ZXs were brought in and Yarar brought in the remaining troops and the gunships. Daelthos, Caenor and a squad were able to penetrate the shield. They then threw automaton poppers at the defending proton cannons, which disabled the cannons. Halthossul then brought the QZ-ZXs in range of the shield generator and destroyed it. After the shield went down, Yarar brought in the gunships and captured Quinrians still at this location. Halthossul and Yarar, along with the rest of their wounded, left to recuperate, leaving Daelthos, Caenor his Novice, and their force to destroy the factory.
