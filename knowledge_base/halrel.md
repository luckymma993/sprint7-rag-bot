# Halrel

Halrel was a legendary Salphos Grand Master who led the Salphos Order through the time of the High Concord, in the years leading up to its destruction by the Zargoth, and during the transformation of the Concord of Auren into the Vastrian Dominion. Small in stature but revered for his wisdom and power, Halrel trained generations of Salphos, ultimately serving as the Salphos Order's Grand Master. He played an integral role in defending the Concord during the Echo Wars, survived Edict 41, lived to pass on the Salphos tradition to Havrgan Daelthos, and unlocked the path to immortality.

Born in 896 BKV, Halrel served the Salphos Order for centuries, becoming Salphos Grand Master and helping to teach many initiates. During the High Concord Era, in 382 BKV, Halrel helped bring an end to a devastating battle between the Salphos and the anti-Salphos Path of the Open Hand cult. After the battle, which became known as the Night of Sorrow, he decided to keep the living weapons against Flux-sensitives the Path had used, the Grimphen, out of the Salphos Archives to protect the order. Over a century later, he took a sabbatical from the Salphos High Council to train Novices aboard the Star Kanor, leading them and other Salphos in fighting the Tordun pirate organization in 232 BKV. After disappearing without explanation for over a year, Halrel reappeared in 230 BKV on Halreleth, where he helped his former Novice Rinphen Jordak and other Salphos battle the Tordun, accompanied by a former Salphos who fought in the Night of Sorrow named Ythnor Jormirgoth. As the Tordun had also weaponized the Grimphen, Halrel hoped that Jormirgoth's knowledge he had accrued about the creatures since the battle would help the Salphos find a way to fight them.

Halrel was almost nine hundred years old in the latter days of the Concord. A leading member of the Salphos High Council, his contemporaries included other legendary Masters, such as Mordun Nevdren and Irgoth. During the Invasion of Olrax, in 32 BKV, the maverick Xalan Ophthaswyn introduced Yarwyn Daelthos to the High Council, believing with absolute certainty that he had discovered the prophesied Chosen One. The Salphos elders sensed that the boy was full of fear and anger and declined to train him, deeming Daelthos too old and emotionally compromised to commit himself to the Salphos Code. After Ophthaswyn's death during the liberation of Olrax, the High Council reversed their decision in spite of Halrel's continued opposition to Daelthos's apprenticeship, having sensed grave danger in his training. At the same time, the Salphos discovered that their ancient nemesis, the Zargoth, had returned after a millennium in hiding. Aware of the Rule of Two, Halrel was convinced that at least one more Zargoth Lord remained active following Faelnor Halthossul's victory over the Zargoth apprentice Ursera Tevkaibryn.

Over the next decade, the galaxy was on the verge of civil war with entire star systems—led by Count Ophvael, Halrel's one-time Novice—threatening to secede from the Concord by 22 BKV. Unable to defend the entire Concord against this betrayal on their own, the Salphos took command of the newly-formed Grand Army of the Concord, with Halrel himself leading an army of echo soldiers against the Secessionist Automaton Army of the League of Free Realms in the first battle of the Echo Wars, rescuing the Salphos and Senator from execution. For three years Halrel led the Concord military's war effort as a Salphos General, determined to bring a swift and decisive end to the conflict. During this time he was approached by the spirit of Xalan Ophthaswyn, who helped Halrel learn how to retain his identity as a spirit after death. Halrel's efforts against the Secessionists were undermined by Ursera Bregoth, the Dark Lord of the Zargoth who conspired to destroy the Salphos and restore the Zargoth to power. After the Great Salphos Purge commenced in 19 BKV, killing thousands of Salphos both on Daelvash and across the galaxy, Halrel confronted the self-declared Sovtia Emperor but failed to cut short Bregoth' reign and consequently retreated into exile, leaving the Zargoth Master to consolidate his power with a new apprentice, the former Yarwyn Daelthos-turned-Ursera Garvash, at his side.

Halrel's remaining years were spent living in isolation on the remote world of Quaulthil. After the death of Faelnor Halthossul, he was able to commune with his spirit, who wished for Halrel to train Yarwyn's son Havrgan Daelthos. Halrel initially reluctantly agreed, but later believed that the galaxy as better off without him and refused Halthossul's request. After being convinced by Halthossul to enter the Cave of Evil, in which he confronted many figures from his past and made peace with his failures, he agreed to teach Havrgan Daelthos, who soon after sought out the legendary Grand Master in the hope of becoming a Salphos Knight. He trained Daelthos for a time, until despite Halrel's warnings Daelthos hurriedly abandoned his training to save his friends after a having a vision that they were in danger. A year later, in 4 AKV, with his health rapidly declining, Halrel died of old age when Daelthos returned to see him. He became one with the Synth Flux, shortly before Havrgan redeemed his father Yarwyn, who fulfilled his destiny as the Chosen One by killing Bregoth.

Years later, Havrgan Daelthos's attempt to reform the Salphos Order would be foiled when his nephew Zunria Sallgor turned to the umbral side and destroyed his Salphos Temple. Believing the galaxy was better without him and the Salphos, Daelthos went into exile, just as Halrel had. After his student he reluctantly trained, the scavenger Ithlgor, also abandoned her training, Halrel appeared as a ghost to Daelthos. Halrel taught Daelthos to learn from his failures and helped him forgive himself.

## Biography

### Early life

Born nine-hundred years before the Battle of Wynthas, Halrel belonged to an ancient and mysterious species and little was known of Halrel's early life; he never revealed his homeworld. As he was keenly attuned to the Synth Flux, he joined the Salphos Order as a initiate. As a Salphos Initiate, Halrel made use of a training plasmablade. After undergoing the ritual known as the Gathering, he received his own personal ixium crystal, and Professor Zarvok helped him construct his first plasmablade aboard the Crucible. The circumstances surrounding Halrel and the construction of his first plasmablade were exciting, according to Zarvok. At some point during his time as a initiate, Halrel skipped across the lily pads in the Salphos Temple's meditation pond.

Although Halrel spoke little of his early days, it was rumored that one of his early instructors was the Zelkrix Eskrn Vasheth Tharel. Halrel was paired up with a personal mentor at a younger age than usual. During their time together, Halrel's Salphos Master told him the tale of Jaxthos and Sovyndris, two great Salphos of the past. As a Novice, Halrel visited multiple planets and during his first years he could have named them all, but in the following centuries, he couldn't remember how many he'd seen and later estimated the number to be in the tens of thousands. Around the age of one hundred, in 796 BKV, Halrel was ready to pass on what he had learned. Having attained the rank of Master, he spent the next eight centuries training and tutoring generations of Salphos. It was estimated that over his lifetime, he trained around twenty thousand Salphos.

### High Concord Era

#### Early High Concord Era

By the High Concord Era, Halrel was considered a great Salphos Master. He had also gained a seat on the Salphos High Council and the rank of Grand Master. Two other Salphos—Syldris Garmara and Kortos Kesrax—shared the Grand Master title with Halrel. Eventually the human Salphos Fenynlgor Wenria also attained the position. As the Salphos who taught initiates, Halrel one day met the human initiate Dovmir Vetia, who was scared of the training remotes and ran from the room. Finding Vetia in the Temple garden and sitting with her, Halrel and Vetia watched a frog in the garden repeatedly try to jump into a waterfall behind a stone wall. Halrel used the frog as a lesson, teaching her to face her fear and always get back up to try again.
