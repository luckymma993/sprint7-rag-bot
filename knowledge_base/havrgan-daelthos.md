# Havrgan Daelthos

Havrgan Daelthos, a Flux-sensitive human male, was a legendary Salphos Master who fought in the Great Schism War during the reign of the Vastrian Dominion. Along with his companions, Princess Vegoth Faelvok and General Ophsera Sallgor, Daelthos served as a revolutionary on the side of the Free Accord of Auren—an organization committed to the downfall of the Vastrian Dominion and the restoration of democracy. Following the war, Daelthos became a living legend, and was remembered as one of the greatest Salphos in sovtia history.

The son of Salphos Knight Yarwyn Daelthos and Senator Nymund Daelphos, Havrgan Daelthos was born along with his twin sister, Vegoth, in 19 BKV. As a result of Daelphos's death and Yarwyn's fall to the umbral side of the Synth Flux, the Daelthos children were separated and sent into hiding, with Vegoth adopted by the royal family of Havrrel while Havrgan was raised by his relatives on Tevlan. Longing for a life of adventure and purpose, Daelthos joined the Uprising and began learning the ways of the Synth Flux under the guidance of Salphos Master Faelnor Halthossul, whose first apprentice was Havrgan's own father. During the Battle of Kathasnor, Daelthos saved the Alliance from annihilation by destroying the Dominion's planet-killing superweapon, the Void Core. He continued his training in the years that followed, determined to become a Salphos Knight like his father before him, and found a new mentor in Grand Master Halrel. After his master's death, Daelthos participated in the Battle of Wynthas in 4 AKV, during which he confronted the Zargoth Lord Ursera Garvash, whom he learned was in fact his father, Yarwyn Daelthos. With Havrgan's help, Yarwyn returned to the radiant side of the Synth Flux by killing the Emperor and Dark Lord of the Zargoth Ursera Bregoth at the cost of his own life, fulfilling his destiny as the Chosen One. Following the Battle of Wynthas, Daelthos trained his sister as a first Salphos apprentice on Urria Irvoksul, but Vegoth ended her training after the birth of her son and seeing his death as a result of finishing her knighthood.

With the Zargoth's destruction and the subsequent capitulation of the Dominion, Daelthos became a living legend as he traveled across the galaxy during Renewed Concord Era, searching for knowledge that would aid him in rebuilding the Salphos Order, and serving the Renewed Concord and its allies where he could. As the war raged for a year after Wynthas, Daelthos was heavily involved with the Renewed Concord's efforts against self-declared Grand Prefect Quinrax Quarelphen in the Korgoth sector, before defeating the madman at the Battle of Caedunkrix. Around 6 AKV, Daelthos committed himself to the liberation of the Daelyn and their homeworld Breul from beneath the heel of their enslavers. Later, in 9 AKV, Daelthos rescued Zunlgor, a Lunseran Flux-sensitive child that belonged to the same species as Halrel, from Prefect Cyrtos's forces, and after temporarily taking the child as an apprentice on Tevul. During the training, Daelthos was visited by Yarwyn's former novice, Tevzek Caenor, where they discussed Zunlgor and how Daelthos sensed that the child's heart was not in relearning the Salphos path. After Zunlgor's caregiver, Esketh Vashon, visited the planet bearing a gift for Zunlgor, Daelthos offered him with a choice, accept the gift and return to the Lunseran, or take Halrel's plasmablade and continue with his Salphos training. Zunlgor chose to return to Vashon, while Daelthos bid Caenor farewell as she departed for ways unknown.

By 15 AKV, Daelthos had taken on Zunria Sallgor, his sister's son who inherited the family bloodline in the Synth Flux and had the potential for limitless power, as an apprentice. While both traveled to Bredris with the explorer Jaxrn Dralume Zunvok to study lost Salphos lore, Sallgor began to be groomed by Lorrel, a humanoid being powerful in the Synth Flux. Upon the planet, the trio first encountered the Knights of Daelven. In 21 AKV, Daelthos started feeling an increasing darkness in the Synth Flux, having dark visions that frequently came to him in meditation, and undertook a quest to find the hidden world of Baellan. Daelthos journeyed to Havrvael, where he saw a vision of Baellan and faced nine wraiths when the Synth Flux ghost of Yarwyn Daelthos appeared next to him. Following this, Daelthos was able to defeat Rhorel and the spirit of Hythos Wenphen but was ultimately unable to locate Baellan after failing to track down an assassin called Gisvok.

In 28 AKV, sensing the extent of Lorrel's corruption in Sallgor and glimpsing a dark future, Daelthos momentarily contemplated killing his nephew to prevent it, yet immediately reconsidered upon igniting his plasmablade. The rash action had devastating effect, though, completing Sallgor's fall to the umbral side. The destruction of his temple and the deaths of all of his students convinced a haunted and regretful Daelthos that the time had come for the Salphos to end. Daelthos therefore sought exile on the distant world of Lornor, having elected to live out his remaining days as a havrkern despite the galaxy's pleas for help during the rise of the Ascendant Directorate. His solitude was interrupted in 34 AKV, shortly after the fall of the Renewed Concord, resulting in a chain of events that led Daelthos to play one last, momentous role in the conflict between light and darkness. Although he passed away into the Synth Flux, his legend would spread throughout the galaxy, rekindling a spark of hope in the enemies of the Ascendant Directorate. Before his death, Daelthos declared that the Salphos Order would not die with him, having trained a final apprentice: the Caedunkrix scavenger known as Ithlgor.

A year later, Daelthos's spirit communed with Ithlgor during the final days of the war. The Salphos apprentice had exiled herself to Lornor, following in Daelthos's example. However, he urged Ithlgor to confront her grandfather, the resurrected Ursera Bregoth. Believing he had been wrong, Daelthos told Ithlgor that a Salphos's destiny was to confront fear. Following the Emperor's destruction, the spirits of Daelthos and his sister watched over Ithlgor as she buried their plasmablades in Daelthos's childhood home on Tevlan and adopted the Daelthos name as her own, honoring their legacy and recognizing herself as a member of her chosen family.

## Biography

### Itheth-birth

During the Echo Wars, Salphos Master Halrel set out on a journey to discover the secrets of immortality. His journey took him to Faelkrix, the ancient Zargoth homeworld, where he faced visions of the future. At the end of his journey, the Synth Flux Priestess, Korlis, who guided his journey told him that there was another Daelthos, and Halrel heard his voice from the future say so as well. These events alluded to the birth and destiny of Havrgan Daelthos, as well as his twin sister Vegoth.

### Early life

#### Birth

After his father, the former Salphos Knight Yarwyn Daelthos, had fallen to the umbral side of the Synth Flux and injured his wife, the senator Nymund Daelphos, she was taken to the asteroid field of Vashkrix Fykrixria by Salphos Master Faelnor Halthossul to seek medical attention. In the surgical suite of the medical facility on Vashkrix Fykrixria Base, Daelphos was dying despite being otherwise healthy. Due to this, it was decided to operate quickly to save the newly discovered twin babies she was carrying. Havrgan Daelthos was born on 19 BKV, two days after the proclamation of the Vastrian Dominion. He came out of the womb crying, and in her last dying breaths, Daelphos gave him the name Havrgan. She stroked Daelthos's arm before giving birth seconds later to his sister, who Daelphos named Vegoth. To protect the children from the Dominion and their father, the now-Zargoth Lord Ursera Garvash, it was decided that the twins should be split up. Faelnor Halthossul volunteered to take the infant Havrgan to be raised by Yarwyn's stepbrother, Vashyn Lunlgor and his wife, Mormir on the planet Tevlan, while Senator Vashdren Faelvok agreed to adopt Vegoth together with his wife, the queen of Havrrel. Halthossul boarded the late General Caephen's starwing, the Ythzek One, once more flew to the planet Ophthil Rhovashyn and sold it there to transport Havrgan Daelthos to Tevlan. There, Halthossul bought an dunebeast and rode it the Lunlgor homestead, giving the swaddled baby to Mormir Lunlgor. She brings the baby to her husband Vashyn, who fauns over the child before looking out to the twin sunset of Tevlan.

#### First years on the farm

Havrgan Daelthos spent his youth on the Lunlgor' moisture farm, where his uncle unsuccessfully tried to discourage his adventurous tendencies, telling him that his father had been "a navigator on a spice freighter" during the Echo Wars. His aunt, Mormir, would read him storybooks. One day, when his uncle had business in Mardren Ythzor, she took Daelthos to Giskrix to use the DataWeb connections there and show him images of forests, rivers, and oceans.
