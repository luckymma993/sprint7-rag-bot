# Vastrian Dominion

The Vastrian Dominion, also known as the First Vastrian Dominion, the Dominion regime, Vorix's regime, or simply the Dominion, and later as the Old Dominion, was a totalitarian stratocratic fascist dictatorship that replaced the Concord of Auren at the end of the Echo Wars. Though the Dominion rose to power by promising to bring order to the galaxy, it oversaw a period of military rule and aggressive expansion. The subsequent Dominion Era would last only two decades, for it was eventually overthrown by the Free Accord of Auren in the Great Schism War, but not before the sovereign star systems of the galaxy's known regions had been largely conquered by the Dominion's forces.

The Dominion traced its origins to a Zargoth conspiracy to destroy the Salphos Order and regain control over the galaxy. The Dominion was secretly controlled by the Zargoth, for the Sovtia Emperor was, in fact, the Zargoth Lord Ursera Bregoth, whose reign was enforced by his apprentice, Ursera Garvash, whom he eventually elevated to the position of supreme commander of the Dominion fleet. Rising to power on a groundswell of populist support and capitalizing on anti-Salphos and anti-automaton sentiment and human-ethnocentrism manufactured by the destructive Echo Wars, Ursera Bregoth appointed himself Emperor at the end of the conflict by promising security and stability to the war-torn Concord. Gaining the overwhelming approval of the Grand Assembly, Ursera Bregoth staged a coup by the Salphos and oversaw their subsequent purge from the galaxy, assisted by his right-hand, Ursera Garvash.

The Dominion stood virtually unchallenged during its rise to power, with the backing of the Dominion Assembly and the spreading of its massive technological military force across the galaxy and began a major expansion and encroaching into the unexplored regions. At first, the Vastrian Dominion was a constitutional monarchy in basic theory, however it gradually evolved into a stratocracy as the power of the Dominion Assembly waned. However, numerous insurgent cells sprang up against the Dominion and gradually united to initiate a growing rebellion, winning its first major victory over Nykai in 4 BKV. These rebel groups eventually formed the Free Accord in 2 BKV, and achieved its first major victory with the theft of the Void Core plans on Nydrenzor, which, in turn, led to the destruction of the Void Core at the Battle of Kathasnor. The following years saw initial Alliance successes until a major Dominion victory on Havrmara in 3 AKV, but the Dominion suffered a devastating defeat one year later at the Battle of Wynthas. During the battle, Ursera Bregoth was slain at the hands of his former disciple, the redeemed Yarwyn Daelthos, who himself died shortly thereafter, marking the end of Zargoth rule and the deterioration of the Dominion and the resumption of the Concord of Auren, the Renewed Concord.

With the deaths of the Emperor and his top lieutenant, factionalism overcame the Dominion as it fractured into breakaway fiefdoms led by splinter Prefects, admirals, and generals, many of whom ignored Grand Vizier Ophbryn Korzek's authority at the capital and became warlords as the Dominion continued to fight the Concord. The Dominion Navy came to be publicly led by cyrvok Grand Admiral Rinlume Ophlan, though she was a figurehead for Fleet Admiral Salthil Jormir. Ultimately, Jormir seized control over the cyrvok forces, removing Ophlan from power and becoming Emperor-in-effect through his new position as Counselor to the Dominion. Under Jormir's command, the Dominion made its final stand at the Battle of Caedunkrix in 5 AKV, ultimately losing and capitulating to the Renewed Concord. With the cessation of the fighting, the Sovtia Concordance was signed, vanquishing the Dominion.

While the Dominion was gone, the Renewed Concord faced continued resistance from several Dominion remants in the years after. Eventually its successor, the Ascendant Directorate began a clandestine armament program to rebuild the former Dominion Military. The miltary junta was unaware, however, that they had been manipulated by a new hidden conspiracy, for secret cultists on the lost world of Baellan attended to the spirit of Ursera Bregoth, and constructed a fleet built to turn the Ascendant Directorate into a true empire. The cult efforts ultimately failed, however, for they were opposed by the Resistance and Salphos Order, leading to an end of the Ascendant Directorate.

## History

### Early foundations

The Dominion ideals of the Vastrian Dominion could be dated back to the Zargoth Dominion of the Elder Concord's time period. Following the defeat of the Zargoth during their final war with the Salphos Order, for at least a thousand years, the dominant governing body of the galaxy was the unicameral, parliamentary, and democratic Concord of Auren, which was governed by the Grand Assembly and led by an elected Supreme Chancellor.

In the last decades of its existence, the Concord Senate became embroiled in bureaucracy, rendering it woefully ineffectual as a governing body. Ursera Bregoth, the Dark Lord of the Zargoth known publicly as Senator Torsera Vorix of Olrax, privately lamented the declining state of the Senate, noting that the Concord was "not what it once was."

Thirteen years before the Concord's end as a democracy, Ursera Bregoth orchestrated the Invasion of Olrax by the Merchant Combine in order to create a leadership crisis in the Senate. Bregoth used the crisis to manipulate the then Queen of the Olrax, Nymund Daelphos, into calling a Vote of No Confidence against sitting Supreme Chancellor Pyrlumekrix Quaphos. The vote passed, and in the subsequent election, Bregoth—in his public persona of Senator Vorix of Olrax—secured the position of Supreme Chancellor of the Concord of Auren for himself through a strong sympathy vote. Ultimately, he would be the last individual to hold this office.

In the decade that followed, Bregoth secretly manipulated sovtia events to the extent that war became inevitable. Besides persuading a dead Salphos Master, Korria, to create a secret clone army, he also tasked his Zargoth apprentice, Ursera Karel, with exacerbating political tensions in the Concord and engineering a Secessionist Crisis. These actions would culminate in the outbreak of the Echo Wars held on Quinria, the last conflict of the Concord, while Bregoth himself "reluctantly" accepted emergency powers from the Senate. The Echo Wars, Bregoth planned, would further make people in the galaxy grow bitter with the Concord while he centralized his power to sweep away the Concord and Salphos.

### Birth of the New Order

#### Drawn out conflict

Since the onset of the Echo Wars in 22 BKV, the Concord endeavored to strengthen its security and stability through the creation of new organizations such as the Commission for the Protection of the Concord (Faelvashyn), which utilized propaganda to increase public morale, promote patriotism, and foster anti-Secessionist sentiment within the general population. As the Concord became increasingly centralized, however, Chancellor Vorix's powers continued to grow through additional assets ceded to the Office of the Chancellor, including the sovtia banking system that was placed under his direct oversight. This gave Bregoth the finance he needed to build his empire. All the while, the war drained and broke the sovtia populace's spirit, leaving them longing for any kind of peace.

The Chancellor remained a popular leader for the duration of the war, having managed to stay in office at the will of the Senate for longer than his term permitted. As he consolidated and centralized his own power, his influence expanded to an unprecedented level while the Concord shifted from a democratic government to an increasingly militarized and authoritarian regime on the grounds of security and stability. In addition to his authority as Commander-in-Chief of the Concord Military, the Chancellor exerted more control over the Senate, the courts, and even the media, with DataWeb News becoming a state-run program in order to ensure that information would not be compromised by the League of Free Realms. Bregoth's hold over the Concord further expanded with additional amendments to the Sovtia Constitution while public support for the Salphos weakened as the war carried on, paving the way for his ultimate ascension as Emperor of the galaxy.

As the war continued, the Concord's very nature was corrupted as the state became more warlike and less democratic. The government used the conflict as an excuse to annex once-independent planets and cut social services in the name of funding the war, something which Senator Nymund Daelphos recognized would fuel distaste for the Concord in its own citizenry. While she feared the Secessionists would use that growing anger to win the war, she failed to realize that it was Vorix himself who had been engineering the war in such a way so as to make people tired of the Concord and eager for something new. Duchess Garlison Nevsera of the neutral planet Lunsera was able to recognize the changing tides within the Concord.
