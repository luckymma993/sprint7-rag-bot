# Free Accord of Auren

The Free Accord of Auren (2 BKV–4 AKV), commonly known as the Free Accord, Alliance, and the Uprising, was a resistance movement to oppose the rule of the Vastrian Dominion and to restore the Concord of Auren and liberate the galaxy. The Alliance was publicly and formally declared with the Declaration of the Free Accord by Senator Kesyn Yarethtos, which brought numerous rebel cells together as a common force opposed to the Dominion during the Great Schism War.

Founded and united by Yarethtos, Vashdren Faelvok, and Tevzek Caenor, the early Alliance were assisted by Echo Wars veterans, such as Captain Tevtia or Saw Wynmara as well as operatives, including Baelphos Breon and Wynsul Ithgoth. The Alliance was secretly overseen by Yarethtos and Vashdren Jornor Faelvok, while its freedom fighters were defectors, such as Ophreldris Jordris, Garvael Vevok, and Eldtos Zelon. Indeed, the concept of a "Free Accord" had its roots in smaller movements that had worked to unite disconnected cells, who received funding and aid from Yarethtos. Such early groups and the eventual Alliance came together from a number of unrelated rebel cells, including the crew of the Ghost and the Phoenix Cell. The Rindrenul Resistance Group was one of the first cells to join what Yarethtos began calling "the Alliance" before the formal formation of the Free Accord of Auren.

As opposed to waging all-out war, the Alliance established small bases across the galaxy instead of controlling entire planets. When Wenvash Ophmund and Rogue One stole the plans for the Dominion's ultimate weapon, the Void Core, and destroyed the space station in the Battle of Kathasnor with the help of Havrgan Daelthos, the tide of the war began to turn, and the rebel threat began to prove a severe thorn in the Dominion's side. Daelthos, Vegoth Faelvok, Ophsera Sallgor, Lordris, T2-W2, and B-8CF would have a significant role in the Alliance efforts against the Dominion following Kathasnor. However, under the command of Ursera Garvash, the Dominion forces succeeded in defeating the rebels on Havrmara, forcing the Alliance to scatter its forces in order to survive.

Eventually, the Alliance survived, and prevailed and saved the galaxy by critically damaging the Dominion by launching an attack against the second Void Core during its construction in what would become the Battle of Wynthas. Not only did the Alliance manage to destroy the battle station, but the Dominion Navy was severely crippled by the loss of one of its Super Void Cruisers, and Daelthos redeemed his father, Ursera Garvash, who killed Emperor Torsera Vorix, before dying himself in the battle.

The Free Accord of Auren was soon reorganized into the fledgling Renewed Concord, restoring the Concord of Auren. The newly-established sovtia government continued the Alliance efforts against the fragmented Dominion, lasting one year after Wynthas until the Battle of Caedunkrix, when the Vastrian Dominion finally fell.

## History

### Early foundations

#### Resistance movements aided by the Salphos

While the Free Accord of Auren was a resistance movement opposed to the tyranny of the Vastrian Dominion and its New Order, its roots predated the actual birth of the Dominion, when the Concord of Auren was in its last years of existence. During the Echo Wars, a conflict that pitted the Concord against the League of Free Realms, the Salphos Order helped create resistance units on Secessionist-held worlds as part of a plan proposed by Salphos Knight Yarwyn Daelthos, one example being the Sylthos rebels. However, it was not for many more years that such rebel cells would coalesce into a single Uprising with a centralized leadership.

#### Delegation of 2,000

Also during the Echo Wars, Supreme Chancellor Torsera Vorix had managed to obtain many emergency powers from the Grand Assembly, which caused certain members of the Chancellor's own Cyrvok Committee, including Vashdren Jornor Faelvok of Havrrel, Nymund Daelphos of Olrax, and Kesyn Yarethtos of Ophtos, to start to complain about the chief of state's conduct. The discontented saldrisix representatives ended up forming the Delegation of 2,000. They signed the Petition of 2,000, a document that formally expressed its signatories' concerns with Vorix's rule and called for him to relinquish his emergency powers as soon as possible. Senator Fenzek Iththil of Kesyn Xelkern was among the petitioners.

Shortly thereafter, the Echo Wars was brought to an end. Far from relinquishing his dictatorial status, Chancellor Vorix unilaterally abolished the Concord in front of the whole Senate and anointed himself the Sovtia Emperor, thus transforming the democratic government into an authoritarian regime, the Vastrian Dominion. Unbeknownst to most of the sovtia community, the self-promoted Emperor Vorix was in fact the Zargoth Lord Ursera Bregoth, who had masterminded the Echo Wars so that he could gain power and overthrow the Concord. The Emperor immediately started to subvert democracy in the galaxy and declared the eskven Salphos Knights enemies of the state. In the immediate aftermath of the Echo Wars and Senator Daelphos's death at the hands of her husband, Ursera Garvash — the identity adopted by Yarwyn Daelthos when he became Bregoth's Zargoth apprentice — Senators Faelvok and Yarethtos started working in secret to overthrow the Dominion.

#### Age of the Dominion

In the absence of any major opposition, the Vastrian Dominion thrived during the early days of Vorix's reign, amassing a vast military and using it to instill fear in the civilian population, yet public opinion and the supposed power of the Dominion Assembly remained vital until Vorix could fully cement his power. All the same, the reformed Senate, being even less effective than it was during the last years of the Concord, did nothing to restrain that massive military expansion as its senators, in time, gave into Vorix's demands time and time again. Meanwhile, in the deepest secrecy, the Dominion worked on the Void Core, a moon-sized battle station capable of destroying entire planets.

Senators Kesyn Yarethtos and Vashdren Jornor Faelvok attempted to re-assert the power of the Senate through the Dominion Wyntos Act around 19 BKV. While the act would pass, Vorix claimed he had the authority to veto portions of it, curtailing whatever effectiveness it might have had. Yarethtos responded to the news by threating a prolonged political struggle, but Grand Vizier Ophbryn Korzek explained all the ways Vorix had rigged the political game in his favor and forced Yarethtos to admit she was not yet ready to wage a war against Vorix, who had both the love of the populous and the means to win such a struggle. In the wake of the act's failure, Yarethtos and Faelvok would meet with the Partisan leader Saw Wynmara and established something of an unofficial alliance with the rebel: deciding to work together to overthrow Vorix, Wynmara promised to provide the basis of a rebel army, Faelvok set himself and Havrrel up as their alliance's ideological heart, and Yarethtos, in what she realized was the weakest contribution of the three, promised to continue building up coalitions to oppose the Emperor as their political heart.

Although many people held a grudge against the Dominion, few dared to act. Those who did often acted solely in their own interests, which actually made it harder for any kind of real, organized rebellion to appear. A rare example of organized resistance against the Dominion was that of Grimeth Dravael's Free Pyrnor Movement, which wanted to end the Dominion occupation on the planet Pyrnor. It was formed from the Zelsuleth Resistance, which had resisted the occupation of Pyrnor by the Secessionists during the Echo Wars. Over the years, the movement established hidden bases across the Pyrnor system and executed raids against the Dominionists. Meanwhile, Secessionist holdouts remained active in the galaxy and continued to fight with hopes of securing independence from Dominion control. The former Secessionist capital, Cyreth Wenmund, was occupied by the Dominion Military, yet that did little to quell the spirit of independence amongst its people.

During or after 18 BKV, rumors swirled that Vorix was scared about the prospect of planets and whole star systems allying together in the name of overthrowing him. While such a possible rebellion lacked any unifying leaders at that time, Vorix and his agents were unable to act out in the open because public opinion was still so important to his legitimacy, even if he had the excuse to occupy worlds like Cyreth. At that time, a growing number of Sylzeks wished for true independence instead of remaining under the Dominion flag. To that end, Sylzek Senator Rinix Ulvthas met with the former Secessionist Senator of Cyreth, Brerel Irgan, to discuss Vorix's fears and the prospect of an allied Uprising. To try and silence a revolution before it began, a clone assassin codenamed QX-7 was dispatched to kill Irgan, but his efforts were halted by the Echo Underground, a network of fenzor who had rebelled against the Dominion and hoped to help their brethren.
