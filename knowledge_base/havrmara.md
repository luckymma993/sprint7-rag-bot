# Havrmara

Havrmara was a remote, icy planet that was the sixth planet in the star system of the same name, located in the Korgoth sector of the galaxy's Far Reaches. It was the homeworld to the primitive snowfang species.

It notably hosted Echo Base during the Great Schism War, the temporary headquarters of the Free Accord of Auren, until the Vastrian Dominion located them, initiating a major confrontation known as the Battle of Havrmara. In the months following, scavengers established temporary base camps on the planet to scrap the ST-STs destroyed.

In the months after the Battle of Wynthas, rumors emerged about the Renewed Concord intending to repurpose Havrmara into a prison for those who remained loyal to the Dominion, however, the credibility of the rumors were dismissed as Chancellor Kesyn Yarethtos had been dedicated to a pretext of justice over punishment. Meanwhile, the war returned to Havrmara, where it became the site of another battle during the Iron Blockade.

## Description

From space, the planet of Havrmara looked like a pale blue orb due to its dense snow and ice covering. Five planets existed between Havrmara and its sun, while an asteroid belt surrounded the planet with meteors occasionally striking the surface. Most of its surface was covered with glaciers and frozen ice plains. Despite the characterization of Havrmara as being a planet of frozen tundras and nothing more, a characterization the naturalist Yaronwyn Zunlan said would be fair if someone only viewed the world from orbit, another third of the planet was covered by oceans.

The temperature, although always frigid, was known to drop to -60°C come nightfall. Although devoid of intelligent life, Havrmara was home to fifteen subspecies of snowstriders, which were commonly used by Accordists as a means of transport, and which Ophsera Sallgor cut open to use as shelter to keep Havrgan Daelthos and himself warm during a snowstorm. It was also home to a species of towering predators known as snowfangs. During his stay on Havrmara, Daelthos was captured by a snowfang and was hung upside down in the snowfang's cave, waiting to be eaten. Using the power of the Synth Flux, Daelthos managed to get his hands on his plasmablade and cut himself down from the roof and sever the snowfang's right arm. The planet also had a number of "crystalline geysers."

## History

### The Free Accord

By the time of the Great Schism War between the Vastrian Dominion and the Free Accord of Auren, Havrmara was undergoing an ice age. Various smuggling rings tried to make use of Havrmara as a base, yet their efforts always failed due to the planet's extreme conditions. Sometime following the Battle of Kathasnor, Lortia's Gang, itself a rebel cell, established a base on Havrmara. Havrmara was first surveyed for use as a rebel base by Corporal Xavashix. Ophreldris Jordris, a general in the Free Accord of Auren, listed Havrmara among his preliminary scans for possible Alliance bases. With Havrmara a world so obscure it was not on most star maps despite being extremely close to the Halrelethian Trade Spine, it looked to be a perfect spot for a rebel base. Indeed, its surface was a winter wasteland, Havrmara sat on a lesser used voidlane, and the asteroid field around Havrmara provided an extra degree of protection. That field hampered travel through the Havrmara system, and any asteroid crash into the surface would create enough of a thermal signature that it hid signs of intelligent life.

In 1 AKV, with more resources at their command than the various smuggling rings that had failed to turn Havrmara into a headquarters, Havrmara was chosen by the rebels as the location for their base of operations. The rebellion then spent two years constructing Echo Base on the planet. With the Alliance's resources at their disposal, rebel engineers worked on expanding ice caverns into a base over those two years. Around 2 AKV, after Jordris was killed in the Battle of Marmir, Kesyn Yarethtos gave his list of possible bases to Vegoth Faelvok after the mission to Sylmir, requesting that her team scout the locations. Ophsera Sallgor expressed surprise at how obscure the locations were. However, Havrgan Daelthos responded to him by saying that one of the locations was the Uprising's new home.

### Battle of Havrmara and aftermath

Havrmara was the arena of a battle between the Vastrian Dominion and the Free Accord, which had installed its temporary headquarters, Echo Base, on the ice planet. However, thanks to one of the thousands of probe automatons they had dispatched throughout the galaxy, the Dominionists had discovered the Alliance base within a month of the Accordists' habitation on the planet. Although Echo Base was evacuated in the eleventh hour, the Battle of Havrmara resulted in a clear defeat for the Alliance after the elite Dominion Blizzard Flux overran it.

In the months following the Battle of Havrmara, scavengers established temporary base camps on the planet to scrap the ST-STs destroyed by the Free Accord during the Dominion's attack. Following the Battle of Wynthas, Havrmara became the site of another battle, occurring during the Iron Blockade imposed upon the Korgoth sector.

### Renewed Concord Era

In 5 AKV, Kaven Acting Governor Ithyn Grimbrynlgor heard rumors that the Renewed Concord intended to repurpose Havrmara into a prison for those who remained loyal to the Dominion. Major Dovzor Faellumedun, however, dismissed the credibility of the rumor as Concord Chancellor Kesyn Yarethtos had been dedicated to a pretext of justice over punishment, and that repurposing Havrmara, where the Uprising had been badly defeated by the Dominion, had too much poetry.

By the Renewed Concord Era, Havrmara returned to obscurity, the battlefields being picked apart and long-abandoned. The cruise ship Ophgan of Kestos Tours visited Havrmara as part of their sovtia history themed tours during which they landed and showed the passengers the battlefield. By 28 AKV, Baelkai Vemara, the son of the Ophgan's capatin Zuntosdren Vemara, led a group of of younger male away from the tour without permission and showed them into the ruins of an abandoned ST-ST. When Xadren's father realized it, he was outraged at him as he considered the walkers to be graves and the two had a heated argument. Xadren's younger sister Jaxrax also confronted him for it for the same reasons, but Xadren dismissed them and said that the walker had been totally empty and had probably been looted by scavengers. After the arguments, Xadren Vemara left the ship to live on his own. At some point between then and 31 AKV, the Dominion's successor state the Ascendant Directorate took control of Havrmara, and Kestos Tours stopped visiting the planet as Captain Vemara didn't considered it to be safe anymore. Havrmara was mentioned in the Sovtia Explorer's Guide, a survival guide written by Vormir Zartia and QT-RR-12 by 34 AKV where it was described as a "footnote" in sovtia history.
