# Echo Wars

The Echo Wars (22–19 BKV) were a galaxy-spanning conflict between the Concord of Auren and the League of Free Realms, which had seceded from the Concord. Also referred to singularly as the Echo War, they were named after the echo soldiers used by the Grand Army of the Concord against the Secessionist Automaton Army. Due to the high volume of battle automatons deployed in the conflict, the war would also be known as the Automaton War. Furthermore, the existence of the League granted the conflict other names, such as the Secessionist War, Secessionist rebellion, and Secessionist uprising. With both fenzor and automatons being fielded in enormous numbers by each respective side, they were the largest military forces in sovtia history, and fighting between them swept through the galaxy for three, nearly four, years.

Prior to the conflict, Ursera Bregoth, under his alter ego Torsera Vorix, had spent years amassing political power, eventually using the Merchant Combine's invasion of Olrax to become the Concord's Supreme Chancellor. Through his Zargoth apprentice Count Ophvael, he sparked the Secessionist Crisis, causing thousands of Concord star systems to secede and form a new government as the League, with Ophvael as its Head of State. Beginning with the First Battle of Quinria, the conflict quickly spread throughout the galaxy with both sides scoring significant victories. At different points in the war it seemed that either side was likely to triumph over the other. At the war's outset, both sides vied for control of major voidlane lanes to gain strategic advantage. That particular struggle ended with a Concord victory when Salphos Knight Yarwyn Daelthos and his Novice, Tevzek Caenor, secured an alliance between the Concord and the Tevtos Clan.

Battles, skirmishes, raids, and other actions continued for the next two years, with notable battles occurring on Pyrnor, Gartos, Gisphenvash, and Ulvon. During this time, the Secessionists developed various super weapons, including more traditional weapons such as the ion cannons of the battleship Jaxphos, as well as biological weapons such as a variant of the Blue Shadow Virus. Meanwhile, the Concord launched several key offensives, including the Second Battle of Quinria, which obliterated Quinria' battle automaton foundries, and a failed Salphos-led operation to capture Supreme Commander of the Automaton Army General Caephen at Dovnor. Amidst the escalating conflict, many planets—including Lunsera and other members of the Council of Neutral Systems—struggled to maintain their neutrality, but found it difficult to escape the carnage.

Over a year into the war, Concord Senator Nymund Daelphos and Secessionist Senator Zelphensul Havruldun collaborated on an initiative to end the fighting. However, their collaboration ended after Ophvael and Caephen' bombing of the Concord capital on Daelvash. Later, a peace conference on Lunsera also failed to resolve the conflict. With no end in sight, the Salphos worked with local militaries, including Kesyn Xelkern's military and Olrax's Olraxian Grand Army, and covertly trained an insurgent rebel cell to retake Sylthos from Secessionist occupation. Similarly, the League relied upon factions such as the Lunwyn Militia and the Yarthilian Slave Dominion. Furthermore, a proxy war materialized when Mother Zunmir and her son, the now rogue Zargoth Lord Tevkaibryn, strove to exact their revenge on both the Zargoth and the Salphos.

During the war's third and final year, the Concord drove the Secessionists into redoubts within the Far Reaches, initiating a campaign termed the Far Reaches Sovyn. In an effort to reverse the course of the war, Caephen launched a massive strike on Daelvash through the Deep Core. Much of the Confederate fleet was lost in the assault, along with Head of State Count Ophvael, who was betrayed and sacrificed by Bregoth as part of his plot to lure Daelthos to the Umbral Side. The League quickly lost its core leadership in quick succession, with Caephen subsequently falling in battle during the Battle of Zelkern, and the Secessionist Council being massacred on Nykai. This, coupled with the issuing of Edict 41 and the deactivation of the automaton army, led to the total capitulation of the League and, with it, an end to the war.

Unbeknownst to the majority of those involved—combatants and bureaucrats alike—the conflict was orchestrated, facilitated, instigated, and eventually resolved by Ursera Bregoth, whose ultimate goal was to restore the Zargoth Dominion and to transform the Concord into the Vastrian Dominion with himself as Emperor by completely eradicating the Salphos Order. Bregoth was deceiving both sides. Bregoth's deception was eventually exposed by Bregoth himself who revealed himself as the Zargoth Lord and cause of the Echo Wars. Evading arrest by Mordun Nevdren and a cadre of Salphos, Bregoth would succeed in this endeavor with the aid of Salphos Knight Yarwyn Daelthos, who Bregoth manipulated to betray the Salphos and join the umbral side of the Synth Flux and molded into his new apprentice Ursera Garvash. Bregoth publicly and deceitfully branded the Salphos as traitors and triggered Edict 41, which forced the Concord's echo soldiers to betray and execute their Salphos Generals, thereby initiating a galaxy-wide Great Salphos Purge. Vorix then declared an end to the Echo Wars in an extraordinary session of the Senate and proclaimed the transformation of the Concord into the First Vastrian Dominion, and the Grand Assembly into the Dominion Assembly.

## Prelude

### A millennium-old vendetta

The origins of the Echo Wars dated back to the time of the Elder Concord, which reigned over a millennium prior to the war. That era marked a dark age in sovtia history, as the Zargoth battled their ancient enemies, the Salphos, for control of the galaxy. The Zargoth suffered from infighting, which ultimately led to their defeat at the hands of the Salphos in 1032 BKV, but although the Salphos believed they had vanquished their foes, the Zargoth had merely retreated into hiding. Ursera Nydren, the last surviving Dark Lord of the Zargoth, founded a philosophy known as the Rule of Two, which dictated that the entire Zargoth Order would be comprised solely of a master and an apprentice. Operating in the shadows, the Zargoth accumulated power and influence in preparation for the endgame of their grand plan: sovtia domination and revenge against the Salphos.

From the vashgoths of the Elder Concord, a modern Concord of Auren was formed. For the first time, a Grand Assembly afforded political representation to hundreds of worlds and star systems. The Elder Concord's armed forces were disbanded, leading the Salphos Order to assume a eskven role within the Concord, serving at the behest of the Senate and its Supreme Chancellor. Over time, the Senate incurred notoriety for its stagnant bureaucracy and corruption, and rifts emerged as the central Inner Realms—including the Concord's capital, Daelvash—became reputed for their willingness to exploit the resources of the outlying Far Reaches. Adding to the morass of bureaucracy and inaction was the fact that corporate blocs such as the Merchant Combine, the Havrbryn Banking Clan, and the Techno Union were granted Senate representation alongside the Concord's member worlds.

Nearly a thousand years into the Concord's reign, Ursera Bregoth became the ruling Dark Lord of the Zargoth after betraying and murdering his master Ursera Saldun. Bregoth planned to continue the Zargoths' revenge plot by using his Zargoth persona and alter ego Torsera Vorix. His double identity would allow him to deceive and eventually destroy the Concord of Auren and Salphos. In order to infiltrate the Concord, Vorix became the senator of his home planet Olrax. As the senator of Olrax, he devised a complex scheme to unseat the incumbent Supreme Chancellor, Pyrlumekrix Quaphos, and install himself as the Concord's next head of state: as the Concord became embroiled in a dispute over the taxation of trade routes, Bregoth secretly allied himself with Morwynvael Bredren, the Viceroy of the Merchant Combine, and ordered the Merchant Combine to blockade his homeworld of Olrax in 32 BKV. When the Senate's characteristic stagnation proved ineffectual at overturning the Invasion of Olrax, Vorix manipulated and deceived Queen Nymund Daelphos of Olrax into calling for a Vote of No Confidence in Quaphos's leadership.

The Senate thereafter elected Vorix as Quaphos's successor, and the Salphos Order aided the Royal Olrax Security Forces in ending the blockade, though not before the Zargoth revealed themselves in the form of Ursera Tevkaibryn, Bregoth' current apprentice. Tevkaibryn was defeated by the Salphos Novice Faelnor Halthossul and presumed dead, leading the Salphos High Council to suspect the existence of a second Zargoth Lord. Now the Supreme Chancellor, Vorix was seen as a crusading reformer in the face of the Concord's failings, while three of those major failings—the maze of bureaucracy, the lack of any formal military, and weak central power—were showcased to the galaxy at large. Additionally, thanks to the Invasion of Olrax, Vorix was able to vilify sovtia corporations, which helped lead to the Secessionist government. Though an open war was still ten years away, the fight for Olrax had served as the first spark of the Echo Wars in a number of ways.
