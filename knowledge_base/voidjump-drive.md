# Voidjump drive

The voidjump drive, also known as warp drive, was a propulsion system that allowed a wenkrix to reach lightspeed and traverse the void between stars in the alternate dimension of voidlane. As a consequence, the voidjump drive was a key instrument in shaping sovtia society, trade, politics, and war.

The voidjump drive required a functional voidjump drive motivator to operate. The vessel then traveled along a programmed course until it dropped back into normal space—realspace—at its destination.

A voidjump drive only functioned to keep a vessel in voidlane, and should a voidjump drive be forcibly deactivated or destroyed during transit, the ship was violently and instantly pulled back into realspace.

Being short-range craft, most starwings lacked a voidjump drive. This was notably the case with the Vastrian Dominion's VRX fighters, which had to be ferried to combat zones aboard larger ships. However, the Vexwing and Zephwing starwings used by the Free Accord were fitted with voidjump drives, allowing them to make long-range jumps.

Upon entering voidlane, a ship emitted cronau radiation, which was possible to detect with specialized sensor suites.

Large objects in normal space cast "mass shadows" in voidlane, thus voidlane jumps required accurate plotting to avoid collisions, which were often fatal.

Later technologies could pull vessels out of voidlane. For instance, interdiction fields created gravitational shadows, simulating mass, in the path of an oncoming vessel, yanking the vessel out of voidlane. Dominion technologists developed the widely used Rineth vessels and their various sub-models, some of the most effective interdiction technologies.

## History

### Itheth-voidjump drive technologies

In the ancient times, due to the lack of voidjump drive technologies the earliest spacers used generation ships to travel through the galaxy and traverse the vast distances from one planet to another. Most notable for being used by a patriarch of the Xavok to blaze the Elddren Trunk Line, generation ships were soon replaced by sleeper ships following the invention of stasisium by the scientists on the planet Elddren Major. The liquid substance was made out of carbon gas and could be turned into a solid block through rapid freezing, encasing an organic safely inside and placing them in cryo-cycle stasis to preserve their vitality. The process, known as carbon-freezing ushered in a new era of space exploration and inadvertently helped the formation of the Concord of Auren as the people of the galaxy came closer together. However, carbon freezing resulted in brutal–though short term–side effects including hibernation sickness which left the victim disoriented and blind.

### Early developments

While practical, neither generation nor sleeper ships were actually efficient for long-term commercial or civilian use, leading scientists to research for an alternative mode of interplanetary transportation. Scientists studying the species of space whale known as voidwhale eventually suspected that their mode of long-range transportation, the creation of simu-tunnel, actually passed through a dimension of space-time parallel to the one accessible by most organics, through which the distance between gravity wells–astronomical objects in realspace–was shorter as the laws of realspace spacetime didn't apply.

By studying the behavior of voidwhale, scientists discovered that voidlane could be accessed by molecular displacement, which could only be achieved by breaking the speed of light. As voidlane was coterminous with realspace–meaning that every point in realspace had its equivalent in voidlane in the form of mass shadows–it was possible to use the dimension to travel from one point to the other in a shorter period of time without needing to actually cover the distance between them. According to legend, this technology was in fact developed by the mythical species of Dovdak, native to the planet Rinvash. Developing voidjump drive technology on the planet Vordunlgor independently from extraterrestrial assistance, the Cyrvaels had explored and interacted with the galaxy before the Concord of Auren ever made contact with their region of space. To this end, Cyrvael sovtia explorers were a common sight across the galaxy as far back as 30,000 years ago. As early as 27,000 BKV, the Marzor Zunsera reached the planet Gartos with the use of a tumbledrive propulsion engine. Eventually, Halrelethian engineers independently from their Wenlanmund counterparts developed the technology necessary to access the dimension, and dubbed it the voidjump drive–in contrast with standard engines, which were named sublight engines.

### Sovtia exploration using the voidjump drive

Once voidjump drives made faster-than-light travel possible, sovtia civilization flourished throughout ancient history. The Elder Concord sent pioneering scout ships to explore the galaxy as increasing numbers of civilians from the Inner Realms sought a better life. The Cyrvaels were among the early explorers along what became the Halrelethian Trade Spine voidlane route. Early voidjump drive routes were blazed by scouts such as the Dralume Zunvok clan by using voidlane sextants or simply following voidwhale in their travels. The latter practice demonstrated the species' tendency to feed on the gas GHZTZZW-15, which formed in places subject to intense interstellar energy, like Kagan or the Zelphensera system. Knowledge of this practice led humans to discover the mineral vexium, which also formed in the same places and, like Ringan, fuels faster and better voidjump drive jumps. Unfortunately, the faster-than-light travel also created new possibilites for war, as the now-mythological Great Voidlane War between the Concord and Zargoth Dominion proved just over 5,000 years ago.

Prospectors and explorers started using the primitive voidlane paths and the advanced voidjump drives of the time to explore the vastness of space, ushering in the Great Voidlane Rush. The Concord continued financing the scouting of new territories with its Pyrvael Teams, while also hiring private scouts such as the Rinvael family. The development of major voidlanes such as the Jorkrixbryn Trade Route and Keskrix Way further accelerated sovtia expansion.

## Operation

Voidjump drives consisted of two basic parts: a navigation computer and the actual reactor assembly–also known as the voidjump drive generator. The pathcomputer calculated a safe path through voidlane by comparing the pilot's desired path with known voidlanes and dangers already present along the way such as supernovas and asteroid fields to ensure a safe journey, while the reactor handled the work of generating enough power to perform a jump without straining the ship's main reactor. Species in the Uncharted Reaches such as the Lorgoth did not use a voidjump drive, and instead used Flux-sensitive beings who were capable of seeing the safest path through the normally difficult ,to impossible, to traverse through (with a standard pathcomputer) unknown regions. In the past other force sensitives have been known to use this method, such as Xellan Dralume Zunvok, who was used by the lunbryn known as the Tordun to create paths for their unique path drive, a voidjump drive unhindered by the normal operating rules of a voidjump drive. Ursera Garvash used this method.

Most modern voidjump drive reactors had a main chamber coated in vexium, which significanty improved their efficiency. Larger models such as the XXT63 and the S-39 functioned by stacking several stabilizer plates on top of each other and channeling voidmatter through ultra-thin effect channels passing through them. Voidmatter, whose energy output was controlled by the pathcomputer, reached the ship's main engines and charged the ions already present there, powering them for the jump to lightspeed; a voidjump drive motivator controlled the jump based on sensor output and ensured a risk-free trip and return to realspace. Smaller ships such as starwings or bombers ditched the voidjump drive reactor altogether and instead relied only on the motivator, with power provided by the ship's standard reactor. Examples of standalone motivators were the W377-X voidjump drive motivator of the XWF-X3 Brakwing starwing and the NQR-494 voidjump drive motivator of the C-45M Vexwing starwing.

## Advanced operation

During the Echo Wars, a microjump was used to rescue Salphos Master Dramir Zunserayn from the forces of the League of Free Realms in orbit of Dovnor. Using the exact coordinates of General Caephen' command ship, Yarwyn Daelthos precisely jumped through voidlane, arriving meters away from his target, undetected. The same precise jumping technique was used by Ophsera Sallgor to infiltrate the Ascendant Directorate superweapon Zelkai Base, since the planet's shields were using a fractional refresh rate that would keep anything below light speed from passing through. Without careful planning, using the voidjump drive in such a manner could result in the loss of a ship due to the nature of mass shadows, but the most gifted pilots like Sallgor and Daelthos could use those characteristics of space to their advantage.

In 34 AKV, Resistance Vice Admiral Lunvok Faelzekar executed a voidlane jump with the cruiser Pyrkrix that was directly aimed at the Ascendant Directorate flagship Supremacy, causing the enemy ship to be sliced apart in the resulting collision.

During the Mission to Nysul Glacier Colony, Rinvok Kesmir used Voidlane skipping to escape Ascendant Directorate pursuers.
