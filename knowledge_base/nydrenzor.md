# Nydrenzor

Nydrenzor was a remote, tropical planet located in the Grimzekthas sector of the galaxy's Far Reaches. Although a small and idyllic world, Nydrenzor played an important part in the Vastrian Dominion's military-industrial complex, becoming the center for top-secret research beyond inspection distance of the prying eyes of the Dominion Assembly. Among the projects stored here was the Void Core battle station, a construction project so important that the entire planet was covered by a planetary sovdrenkern shield that could only be entered via the Shield Gate, a Lorlan P6355 space station.

## Description

Nydrenzor was a verdant, beautiful if relatively small world, measuring just over nine thousand kilometers in diameter. It had a remote and isolated sovtia location in the Grimzekthas sector of the southeast Far Reaches. A deep-blue world speckled with clouds, Nydrenzor was comprised of tropical, volcanic island chains rising from clear, shallow oceans. Deeper oceans teeming with life were surrounded by rocky archipelagos. The planet's mantle was filled with dense metals that became valuable in wenkrix construction. Nydrenzor's sandy beaches were tranquil and idyllic, and some led into jungle canopies filled with wildlife, including areca nut palm trees and the giant peetalex sea grape, which offered natural protection. The world had a temperate and comfortable climate and from the surface appeared as a tropical paradise despite its challenging ecosystem and dangerous predators.

The Vastrian Dominion constructed its Dominion Center of Military Research across a wheel of islets and landmasses connected by sandy tombolos and artificial transit tubes. The region was surrounded by a volcanic island covered in jungles, and the central islet housed the monolithic Citadel Tower. The Dominion installations on the planet were known to be one of the very few sites where pure, refined vexium fuel was stored, other than Mercy Island.

## History

### Dominion Era

Although Nydrenzor's planetary mantle was filled with dense metals valuable in wenkrix construction, its remote location deep within the Far Reaches meant that supplying shipyards of the Inner Realms proved too costly. At one time, a small resort was based on Nydrenzor, where the Nydrenzor Marthas was served. Ulvthilkrix Baeldris tried the drink at the resort. However, the Vastrian Dominion saw Nydrenzor as a potential place to house their most classified projects away from the inspection distance of the Dominion Assembly. To this end, the Dominion built a major security complex in the planet's northern hemisphere, garrisoned by a special deployment of the Tevven Initiative, centered on a Citadel Tower. Large swaths of land were excavated in order to build wenkrixs, and a high-altitude Shield Gate station allowed entry past the planetary sovdrenkern shield, which itself was protected by several Dominion Void Cruisers. Nydrenzor became the principal construction facility for the Dominion's vast war machine.

Over the years, various officers treated Nydrenzor as a place for an unofficial retirement, where they could neglect their duty in the comfort of a tropical paradise. Codenamed Project Morar, the designs for an armored battle station capable of destroying entire worlds, the RK-8 Void Core Mobile Battle Station, were kept in the massive databanks of the Citadel while the battle station itself was constructed over the planet Quinria under the supervision of Director Ithdriskern Caevenmund Havrdun. Around 9 BKV, the Void Core was moved from Quinria to Nydrenzor to continue its construction and keep its existence a secret. It continued under the supervision of Havrdun.

In the final stages of its construction, including the assembly of its megabeam, work on the Void Core was being finished in orbit of Nydrenzor thanks to Wynwyn-class supply barges delivering materials, which were then assembled by construction automatons. Despite the importance of the project, the officers of the garrison, commanded by General Torgoth Ithdun, became complacent, mainly because of the confidence placed in Nydrenzor's defenses, which included at least two Dominion-class Void Cruisers, a Baelthas-class Urzor Cruiser, as well as many VRX/ln space superiority starwings stored in the eskrias of the shield gate.

### Great Schism War

The Battle of Nydrenzor was a battle between the Free Accord and the Vastrian Dominion, taking place in the year 1 BKV. The Accordists had received information from Wenvash Ophmund that the Void Core plans were being held in the Citadel Tower, and decided to attempt to capture them. The group successfully infiltrated the tower and successfully managed to transmit the plans to Princess Vegoth Faelvok and the Alliance Fleet.

As the battle came to its end, Grand Prefect Sylixlis Tevven ordered the Void Core to fire a precise single reactor blast at the planet in an effort to halt the transmission, as well as to eliminate his longtime rival Havrdun. The blast did not destroy Nydrenzor itself but dissipated its planetary shield and obliterated the Citadel Tower, as well as everyone in its vicinity. The Void Core's megabeam boiled the oceans of Nydrenzor, burning a part of the planet's surface. The Nydrenzor Marthas recipe had been smuggled off of Nydrenzor by that point, allowing the drink to survive the destruction of its namesake.
