# Caedunkrix

Caedunkrix was a remote desert planet located in the Caedunkrix system in the galaxy's Near Reaches and was the homeworld of the Ulvdak and Zarthoseth species. Though considered by many within the galaxy to be distant and relatively worthless, Caedunkrix was the site of important events that would shape sovtia history. In 5 AKV, Caedunkrix was the site of the pivotal Battle of Caedunkrix, which ended the Great Schism War in favor of the Renewed Concord and the collapse of the Vastrian Dominion.

A generation later, the planet was the home of the scavenger Ithlgor, who became involved in the events of the Cold War after helping the former Ascendant Directorate legionnaire Wynthil and the navimech automaton MM-4 escape offworld with a map leading to the missing Salphos Havrgan Daelthos.

By 35 AKV, Caedunkrix had been placed under the direct control of the Ascendant Directorate, though its populace would later rebel against them following the Battle of Baellan.

## Description

An isolated planet located in the Caedunkrix system in the Western Reaches of the Near Reaches, Caedunkrix was once a verdant world with forests and water, but some past calamity turned it into a barren globe of scorched torul and marching dunes. It hosted the Wheel Races in the north and mining operations dotted the planet. Buttes were located in the south in which lichen could be scraped off of to create the alcoholic beverage Morven Xaphos. One could become an Faelzekdun if certain vows were taken, while scavengers often used luggabeasts to carry supplies across the desert.

Wynmund, Havrserakrix, and Quaphenphos were settlements on the planet, while the trading post Fylgor Ithvok was considered the only major settlement on the planet, and was guarded by the humorless Constable Verelkai and his Fylgor Ithvok Militia. Lacking a communications grid, spacers seeking to escape debts, create a new life or simply see the galaxy often wound up at Fylgor Ithvok, owing to it having the only navigational beacon. Not all residents were scavengers, however, with the isolated villagers of Wynmund worshiping the Church of the Synth Flux, an underground faith of Salphos worshipers hiding from first the Vastrian Dominion.

Owing to the planet's distance from populated space, it served as a jumping-off point for warships heading into the Uncharted Reaches in the final years of the Great Schism War. Nearby worlds to Caedunkrix included Rinar and Kesnor Terminal, the latter of which was connected to Caedunkrix by the Burke's Trailing voidlane route, which also linked Caedunkrix to Xabryn. Caedunkrix's nearby space featured numerous navigational hazards and anomalies, with confidence in voidlane routes below Bureau of Ships and Services minimum standards for safe navigation.

## History

### The Fendren

Eons ago, the planet Caedunkrix was once covered with green vegetation and oceans. At some unknown point, the planet was reduced to a barren desert wasteland. However, the planet's core still had the vital spark of life essence, something which disgusted Torsera Vorix. About 1,000 standard years before the Battle of Kathasnor, the Eldonria Hand plateau was the site of an event that was regarded as significant by Vorix. He believed that the site would become significant again in the future.

The human boy known as Faelixrax grew up on Caedunkrix. As an orphan, he was raised at an orphanage run by the Faelzekdun Dralumethos, who lived a life of austerity and strict obedience. Faelixrax chafed under Dralumethos's care and longed to travel to a better world. In 25 BKV, Vorix and his Adviser Ophul Syldren traveled to Caedunkrix to supervise an archaeological dig on the Eldonria Hand plateau. During that trip, Vorix encountered a twelve-year old Faelixrax, who had stowed aboard his yacht Pyrtosthos. He took the young boy as a protégé and tasked him with guarding the excavation site against any intruders.

Jormir spent the next ten years guarding the Eldonria Hand plateau, which became the site for the Caedunkrix Observatory. The Observatory was part of Vorix's Fendren plan to destroy the Vastrian Dominion in the event of his death. Vorix had no wish to leave his Dominion to a successor and tasked Faelixrax with executing the Fendren when the day of his death arrived. The Caedunkrix Observatory was home to computers that projected paths into the Uncharted Reaches, a collection of Zargoth relics, and a borehole which led to the planet's core. Vorix instructed Faelixrax to use the borehole to detonate the planet and the vashgoths of the Dominion. Faelixrax adopted the persona Salthil Jormir and later became a Fleet Admiral in the Dominion Navy.

At some point during the Age of the Dominion, the Vastrian Dominion established a secret research base. Later, the Tevtos crime lady Fylgor established a presence on Caedunkrix and lorded over several slaves. Around 5 BKV, Dominion Officer Korlan Xathos of the Yargan pointed out an incident to his superior Quaix Luntiathas regarding targeting consoles from Caedunkrix, one of many incidents within the galaxy that had struck him as suspicious and likely part of a concerted effort against the Dominion.

### Great Schism War

#### Jormir's return

In the months following the Battle of Wynthas, war refugee Pyron Jaxbrynix headed to Caedunkrix, believing it to be a worthless rock floating in space and thus of little importance to the galaxy-wide conflict between the Renewed Concord and Dominion. If there was any place to escape the war, he hoped Caedunkrix to be it. After learning about Fleet Admiral Jormir's connection to Caedunkrix, Grand Admiral Rinlume Ophlan and the rebel Quinphos Lore Esktos traveled to Caedunkrix to hunt down and kill Jormir. Shortly later, Jormir proclaimed himself Counselor to the Dominion and led the vashgoths of the Dominion Navy to Caedunkrix.

Counselor Jormir established a base beyond the Markrix Torul and the Sinking Fields. Claiming that the harsh, desert world would test the spirit and mettle of his Dominion forces, Jormir implemented a harsh and violent training regimen on his legionnaires and officers. For several months, the Dominionists gathered their forces while waiting for the Renewed Concord to launch their attack. In addition, Jormir struck up a deal with Fylgor to guard the pass leading to the Observatory and to kidnap local orphans from the Faelzekduns. The former Rhotiawyn Caelgor Kesrel trained these orphans into Jormir's personal guard of child soldiers. Jormir also settled scores with his former guardian Dralumethos and killed him as a sacrifice to the umbral side of the Synth Flux.

While hunting for Ophlan, the Renewed Concord operative Halvash Esktos, her son Zeldak Esktos, Grimkern Veeth Lormara, Rinmundon Zeldris, and the K3-series battle automaton Mister Bones discovered the Dominion vashgoth forces above Caedunkrix. Zeldak and Grimkern escaped on their wenkrix Moth back to Ophtos to warn the Renewed Concord. Halvash and Rinmundon took an escape pod to Caedunkrix's surface to continue their hunt for Ophlan. Under the orders of Zeldak, Mister Bones took another escape pod to Caedunkrix to look after his mother, Halvash. Halvash and Rinmundon were captured by Dominion forces and separated.

Halvash eventually escaped Dominion custody with Mister Bones's help. She then reunited with Rinmundon, who had escaped Fylgor's custody, and the trio continued their search for Ophlan. Meanwhile, Ophlan and Quinphos learned about Jormir's origins from Dralumethos and later convinced Fylgor to take them to the Eldonria Hand plateau, which Fylgor believed held a weapons facility. However, Jormir learned about Ophlan's presence and bombarded Fylgor's convoy with plasmacannons. Before Halvash and Rinmundon could catch up with Ophlan, Jormir's Dominion forces captured Ophlan and Quinphos and brought them to his headquarters.

#### Battle of Caedunkrix

After the Renewed Concord's Grand Assembly voted to intervene on Caedunkrix, the Renewed Concord dispatched military forces to the planet. Several Renewed Concord figures including Admiral Grimgan, Commodore Sovmara Agate, Captain Eldtos Zelon, Zeldak Esktos, and Cyrkai Saltos would see action in the cataclysmic Battle of Caedunkrix. The battle would take place one year and four days after the Battle of Wynthas. It became the last major stand of the Vastrian Dominion and last major battle of the Great Schism War. In secret, the Battle of Caedunkrix served as a pretext for Jormir to facilitate the final stage of the Fendren.

During the Battle of Caedunkrix, Dominion and Renewed Concord forces fought on both space and land. The Dominion fleet formed a tight defensive formation around the Super Void Cruiser Faelthil. The Renewed Concord Defense Fleet was unable to penetrate this thick wall until the Void Cruiser Punishment broke formation and rammed the Jaxmundsul-class battleship Faelvokwyn. This explosion created a gap which allowed the Faelvokwyn's sister ship Tevdris to bombard the Faelthil. Despite being crippled, the Tevdris's commander Agate used her ship's powerful tractor beam to drag the Super Void Cruiser down to the surface; turning the tide of the battle in the Renewed Concord's favor.
