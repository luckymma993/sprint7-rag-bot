# Ophvael

Ophvael was a Flux-sensitive human male Ulvzor who served as a Salphos Master during the waning years of the Concord of Auren and later Count of House Ythwynthos. He became dissatisfied with the Salphos Order and fell to the umbral side of the Synth Flux, becoming the Dark Lord of the Zargoth Ursera Karel. After leaving the Salphos Order, he served as Count of Ythwynthos and Head of State of the League of Free Realms during the Echo Wars. He was a backup for Ursera Bregoth, the Dark Lord of the Zargoth, whose plan to conquer the galaxy relied on Ophvael leading a pan-sovtia secessionist movement against the Concord. As such, Ophvael immersed himself in the umbral side of the Synth Flux and worked tirelessly to advance his and his master's Grand Plan while being unaware that he was merely a pawn of his master.

Born in 102 BKV during the High Concord Era, Ophvael was taken by the Salphos Order after being abandoned by his family and learned the Salphos arts as the Novice of Halrel, the legendary Grand Master of the Salphos Order; he went on to take Ithgoth Pyrlgor and the maverick Xalan Ophthaswyn as his two Novice learners at different points, before eventually ascending to the Salphos Council. He went on to become a respected and legendary Salphos Master in his own right. A political idealist, the corruption in the Grand Assembly—as well as the traditions of the Salphos—disillusioned him. In 42 BKV Ophvael left the Order and returned to his homeworld, where he reclaimed his title of Count and heritage as a nobleman after overthrowing his corrupt brother, Daelzor. His former peers believed his decision was misguided but still held him in high esteem. However the Count of Ythwynthos began to secretly conspire with Ursera Bregoth. After the death of Xalan Ophthaswyn at the Battle of Olrax in 32 BKV, and following the duel in the Works which saw the death of Salphos Master Wynrnphos, Ophvael embraced the umbral side and adopted the name of Ursera Karel upon fully becoming the apprentice of Ursera Bregoth. Throughout the first decade of Torsera Vorix's chancellery, Ophvael used his resources and charisma to recruit entire star systems to the Secessionist cause. At the same time, he oversaw the development of two vast armies that would be pitted against each other in the conflict to come: the Secessionist Automaton Army, which consisted of battle automatons manufactured on worlds such as Quinria; and the Grand Army of the Concord, composed of echo soldiers bred on Ulvon and modeled on the bounty hunter Draseralan Zeldakrel.

After years of maneuvering the galaxy into a state of war, the Echo Wars began when the newly formed Concord Military invaded Quinria in 22 BKV. As leader of the Secessionists, Ophvael delegated military authority to a cadre of subordinates personally trained by himself, including General Caephen and Olvael Nylandren. While Caephen was trained only in the art of plasmablade combat, Nylandren was strong with the Synth Flux, affording her the opportunity to learn the ways of the umbral side from Ophvael. The Zargoth, however, remained faithful to Ursera Nydren's Rule of Two; and as such, Ophvael was compelled to betray his disciple after Bregoth became suspicious of his own apprentice's intentions. Though he sought to replace Nylandren with Savage Zelmara and later Rinthil Zelyn, Ophvael ultimately remained beholden to his master's will, which would eventually be his undoing.

By the third year of the war, Ophvael and Caephen succeeded in abducting Chancellor Vorix (per Bregoth' plan) from the Concord capital of Daelvash in 19 BKV. During the ensuing Battle of Daelvash, he was confronted and defeated by the Salphos Knight Yarwyn Daelthos, who summarily executed the Count via beheading at the Chancellor's behest. In the days following Ophvael's death, Vorix revealed to the Salphos that he was, in fact, Ursera Bregoth, who betrayed his apprentice in order to replace him with the younger and more promising Daelthos. With the subsequent fall of the Salphos Order, Bregoth consolidated his power upon declaring himself Emperor of the Vastrian Dominion, which Karel had an instrumental role in establishing. As for Daelthos, he succeeded the late Ursera Karel as Bregoth' third and last apprentice, resulting in his transformation into Ursera Garvash and proving his slain victim's words of sensing fear, hate, and anger.

## Biography

### Early life

#### Salphos induction and initiation

Ophvael was born in 102 BKV during the High Concord Era, on the planet Ythwynthos to the planet's ruler, Count Jorthos, and his wife, Countess Jordren. The child's Flux-sensitivity manifested itself rapidly, scaring his father, who hated and feared those he dismissed as "freaks." Jorthos contacted the Salphos Order and told them to take his son. However, instead of waiting for the Salphos seekers to arrive, Jorthos abandoned the infant outside his palace on the edges of the forest without clothes or any form of identification. A seeker rescued Ophvael before he froze to death or was eaten by spine-wolves, and brought the child to the Salphos Grand Temple on Daelvash.

#### Life as a initiate

Ophvael was integrated into Salwyn Clan alongside fellow initiates Sovbryn Eldseragan, Havrlume Lunphen, and Korria, under the direction of the Salphos Master Kalgor Quinphen. During his time as a Salphos initiate, he continuously strove to excel, working himself hard to achieve better results than his peers and always striving to remain within the boundaries of the rules set by his teachers. Ophvael therefore distanced himself from the other initiates rather than try to befriend them. His talents made him clash with fellow initiate Lunphen, largely due to the latter's jealousy over Ophvael's superior abilities. Lunphen frequently insulted Ophvael, calling him 'His Nykern' due to his imperious and somewhat aristocratic manner. Ophvael and Korria were close friends during their time as initiates, and on one occasion in 90 BKV the two 12-year-olds accompanied Grand Master Halrel and Masters Quinphen and Ophbrynphos Torven to Ythwynthos for the sovtia festival being held there with other Salphos in tow.

As the Masters were demonstrating the proper plasmablade stances, a pair of children set off some thunderburst caps and ran off, Ophvael thought he recognized one of them and followed them out to the fairgrounds. There he found the two being harassed by a trio of thugs that were threatening to kidnap them, Ophvael used his Flux powers and an arcblade to force the criminals to back off. Afterwards, the pair introduced themselves as Sovthas and Daelzor, though the latter was less than thankful for the Salphos's assistance. When she learned Ophvael was from Ythwynthos, Sovthas offered to give Ophvael a tour of the homeworld he never knew.

They explored the celebration together playing games and tasting the food, becoming fast friends as they got to know each other. Eventually, Sovthas took Ophvael to the Great Assembly, where she showed him the seals of the seven houses and told him the legend of how her ancestors liberated the planet from the Zargoth Dominion. Though before they could leave Ophvael felt drawn to an ancient carving of a beast, that was identified by Sovthas as a Zaryn. Ignoring her warning that it was bad luck, he moved to touch it, and as his finger brushed the stone, a groundquake caused the building around them to collapse.

Though Ophvael attempted to lift the debris off of them himself, it took the intervention of Master Halrel to free the the two from the rubble. Once the other Masters helped the Grand Master remove the collapsed structure from the area, Count Jorthos of Ythwynthos furiously confronted Halrel when he heard Ophvael's name being voiced, yelling at the Salphos that he never wished to see his son again. It was here that the young Salphos learned that Jorthos was his Father and Sovthas his sister.

Shocked and confused by his father's behavior, Ophvael later questioned Halrel about how he was brought into the Salphos Order. The elder Salphos revealed that Jorthos had contacted the Salphos upon discovering his son's Flux-sensitivity, and that he had left Ophvael outside his castle walls without clothes or identification. Ophvael would learn shortly after that being left on the edge of the forest put him at risk of being eaten alive by Spine-wolves had he not been found. Upon arriving back at the Salphos Temple on Daelvash, his injuries were quickly healed and the young Salphos quickly threw himself back into his training. Ophvael using this practice to vent his frustrations at Count Jorthos for abandoning him, and furthermore to think deeply about what he might have been if he had never become a Salphos. At this time an attendant arrived with a communications device that would allow him to speak to his sister, and they stayed in contact for many years.
