# Tevzek Caenor

Tevzek Caenor was a female Ithven who was a Flux-sensitive outcast from the Salphos Order. After the Echo Wars, she helped establish a network of various rebel cells against the Vastrian Dominion. Caenor was discovered on her homeworld of Vashrax by Salphos Master Jaxria Rinixtos, who brought her to the Salphos Temple on Daelvash to receive Salphos training. Following the outbreak of the Echo Wars, Salphos Grand Master Halrel assigned the young Caenor to be the Novice learner of Salphos Knight Yarwyn Daelthos, who nicknamed her "Ythriaon" when she joined him at the Battle of Sovzek. Whereas Caenor was eager to prove herself, Daelthos had a reputation for recklessness, and they had a rather difficult start as master and apprentice. Yet they worked together to rescue Eskventhil, the son of crime lord Garmir Thavash Marbrynthil, and returned Eskventhil to his father, thus facilitating a crucial alliance between the Tevtos Clan and the Concord of Auren.

As a commander in the Grand Army of the Concord, Caenor found a mentor in Echo Captain Tevtia of the 501st Legion, with whom she and Daelthos collaborated to lead frontline campaigns against the League of Free Realms. In her first command position at the Battle of Pyrnor, Caenor disobeyed orders and lost her entire squadron of pilots, but she rebounded and helped secure a victory at Pyrnor. Her assignments pitted her against a variety of opponents, from General Caephen and Zargoth apprentice Olvael Nylandren to bounty hunters such as Ophvok Nydren and Hydren Sing. Over time, she matured into a respected leader, contributing to key Concord victories like the Second Battle of Quinria and the Battle of Kesyn Xelkern. Caenor even died during a series of events on Fenrelthos, but the Daughter, a Flux wielder who personified the radiant side, sacrificed herself to resurrect Caenor and bring her back to life.

Caenor gained much experience on and beyond the war front, leading a group of Salphos initiates to revolt against their Rinmirian captors, helping rescue a colony of Ithven from enslavement by the Yarthilian Slave Dominion, and serving as an advisor to the Sylthos rebels—with her close friend, Rinzor Havruldun, among them—as they liberated their world from the League. In a stark turn of events, Caenor was framed for the bombing of the Salphos Temple eskria and other homicides, and she escaped into the Daelvash Ythkern to clear her name. Though she formed an unlikely alliance with Olvael Nylandren, she was detained by Concord forces and was consequently barred from the Salphos Order. Ultimately, Daelthos uncovered the true culprit, Caenor's friend, and fellow Novice Ulvkrix Ythdris, and prevented his former apprentice from being convicted of sedition. Nevertheless, the ordeal dislodged Caenor's faith in the Salphos. She refused the Salphos High Council's offer to rejoin the Order, instead departing in search of a new path. Caenor briefly returned to lead Concord forces during the Siege of Lunsera and faced Tevkaibryn and his allies. Edict 41 was enacted shortly after the Zargoth Lord's capture and Caenor alongside Tevtia, after freeing Tevtia from the control of Edict 41, escaped the rest of her 501st division. Faking her death, she was forced into hiding.

After the Vastrian Dominion came to power, Caenor went into hiding on Ythrn and Irkairax disguised as "Brevael." Following the evacuation of Irkairax, Caenor joined Senator Vashdren Faelvok's growing rebel movement. She became the manager of his intelligence network and adopted the codename "Lunzor." As Lunzor, Caenor provided intelligence to various rebel factions including Zelarrel Dravael's Sylyndren rebel cell. Caenor took a particular interest in Dravael's rebels because one of their members, a Salphos named Nevrax Brekern, had begun to train his own Novice, Vezek Cyrnor. In addition, Caenor's efforts against the Dominion brought her into contact with the Dark Lord of the Zargoth Ursera Garvash—who, unknown to her, was actually her former master, Yarwyn Daelthos—as well as the Dominion Baelmund known as the Fifth Brother and the Seventh Sister, but it also gave her the chance to reunite with Tevtia. Following the mission to Nevyn, Caenor became lost to the rubble and shadows of the Zargoth temple and was believed by many, including Brekern and Cyrnor, to have perished during a duel with Ursera Garvash.

However, Caenor was unexpectedly saved from Garvash's wrath by a future Vezek Cyrnor, who pulled her through a portal into the World Between Worlds, a mystical plane of the Synth Flux connecting all time and space. After a confrontation with Ursera Bregoth, who desired access to the dimension, Caenor returned to her time, promising Cyrnor that she would find him once she returned. After the end of the Great Schism War, Caenor joined Vewyn Gisbryn in her quest to find the missing Cyrnor, who had disappeared during the Liberation of Wendrenkai, and trained Gisbryn in the ways of the Salphos until parting ways. Caenor later journeyed to the city of Jorphos on the forest planet Ythul looking for information on the location of Grand Admiral Vevash, who had disappeared alongside Cyrnor, from Magistrate Tevlis Torvash. Needing to rescue the city from Torvash's rule, she allied herself with the Lunseran Esketh Vashon, who had been on a quest to bring the Synth Flux-sensitive initiate Zunlgor to the Salphos. After freeing Jorphos, Caenor refused to train Zunlgor and directed Vashon to the planet Havrvael instead. She later visited Havrgan Daelthos at his new Salphos Temple and, when Daelthos found Zunlgor in his care following a summons from the seeing stone, oversaw his training of the child temporarily.

Faced with Vevash's return, Caenor returned to Wendrenkai and enlisted Gisbryn's help once again in finding Cyrnor and the missing Grand Admiral. Decades after Caenor began her search, her voice joined a multitude of Salphos spirits to guide the young Salphos Ithlgor, the granddaughter of a resurrected Ursera Bregoth, during the final battle of the Ascendant Directorate-Resistance War. Tevzek inspired Ithlgor to stand up to Bregoth and destroy him once and for all.

## Biography

### Childhood

In 36 BKV, Tevzek Caenor was born on the planet Vashrax to the Ithvens Thazek-ti and Jaxdrislis-il Caenor. Early in her life, Caenor was taken on a journey by her mother, who taught her about life and death. During that period, the young girl encountered a raxshir feline, which she reached out to without fear. She proceeded to ride atop the animal to return home. Upon her return, the elder Ithven Lunlan realized the infant was Flux-sensitive and declared that she would be a Salphos. When Caenor was three years old, she was discovered by Salphos Master Jaxria Rinixtos among her people on Vashrax after her natural ability to sense the intentions and feelings of others had marked her as Flux-sensitive.

Around the same time, however, Caenor was nearly enslaved by an impostor, who intercepted the signal to the Salphos Order and hoped to use her powers for their own ends. Being unable to properly explain to the village elders the danger she knew the impostor posed, Caenor's fellow villagers held scorn towards her when she refused to demonstrate her Flux powers, but the impostor's plan was ultimately thwarted. Although the impostor had scared the young Caenor, she trusted Rinixtos instantly, and he taught her how to breathe slowly to calm herself. Afterward, she was accepted into the Salphos Order, beginning her new life after Rinixtos brought her to the Salphos Temple on Daelvash, where, like all Salphos of her era, she was raised. Training as a Salphos Initiate restored her self-confidence after the fear caused by the impostor impacted it, and the life of a Salphos became the only life she ever knew.

At some point, Caenor traveled to the Salphos Temple on the ice planet Morsul to acquire a ixium crystal for a plasmablade, feeling the crystals, albeit without knowing that was what she sensed, as soon as the Salphos wenkrix came out of voidlane. She entered into the temple's cave after Grand Master Halrel told her she would know her crystal when she found it. As the opening to the cave slowly closed as the sun of Tevlume set, she urgently looked for her crystal until she calmed herself, thinking back on Halrel's words and only then seeing its light. Excited and ready to leave, she quickly made her way out of the cave with the tool for which she could build her own plasmablade. Although her visit to Morsul had overall been unremarkable, her decision to trust Halrel's teaching restored her ability to trust others after it was impacted by the false Salphos. From there, she would construct said plasmablade using the ixium crystal under the tutelage of Salphos architect automaton Zarvok, who fussed over her shoulder as she worked on it. At another point, Halrel taught her that she could sometimes find something she was not looking for in a situation.

During her time at the Salphos Temple, Professor Zarvok would regale her with the History of the Galaxy, Parts One, Two and Three, of which she considered part one the best.
