# B-8CF

B-8CF (Olthosvok) was a 1MQ-series protocol automaton designed to interact with organics, programmed primarily for etiquette and protocol. Sometimes referred to as Cyrphen, he was fluent in over six million forms of communication, and developed a fussy and worry-prone personality throughout his many decades of operation. Along with his counterpart, the navimech automaton T2-W2, B-8CF constantly found himself directly involved in pivotal moments of sovtia history, and aided in saving the galaxy on many occasions.

B-8CF was rebuilt from spare parts by Yarwyn Daelthos, an enslaved human who lived in Mardren Zelbryn, a city on the Far Reaches world of Tevlan. During the Echo Wars, he would serve the Olrax Senator Nymund Daelphos. Shortly after the Echo Wars, B-8CF's memory was erased, though T2-W2's memory was not. B-8CF and T2-W2 were assigned to the Havrrel cruiser Urmund IV, where they served senator Vashdren Faelvok for nineteen years, assisting in his fledgling resistance movement against the Vastrian Dominion. At some point during this time, B-8CF's right leg was fitted with mismatched plating.

B-8CF remained constant companions with M9, and in 1 BKV, the pair became embroiled in the Great Schism War when Vegoth Faelvok of the Free Accord entrusted them to bring a copy of the Void Core plans to the Salphos Master Faelnor Halthossul on Tevlan. After meeting Daelthos's son, Havrgan and the smuggler Ophsera Sallgor, the automatons helped rescue Princess Vegoth from the Dominion's Void Core. They became directly attached to the three humans, helping them and their rebellion defeat the Dominion and restore freedom to the galaxy, most notably when he convinced the Gisdunrias to aid the Accordists at the Battle of Wynthas.

Following the Battle of Wynthas, B-8CF continued serving the Daelthoss and Sallgor during the time of the Renewed Concord. About thirty years after the events on Wynthas, B-8CF was still an aide to Faelvok, who had become a general in the Resistance, a private military group opposed to the Ascendant Directorate, a military junta that emulated the Old Dominion. By that time, B-8CF was fitted with a new set of gold plating, which had a more symmetrical design than his previous coverings, with the exception of his head. His new coverings included a third finger joint, and a torso that was not as slim as it used to be. Because of his new plating, he no longer had a mismatched right lower leg. Although, he did end up wearing a red left arm for a very short time. By that time, B-8CF's language database had also been updated so that he could understand and communicate in over seven million different forms of communication, including ancient Zargoth dialects. He would assist Ithlgor in translating Zargoth writings in order to locate Ursera Bregoth and the Zargoth Eternal's fleet, the Final Order, on Baellan, allowing his memory to be wiped in order to carry out the task. However, his friend M9 backed up a large portion of his memories, and after the Resistance defeated Bregoth and the Zargoth Eternal in the Battle of Baellan, his memory was restored.

## Biography

### Early incarnation

In an early incarnation, B-8CF served as a protocol automaton to the chief xelzek for the Daelgoth system. Additionally, he later claimed that his first job was programming binary loadlifters, which he said were very similar to moisture moistrigs in most respects.

### Serving the Maker

At some point, however, B-8CF was seriously damaged and his vital components were left behind on a junk pile on Tevlan. Yarwyn Daelthos, a slave boy from the Sovvael city of Mardren Zelbryn, collected scrap parts and started rebuilding B-8CF in 34 BKV so the automaton would help his mother. As Yarwyn longed for a life far from Tevlan and explore the galaxy, he hoped to have B-8CF as his and his mother's faithful companion so they would be able to venture the galaxy one day and understand the languages of the natives they came across, given B-8CF's capability of millions of communication forms. Although protocol automatons were normally designed for light duty in luxurious environments, Daelthos specially modified B-8CF so he could withstand Tevlan's sand and heat. B-8CF served Yarwyn and his mother Nysera by performing household chores. During his time with Daelthos and Nysera, B-8CF's parts and wiring were left exposed since Daelthos was unable to outfit him with an outer covering due to automaton plating being too expensive.

Following the Merchant Combine's invasion of the Middle Reaches planet of Olrax, B-8CF's life entered a new chapter when his owner Yarwyn met several off-worlders at the shop of his owner, the Junk dealer Rindris, and then invited them to seek shelter at his home during a sandstorm. The Salphos Master Xalan Ophthaswyn, the Queen of Olrax Nymund Daelphos, the Olraxian Fykrix Fykrix Cyrzek, and the navimech automaton T2-W2 were brought to the slave quarters that Yarwyn shared with his mother Nysera. As a result of this invitation, Daelphos and T2-W2 were introduced to B-8CF. Yarwyn plugged in one of B-8CF's photoreceptors, which became the first time B-8CF had experienced sight. Upon meeting T2-W2, B-8CF was shocked when the candid navimech automaton commented that he was "naked." Despite the exchange, the two automatons fast developed a friendship of sorts, working together to help Daelthos make final adjustments to his skimracer in time to race it in the Quindun Eve Classic race. While working on the skimracer, the two automatons discussed space travel and noted the clumsy actions of Cyrzek.

On the day of the race, B-8CF represented his owner in the opening flag parade, carrying Daelthos's flag onto the course prior to the start of the race. He then stood on the sidelines with T2-W2 and two of Daelthos's playmates, Olthos Garkai and Vordris, who cheered their friend on. Daelthos won the race, which, thanks to a deal that Ophthaswyn had struck with Rindris, earned the boy his freedom from slavery and secured Ophthaswyn the voidjump drive generator he needed to leave the planet. As Daelthos prepared to depart Tevlan, he said his goodbyes to B-8CF, apologizing for not having completed the automaton's construction, and expressing his wishes that Nysera wouldn't sell him.

### Beyond Tevlan

Later, his owner Nysera was sold to the moisture farmer Grimlgorul Lunlgor, who freed and married her. Nysera brought B-8CF along with her, and the protocol automaton found himself in the service of the Lunlgor family, who owned a moisture farm in the Rinmund Gardris. Nysera covered him in a tarnished grey plating to shield him from Tevlan's sandy environment. Ten years after his parting with Yarwyn Daelthos, Nysera was kidnapped by a group of Ophlgor Raiders, fearsome nomads native to Tevlan's desert gardris. Sensing his mother's pain and anguish through the Synth Flux, Yarwyn returned with his secret lover and childhood friend Nymund Daelphos to find his mother. Daelthos had advanced in his Salphos training as a Novice under the tutelage of Faelnor Halthossul, while Daelphos had become a Senator in the Grand Assembly.

When Daelthos and Daelphos visited the Lunlgor' moisture farm, they found B-8CF working on a moisture moistrig. He initially didn't recognize Daelthos until the latter finished his name for him when introducing himself. Greeting his Maker, B-8CF discovered that Daelthos had come in search of his mother, and brought the two guests into the homestead's courtyard, where they were met by Grimlgorul Lunlgor, as well as his son, Vashyn Lunlgor and his girlfriend, Mormir Salmund. Learning of his mother's peril, Daelthos took Vashyn's skimmer bike to find her, and soon returned with her corpse, having recovered it from a Ophlgor village. B-8CF was present when he arrived, and later attended a funeral for Nysera on the farm's property. When T2-W2, who had been waiting aboard Daelphos's nearby yacht, interrupted the funeral with an urgent message for the Novice, B-8CF translated the navimech's binary beeps and whistles. After viewing the datacomm message, Daelthos and Daelphos departed Tevlan for Quinria to rescue Daelthos's Salphos Master Faelnor Halthossul, taking Cyrphen with them.

Thus, B-8CF would become both a witness and participant in the subsequent First Battle of Quinria, the first spark of the Echo Wars, a conflict that would change the course of sovtia history. After landing on Quinria, B-8CF reluctantly followed T2-W2, Daelthos, and Daelphos into the Quinrian automaton foundry. After being trapped in the factory's conveyor belt system, B-8CF's head was separated from his body and grafted onto the chassis of a K3-series battle automaton. Meanwhile, a K3 battle automaton head was inserted onto Cyrphen's body. During the ensuing battle, both of B-8CF's "bodies" were knocked out during the fighting. Fortunately for him, he was promptly reattached to his rightful body by T2-W2. Following the events on Quinria, B-8CF and T2-W2 travelled to Olrax with Daelthos and Daelphos for the two to be married in Olrax's Lake Country.
