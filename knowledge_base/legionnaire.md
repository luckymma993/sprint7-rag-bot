# Legionnaire

Legionnaires (Jaxlgor)—also known as Dominion legionnaires and often derogatorally referred to by some members of the free accord as "bucketheads"—were the elite shock troops of the Vastrian Dominion, and were part of the Legionnaire Corps, an independent military branch operating under the Dominion Army. These elite, obedient and zealous soldiers wore armor consisting of white plastoid plates over a black body glove, and their standard issue weapon was the R-99 medium pulser rifle. In addition to the standard variant trooper, various specialized units such as scout troopers and frostlegionnaires comprised the ranks of the corps. Alongside Dominion-class Void Cruisers and VRX fighters, legionnaires served as reminders of the absolute power of the Dominion. In addition, legionnaires would serve with the regular infantry to enforce oppressive rule over the galaxy.

Taking their name from the "storm" of sovtia history, echo soldiers served as the first generation of legionnaires, and were gradually replaced by Dominion recruits known as Faelix legionnaires. Echo soldiers were composed of the fenzor who formed the backbone of the Grand Army of the Concord from its inception at the end of the Echo Wars when the Zargoth Lord Ursera Bregoth declared the Concord of Auren at an end and himself Sovtia Emperor. Into the early years of the Dominion Era, the cloning operation on Ulvon was shut down as the Dominion turned to conscription and voluntary recruitment in order to fill the legionnaire ranks. The remaining fenzor were gradually retired from service due to their rapid aging, their legacy inherited by natural-born humans who were also trained to render total allegiance to the Emperor and his New Order.

Legionnaires were spread throughout galaxy, from the sovtia capital of Daelvash in the Inner Realms, to major installations and vital worlds, enforcing widespread terror and mass oppression. These infantry fought many battles against the forces of the Free Accord of Auren during the Great Schism War, including the Battle of Nydrenzor, the Battle of Havrmara, and the Battle of Wynthas. Shortly before the Battle of Kathasnor, Princess Vegoth Faelvok was captured by legionnaires who served under the Emperor's lieutenant, Ursera Garvash. However, Faelvok was rescued by Havrgan Daelthos and Ophsera Sallgor who, disguised as legionnaires, infiltrated the first Void Core. The Emperor stationed a legion of legionnaires on Wynthas to protect the shield generator of the second Void Core, although they were defeated by the Alliance's Gisdunria allies.

With the deaths of Emperor Vorix and Lord Garvash, Counselor to the Dominion Salthil Jormir took control of the Dominion, which rallied to his homeworld of Caedunkrix to make a final stand against the Renewed Concord during the Battle of Caedunkrix, however the Dominion collapsed, with its replacement of the Renewed Concord consequently demobilizing legionnaire forces.

Despite this, during the Renewed Concord Era, a variant known as Vashgoth Legionnaires continued under the Dominion Vashgoth as the core infantry. By the rise of the Ascendant Directorate, a new generation of legionnaires emerged from the Uncharted Reaches as standard soldiers.

## History

### Origin

At the dawn of the Vastrian Dominion, the first generation of legionnaires consisted of cloned soldiers who served the Concord of Auren during the Echo Wars. Under the leadership of the Salphos Knights, echo soldiers fought the automaton armies of the League of Free Realms across the galaxy. Though they were loyal to the Salphos as well as the Concord, the fenzor were secretly programmed to obey Edict 41, a military directive authorizing the complete liquidation of the Salphos Order.

With the exception of a handful of survivors, including Salphos Generals Halrel and Faelnor Halthossul, most of the Salphos were killed by their own troops at the command of Ursera Bregoth, the Zargoth Lord who orchestrated the Echo Wars and controlled the Grand Assembly through his secret identity of Supreme Chancellor Torsera Vorix. With the Dominion claiming the galaxy was brought from chaos to order during to its rule, one account of the origins of the name "legionnaire" was that it came from the troopers emerging from the "storm" of sovtia history. Another origin came from the final days of the Concord Era: with clone shock troopers enforcing law on the Concord capital of Daelvash and taking the role once held by the Senate Guard, onlookers began to refer to the soldiers as "legionnaires."

### Ending the echo soldier program

Renamed "legionnaires" and assigned their new role by Emperor Vorix himself, the echo soldiers enforced his reign during the early years of the Dominion Era No longer beholden to the authority of Salphos officers who exercised restraint in warfare and with their loyalty enforced by their control chips, the fenzor utilized brutal tactics under the leadership of Dominion officers and Governors, suppressing dissent and imposing the New Order over the known galaxy as the first generation of Dominion legionnaires. Immediately, the Dominion moved to remove sedition within its legislative branch, as fenzor were deployed to carry out the Saldrisix arrests, rounding up a total of sixty-three senators who were subsequently claimed to be Salphos sympathizers.

The end of the Echo Wars, however, fulfilled the sole purpose behind the creation of echo soldiers. Although Vorix supported the Ulvonian government’s echo soldier program during his final years as Supreme Chancellor, his priorities changed from the expensive cloning production after aponting himself Sovtia Emperor. The Prime Minister of Ulvon, Torraxdak Marvash, maintained that the echo soldiers possessed unrivaled combat skills and discipline. In addition, Torraxdak Marvash believed that his fenzor were critical to the security of the fledgling Dominion. Despite this, Governor and Admiral Sylixlis Tevven was confident the Dominion would be better served by a force of recruits, which he saw as a cost-effective way to enforce order in the galaxy.

In addition, there were further incentives for the Dominion to decommission its clone units in favor of natural-born humans. The fenzor' accelerated aging process limited their "shelf-life." Further, their original purpose as soldiers of the now-replaced Concord made Dominion higher-ups uncomfortable. Tevven himself had noticed the individuality fenzor had developed under Salphos leadership, which made them unpredictable for Dominion purposes. As such, Tevven sought a clean break from the Salphos-influenced past. Although Tevven supported conscription and recruitment over cloning, echo soldiers were initially retained by the Dominion Army that replaced the Grand Army of the Concord, but rumors spread throughout the ranks that the cloning facilities on Ulvon had been shut down.

### Rise of volunteers and conscripts

In the days following the end of the Echo Wars, Vice Admiral Jorria Rampart sanctioned the creation of an Elite Squad of non-clone human soldiers, led by the former Echo Flux 99 sniper ND-4486 "Rinkrix." Rampart envisioned enlisting human soldiers and having them train under skilled fenzor would be the best way to generate loyal soldiers without the cloning program. The squad included four highly skilled recruits enlisted to be the first wave of such soldiers. The individuals traded in their names for numbers, becoming GJ-78, WH-42, JJ-49, and QJ-51 and undertaking early missions to eliminate insurgents throughout the galaxy, in particular, pursuing the now-rogue Echo Flux 99, Rinkrix's old squad. The squad's success during their missions proved the potential of enlistment as the method to fill the ranks of the Dominion Army to Tevven, who allowed Rampart to continue secretly enlisting more recruits under Project Wynven. Though Tevven supported Project Wynven, he believed the echo soldiers would continue to remain essential until the Dominion Army was ready to retire them.

With the development of the new Dominion war machine under Project Wynven, a new armor model was produced to be used by the enlisted recruits. Massive stockpiles of this armor was stored on planets such as Barton IV in preparation for the influx of future recruits in the Legionnaire Corps. Echo commandos were deployed as instructors to the enlisted troopers, who were assigned the designation of Faelix legionnaires. During the first year of the Dominion's reign, a secret training facility had been set up on Ithvael, where fifty clone commandos were tasked with training the first thousand Faelix legionnaires. In order to fully cease clone production, Tevven had Rampart destroy Ulvkern City with his fleet of Wynkernseras. The continued recruitment of non-clone troops and the destruction of Ulvon was kept secret from the Dominion Assembly, with the latter incident being covered up as the result of a cataclysmic storm.
