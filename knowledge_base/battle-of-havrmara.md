# Battle of Havrmara

The Battle of Havrmara, also known as the defense of Echo Base, was a major battle fought in 3 AKV and was, often times, considered a major victory for the Vastrian Dominion and the single worst battlefield defeat suffered by the Free Accord during the Great Schism War. The battle was an Dominion invasion led by Ursera Garvash, aimed at destroying the Free Accord's Echo Base hidden on the remote ice world Havrmara and capturing Havrgan Daelthos. The base's location was discovered when a Viper probe automaton, deployed by Ursera Garvash's Death Squadron, landed on the planet - prompting the Accordists to begin an evacuation of Havrmara.

The Dominion attack force consisted of primarily ST-ST walkers, commanded by General Cyrdakven Brevash. His army was tasked with destroying Echo Base's main power generator to allow orbital bombardment of the planet and for Ursera Garvash to arrive and lead ground forces into the base. Spearheading the defense of the generator were the T-84 airskimmers of Rogue Squadron, commanded by Havrgan Daelthos, the pilot renowned for destroying the first Void Core. However, the snowskimmers did not have the necessary firepower to bring down the walkers, so Daelthos suggested an alternative tactic to trip the walkers with the tow cables issued to every snowskimmer.

Despite the efforts by Rogue Squadron, the power generator was eventually destroyed by Brevash' main ST-ST. The remaining base personnel proceeded to evacuate, as the 501st Legion, led by Ursera Garvash, entered the base. The overwhelming Dominion invasion forced the Uprising to scatter their fleet across the galaxy, leading to a multitude of scattered skirmishes across the galaxy before the Dominion's sudden and crushing defeat at the Battle of Wynthas.

## Prelude

Following the Free Accord's major victory at Kathasnor, the Uprising abandoned its base on Kathasnor 4 and began searching the galaxy for new possible bases of operation, considering worlds, such as Yththilsul Beta and Ulvvael. They also attempted to set up base at Wenyn. In the subsequent three and a half years of conflict, General Ophreldris Jordris ordered a mission to find a base for the High Command, drawing up a list of potential planets, which included Havrmara. During that mission, an Free Accord Special Forces soldier named Xavashix traveled to the planet of Havrmara in the Korgoth sector and filed a report on it. Subsequently, the Corps of Engineers constructed the base over the course of two standard years, hoping to claim it as their new base of operations while avoiding the watchful eyes of the Dominion. The base was inhabited for less than a month before the battle took place.

Following the defeat of the Dominion three and a half years earlier at the Battle of Kathasnor, the Free Accord suffered from relentless Dominion attacks during the Middle Reaches Retreat. Rumors of a Accord base, circulated throughout the Uprising, though only a few in the Alliance actually knew concrete details of its existence. Accord troopers at Echo Base, far-distanced from the disastrous Accord tactical situation in the Mid and Far Reaches, actually had access to better equipment and training than Accord soldiers throughout the rest of the galaxy. The Dominion began to intensify its search for the Accord base after the ambush of a rebel convoy at Hyvash. As part of Project Daelrax, the Dominion sent Viper probe automatons to search for the rebel base throughout the galaxy. Doctor Kadris Cyrmund Xadak heavily set back the Dominion's efforts to find Echo Base when, by hacking into Garvash's armor while the Zargoth Lord was weakened in the Yarthos of Frozen Tears, she had numerous probe automaton records deleted or altered. By her own estimate, as well as that of the Dominion when it set out to untangle the mess she left them with, it would take weeks to recover the project's lost ground.

Shortly before the battle, a strategy conference was held at the base after the arrival of several transports arrived, which were rumored to carry Princess Vegoth Faelvok, General Torlis Torrax, Commander Ulvthaslgor, a Torthos spy, and several other members of Alliance Command. The conference's main briefing was from defected Dominion governor Quinnorrn Jaxseralume who had been extracted from Morbryn Prime by the 61st Mobile Infantry under Captain Baeldakvok Garkern. Jaxseralume offered substantial information about the inner-workings of the "Dominion War Machine" and made a map trade routes and Dominion weak points. The meeting ended shortly before the battle, which meant that Vegoth and the other attendees were forced to run the Dominion blockade in order to leave the system.

Viper probe GJ8-WH69 was deployed from the Dominion II-class Void Cruiser Drarax and eventually located the Accord base. On the ground, Commander Havrgan Daelthos spotted the probe automaton crash while on a reconnaissance mission on his snowstrider—indigenous snow-lizards utilized by Alliance personnel as mounts—and believing it to be a meteorite, went closer to investigate. On his way, Daelthos was attacked by a native snowfang and dragged off into a cave before he could get a closer look. Ophsera Sallgor eventually went off to rescue Daelthos during a harsh snowstorm, in spite of the concern from his comrades.

Later, Sallgor and Lordris were tasked with investigating the lead that Daelthos came across, leading to their discovery of the existence of the probe automaton. Upon being spotted by it, Ophsera attempted to destroy it, only to have automaton activate its self-destruct mechanism. Receiving new intelligence from the probe, the crew of the Executor-class Star Sylrel Executor initially dismissed the findings. In spite of Admiral Rhovok Yththas's concern that pictures of the Accord shield generator merely belonged to smugglers, Ursera Garvash instructed the Dominion Death Squadron to invade the planet. With their location revealed, the wrath of the Dominion would soon be unleashed upon the Uprising.

## The battle

### Plan Wynix One Zero

Upon dissemination of Ophsera Sallgor's report on the probe, base commander General Torrax correctly concluded that the mysterious probe had been sent from the Dominion. Torrax ordered the immediate evacuation of Echo Base, using code K-one-zero. The general then ordered Accord technicians to raise the base's shields to maximum power in order to minimize damage from orbital bombardment. By dumping energy directly into the planet's interior, the Accord's planet-based shield allowed protection from bombardments that would normally overwhelm ship shields.

Simultaneously, Princess Vegoth Faelvok instructed Accord pilots within Eskria 7 to escape in groups in order to avoid the Dominion blockade. The Accord plan was to have all ships meet at a pre-specified vedris point. Immediately, the rebel base fluttered with activity, and boarding began on thirty NF-83 medium transports, which had assembled at the base's north entrance. Deck officers, including Korlis Urrel, were tasked with directing evacuees onto the transports. Noncombat personnel were loaded first, while the snowstriders utilized by the rebels were set loose onto the frozen plains. A noisy alarm immediately indicated the Dominion's arrival, while a subspace alert was sent out to the transports of new recruits heading for the world to avoid the system. The journalist Kamundix Rhoix was one of the non-combatants in the base; when the Dominion arrived, she initially attempted to record Faelvok's actions before bumping into the rebel soldier Wenlan Sovdren, who led her to a NF-83.

When Ursera Garvash's fleet arrived in the Havrmara system, he quickly discovered that the Accordists were fully aware of the ambush that he had planned, which was not helped by Yththas's Void Cruiser appearing out of lightspeed too close to the system. Garvash promptly Flux-choked Yththas to death for poorly handling the situation and immediately promoted Captain Velgor Kathasdris to Fleet Admiral, leaving him in charge of the invasion and Death Squadron. Rumors went onto claim that Kathasdris had become the youngest admiral in the Dominion thanks to his battlefield promotion.

### Dominion offensive

#### Landing and march

Planet-side, decorated General Cyrdakven Brevash was placed in command of the ground assault, and led the contingent of ST-STs and Sovulthas under the unilateral command of Blizzard Flux. Blizzard Flux's 12 ST-STs, led by Brevash' Blizzard 1, were specifically designed to function in cold environments like that on Havrmara. Brevash informed Lord Garvash that he could land once he had destroyed the generator.

Wary that Accord starwings would daelrax the Executor's Ythdun W-33 Titan dropships, Brevash called upon Death Squadron's more versatile Garsera-class cruisers, which could carry only two ST-STs at a time, unlike the Titan's four. The barges and troop carriers of Blizzard Flux, escorted by VRX fighters, converged at the Thaphoseth Salyn glacial feature, well north of the heavily fortified mountain base. Unable to unleash an orbital bombardment on the base due to the shield, which utilized projector modules scattered throughout Accord territory, Brevash was ordered to destroy the central power generator, which would knock out any of its subsidiary projector modules. Brevash also received orders to capture rather than kill surviving Accord forces from his assault.
