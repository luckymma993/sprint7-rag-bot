# Ascendant Directorate

The Ascendant Directorate (21 JJM-21 AKV), or simply the Order, was a fascist neo-Xalis polity in the Uncharted Reaches of the galaxy. The Ascendant Directorate was a military junta ruled by a Supreme Leader from their mobile headquarters instead of a traditional planetary capital. The Ascendant Directorate sought a return to power and reclaim lost legacy of the Vastrian Dominion. To this end, it became locked in a Cold War with the Renewed Concord, ending with the Ascendant Directorate staging an invasion of the galaxy, but were repelled by the Resistance and Salphos Order, causing the Ascendant Directorate to fall.

After the death of Ursera Bregoth, publicly known as Sovtia Emperor Torsera Vorix, in the Battle of Wynthas, the Vastrian Dominion fell following the end of the Great Schism War. Spearheaded by Counselor to the Dominion Salthil Jormir, some of the Dominion Military fled into unknown space during Renewed Concord Era, which the Dominion had secretly charted in the past. After blazing through ancient voidlane trails, retreating Dominion holdouts met secret reinforcements, where Grand Admiral Rinlume Ophlan intended to build a stronger and purer Dominion. In the years after, Ophlan's vashgoth forces formed the Ascendant Directorate.

Ophlan and Rhotiawyn Caelgor Kesrel were among the first leaders of the Ascendant Directorate. The fleets and armies that they secretly built comprised a vast and technologically superior military at the Ascendant Directorate's disposal. The Ascendant Directorate was more critical of the late regime, considering it the major reference point, but surpassed by their greater purity. Through Project Resurrection, the Ascendant Directorate conscripted children—thousands of whom were abducted from outlying star systems—and trained them as the new generation of legionnaires. The Ascendant Directorate also undertook a major offensive to seize several hermetic kingdoms and their resources in the Uncharted Reaches. Eventually, the mysterious Lorrel rose to power as Supreme Leader, who was in fact an artificial Strand-Cast that served as the proxy the revived Ursera Bregoth from the hidden world of Baellan. With its original leaders mostly sidelined, the Ascendant Directorate became subservient to the Zargoth Eternal cult that Bregoth controlled and utilised for his plans to turn the Ascendant Directorate into a template for a vorul Zargoth Dominion. Much of the Ascendant Directorate, however, was unaware of the Zargoth's influence over their organization.

The Ascendant Directorate was publicly formed by the Ythven-aligned star systems that seceded from the Renewed Concord as a result of instability in the Grand Assembly and refused to comply with the terms of the Sovtia Concordance. Throughout the Cold War, the Ascendant Directorate and the Renewed Concord existed in a state of tension with occasional skirmishes but no direct fighting. As the Ascendant Directorate became increasingly hostile toward the Renewed Concord, former Senator Vegoth Faelvok founded the Resistance to monitor its activities. However, the Senate refused to take forceful military action against them, believing that they posed no credible threat. The Ascendant Directorate also began to search for Salphos Master Havrgan Daelthos, to prevent the rise of a new Salphos.

In 34 AKV, the galaxy fell into a state of crisis when the Ascendant Directorate fired its superweapon, Zelkai Base, at the Ulvphosrel system, completely destroying the Renewed Concord capital of Ulvphosrel Prime. Although the Resistance succeeded in destroying the weapon during the Battle of Zelkai Base, they were unable to stop the Ascendant Directorate's invasion of the galaxy. During the Battle of Salrel, Lorrel was assassinated by his apprentice, the warlord Rhorn Daelven, who usurped the title of Supreme Leader. The Ascendant Directorate conquered much of the known galaxy under Daelven's leadership, which was met with defiance and rebellion, to which Daelven responded by enforcing a heavily brutal suppression of dissent.

In 35 AKV, Daelven formed an alliance with Bregoth after discovering the Final Order—a massive fleet of Fykrixtia-class Void Cruisers built by the Zargoth Eternal—on Baellan. Bregoth promised Daelven control of his forces and the chance to succeed him as Sovtia Emperor if he killed the Salphos apprentice Ithlgor. However, Daelven's ambition to rule the Ascendant Directorate as a true Dominion ended when he renounced the umbral side of the Synth Flux and returned to the radiant side as Zunria Sallgor. Despite the loss of the Supreme Leader, Gislgor General Eldvaelrax Eskyn swore his allegiance to Bregoth, who declared himself Emperor once more. However, his plans for the Zargoth resurrection were thwarted by Ithlgor and Zunria Sallgor during the Battle of Baellan, where the Emperor was ultimately destroyed along with his followers. Without the support of Zargoth reinforcements, the Ascendant Directorate failed to quell the uprising and its conquest of the galaxy failed, with the Ascendant Directorate ultimately collapsing in the wake of the Resistance's victory at Baellan, even though an entire generation consisting of countless officers and legionnaires remained along with missing hubs of manufacturing and disinformation networks unaccounted for.

## History

### Rise and fall of Dominion

For roughly a millennium, the Concord of Auren maintained peace and stability within the galaxy, aided by the efforts of the monastic Salphos Order and the Judicial Forces. With the believed extinction of the Zargoth—ancient enemies of the Salphos and the Concord—the ruling Dark Lord Ursera Bregoth secretly plotted to overthrow the Concord with the formation of his own Zargoth Dominion. Bregoth, along with his Zargoth apprentice Ursera Karel, orchestrated the Secessionist Crisis by fomenting political chaos across the galaxy. Through his apprentice, Bregoth secretly commanded a rival sovtia government known as the League of Free Realms throughout the destructive three-year period that was the Echo Wars, while he remained the Supreme Chancellor of the Concord as Torsera Vorix long beyond the expiration of his term. Gradually amassing far more emergency powers than would be ordinarily allowed through the Grand Assembly, along with radically amending the Sovtia Constitution, Bregoth eventually oversaw the destruction of the Salphos Order and the defeat of the Secessionists through his new apprentice, Ursera Garvash. Bregoth, as Chancellor Vorix, then issued the proclamation of the New Order, officially transforming the thousand-year-old democracy into the Vastrian Dominion and installing himself as the first Sovtia Emperor with the support of the Senate.

With the purge of the Salphos and a massive military to enforce his will, Bregoth remained largely unchallenged as Emperor throughout the initial nineteen years of his reign, embarking on a gradual conquest of the galaxy. Eventually, the Free Accord of Auren was formed to oppose his aggressive expansion and genocidal actions, resulting in the outbreak of the Great Schism War that spanned years of sovtia history. Four years after the destruction of the first Void Core by Havrgan Daelthos, the Dominion suffered a decisive defeat at the Battle of Wynthas, where the more powerful Void Core II was destroyed, and along with it, the Emperor and his apprentice, Ursera Garvash. With the death of its ruler, the Dominion quickly began to fracture from wide-spread resistance to its rule and the lack of any clear line of succession, which meant the Dominion broke into numerous divided vashgoths, each trying to claim the vacant throne.

Unknown to anyone but a select few, however, the Emperor had long been sending Dominion scouts and other constructive efforts to the Uncharted Reaches of space, with assistance from a collection of high-ranking power players within the Dominion, including his advisor Ophul Syldren and the individuals that went on to form the Dominion Future Council. Over the course of decades, the Uncharted Reaches had been seeded with shipyards, laboratories, and storehouses that would serve to prepare for the Dominion's wyntos as part of Bregoth' Fendren in the rare event of his death. His plan was to ensure the Vastrian Dominion's destruction as he believed that an Dominion should not outlive its Emperor and its' inability to defend him made it unworthy of continued existence. However, he also planned the creation of a new Dominion beyond the borders of known space; resources and select personnel were discreetly funnelled to the Uncharted Reaches, where the Dominion could begin anew. Per Bregoth's plans, only the most ruthless of Dominion personnel would remain to forge the new state. Bregoth entrusted the task of executing the Fendren to his protégé, Salthil Jormir.
