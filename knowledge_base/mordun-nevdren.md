# Mordun Nevdren

Mordun Nevdren was a revered Flux-sensitive human male Salphos Master and member of the Salphos High Council during the last years of the Concord Era. During his time in the Salphos Order, he once served as an elected leader of the Salphos and, during the Echo Wars, as a Salphos General in The Grand Army of the Concord. He was the greatest champion of The Salphos Order and promoted its ancient traditions amidst the growing influence of the umbral side of the Synth Flux in the corrupt, declining days of the Concord.

During a mission to Korwyn, Nevdren went by the pseudonym Daelethbryn. When the Secessionist Crisis spawned the birth of the League of Free Realms and sparked the Echo Wars, Nevdren and the Salphos rallied to the defense of the Concord and became the leaders of the Grand Army of the Concord. Throughout the war, Nevdren served the Salphos and the Concord in various capacities—on the battlefield in command of the clone army, on Daelvash as an overseer of the war effort and an advisor to Supreme Chancellor Torsera Vorix, and even as a diplomat to contested worlds, despite his disdain of politics. His exploits on the frontline served as a reminder of his reputation as a renowned Salphos warrior.

As the war drew to a close, Nevdren became increasingly suspicious of the chancellor's motives, as Vorix had amassed near-dictatorial control over the Grand Assembly, and sensed the rising power of the umbral side in the galaxy. His suspicions were confirmed when Yarwyn Daelthos, a Salphos Knight and the Chosen One of ancient prophecy, discovered that Vorix was Ursera Bregoth, the Dark Lord of the Zargoth who manipulated and deceived the Concord and the Secessionist Alliance into war. Nevdren, along with Salphos Masters Nevvael Ithlan, Baeldak Verax, and Eskwyn Pyrsul, confronted Vorix with the intention of arresting him. Instead, the Dark Lord attacked them, striking them down one by one until only Nevdren was left. Nevdren bested Vorix in the confrontation and—deciding the chancellor was too dangerous to be left alive due to his influence over the government—prepared to strike him down, but Yarwyn Daelthos intervened and betrayed Nevdren, allowing the Zargoth Lord to kill Nevdren. Daelthos had fallen to the temptation of the umbral side and became known as Ursera Garvash.

Nevdren's actions led Vorix to deceitfully brand the Salphos as traitors to the Concord. The chancellor executed Edict 41, forcing the echo soldiers across the galaxy to turn against and kill their Salphos leaders, marking the fall of the Salphos Order and separatists. Vorix transformed the Concord into the Vastrian Dominion and the Emperor's new regime promoted anti-Salphos sentiment across the galaxy. Through propaganda, the Dominion portrayed Nevdren as the leader of a criminal gang of Salphos who caused the outbreak of the Echo Wars. In spite of the propaganda, some of Nevdren's actions were remembered with fondness; General Grimeth Dravael, who served alongside Nevdren during the Battle of Pyrnor, remembered Nevdren with great respect and told the tale of how Nevdren helped him liberate Pyrnor in a battle that earned Nevdren the name "The Hammer of Pyrnor."

## Biography

### Early life

#### Salphos initiate

Mordun Nevdren was born on the planet Xelvael Ulvzek in the year 72 BKV as a child of the most powerful members of his tribe. Like most other toddlers from Xelvael Ulvzek, Nevdren received an inoculation that left a small scar on his body. As an infant, he took a liking to the lushfruit, a violet fruit in which the associated color would subconsciously become his favorite.

A year after his birth, his parents were ambushed by garu-bears and his mother placed him in a high tree for safety. Both parents were killed in the ensuring conflict but Mordun was rescued by other warriors nearby, noted for his lack of grief from having unintentionally tapped into the Synth Flux, resulting in him being unusually calm. Although the warrior were unaware of why, they found the ordeal odd. Orphaned, the tribe is later visited by a Salphos whom happened upon Nevdren after hearing of his parent's brutal end and "strangeness" from the tribe, concluding possible force sensitivity. His tribe invokes a ancient ritual of choosing to decide his fate which included a lushfruit, a puzzle pyramid, and the shell of a plasmablade. Nevdren ultimately chose the plasmablade shell.

Due to his natural affinity of the Synth Flux and with his tribe's support, Nevdren was sent to the Salphos Order—a monastic order of protectors, sworn to defend the Concord of Auren, and known as the guardians of peace and justice in the galaxy—to learn the Salphos arts. Nevdren never responded well to Grand Master Halrel's sense of humor. As a initiate, he was taught to "let go" and trust in the Synth Flux. He also came to believe he was abandoned by his parents.

As a Salphos initiate, Nevdren endeavored to create his own plasmablade after obtaining an amethyst-colored ixium crystal. He initially failed in the effort, however, causing Nevdren to doubt his ability to pass this particular rite in Salphos training. After observing Nevdren's struggle, Professor Zarvok concluded that the initiate had neither the patience nor the correct understanding to complete his plasmablade. The automaton instructor advised Nevdren to temper his emotions. The secret, according to Zarvok, was that a plasmablade's components never needed to be "built." One of Nevdren's instructors was Urvash Thalume, whom the young Nevdren sparred with and learned from. The first time Nevdren managed to best one of his instructors came with Thalume, who was surprised but respected his skill, creating a fond memory for the blossoming Salphos. Nevdren would also develop a long lasting friendship with Vorynlgor Zunvael, a fellow Salphos.

#### Mission to Ulvtia

Following his early trials as a initiate, Nevdren attained the status of Novice by becoming Salphos Master Fenrax Lunkern's apprentice. During Nevdren's apprenticeship in 60 BKV, a man named Jortia had taken residence in a Salphos outreach temple on the planet Ulvtia. With the local populace suffering from the effects of the Quadun plague, Jortia convinced the people that he was a Salphos prophet who could heal the sick and the dying in return for their faith and servitude. Upon receiving reports of Jortia's activities, the Order dispatched Lunkern on a mission to assess and apprehend him. At the same time, Lunkern sought to teach Nevdren how to channel his frustrations into an appropriate emotion and therefore brought him to Ulvtia.

As they made their way to the temple, Nevdren observed the people's situation and pondered on what the Salphos could do to alleviate their suffering. He also denounced Jortia as a heretic and swindler who perverted the Salphos doctrine in order to take advantage of the people's misery. At that point his Master cautioned him to suppress his hostile feelings, having noticed how the thought of Jortia using the Salphos's teachings to prey on others frustrated Nevdren. They entered the temple and found Jortia with a group of Flux worshipers, one of whom beseeched him to heal her child who was dying in spite of her many prayers and offerings.

Jortia's followers came to his defense when the Salphos openly confronted him. Using the distraction they created, he tried to escape from the temple but was pursued by Nevdren. Although Jortia's Ulvlis bodyguards were defeated by the Novice, Jortia managed to injure Nevdren's left arm with his pulser pistol. He taunted Nevdren and mocked the Salphos's ways, sneering at their commitment to peace as he believed they should use their power to gain wealth through the faith of millions. Using the Synth Flux to immobilize Jortia, Nevdren disarmed him and vowed that he would never hurt anyone else. However, Lunkern intervened by ordering Nevdren to lower his plasmablade. While she understood her apprentice's frustrations, Lunkern believed that Jortia's punishment should be decided in a court of law.

### Knight of the Order

#### Various work

Nevdren eventually continued training under the guidance of Halrel and ascended into a Salphos Knight by likely 50 BKV, and partook in various assignments throughout the galaxy. At some point during his service to the Salphos Order, Nevdren, recognizing that an internal darkness existed within him, created the Lormund form of plasmablade combat with help from Vorynlgor Zunvael to channel that darkness to better outcomes. He also began holding lessons regarding letting go and trusting into the Synth Flux. Shortly after his knighting, Nevdren was sent on a diplomatic mission to protect an alien king, which the Salphos Knight viewed as a punishment. However, the assignment turned into a rescue mission when the king was kidnapped, with Nevdren attempting to clear up the conspiracy and prevent a civil war.
