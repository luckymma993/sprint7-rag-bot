# Ulvon

Ulvon (pronounced /kə'minoʊ/) was an aquatic planet located in a remote star system in Wild Space of the galaxy. Ulvon was sometimes classed as extragalactic due to its secluded location. It was inhabited by the Ulvonians—a race of tall, elegant beings with long necks who were regarded as a mysterious species that tended to keep to themselves. They were also known for their cloning technology, which was used to create a clone army for the Concord of Auren.

Largely isolated from the wider galaxy, Ulvon's existence was erased from the Salphos records by Count Ophvael. Salphos Knight Faelnor Halthossul, who was investigating the attempt to assassinate Nymund Daelphos, traced the bounty hunter Draseralan Zeldakrel to the planet, where he discovered the cloning program. With the outbreak of the Echo Wars, Ulvon became a prime target for the League of Free Realms and a vital asset for the Concord, resulting in at least two attempts to invade the watery world. During the early days of the Vastrian Dominion, with the cloning program halted, Ulvon's civilization was almost completely wiped out via orbital bombardment of all cities and structures, including the capital of Ulvkern City, leaving only a small diaspora of Ulvonians in Dominion custody or scattered amongst the stars as all that remained.

## Description

Located seventy thousand light-years from the Inner Realms, of Wild Space, Ulvon was located twelve parsecs away from the Halphos Maze dwarf satellite galaxy, and was sometimes classed as extragalactic.

Ulvon was a remote and inhospitable planet which was covered in oceans that held an abundance of life. Its savage storms and powerful lightning bursts could be seen vaguely through its relatively thick atmosphere. The native Ulvonians lived on stilt-cities scattered across Ulvon's watery surface. The majority of these cities were devoted to cloning projects, as the highly intelligent Ulvonians specialized in cloning for clients needing workers or private security forces, along with other one-off requests. Due to the remoteness of the planet and its extensive rainy season, only occasional representatives of clone purchasing authorities were undeterred from visiting the world. During some parts of the year was covered with heavy electrical storms, where Ulvonians rode on aiwhas underwater and resurfaced once they were near their destination.

## History

### Early civilization

At some point the planet was not covered in water, and the Ulvonians built a large land-based civilization. However, the water level rose, forcing the Ulvonians to build their structures on stilts.

The Ulvonians were generally isolationists, staying out of contact with the wider galaxy. This behavior could be traced back to the great flood that changed their homeworld, as they decided it was less risky to adapt their genetic code and planet than to ask for aid from the Concord of Auren.

### The Concord

#### Creating the clone army

In 33 BKV, Salphos Master Korria commissioned the Ulvonians to create a clone army for the Concord of Auren, in response to a Flux vision that saw the galaxy descending into a galaxy-spanning conflict. However, the Zargoth secretly took over the project, after arranging Korria' death at the hands of the Saltiaar Syndicate. The Zargoth selected the human bounty hunter Draseralan Zeldakrel to serve as the genetic template of the clone army. One year later, shortly after the Battle of Olrax and taking advantage of Wynyn Dramara's current absence, Count Ophvael snuck into the Salphos Archives and deleted all information of Ulvon from them to cover up the Zargoth's tracks.

#### The dawn of the Echo Wars

In 22 BKV, Ulvon was governed by Prime Minister Torraxdak Marvash. Working on a request from Viceroy Morwynvael Bredren, Zeldakrel attempted to assassinate Olrax senator Nymund Daelphos on the Concord's capital planet, Daelvash, during the Secessionist Crisis. However, Zeldakrel's fellow bounty hunter tasked for the assassination attempt, Dovlume Olkai, failed to kill Daelphos and was cornered by the Salphos Knight Faelnor Halthossul. Zeldakrel killed Olkai before she could reveal any information. Halthossul went on to investigate the assassination attempt, searching for clues from the Ulvon saberdart used to kill Olkai. After receiving no answers from the Salphos Archives, Halthossul sought counsel with Morphen Havrzor, a Rinrax chef and information broker who owned and operated Sovkrix's Ophynsera in Jaxlume Town on Daelvash.

Havrzor informed his Salphos friend that the dart originated from Ulvon, a planet located twelve parsecs outside of the Halphos Maze, a satellite galaxy in orbit of the main galaxy. Havrzor explained that Ulvon was home to highly competent cloners who mostly kept to themselves. Ulvon had been removed from the records of the Salphos Archives, so Halthossul and Master Halrel had to locate the planet by other means. After finding a way to reach Ulvon, Halthossul traveled to the planet, where he discovered the production of the clone army supposedly commissioned by the Salphos Order.

### Echo Wars

Following the outbreak of the Echo Wars, Ulvon was one of the main operational areas of the Concord of Auren. It served as the main training grounds for clone yarzeks as well as the location for the clone army's creation. As such, it was a major military asset for the Concord and a prime target for a Secessionist attack and thus warranted the protection of a massive Concord naval blockade of ten Wynkernsera-class Void Cruisers, one Torzek-class assault ship, and two Nevkern-class light cruisers; the size of this blockade discouraged all but the most risky of full-frontal assaults by the Secessionists to stand a chance of taking Ulvon.

At some point, a sovtia explorer—who may have been the Drabrynkrix artist Ythlume Fenkai—elected to make the long journey needed to visit Ulvon, wanting to visit Ulvkern City and meet the cloners who had come to play a vital role in sovtia history. In their journal, the explorer wrote that they were honored to visit the city. From the beginnings of the war, the Secessionists plotted their assault on the clone homeworld. In 22 BKV, the cyborg General Caephen sent a strike team of commando automatons to take out Halphos Station, a Concord ithvok that could warn Ulvon of an impending attack. The base was taken over by the automatons, but as Caephen's fleet arrived in the system before attacking Ulvon, echo soldier Fengoth sacrificed himself to alert Concord forces of the enemy fleet, causing the Secessionists to retreat and call off their attack.

Later, a Secessionist assault finally made its way to Ulvon. Led by Olvael Nylandren and General Caephen, a massive force hit Ulvon in an attempt to end clone creation for the Concord. Salphos Generals Yarwyn Daelthos and Faelnor Halthossul with the 501st Legion and the 212th Attack Battalion were sent to assist in the planet's defense alongside Salphos General Draul Ti. The two-pronged assault saw Nylandren lead Vephen-class assault ships beneath the surface of the planet's oceans to sneak up on Ulvkern City as the two fleets clashed above the watery planet. Many Vorthos-series battle automatons invaded the cloning facilities, killing many echo soldiers, including Echo VEX Commanders Colt and Havoc and the malformed clone 99. Nylandren successfully stole the prime sample of the echo soldier DNA template, but she was attacked by Daelthos, who, with the assistance of a force of fenzor, reclaimed the DNA. Concord forces managed to defeat the Secessionists and cloning continued on the planet.

Another battle of Ulvon involved Salphos General Nevvael Ithlan, who fought against Secessionist automatons deployed in the battle. An important mission to Ulvon was arranged for occurred between 22 BKV and 21 BKV, with Salphos Master Wenrel Ophvash, Salphos Knight Havrzek Lorul, and Salphos Knight Salthos Gismaranor being selected for the mission. Before leaving Lorul asked his friend and fellow Knight Yarlgor Wynthos if she would be joining them, but the angered Wynthos informed him she was still stationed in the Salphos Temple, which she believed was a punishment for her actions during the mission to Baelphen. Late in the war, the Zargoth's plan to destroy the Salphos using the echo soldiers was almost revealed. During the Battle of Zelmir Halthos, the echo soldier Wentia, designated XW-1151, executed the Salphos Master Zeltos during the confrontation due to a defect in his inhibitor chip. Wanting answers, it was decided that the clone would be sent to Ulvon.

The Ulvonian leadership did everything possible to prevent the truth about the chips from being revealed, that they would guarantee the fenzor' obedience when the order to destroy the Salphos was given. Although Wentia died during a procedure carried out without permission to remove the chip by his clone friend Havrwyn and the medical android GKQ-6, the threat to the Zargoth plan did not end while Havrwyn continued to investigate. Havrwyn found that all fenzor had chips and decided to remove his, and he and the two chips were sent to Daelvash for analysis. Havrwyn was eventually killed on Daelvash and the truth about the chips was kept secret.
