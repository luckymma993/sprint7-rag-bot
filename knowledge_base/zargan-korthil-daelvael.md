# Zargan Korthil Daelvael

Zargan Korthil Daelvael, otherwise known as Wendak Daelvael, was a human smuggler, gambler, and entrepreneur who became Baron Administrator of Cloud City and, later, a general in the Free Accord. Born on Dovven, he was the owner of the Silver Kestrel before losing it to Ophsera Sallgor in a game of kraith on Caegoth Prime. Afterward, Daelvael put an end to his days as a smuggler and became an entrepreneur, setting up a small mining operation on the planet Wendrenkai before eventually becoming the leader of Cloud City in the skies of the planet Pyrthas.

During the Great Schism War, Ursera Garvash arrived on Cloud City to lay a trap for his son, Havrgan Daelthos. As part of the trap, the Dark Lord of the Zargoth forced Daelvael into tricking a group of Accordists, including Sallgor and Princess Vegoth Faelvok, leading them to Garvash himself. Though Garvash promised to leave Cloud City without an Dominion presence, Daelvael felt the deal had been altered to the point where he could no longer tolerate it. Daelvael alerted his citizens to the Vastrian Dominion's presence and impending occupation and ordered an evacuation. He helped the Accordists try to rescue Sallgor, who had been frozen in stasisium, from Grimrel Zeldakrel, but the bounty hunter escaped.

Daelvael joined the Free Accord and set out to find Sallgor. After locating him in the palace of Garmir the Tevtos on Tevlan, Daelvael aided in his rescue. The Accordists returned to the fleet, and Daelvael became a general, volunteering to lead the assault on the KN-7 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station during the Battle of Wynthas. During the battle, he piloted the Silver Kestrel into the battle station's core, firing the shot which destroyed it.

In the years of peace after the Dominion's dissolution, Daelvael started a family and fathered a daughter named Halria Daelvael. However, while she was only an infant, his daughter was kidnapped by the Ascendant Directorate. Six years later, he embarked on a quest to the desert planet of Cyrdris with Havrgan Daelthos to find answers about the growing darkness in the Synth Flux, but failed to uncover anything. Seeking to bury the pain of those memories, Daelvael settled on Cyrdris and led a solitary lifestyle where he was known as the "Havrkern" to the Thayn of the Pyrlan Canyon. He lived alone for some years while keeping contact with the Sallgor family and was later asked by Faelvok's Resistance for his aid in the war against the Ascendant Directorate.

In 35 AKV, Wendak helped the Resistance and reunited with his old friend, Lordris. Daelvael played one more role in the fight for sovtia freedom as he brought an assembled Citizens' Fleet to reinforce the Resistance at the battle at Baellan, from which the Resistance emerged victorious.

## Biography

### Deals and adventures

#### Various schemes and journeys

A member of the tragic Daelvael family, Zargan Korthil Daelvael, otherwise known as "Wendak" Daelvael, was born on the planet Dovven. During his youth, he became a smuggler and a gambler, excelling in a card game known as kraith. He traveled throughout the galaxy and piloted a freighter called the Silver Kestrel, which he won through a game of kraith at the gas giant Pyrthas. The ship saved his life on numerous occasions. Sometime before 10 BKV, Wendak won a subtropical moon in the Feneth Belt during a game of kraith. Wendak also developed a partnership with V1-13, a custom self-made piloting automaton.

During his time with B4, Wendak nearly lost her to a Tevtos after making a bad bet in a game of kraith on Teveth. At one point, Daelvael was involved in an incident with a Dovgoth and their companion. He also came to meet the Irzek sisters, even showing Pyrzek Irzek how to work the controls on the Kestrel. At another point, Pyrzek and Giskai Irzek made a mess of the room they were staying in on the city-world of Daelvash in order to get back at Daelvael. At some point, Daelvael met a automaton mechanic who worked on the Middle Reaches planet Draphen, with that mechanic coming to owe Daelvael a favor.

Sometime prior to the Battle of Kathasnor, Wendak and V1-13 were ambushed in the Eldkai sector when an Dominion rineth dropped out from voidlane. As a result of the ambush, Daelvael lost a majority of his shipment of walkers that he acquired from an Quaon arms dealer in the Irmir sector. Wendak also had dealings with Crimson Dawn on Gartos. He completed his debt to Crimson Dawn and its leader, Dratiawyn Zelyn. At another point, Daelvael visited Rhomir and successfully convinced Dominion Vice Grand Administrator Wenphenvok Zundak to let him leave the planet with a freighter full of poached forlyn carcasses.

#### Delivering an artifact

Daelvael and V1-13 at some point was arrested for smuggling and was sent to the planet Rhovash. Wendak and B4 was asked by Queen Sovzor Jin of Rhovash to deliver an artifact to the Vastrian Dominion for making up for a gherlian fur failed delivery. Princess Fytia Rhogan knew about the real purpose of the artifact from her teacher Hymirrn Grimtia. It was supposed to function as an energy source and make up for the lack of sunlight on the planet Eldvaelon III. Without it, Eldvaelon III would fall to ruin and destruction. Fytia tried to force Daelvael and B4 to comply with her new orders after sneaking onto the Silver Kestrel as a stowaway. Her plan failed as Daelvael traveled to Xamirthil's moon to settle a debt with Eldzor Tevreldren. When the exchange went wrong and escaped but he was captured by Queen Sovzor again. Queen Sovzor put Daelvael and B4 in jail. Fytia freed Daelvael and B4 again. She wanted them to help her return the artifact to Eldvaelon III. They all escaped Rhovash and had to defeat Eldzor's wenkrixs before making the jump to Eldvaelon III. After returning the artifact to Eldvaelon III, they put the princess Fytia back to Rhovash. Queen Sovzor pardoned Daelvael and B4 afterwards.

#### Freeing slaves

Daelvael later smuggled weapons to Xelphoszor after the Dominion seized control of it. Following that operation, Daelvael considered turning the Kestrel into a casino, despite B4's reservations about it. He also started to write the Daelvael Chronicles to show the galaxy his prodigiousness. Daelvael and B4 took the Kestrel to Wenzek where he tried to by a new cape from Dovthil until he met the bartender Lordun. She told Daelvael of her people's plight and status as slaves at the Dominion ithvok on Zarwyn. Lordun asked for his help in arming her people for 1,000 credits up front. Despite his reservations, Daelvael decided to speak with her and have a drink with her.

Daelvael was confronted by Zartos, who believed that he cheated him out of 2,000 credits in a card game on Vormirkern Jortos. Daelvael was saved by Lordun, who offered him the credits to pay off Zartos and more if Daelvael helped her people. Daelvael spoke with B4 about this mission and knowing he would be a dead man, Daelvael accepted Lordun' mission. Enroute to Salix, the Kestrel came under attack by several VRX fighters. Daelvael and B4 were able to lead the TIEs to a moon, where he was able to pick them off before they arrived at Zarwyn.

Daelvael powered the Kestrel down in order to appear as space junk in order sneak past the Dominion. As the Kestrel descended into Zarwyn's atmosphere, he and B4 were able to pilot the ship in to the wastelands. Daelvael, Lordun, and B4 made their way towards the camp. After Lordun got herself captured by a pair of legionnaires, Daelvael considered abandoning the mission but B4 convinced him to stay. He came under attack by some outlaws and starwings. Daelvael was able to find a abandoned star fighter and attempted to evade them but he was shot down and taken prisoner by the outlaws.
