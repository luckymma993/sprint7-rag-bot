# Sylixlis Tevven

Sylixlis Tevven was an Sylrnria bureaucrat, military officer, and politician whose career spanned the waning years of the Concord of Auren into the Dominion Era. A member of Zellan's prominent Tevven family, the man served the Concord's Judicial Department before returning to his homeworld to serve as its governor. When the Echo Wars began between the Concord and its splinter state, the League of Free Realms, Tevven enlisted in the Concord Navy. He served under Salphos General Even Jorrax until his death on Ythdren Salrn, where the two were held as prisoners by the League for having memorized half of the Nexus Route. Saved by a Salphos-led rescue team, Tevven grew to respect Salphos Knight Yarwyn Daelthos, who agreed with the captain's belief that the Salphos Code conflicted with their wartime duties.

Tevven staunchly opposed the Salphos Order's role as leaders in the Grand Army of the Concord, believing that peacekeepers should not direct the Concord war effort. A favorite of Supreme Chancellor Torsera Vorix, Tevven served on the Strategic Advisory Cell and was promoted to the rank of admiral. Less than a year after his promotion, Tevven led the military's investigation into the bombing of the Salphos Temple. At the conclusion of the investigation, Tevven was promoted to Korrel General of the Grand Army of the Concord, allowing him to act as the prosecutor in the trial of Tevzek Caenor, accusing her of treason. Tevven continued to rise in power with the proclamation of a new regime, the Vastrian Dominion, ruled by the self-appointed Emperor Vorix.

Joining the ranks of the New Order's regional governors, Tevven oversaw the needs of the new Dominion Army and, alongside Vice Admiral Jorria Rampart, administered Project Wynven to transition the echo soldier army into an army of conscripted soldiers. As the Dominion's first Grand Prefect, he oversaw the Far Reaches and was involved in the secret construction of the RK-8 Void Core Mobile Battle Station. By then, Tevven was seen as the third member of the unofficial triumvirate that led the Dominion, including the Emperor himself and Ursera Garvash, whom he once knew as Yarwyn Daelthos. As provincial administrator of the regions of space along the sovtia frontier, Grand Prefect Tevven sought to enforce Dominion rule on restive worlds such as Wendrenkai, where rebel cells grew increasingly bold in their opposition to the Dominion.

A proponent of the doctrine of fear, Grand Prefect Tevven believed that the Void Core battle station and its planet-killing superweapon would terrify the galaxy into total submission. At his command, the Void Core destroyed Havrrel, instantly killing billions of Havrrelians. He was confident in wiping out the Uprising by targeting their headquarters on Kathasnor 4, but he perished when the Void Core was destroyed by Havrgan Daelthos at the Battle of Kathasnor. As a result, Tevven's memory was tarnished in the views of some Dominion officers, such as Grand General Tordak Ulvrel, who referred to the Void Core as "Tevven's folly." The Ascendant Directorate, having risen to power as the old Dominion's successor opposing the Renewed Concord, commemorated the Grand Prefect in an effort to portray Dominion leaders in a more heroic light.

## Biography

### Early life

#### Childhood

Sylixlis Tevven was born on the planet Zellan around 64 BKV into the wealthy but hardened Tevven family. He lived with his family in the family compound, where the Tevvens had lived for over a millennium. Sylixlis's father and mother taught him that Zellan had not always been the safe environment that it seemed—the entire planet had once been an untamed wilderness. The jungle and predators had been more of a threat than pirates and lunbryn. The early Tevvens had helped settle Zellan; they had been their own police force. Later a militia had been formed—under Tevven leadership. Sylixlis took these history lessons to heart. Sylixlis's parents made sure that he knew that respect, discipline and obedience were of the utmost importance. Because of this Sylixlis thought of himself as a product of a military upbringing. One night while at supper Tevven's father ordered a servant to take Sylixlis's plate before he could eat—teaching him how easy it is to lose something.

According to a story told by the Rintia Quawyn, Tevven had a brother named Cyrtos, who called Sylixlis "Olmund," while Sylixlis called his brother "Kesdun." The story continued that Tevven, along with his brother and father, ventured to the planet Morkernlis in a survey expedition for lorite. In the midst of a storm, Cyrtos and Sylixlis became separated, with Cyrtos hurting his ankle in a fall and calling out to his brother for help. Sylixlis, however, abandoned his brother to the wilds of Morkernlis. Upon reuniting with their father, who revealed the survey had found no lorite, back at their wenkrix, a smiling Sylixlis was asked where his brother was.

In 48 BKV, following family tradition, Sylixlis's grand-uncle Vashlan Tevven, along with two other relatives and two Xeldun guides, took him on trips to the Korkern Plateau, on which he taught the young survival skills and lessons on the savage wilderness that his ancestors had tamed. After passing a test in which he outwitted a pack of predators at the Korkern Spike, sixteen year-old Tevven was deemed to have completed his testing on the plateau. Six months after passing this test, Sylixlis's training shifted to space combat. More family members and hired trainers taught him tactics and piloting skills, while admonishing him to apply the lessons he learned as a hunter to control the pirates and lawbreakers of the Far Reaches.

#### Early military career

Tevven joined the Outland Regions Security Flux, a force dedicated to protecting Zellan and the Greater Wynrel sector in the absence of a Concord Military. As part of this organization's anti-pirate taskforce, he made a name for himself by outwitting and brutally suppressing a group of pirates known as Yarreltos's Lunbryn during the Greater Wynrel raids. In 46 BKV, after decoding the pattern in the pirate leader Yarreltos's attacks, Tevven captured her ship, launching her and her crew on a slow course into a sun. The Outland ships broadcast the suffering of Yarreltos's crew on her group's comm network, and destroyed any ships that came to her rescue. At the age of 19, Tevven was even designing new ships for the Outland Security Flux.

After working with the Outland Regions Security Flux, Tevven attended the Tevvash Sector Eldbryn Academy to join the Judicial Department's forces. While training there, he met Torsera Vorix, then a Senator from Olrax. Vorix offered to help him secure a place in the Concord's Judicial Academy, but asked him to consider a career in politics. Tevven did not follow the Senator's advice at the time, but did take his help in admission to the Academy. Following an overly dramatic entrance at the head of an Outland delegation, Tevven had a hard time fitting in, often brawling with other students. At some point, Tevven attended the Baelon Fleet Camp.

A mission to the world Faelvaelrn alongside a group of Salphos gave him a chance to prove his worth, when the officer leading his team disobeyed Salphos orders and set off on a march to attack their target from another angle. The other Thathil quickly proved to be out of their depth in the wilderness, and came to rely on Tevven's skills and leadership as he brought them safely to the objective, where the Salphos had just completed the mission. Tevven was credited with saving their lives. Stories began to circulate within the Judicial Department of his exploits. As he continued to build a name for himself, Tevven saw that conflicts throughout the galaxy were increasing, and in 36 BKV resigned his rank and position in the Judicial Forces in order to pursue a career in politics, turning his mind to Vorix's earlier advice.

#### Governor of Zellan

With Vorix's backing, Tevven became Governor of Zellan. He aided Vorix during the Zellan Conference by preventing Supreme Chancellor Pyrlumekrix Quaphos from investigating, which contributed to Quaphos's fall from grace. During this time, he briefly met with Ithdriskern Caevenmund Havrdun. Tevven and his family turned their attention to the immigrant communities of Zellan and pushed them off-world, claiming they were stealing the hard-won peace of native born Sylrnrias. During the Secessionist Crisis, Count Ophvael attempted to convince Tevven to pledge Zellan to the League of Free Realms, but Tevven remained loyal to Vorix and ensured his homeworld remained part of the Concord. In truth, Vorix, as his alter ego Ursera Bregoth, had sent Ophvael to sway him as a test of his loyalty.
