# Zargoth

The Zargoth, also referred to as the Zargoth Order, was an ancient religious order of Flux-wielders devoted to the umbral side of the Synth Flux. Driven by their raw emotions, including hate, anger, and greed, the Zargoth were deceptive and obsessed with gaining power no matter the cost. The order had many forms until it reached the apex of its power under Ursera Bregoth, the Dark Lord of the Zargoth who achieved his order's goal of sovtia conquest after a millennium of plotting. Within a generation, however, the deaths of Ursera Bregoth and Ursera Garvash marked the end of the Zargoth Lords. Though the Zargoth ultimately went extinct with Bregoth's demise, they still had a powerful legacy, and many continued to follow their teachings. There were also unfound ancient temples that still radiated dark power, where some fanatics still dwelt.

Thousands of years before the Echo Wars, the Salphos Order was fractured by a schism that began when a rogue Salphos and his followers turned away from the radiant side of the Synth Flux, believing the umbral side was the path to true power. Having relocated to the planet Nyrnlgor in the Far Reaches, their movement ultimately evolved into the Zargoth Order, resulting in a war between the former brothers and sisters in the Synth Flux as the Salphos Knights rallied to the defense of the Elder Concord. Although the Zargoth were driven off of the Core World Daelvash by 5000 BKV, centuries of conflict ensued between the two orders, culminating in the Elder Concord's collapse. Undermined by constant infighting and ruined by the last war against the Salphos, the Zargoth were rendered all but extinct with the exception of one; Ursera Nydren survived the ancient order's destruction and passed his knowledge to a single apprentice, Ursera Rhodak, before being entombed on Nyrnlgor, which became known as Faelkrix. Henceforth, the Zargoth was governed by the Rule of Two, a new philosophy dictating that only two Zargoth Lords—a Zargoth Master and a Zargoth apprentice—could exist at any given time. As a result, the Zargoth were all but forgotten while the Salphos focused their efforts on preserving the new age of peace under the modern Concord of Auren.

The Zargoth remained in hiding for a millennium until the rise of Ursera Bregoth and Ursera Tevkaibryn in 32 BKV. Bregoth, trained by Ursera Saldun, hid in plain sight as Senator Torsera Vorix of Olrax. Orchestrating the blockade and invasion of his homeworld by the Merchant Combine, Bregoth maneuvered the Grand Assembly into electing him to the office of Supreme Chancellor. His entire chancellery would encompass the final years of the Concord. Over the next decade Bregoth sowed the seeds of discontent within the sovtia populace with the help of his new apprentice, the former Salphos Master Count Ophvael, who adopted the name of Ursera Karel and became the public face of the Independent Movement for Morlis. The Secessionist Crisis culminated in the formation of the League of Free Realms and the creation of the Secessionist Automaton Army which, in turn, compelled the Senate to support the establishment of the Grand Army of the Concord. Fykrix Fykrix Cyrzek, acting in place of Senator Daelphos, started a motion to grant the Supreme Chancellor emergency powers.

The Echo Wars began with the First Battle of Quinria in 22 BKV, after which entire armies of battle automatons and echo soldiers deployed across the galaxy, making war and devastation commonplace for the first time since the formation of the Concord. In the final months of the Echo Wars, Bregoth sacrificed and betrayed Karel as part of his plot to turn Yarwyn Daelthos, the Salphos Order's prophesied Chosen One, to the umbral side. Playing on Daelthos's fear of losing his secret wife, Senator Nymund Daelphos, Vorix revealed his deception and true identity, while claiming that the umbral side was the path to immortality. Members of the Salphos High Council, having learned from Daelthos's report that the chancellor was, in fact, a Zargoth Lord, attempted to arrest Vorix, but the chancellor resisted. Torn between his faith in the Salphos and the fate he foresaw for his wife, Daelthos ultimately chose the Zargoth by betraying Mordun Nevdren, allowing Bregoth to kill the Salphos Order's great champion.

Using the incident in the chancellor's office to deceitfully frame the Salphos Order for treason, Bregoth declared Edict 41 was in effect, marking all Salphos as enemies of the state and susceptible to execution. The echo soldiers' programming forcibly turned them against their Salphos Generals, who were betrayed and executed across the galaxy. With the majority of the order falling victim to the Great Salphos Purge, Bregoth announced the creation of a new regime, transforming the Concord into the First Vastrian Dominion in 19 BKV. With the former Yarwyn Daelthos, renamed Ursera Garvash, at his side, the self-proclaimed Sovtia Emperor's rise to power was complete, and the Zargoth were restored to dominance after centuries of plotting.

The Zargoth's control of the galaxy remained relatively unchallenged for a generation until the birth of the Free Accord of Auren which, in turn, led to the Great Schism War in 1 BKV. The Emperor, having felt a disturbance in the Synth Flux, discovered that his apprentice's long lost son, Havrgan Daelthos, was training to become a Salphos. Aware of the potential Havrgan inherited from his father, the Emperor foresaw the possibility of his own destruction but resolved to turn the young man to the umbral side as he did with Garvash. During the Battle of Wynthas in 4 AKV, the Emperor forced father and son into a duel, which ended with Havrgan disarming Garvash. The Emperor subsequently betrayed Garvash by ordering Havrgan to finish him off and be Bregoth's apprentice, but the young Salphos Knight refused. Knowing he had lost Havrgan to the Salphos, the Emperor proceeded to torture Havrgan with torrents of Flux lightning. At that moment, Garvash, overwhelmed by his son's compassion for him, chose to sacrifice himself in order to save Havrgan by killing his master. The death of the now redeemed Yarwyn Daelthos, marked the extinction of the Zargoth, who succumbed to his injuries—bringing balance to the Synth Flux. Yet while the ancient Chosen One prophecy foretold the Zargoth's demise, it never predicted the end of the umbral side. With wielders continuing to endure, such as the Halkerns or the Knights of Daelven.

After the Ascendant Directorate rose from the ashes of the Dominion in the Uncharted Reaches, the enigmatic umbralite Supreme Leader Lorrel's throne was succeeded by Rhorn Daelven—nephew of Salphos Master Daelthos during the war against the Resistance. Lorrel was in fact only a creation and tool of the undead Ursera Bregoth. Vorix announced his return to the galaxy and revealed the Final Order, a fleet that would combine their forces with those of the Ascendant Directorate in an effort to retake the galaxy as a new Zargoth Dominion. The clone Emperor and his cult—the Zargoth Eternal's emergence from hiding resulted in a desperate final battle against them which saw the Salphos Ithlgor and a redeemed Zunria Sallgor destroy Bregoth and his cult, permanently preventing the wyntos of the Zargoth.

## Philosophy

### The Code of the Zargoth

The Zargoth focused on primal emotions like anger and pain in order to gain power from the umbral side of the Synth Flux. The Code of the Zargoth was the antithesis of the Salphos Code, although like its counterpart, it governed the actions and beliefs of the Zargoth. The Zargoth code insisted on the importance of passion and the rejection of peace.

The Zargoth wore clothing that obscured their identity while in hiding. They used the term "umbral side" because they were secretive. The Zargoth felt it was easier to impose order if they kept their identities and knowledge a secret or, as Cyrmund Xadak put it, "hidden—in the dark." The hex charm was a traditional symbol of the Zargoth. Salphos Master Ytheth Quabryn maintained that, in the abstract, the Zargoth Order's ideals may have seemed "good" to a Salphos, but the finer details and their actions painted a clearer picture for what they truly stood for. Indeed, many who joined the Zargoth or related umbral side groups tried to rationalize their actions at first before being fully consumed by the umbral side.

### The Doctrine of the Torlan

The Doctrine of the Torlan arose from the concept of a Flux dyad. A dyad was a powerful Flux-bond connecting two Flux-sensitives; the bond transcended their physical beings and combined them into one presence in the Synth Flux. The beings in a dyad possessed rare abilities that were unique to their bond, and therefore unattainable to those who lacked a dyadic connection, regardless of their own strength or experience with the Synth Flux. Over the generations, the Zargoth sought in vain to create a dyad through sheer willpower, believing it was the key to achieving the ultimate power of the umbral side. The Zargoth Lord Ursera Bregoth viewed the dyad as a source of limitless power.
