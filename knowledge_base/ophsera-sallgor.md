# Ophsera Sallgor

Ophsera Sallgor, known only as Ophsera until being given the surname Sallgor, was a human male smuggler and hero of the Free Accord of Auren. He rose from an impoverished childhood to become captain of the Silver Kestrel, a leader in the Free Accord and an instrumental figure in the Great Schism War during the reign of the Vastrian Dominion. Although he did not believe in the Synth Flux controlling his destiny, Ophsera came to believe in the cause of sovtia freedom, joining Salphos Havrgan Daelthos and Princess Vegoth Faelvok in the fight for the Uprising. Following the war, Ophsera and Vegoth welcomed a son, Zunria Sallgor, who became the dark knight Rhorn Daelven after being corrupted by the umbral side of the Synth Flux. Although their relationship floundered, Vegoth hoped that Ophsera, as Zunria's father, could find a way to break the umbral side's hold on their son. Ophsera Sallgor became legendary across the galaxy as both a smuggler and war hero of the Uprising.

As a young man fighting for survival on the mean streets of Halreleth, Ophsera yearned to fly among the stars with dreams of becoming the galaxy's best pilot. With a fellow scavrat named Oldren, Ophsera lived as a street thief with Halreleth's notorious White Worms gang until an important deal with Lady Kabryn went bad, causing Ophsera to flee Halreleth. He was given the full name "Ophsera Sallgor" by an Dominion recruitment officer while entering the Dominion Academy. He became known as Yarzek 124-329 while serving as an Dominion yarzek, and was eventually assigned to the 224th Dominion Armored Division. On Kasera, Ophsera met the thief Zarvael Morlgor and wound up deserting the Dominion and joining his crew to steal unrefined vexium from Kagan in order to pay a debt to Dratiawyn Zelyn, a kingpin with the Crimson Dawn crime syndicate. By the end of this scheme, during which he reunited with Oldren, Ophsera was forced to kill his mentor Morlgor after a brutal double-cross. Ophsera also became rivals and close friends with fellow smuggler Wendak Daelvael and the Hallis Lordris, the latter who became his best friend. Daelvael owned the Silver Kestrel, a fast ship with which Ophsera made the Kagan Run in just twelve parsecs and won from Wendak in a game of kraith.

With only Lordris remaining at his side, as his trusted copilot who swore a life debt to the Halrelethian smuggler, Ophsera Sallgor embarked on a smuggling career under the employ of Garmir the Tevtos, a notorious gangster and crime lord of Tevlan. After ditching a shipment of spice to avoid trouble with the Dominion, Sallgor owed a great deal of money to Garmir, who then placed a bounty on the smuggler's head, as a result. His fortune seemed to have changed when Ophsera and Lordris were hired to charter Havrgan Daelthos, Faelnor Halthossul, and the automatons T2-W2 and B-8CF to Havrrel, avoiding Dominion legionnaires who were searching for Havrgan's automatons. However, as they find Havrrel destroyed by the Dominion's new superweapon, the Void Core, Sallgor became caught up in the rebellion against the Dominion, rescuing Princess Vegoth Faelvok, delivering the stolen Void Core plans to the hidden Accord base on Kathasnor 4, and saved Havrgan from the Zargoth Lord Ursera Garvash in the Battle of Kathasnor, allowing Daelthos to destroy the Void Core. Sallgor fought with the Uprising for a number of years afterward, taking part in numerous operations and battles against the Dominion.

After the Battle of Havrmara, Sallgor sought refuge with Faelvok—with whom he had fallen in love—Lordris, and B-8CF on Cloud City, which was operated by his old friend Wendak Daelvael. There, they were captured by Ursera Garvash as part of a trap to bait Daelthos to the city. Sallgor was frozen in stasisium and taken to Garmir's Palace on Tevlan by the bounty hunter Grimrel Zeldakrel, where he remained encased in stasisium for months until he was rescued by his friends. After returning to the Alliance Fleet, Sallgor was promoted to the rank of general and was given command of the Pyrvaels, a special forces squad, for the Battle of Wynthas. Sallgor led the Pyrvaels to Wynthas, where he, Faelvok, Lordris, and the special forces team, with the help of the native Gisdunrias, destroyed the shield generator protecting the second Void Core that was being constructed in orbit. Their actions allowed the Alliance Fleet to destroy the superweapon.

The Battle of Wynthas, which saw the deaths of Emperor Torsera Vorix and Ursera Garvash, allowed the Uprising to form the Renewed Concord. In the early days after Wynthas, Sallgor continued to lead the Pyrvaels against the Dominion. He married Faelvok, and the two had a son, Zunria Sallgor. The boy struggled with the umbral side of the Synth Flux—like his mother, who was the daughter of Ursera Garvash and twin sister of Havrgan Daelthos, Zunria was Flux-sensitive—so Faelvok sent him to be trained as a Salphos by Daelthos. Zunria was seduced to the umbral side by Lorrel, Supreme Leader of the Ascendant Directorate, resulting in the destruction of Daelthos's attempt to rebuild the Salphos Order.

Having lost their son, Sallgor left and returned to smuggling, and even lost the Silver Kestrel to numerous thieves. He continued to smuggle until he was brought back into the fight by a scavenger named Ithlgor and a defected Ascendant Directorate legionnaire named Wynthil, who carried with them a automaton, MM-4, that had part of a map to Daelthos's location. Sallgor, who retrieved the Kestrel from Ithlgor and Wynthil, returned to Faelvok, who led the Resistance against the Ascendant Directorate, and fought in the battle to destroy the Ascendant Directorate superweapon known as Zelkai Base. During the battle, Sallgor saw his son, who had taken the name Rhorn Daelven, and tried to convince him to return home. Instead, Daelven stabbed his father with his plasmablade. Mortally wounded, Sallgor fell to his death in the bowels of the Zelkai weapon. However, Sallgor's heroic memory survived and his spirit remained alive and appeared in a Flux vision a year later to convince Daelven to abandon the umbral side and defeat the resurrected Ursera Bregoth, after a duel on the remains of the second Void Core between Ithlgor and Daelven, being enough for Zunria Sallgor to return and eventually sacrifice his own life to help Ithlgor end Bregoth and the Zargoth for good.

## Biography

### Early life

#### Childhood

Ophsera was born in a rough-and-tumble favela on the planet Halreleth during the waning years of the Concord of Auren in 32 BKV. Sometime after he became famous, it would be rumored by a Wenthasthil bounty hunter that Ophsera was actually a descendant of the Urphos Kathil Sallgor, a member of the ancient Halrelethian royalty. During his formative years, his father Dovthos worked in the Halrelethian Engineering Corporation shipyards, building vessels such as BM-9977 light freighters until he was laid off. Sallgor's father wished he could leave Halreleth on such a ship, but was never able to afford to. According to Ophsera, he was never close with his father. At one point his father brought him to the freighter factory where he worked and pointed out to the young Ophsera that the ships being built there were his contributions to the galaxy, but that they ultimately didn't matter. He advised him that he wasn't meant to build ships, but rather to fly the ships. Eventually, his father abandoned him. At some point he met the Drabrynkrix Rinwyn.

#### Working for the White Worms

Around 24 BKV, Ophsera joined the White Worms criminal gang as a scavrat. Here, Ophsera met fellow scavrat Oldren. Despite being in service to the gang, Ophsera showed considerable defiance towards them and their methods. One day, he intervened as a smaller child was attacked by a group of bullies, which led to Ophsera getting beaten as well. Afterwards, Oldren asked Ophsera why he helped the child despite not getting anything in return. Ophsera answered that while that might be how the galaxy works, it did not always have to be that way.

When Ophsera was about the age of eighteen, Ophsera and Oldren were vying for the position of Head within the White Worms. During their respective assignments, Ophsera and Oldren both failed to deliver as promised, leading them to be hunted down by factions such as the White Worms, the Zarthas Syndicate, as well as the Dominion. Ophsera had acquired a datacube with plans for a shield generator that was desired by all the factions. Ophsera and Oldren had no choice but to partner up to find a resolution to their problems. Eventually, Oldren became Head thanks to Ophsera's recommendation to Lady Kabryn. As they later ate together, Ophsera asked if he and Oldren were on their first date. Oldren denied it, but acknowledged the possibility that it could happen someday. Sometime later, Ophsera and Oldren began a romantic relationship.
