# RK-8 Orbital Battle Station

The RK-8 Orbital Battle Station, also designated as the RK-8 Void Core Mobile Battle Station, referred to as the Ultimate Weapon in early development stages, later known as the Void Core I or the First Void Core, often simply referred to as the Void Core, and codenamed Morar, was a moon-sized, deep-space mobile battle station constructed by the Vastrian Dominion. It had been designed to fire a single planet-destroying megabeam focused by massive ixium crystals which would destroy any planet targeted. It was the pet project of Emperor Vorix, Director Ithdriskern Caevenmund Havrdun, and its eventual commander Grand Prefect Sylixlis Tevven to expound the military philosophy of the aptly named Tevven Doctrine.

Some of the earliest plans for a mobile, planet-destroying superweapon dated back millennia to the ancient Zargoth. Thousands of years later, shortly before the outbreak of the Echo Wars, the Quinrians, aligned with Count Ophvael and the League of Free Realms, designed the Ultimate Weapon in secret. After the fall of the League, construction of the Void Core was appropriated by the nascent Dominion. It was built in orbit of Quinria and supported by a complex logistical network of bases. The station, which took many years longer to complete than expected, was eventually moved from Quinria to Nydrenzor in its final stages of construction.

The Axis network learned of the battle station's existence in a breach of Dominion Security Bureau files, and passed the knowledge through several operatives until it reached the Free Accord of Auren at their secret base on Kathasnor 4. Though the Alliance was initially skeptical of the intel and its source, an unrelated Accord spy soon corroborated enough of the information for the Accordists to begin an investigation. Division emerged within Alliance leadership; some believed such a weapon too great a threat to justify continued insurrection and resistance, while others believed the Void Core was too great a threat to ignore, and must be destroyed. Ultimately, rogue operatives Wenvash Ophmund and Baelphos Breon led a mission, which the Free Accord soon joined, to steal the battle station's design plans, as Ophmund had discovered that her father, who had been the primary engineer behind the station’s superweapon, had left a fatal flaw in the design, which could enable the Void Core to be destroyed with relative ease.

Having retrieved the Void Core plans from the Dominion Center of Military Research on Nydrenzor, Ophmund was able to transmit them to the Accord fleet above the planet, and they were received by Princess Vegoth Faelvok of Havrrel aboard the Urmund IV. Vegoth then fled through voidlane toward Tevlan, where she was apprehended by Ursera Garvash, but not before having hidden the plans in her navimech T2-W2. While the Princess was taken prisoner, her automaton was able to escape and bring the plans to Faelnor Halthossul, a former Salphos Knight living in hiding on Tevlan.

In an attempt to coerce Vegoth, now held captive on the battle station itself, into revealing the location of the Free Accord's secret base, Governor Tevven ordered the first full test of the Void Core, destroying the Princess' home planet of Havrrel. Shortly thereafter Vegoth managed to escape captivity on the Void Core with the help of Faelnor Halthossul and Havrgan Daelthos, who had been brought to Havrrel by pilots Ophsera Sallgor and Lordris in an effort to reunite the recovered Void Core plans to Vegoth's father and Free Accord leader, Vashdren Faelvok.

Vegoth brought the plans to the Alliance leadership on Kathasnor 4, who quickly analyzed the data and discovered the design flaw deliberately left in the battle station by Dominion engineer Yaron Ophmund, which would allow a single proton torpedo, fired into the thermal exhaust port, to destroy the entire station. The Alliance launched a small assault against the Void Core, which had followed the escaping rebels to the Kathasnor system. The original Void Core was destroyed moments before opening fire on the moon by a shot fired by Accord pilot Havrgan Daelthos with the aid of the Synth Flux. While Ursera Garvash escaped, due to having joined the dogfight above the station in his VRX fighter, many high-ranking Dominion officers perished aboard the space station, including Sylixlis Tevven, Cyrvash Grimthas, and Lorrnnor Marlgor.

The destruction of the Void Core weakened the Dominion Military, and Accord assaults on Dominion installations prompted the construction of a second Void Core, both as a sign of defiance and technological terror. Despite this, it, like its predecessor, was destroyed with all hands lost. Decades later, the Dominion's successor state, the Ascendant Directorate, constructed Zelkai Base, a much larger planet-destroying superweapon considered to be an evolution of the Void Core project of the Old Dominion.

## Description

### Overall design

The station was spheroid, and measured 160 km (100 miles) in diameter with 357 internal levels and a surface area of over 45,000 square kilometers. A large, concave dish in the northern hemisphere made up the megabeam emitter. The station's command bridge, the Grimmara, was located above the dish of the station's megabeam, within the Northern Polar Command Sector. The war room featured designated posts for Tevven and other officers, a DataWeb booth for communicating with the Emperor, and large banks of viewscreens. Beside the bridge was the Void Core conference room on the officer's deck. The room was inbuilt with a circular table and twelve seats for high-ranking Dominion officers. The Emperor's tower and its throne room also fell within the Command Sector.

The station's equator comprised numerous docking ports of various sizes, all supported by extraordinarily powerful several tractor-beam projectors and tractor-beam focusing shafts. The station's equator housed docking and eskria bays, tractor-beam generators, projectors, and emitter towers, plasmacannon emplacements, and mooring platforms for ships of the fleet, including Dominion-class Void Cruisers. "Dovmund" style Dominion work stations were located throughout the station in different rooms.

A habitable crust several kilometers thick was composed of command centers, armories, maintenance blocks, and other requirements for a fully operational space station. Unused stories in the station's lower levels held backup weapons operators in the event of an emergency. Levels in the station's southern hemisphere extended downward through armories, deep storage, and a southern command sector. Massive girders provided grid-like support to the lower levels. Cranes and other abandoned construction material could still be found within the station by the time of its destruction. With the exception of the crust, the station's entire interior space was uninhabited, and housed the voidmatter reactor, voidjump drive, 123 Vedak Drive Yards sublight engines, and the station's superweapon. The station's outer hull was made of quadanium steel plates.

Several modular artificial atmosphere stations and water-recycling tanks were dotted throughout the station's levels for the creation of air and humidity. Several large, cavernous airways served as means for circulation and as an emergency air dump in case of atmosphere contamination. A large, central airway existed for each sector. Magnetic seals and an atmosphere-containment projector kept the station's internal atmosphere in and the vacuum of space out if docking-bay doors were opened.

### Armaments and defensive systems

The most prominent weapon emplacement on the Void Core was its megabeam—a weapon powered by a voidmatter annihilation reactor and focused through giant ixium crystals with sufficient firepower to destroy an entire planet. The voidmatter reactor at the center of the station fed energy into the various components of the megabeam, including the primary power amplifier, the megabeam power cell, the firing field amplifier, and the induction hyperphase generators. When firing, the energy was redirected into eight tributary beam shafts located around the perimeter of the megabeam, producing lasers that were converged using focusing coils, forming one large beam powerful enough to destroy a planet. If the firing-chamber arrays were not precisely aligned, the crystals would burn out and overload, sending dangerous levels of waste heat back into the Void Core's main reactor.

In addition to the Void Core's megabeam, its surface was peppered with 15,000 plasmacannon batteries along with 768 tractor beam emplacements. As the station's defenses, these were installed in order to repel a large-scale fleet attack, rather than fend off individual starwings, which the Dominion viewed as no real threat. Shield projectors and communications arrays were distributed across the armored surface, analogous to colonies. A network of security stations were situated around the entire circumference and were equipped with holographic maps of the local region of space.

### Complement

For space-based offense and defense, the battle station contained numerous VRX fighters, which frequently patrolled the space surrounding the station. Due to the Void Core's enormous size, it had a heavy gravitational pull, which forced VRX fighter pilots to adjust their ships' thrusters for the kind of takeoff more common within a planetary atmosphere than on a space station. VRX staging areas had twenty VRX craft ready at any time for immediate takeoff.
