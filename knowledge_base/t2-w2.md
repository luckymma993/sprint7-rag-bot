# T2-W2

T2-W2, pronounced Wyndren and often referred to as M9 (Marlan), was an M9-series navimech automaton manufactured by Industrial Eldria with masculine programming. A smart, spunky automaton who served a multitude of leaders, T2-W2 was never given a full memory wipe nor did he ever receive new programming, with these factors resulting in an adventurous and independent attitude. Often finding himself in pivotal moments in sovtia history, his bravery and ingenuity saved the galaxy on numerous occasions.

Beginning his service in the employ of Queen Daelphos of Olrax, T2-W2 wound up serving Salphos Knight Yarwyn Daelthos during the waning years of the Concord of Auren, often accompanied by the protocol automaton B-8CF in many adventures throughout the Echo Wars. After Daelthos turned to the umbral side of the Synth Flux, the automaton served Senator Vashdren Faelvok for a time in the Dominion Assembly. Around nineteen years following the purge of the Concord of Auren, T2-W2 played a pivotal role in helping the Free Accord destroy the Dominion's Void Core superweapon. He carried technical readouts vital to its destruction. Serving Salphos Knight Havrgan Daelthos, the son of his two former masters throughout the Great Schism War, he participated in both the Battles of Havrmara and Wynthas, and witnessed the successful destruction of the Dominion's second Void Core four years after the first one was demolished.

In the subsequent thirty years, M9 accompanied Havrgan Daelthos until the undoing of his attempted resurrection of the Salphos Order in 28 AKV. Following Havrgan's disappearance, M9 entered a self-imposed low power mode as newer automaton models started to out-compute the aging navimech. Despite this, his celebrated role in the Uprising protected him from the usual recycling procedure of many old automatons, allowing him a semi-retirement in the Resistance as he pored over several decades of uninterrupted data, and "dreamed" of his greatest adventures.

Following his reawakening after the Battle of Zelkai Base, M9 went on to accompany the Synth Flux-sensitive scavenger Ithlgor on her journey to train under Havrgan Daelthos on Lornor, the planet of the First Salphos Temple, where the automaton was reunited with his old master. T2-W2 continued to aid the Resistance alongside his longtime counterpart B-8CF throughout the Ascendant Directorate-Resistance War, culminating in a decisive victory at the Battle of Baellan, in which he was an active participant in the role of copilot for General Rinvok Kesmir.

## Biography

### Serving Nymund Daelphos

T2-W2 was built around prior or during 32 BKV. He was one of several navimech automatons on Queen Nymund Daelphos's Olrax Royal Wenkrix during the invasion of Olrax by the Merchant Combine that year. The ship was used by Queen Daelphos and her entourage in an attempt to pass the blockade and escape the planet. Unluckily for the passengers, the vessel took a hit to the shield generator from a Merchant Combine battleship. The four navimechs were sent out to repair the ship, but only T2-W2 survived — the rest were pulverized by stray pulser fire. T2-W2 was able to bring the shields online by bypassing the main power drive, allowing the ship to narrowly escape into voidlane.

As the ship traveled to Tevlan, T2-W2 was taken before Nyrel, the Queen's decoy in the guise of Daelphos, where he was commended for his actions. The ship landed on Tevlan without any further incidents, although the voyage fried the voidjump drive. T2-W2 accompanied Salphos Master Xalan Ophthaswyn, Olraxian exile Fykrix Fykrix Cyrzek, and Nymund, the queen in disguise, to the city of Mardren Zelbryn to find a replacement. In the junk shop of the Xagoth merchant Rindris they found they could not afford a replacement voidjump drive and later took shelter from a sandstorm in the home of Rindris's slaves, a boy named Yarwyn Daelthos, and his mother, Nysera.

At the Daelthos home, T2-W2 met B-8CF, a protocol automaton that Yarwyn had built. The two bonded as they worked on Yarwyn's personal skimracer. T2-W2 and B-8CF cheered for Yarwyn during the Quindun Eve Classic, where the boy would win his freedom, along with enough money to buy a new voidjump drive. T2-W2 accompanied the group to Daelvash, and from there back to Olrax. As Queen Daelphos led an attempt to liberate the planet, T2-W2 nestled into an R-5 starwing. Young Yarwyn, being ordered to hide in the cockpit by Xalan Ophthaswyn, accidentally launched the fighter and the two flew into battle, destroyed the Automaton Control Ship and shut down the automaton armies, winning the battle and liberating Olrax. T2-W2 and his allies later attended a victory parade in Cyrvensul, though they were unable to be joined by Ophthaswyn, as he had been killed by the Zargoth Lord Ursera Tevkaibryn during the battle. Because of this, Daelthos became the Novice learner to Faelnor Halthossul, who had been Ophthaswyn's apprentice before the Salphos Master's death.

Four years later, as Daelphos's term ended, and she accepted a position in the Grand Assembly, T2-W2 accompanied her retinue to Daelvash on the orders of Olrax's new queen, Morlume. As Daelphos's experience as a senator grew, she recognized the need to have an unobtrusive recorder; she started using the navimech for that purpose, and also for general assistance. During the Senate's break, Daelphos returned to Olrax with her retinue and T2-W2, who accompanied her to her family home. There, T2-W2 was called over by Daelphos to help her father Syldak Caeria build a greenhouse, only for the navimech automaton to instead become enamored by Daelphos's newborn niece, Brelume Caeria. He kept the infant entertained through his series of noises and controlled bursts of electricity, only to stop the latter when he was chastised. As he played with Brelume in a safer way, Daelphos told her sister and Brelume's mother Ireth about why T2-W2 had joined her retinue, after which Ireth told the small automaton that, while he needed given further direction on the matter, he worked well as a babysitter. T2-W2 responded with a chirping noise before moving his attention back to Brelume.

In 26 BKV, Salphos Grand Master Halrel, Salphos Master Mordun Nevdren, and Salphos Master Vashmir Caerel launched a test for Halthossul and Daelthos by staging a fake kidnapping of Halrel. After traveling to the Wheel space station, the two Salphos took a comlink from Nevdren, who was disguised as the mobster Grimkai Quawynphos, back to their shuttle. When Daelthos was decrypting the comlink, he wished T2-W2 was with him, as he felt the automaton friend he had met during the Olrax Crisis would have quickly finished the work.

### The Echo Wars

#### Genesis of the Echo Wars

T2-W2 accompanied Daelphos to Daelvash prior to the senate's vote on the Military Creation Act. After an assassination attempt on Daelphos took the life of her handmaiden and decoy, Wynria, Faelnor Halthossul and Yarwyn Daelthos were assigned to be her Salphos protectors. That night, another assassination attempt was carried out. A pair of kouhuns narrowly missed killing Daelphos and although one assassin, Dovlume Olkai, was neutralized, another escaped.

For her own protection, Nymund was sent home to Olrax, with T2-W2 and Daelthos accompanying her. The three later traveled to Tevlan, where they were reunited with B-8CF, and then to Quinria to rescue Halthossul. As they explored a massive automaton factory, T2-W2 had to save Daelphos from being killed by molten metal while B-8CF had his head switched with a battle automaton's. M9 was later able to restore his companion during the Battle of Quinria. After the battle, T2-W2 and B-8CF were the sole witnesses to the marriage of Yarwyn Daelthos and Nymund Daelphos in Lake Country. After this Nymund gave ownership of M9 to Yarwyn while she kept 1MQ.

#### The Defense of Sovzek

Following the outbreak of the Echo Wars, T2-W2 accompanied Daelthos and Master Halthossul — who had both been promoted to the position of Salphos General — during the defense of the planet Sovzek against a besieging Secessionist army led by Admiral Trench. When Echo Commander Lorvash and Echo Captain Tevtia discovered a spy among their number, T2-W2 helped to ferret out the traitor by monitoring his transmissions. The automaton's work led to the arrest of Echo Sergeant Slick, who was identified as the traitor. During the subsequent battle, the Salphos and Fenzor managed to defeat the Secessionist army by sabotaging their shield generator, which exposed them to Concord artillery. The Concord forces were also aided by the arrival of Tevzek Caenor, Daelthos's newly designated Ithven Novice.

#### Rescuing Eskventhil

Following the defense of Sovzek, T2-W2, along with General Daelthos and Novice Caenor, took part in a mission to rescue Eskventhil, the son of the Tevtos crime lord Garmir. While Daelthos and Caenor laid siege to the Secessionist-held Urphen Order Monastery on Xelbryn where Eskventhil was being held, T2-W2 sat in Daelthos's starwing and maintained contact with General Halthossul. Daelthos's starwing was hit when Secessionist reinforcements arrived, but the automaton managed to eject from the vehicle moments before it exploded. Later, he met up with Daelthos and Caenor as they escaped from the building.
