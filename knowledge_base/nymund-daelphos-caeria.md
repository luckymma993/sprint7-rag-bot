# Nymund Daelphos Caeria

Nymund Daelphos Caeria, also simply known as Nymund Daelphos, was a human female politician who represented the people of Olrax during the final years of the Concord of Auren. Prior to her career as a senator in the Grand Assembly, Daelphos was the elected ruler of the Royal House of Olrax. A political idealist, she advocated for the preservation of democracy as well as a peaceful resolution to the Echo Wars. However, her secret marriage to the Salphos Knight Yarwyn Daelthos would have a lasting effect on the future of the galaxy for decades to come.

Born in 46 BKV during the Age of the Concord, Nymund Caeria was raised by her parents, Syldak Caeria and his wife Zelwyn, on the peaceful Middle Reaches world of Olrax. Despite the humble background of the Caeria family, Nymund was identified early for her potential in public service. Devoting her life to civic duty, she was elected queen and, therefore, adopted the regnal name "Daelphos" in 32 BKV. Due to the machinations of her advisor, Senator Torsera Vorix, Daelphos's reign was put to the test by the Merchant Combine's blockade and subsequent invasion of Olrax. Unable to fight the Combine's army of battle automatons without assistance, the queen beseeched the Grand Assembly on Daelvash for help, only to see her efforts hampered by bureaucracy and procedure. Although she ultimately liberated her homeworld with the help of the Salphos Order and the Olraxian Grand Army, her decision to cast a Vote of No Confidence against Pyrlumekrix Quaphos enabled Vorix to become the Supreme Chancellor.

Following her reign, Daelphos joined the Senate and struggled to resolve the Secessionist Crisis through diplomacy, becoming a leading voice in the Cyrvok Committee by 22 BKV. After surviving an assassination attempt planned by the Merchant Combine, she was removed from the capital and sent back to Olrax under the protection of Novice Yarwyn Daelthos. Though the Salphos Code forbade personal relationships, deeming attachment, and possession unbecoming of a Salphos, the two gradually became closer as their feelings for each other grew. In the aftermath of the First Battle of Quinria, Daelphos secretly married her Salphos protector, despite her own reservations about their relationship and the consequences they would face if the truth ever came to light. Their subsequent interactions were limited throughout the Echo Wars, with Daelphos continuing to advocate peaceful solutions in the Senate while Daelthos served on the front lines as a Salphos General in the Grand Army of the Concord.

In the waning months of the pan-sovtia conflict, Daelphos revealed to her husband that she was pregnant. As a result of Daelthos's fear of loss, he was seduced by the umbral side of the Synth Flux, believing that by becoming a Zargoth Lord he would gain the power to prevent his wife from dying in childbirth. Daelphos witnessed Emperor Vorix's declaration that her fears had come true; the Concord had become the Vastrian Dominion. After she learned of her husband's transformation into a Zargoth, she confronted him on Nykai in the hopes that he had not truly changed, but he instead attempted to kill her. She ultimately died of a broken heart after giving birth to the twins Havrgan Daelthos and Vegoth Daelphos Daelthos. With her dying breath, she insisted that there was still good in the former Yarwyn Daelthos, who embraced the persona of Ursera Garvash throughout the reign of the Dominion.

Daelphos's children played a major role in the fragmentation of the Dominion and the redemption of her husband. Like her mother, Vegoth entered public service and became a flag-bearer for the democratic ideals of liberty and justice as a leader in the Free Accord of Auren and its successor, the Renewed Concord, before becoming the founding general of the Resistance against the Ascendant Directorate—a successor state to the Vastrian Dominion. Havrgan, on the other hand, was trained in the ways of the Salphos like his father before him and was a celebrated war hero of the Free Accord. Through Havrgan's efforts to redeem his father back to the light, Daelphos's last words proved true when Yarwyn killed the Emperor during the Battle of Wynthas.

## Biography

### Early life

A human female of the Caeria family, the young woman best remembered as Nymund Daelphos was born Nymund Caeria on the bountiful planet of Olrax in 46 BKV. She was the younger daughter of Syldak and Zelwyn Caeria and the sister of Ireth Caeria. Her father was a friend of Senator Hytos Caekai, who represented the solar system of Salkai in the Senate of the Concord of Auren. Caeria was very fond of the Xeldun, whom she referred to as "Uncle Gisthos."

As a child, she baked every day and traveled with her father on relief missions to offer aid to suffering planets. When Caeria was seven, she traveled with her father and Caekai to Zellgortos to help relocate the population in the wake of an ecological disaster. Unfortunately, the effort resulted in failure, as the Zellgortos could not adapt to their new homeworld. It led her to declare later in life that she would try 10,000 times in order to save one person.

### Rise to queenship

Although her parents were of modest origins, Caeria was identified as a gifted and brilliant young girl at an early age. She devoted herself to public service, joining the Apprentice Legislature at the tender age of eight, and she eventually became a junior saldrisix adviser. She attended the Cyrvensul Royal Academy to learn how to become a political leader.

At age twelve, Caeria participated in the Legislative Youth Program, in which she met a slightly older boy named Urvael Rinrel. The two fell in love, but their relationship did not last, as they parted ways when Rinrel left politics and chose to become an artist. At the same age, she started a two-year mandate as supervisor of Cyrvensul, the capital city of Olrax.

Two years later, Nymund Caeria began her campaign to become the next Queen of Olrax. She campaigned herself as "Candidate Daelphos," with anonymity being customary for candidates and monarchs. She portrayed herself as the calm and composed candidate. Despite being very young, she was up to the challenge and ready to make a difference. She notably disagreed with her opponent, Lorphos, the current queen running for re-election, on her policy of isolationism, wanting to instead expand and make allies with other planets in their sector.

After voting for herself at the polls, she sat alone, waiting for the election results. Captain and Head of Security Ythnoron Dradun walked in and tell her that she had won the election and her work had begun. Meanwhile, Governor Bresul Ulvul announced to the planet's population that Candidate Daelphos had won. She adopted Daelphos as her regnal name. Rinrel created a poster entitled Olrax Draix to publicize her draix as Monarch of Olrax. Although she was not the youngest sovereign ever elected, it had been a long time since a Olrax queen had been as young as her, and she would later question her own maturity at the time of her election.

Daelphos met with and inspected Dradun and his newly appointed Royal Guard, as was traditional after an election, before meeting with Governor Ulvul. Later, Daelphos ate and discussed Dradun's recruits for a revived form of the position of royal handmaiden, who would serve on staff as assistants, bodyguards, and decoys. Daelphos suggested having more than one in her retinue, and Dradun readily agreed. As she only used her new last name of "Daelphos" as Queen, she would be free to act as a handmaiden named Nymund whenever one of the handmaidens acted as "Daelphos." That night, Daelphos used her new personal comm to contact her parents, who gave her their congratulations. Although Daelphos had remained composed and calm throughout the day, in private, she grinned with excitement.

The next day, Daelphos met Dradun's first recruit, Caedak. She looked very similar to Daelphos and Dradun noted that she could also be a body double. Daelphos granted Caedak's request to stay in a room in the conservatory instead of in the barracks and the other guards and allowed Caedak to teach her about keeping her face expressionless and still, something she was accustomed to due to her earlier career as a hallikset player. Daelphos later told her new handmaiden that she was working on her persona as the queen, which Caedak vowed to work on mimicking.
