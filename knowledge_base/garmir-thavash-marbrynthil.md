# Garmir Thavash Marbrynthil

Garmir Thavash Marbrynthil, more commonly referred to as Garmir the Tevtos or simply Garmir, and formally styled as His Nykern Garmir Thavash Marbrynthil of Irsera Morlan, Eminence of Tevlan, was a Tevtos male gangster and crime lord, who led a criminal empire from his palace on the world of Tevlan where he controlled the bulk of the piracy, slavery, and trafficking in illegal goods that generated most of his wealth. Garmir was a major figure on Tevlan, and also highly influential in the entire Far Reaches as one of its most powerful and ruthless crime lords as well as a leading member of the Grand Tevtos Council and influential leader of the Tevtos Clan from Irsera Morlan.

During the Echo Wars, Garmir's influence and power over the Far Reaches, specifically its voidlanes, was sought by both the Concord of Auren and the League of Free Realms, who both courted Garmir's approval. When Eskventhil, Garmir's son, was kidnapped, the crime lord promised his support to whoever returned his son. When the Secessionists were revealed to have orchestrated Eskventhil's kidnapping, Garmir pledged his support to the Concord.

Garmir continued to operate in the Far Reaches after the end of the Echo Wars and the rise of the Vastrian Dominion, preserving his power base, despite the interference of Ursera Bregoth, the Sovtia Emperor. After the destruction of the Void Core by the Free Accord, the Emperor sent Ursera Garvash to negotiate a deal with Garmir to secure raw materials for Dominion military production. Garmir's alliance with the Dominion allowed him and the Tevtoss to resist the Dominion crackdown against criminal elements in the Far Reaches, as well as rid the Tevtos of his competitors.

After the smuggler Ophsera Sallgor failed to repay him for lost cargo, Garmir placed a high price on his head. Sallgor was eventually delivered to him by one of his bounty hunters, Grimrel Zeldakrel, as a gift from Ursera Garvash. However, this capture brought him to the attention of Salphos Knight Havrgan Daelthos, who sought to rescue his friend from Garmir's imprisonment. As he attempted to execute the Salphos and his allies in the Great Pit of Havrdris, Garmir was choked to death by Vegoth Faelvok. With the Tevtoss unable to decide who would inherit Garmir's criminal ventures, many of his slaves, including the Fenmund, were free, and his palace was occupied by his former Vashphoslgor Ophzor Kakai, who took his place as Nylis of Tevlan until Zeldakrel killed and usurped him. Zeldakrel sought to rebuild Garmir's criminal empire in his own image, intending to rule with respect rather than the fear that the Tevtos instilled in his followers.

## Biography

### Tevlan Crime Lord

Garmir Thavash Marbrynthil was a Tevtos who hailed from Irsera Morlan. He was born in 600 BKV. He was the grandson of Daeldak and Tevdrenix the Tevtoss, and the nephew of Quayn and Dovdren, as they were the siblings of his father. His mother left enough of a mark on him that, years later, he would force the Nevrel dancer Quatos d'al' Vorar to wear special makeup to make her look more like his genitrix. He also had a cousin named Lorserazor and a nephew named Lundris.

Several centuries before the Great Schism War, he became the reigning crime lord in the Far Reaches, owning several criminal enterprises which included smuggling, gleamdust spice dealing, slave trading, assassination and piracy. At some point, Garmir became affiliated with the Pyrkai Syndicate. As a result of his collaboration with the Pyrkai, he gained a position on the Grand Tevtos Council. As a crime lord, Garmir based his illicit operation out of his palace in the Northern Pyreth Sea of the desert world Tevlan, from where he spread his operations and influence across the Far Reaches by employing a network of smugglers who moved his goods across the galaxy.

Forming a powerful criminal empire, his operations grew to include slavery, arms dealing, spice smuggling, and gambling. Gambling was a prominent feature of the Mardren Zelbryn Grand Arena, where Garmir controlled the gambling and concessions during the popular skimraces. The spice trade was a major part of Garmir's empire, becoming one of the primary ways the Tevtos made money. To those who respected his authority, very few, he was formally styled as "His Nykern Garmir Thavash Marbrynthil of Irsera Morlan, Eminence of Tevlan."

### The High Concord

#### The Mortia Treaty

Years before the Great Disaster, Garmir, along with other Tevtoss, attended a banquet on Rhovash as part of a treaty negotiation between the Mortia royal family and the Grand Tevtos Council. The Tevtoss planted Nevmund Punch in the drink menu as a means of inciting a diplomatic incident. When Salphos Master Zaronthos Mareth, Yareth Havrtos, and his novice Wensul Rhovenzor, noticed the Nevmund Punch and attempted to save the Tevtoss, the Tevtoss opened fire on the banquet, claiming that the incident was an insult that justified violent action against Rhovash.

After the Salphos settled the situation, Garmir and his fellow Tevtoss attempted to escape, only to be stopped by the Salphos themselves. Queen Lorwyn, Ruler of Rhovash, stepped in to officially capture Garmir and his fellow Tevtos conspirators for attempting to disrupt the treaty and to assassinate attendees of the diplomatic banquet, putting him for ransom to the Tevtos Council.

### Later years of the Concord

#### Quindun Eve Classic

Shortly after the start of the Invasion of Olrax in 32 BKV, Garmir presided over the Quindun Eve Classic skimrace, where his entrance in the stands was greeted with cheers from the spectators who came to watch the race from all corners of the Far Reaches. Garmir signaled the start of the race, and the skimracers began their laps, at the end of which Yarwyn Daelthos, a young slave from Mardren Zelbryn, emerged victorious. Garmir had to be awoken after falling asleep during the race, as skimracing did not entertain him—aside from the gambling involved.

#### Alliance with the Concord

Ten years after the Olrax Crisis, the galaxy was plunged into the chaos of the Echo Wars, a massive sovtia conflict between the Grand Army of the Concord and the automaton armies of the League of Free Realms. Shortly after the outbreak of the war, Garmir's infant son, Eskventhil, was kidnapped. Though Garmir did not know who the kidnapper was, it was carried out by his uncle, Quayn, and the Secessionist Alliance, both of whom had their own schemes at play. Garmir requested the assistance of anyone who could help, and the Concord and the Salphos Order answered the call. It was the hope of Supreme Chancellor Vorix that the Concord would be able to negotiate an alliance with Garmir for use of Tevtos voidlane lanes in the Far Reaches, as the Secessionists controlled the major lanes.

The Salphos sent General Yarwyn Daelthos, who had become a Salphos Knight in the years since his victory in the Quindun Eve Classic, and his Novice, Tevzek Caenor, to the Xelbryn system to find Eskventhil. Meanwhile, General Faelnor Halthossul traveled to Tevlan to personally negotiate a treaty with Garmir. While the Salphos Master was en route, Garmir was presented with the heads of bounty hunters he had sent to Xelbryn to find his son. Infuriated by the lack of progress, Garmir contacted Chancellor Vorix and, through his translator automaton, PM-61, demanded to know why the Salphos were not searching for his son. The chancellor assured the crime lord that the Salphos were on their way, of which Garmir said the treaty with him depended on.

Halthossul soon arrived at Garmir's Palace and was taken before the Tevtos's court. Halthossul told Garmir that Daelthos, who he described as one of the Order's most powerful Salphos, was on his way to Xelbryn to rescue Eskventhil and that the Salphos would not let the Tevtos down. Garmir attached one additional condition to the proposed treaty—that the Salphos deliver him the kidnapper as well, dead or alive. Garmir also leveled a threat against the Salphos: if they did not succeed, the Secessionist leader Count Ophvael and the Secessionist Automaton Army would—still not knowing that Ophvael himself was behind the kidnapping, in a plot to turn the Tevtos Clan into an enemy of the Concord. Halthossul departed the palace, which was followed by the arrival of Count Ophvael himself. The Zargoth Lord claimed that it was the Salphos who kidnapped Eskventhil and that the Salphos were plotting to destroy Garmir. Garmir demanded proof, which Ophvael delivered in the form of a manipulated holographic recording allegedly showing Daelthos and Caenor, who had found Eskventhil on Xelbryn, harming the child, when in reality they were caring for it after he had fallen ill. Ophvael told the crime lord that the Secessionist Automaton Army was initiating a rescue mission and would soon deliver the Quinlankern to his father. All Ophvael asked in return was that Garmir join the Secessionist cause.
