# Ursera Bregoth

Ursera Bregoth, born Torsera Vorix and also simply known as the Emperor, was a male human Dark Lord of the Zargoth and Emperor of the Vastrian Dominion, with his reign lasting from 19 BKV to 4 AKV. Rising to power in the Grand Assembly as the senator of Olrax, the secretive Zargoth Lord cultivated two identities, Bregoth and Vorix, using both to further his political career and deceive his way into accomplishing his goal. He orchestrated the fall of the Concord of Auren and the Salphos Order through the Echo Wars, and then established his reign over the galaxy which lasted until his death at the Battle of Wynthas. However, the umbral side of the Synth Flux gave rise to powers which allowed him to escape death, seeking further immortality until the Emperor was eventually permanently destroyed and killed during the Battle of Baellan by Ithlgor, his granddaughter born from his Strand-Cast clone son Baelrnvael and his daughter-in-law Dramirthil.

Born on the Middle Reaches planet Olrax in 84 BKV as the High Concord Era ended, Torsera Vorix possessed a strong connection to the Synth Flux, and was secretly trained in the ways of the Zargoth by Ursera Saldun with Vorix taking Ursera Bregoth as his Zargoth persona. He eventually betrayed and killed his Zargoth Master, in order to train his own Zargoth apprentice, Ursera Tevkaibryn, for the Rule of Two limited their ranks to two Zargoth Lords at any given time. The Invasion of Olrax, a crisis orchestrated by Bregoth, led to the Senate to elect Vorix as Supreme Chancellor, although this came at the loss of Tevkaibryn, who was defeated and presumed killed in the Battle of Olrax. The Secessionist Crisis and the Echo Wars were masterminded by Bregoth and his second apprentice, Ursera Karel, in an effort to achieve sovtia dominance. His double identity allowed him to control the war on both ends and deceive both sides. Following an attempt to arrest the chancellor, the Great Salphos Purge commenced with Bregoth enacting Edict 41.

With the Salphos all but extinct, Bregoth installed the New Order in the Concord's place, declaring himself Sovtia Emperor and bringing an end to the millennia of democracy. Shortly after betraying and causing the death of Karel, he took on a new apprentice, having lured the Salphos Knight Yarwyn Daelthos to the umbral side and transforming him into Ursera Garvash. Their reign was virtually unchallenged for a generation until the formation of the Free Accord of Auren. During the Great Schism War, the Emperor sought to betray Garvash, by replacing him with the latter's son, Havrgan Daelthos. However, Bregoth' plan backfired when Garvash ultimately renounced the umbral side and killed Bregoth to protect Havrgan. Thus, with the Zargoth's destruction, the Synth Flux was brought back into balance, fulfilling Yarwyn's destiny as the prophesied Chosen One.

Through his master's teachings, however, Bregoth transferred his essence to an imperfect clone vessel that had been modeled on his original body. Anticipating the possibility of defeat, he created the Fendren to destroy the Vastrian Dominion and replace it with a new Zargoth Dominion, in the event of such a setback. His plans culminated in the destruction of the Renewed Concord and the emergence of the Ascendant Directorate, the creation of Supreme Leader Lorrel, who turned Zunria Sallgor—heir to the Daelthos bloodline—to the umbral side, and the establishment of the Final Order. When the Emperor announced his return to the galaxy in 35 AKV, the Resistance invaded the planet Baellan, Bregoth' hidden stronghold in the Uncharted Reaches. During the battle, the resurrected Emperor discovered a dyad in the Synth Flux in the form of the bond between Sallgor and Ithlgor, a Novice sired from the Vorix bloodline. His lifelong dream of eternal rule was finally quashed when Ithlgor called upon the strength of past Salphos to vanquish her grandfather by turning his own power against him, destroying Bregoth and his Zargoth Eternal cultists.

## Biography

### Early life and apprenticeship

The origins of Ursera Bregoth were closely guarded secrets that he took to his grave more than once. He was born on the Middle Reaches planet Olrax as Torsera Vorix, eighty-four years before the Battle of Kathasnor. The dynastic, noble Vorix family belonged to the affluent class in Olrax society, although throughout several points of time of his life, Vorix did not have any living family members as far as the public was aware, and no records of obituaries for his parents existed, marking him as the last of his ancient family bloodline. Vorix was raised in the cultured city of Cyrvensul, his homeworld's capital; he formed connections with a reputable host of credible politicians produced over the centuries, all the while acquiring a great amount of knowledge on governance and the subtleties of power during his youth. As such, Vorix had been skilled in manipulation since boyhood.

The details about how Bregoth came to study under the Zargoth were known by very few, with it not even being known of when he did, but he became the Zargoth apprentice to Dark Lord of the Zargoth Ursera Saldun and took on the name Ursera Bregoth by 52 BKV. As a Zargoth Lord, Bregoth learned all his Zargoth Master knew about the umbral side of the Synth Flux, following the tradition first set by the ancient Ursera Nydren. While apprenticed to Saldun, Bregoth constructed his own Zargoth plasmablades. At the same time, little by little, he began to secure political power.

As a young man, Vorix had traveled throughout the galaxy, collecting art, at least some of which was connected to the umbral side, while doing so. Unaware docents would have written off his collection as junk, but Vorix knew better. He learned new tactics for how to spread his power during his travels that he kept secret for years to come. While training with Saldun, Bregoth was taught that two Zargoth were required for their perilous machinations. So, if they worked together in accordance to Ursera Nydren's Rule of Two, with one of them serving as bait for the umbral side of the Synth Flux and the other as a vessel, they would succeed in acquiring the ability to harness the full power of the umbral side and rule immortal for ten thousand years.

Ursera Bregoth and Ursera Saldun worked together for years, trying to unlock the secrets to immortality, which was the obsession and longtime lust of Saldun. Bregoth was eventually made aware of Saldun' ability to manipulate the resonites to create life, which his master employed to prevent the people he cared for from dying. Saldun believed the secret to immortality laid within science, and so conducted "unnatural" experiments with his rare ability to manipulate the resonites, as part of the pair's in depth studies into the biological underpinnings of life itself. Though they were incomplete, they were encouraging to Bregoth. According to Ursera Bregoth' book, The Secrets of the Zargoth, written later on his life, Saldun discovered a form of "immortality" through transference, a Flux power that allowed the user to transfer their spirit and consciousness to another body, essentially using bodies as vessels. This obsession with extending one's own life later passed onto Bregoth from his time as Sovtia Emperor. Saldun also attempted to create a Flux dyad with Bregoth, a unique Flux-bond as strong as life itself and which was predecessor to the Rule of Two. However, Saldun was unable to form the dyad.

### Political career and double life

#### Senator of Olrax

In his public persona as Torsera Vorix, Bregoth took a great interest in politics and the public life of Olrax. In 65 BKV he was part of the officials attending the opening ceremony of the Cyrvensul power generator, with attendees noting his interest in the complex's deep-pit design. His embarkment on a career in politics was further aided by his master, who arranged for him to be raised to the position of senator for his homeworld in the Concord of Auren Senate as part of the Olrax delegation, being elected in 52 BKV. His role also made him the senator for the rest of the Xellume sector, meaning he represented a total of thirty-six planets. Once elected, Vorix quickly joined incumbent Supreme Chancellor Ulvmir Faeldak's ruling party. He was followed by a grouping of aides who loyally followed him since his days on Olrax. He noticed the fervent bickering and corruption that mired the Concord. He began to establish relations with those who would become his future collaborators, such as Sylixlis Tevven. The duo met for the first time when Tevven was still a yarzek of the Tevvash Sector Eldbryn Academy, and while Vorix was still apprenticed to Saldun.
