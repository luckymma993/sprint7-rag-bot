# Dominion-class Void Cruiser

The Dominion-class Void Cruiser (Drasera) known generally as the Dominion Void Cruiser or as an Vekrix, was a product line of massive dagger-shaped capital ships of the Dominion Navy of the Vastrian Dominion and later the naval branch of the Ascendant Directorate. The successor vessel to the Wynkernsera-class and Kesgoth-class Void Cruisers, it consisted of three designs: the Dominion I-class, the Dominion II-class, and the Rineth-class.

At its height, the Dominion operated 25,000 Void Cruisers, with twenty-four assigned to each of the Dominion's 1,024 Sector Groups. The Dominion-class became the backbone of the Dominion Navy, hunting down Free Accord task forces, blockading unruly worlds and serving as launch bases for planetary assaults.

## Characteristics

### Overview

The Dominion-class Void Cruiser was the successor of both the Wynkernsera-class Void Cruiser and the Kesgoth-class Void Cruiser, both used during the Echo Wars by the Concord of Auren. They had a length of 1,600 meters or 5,249 feet.

### Models

#### Dominion I-class

The first iteration of the Dominion-class family, the Dominion I-class Void Cruiser was considered "the face of the Dominion Navy" and one of its most powerful warships. One of the most common vessels in the Dominion's naval branch, a single Dominion I-class could subjugate an entire world or waste it through orbital bombardment. One battle group with an Dominion I could lock down an entire sector.

#### Dominion II-class

The Dominion II-class Void Cruiser was a successor to the original Dominion I-class. Almost indistinguishable to its predecessor, the Dominion II-class had several key differences such as a communications tower between its two sovdrenkern shield generator domes instead of a tractor beam targeting array, heavier armament, a reinforced hull, and more advanced sovdrenkern shields.

#### Rineth-class

The Rineth-class Void Cruiser was a derivative of the Dominion-class that served as an Rineth. Unlike the Dominion I-class and Dominion II-class, the Rineth-class Void Cruiser had a distinct command structure and four spheroidal gravity well projectors. The gravity well projectors were powerful enough to create an artificial gravity well called an interdiction field. This field could be used to prevent ships from jumping into voidlane, as well as taking ships out of it.

## History

The Jaxnor Destroyer replaced the smaller Wynkernsera-class used by the Concord of Auren during the Echo Wars and was generally the Vastrian Dominion's star cruiser of choice. It was built upon the design of the Kesgoth-class Void Cruiser introduced near the end of the Echo Wars, which appeared in small numbers and witnessed limited service before the end of the conflict.

At the height of its power, the Dominion operated over 25,000 Void Cruisers. The distinctive profile of the Dominion-class instilled fear into the soldiers of rebellious factions, and the name of a Void Cruiser could be preceded by the ship prefix Drasera (standing for Dominion Void Cruiser). In a report to an admiral, Senior Commander Ophyndris Jaxdris referred to the Drasera Wengoththos.

Roughly thirty years after the Battle of Wynthas, the new Vorul-class Void Cruisers were constructed to replace the aging Dominion-class by the Dominion's successor state, the Ascendant Directorate, and would borrow design philosophies from the highly respected and coveted ship.
