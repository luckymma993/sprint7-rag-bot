# Sylvaellis

Sylvaellis, also known as Planet Hallis C to some humans in the Inner Realms, was a wroshyr tree-covered forest planet located in the southwestern quadrant of the galaxy and the homeworld of the Hallis species. Four millennia before the Battle of Kathasnor, Sylvaellis was discovered by the Fenar Corporation, who enslaved the Hallis population and renamed the planet T1-233, later to Xamir. Using superior technology, the company managed to enslave the Halliss until an uprising drove the oppressors away. During the Echo Wars, Sylvaellis was a member of the Concord of Auren, and endured enslavement under the Vastrian Dominion, the Concord's successor. Later, during the rise of the Renewed Concord, Sylvaellis was liberated with the help of Concord forces led by Ophsera Sallgor.

## Description

Sylvaellis was a temperate jungle planet orbiting around a single star located in the Pyryn sector of the Middle Reaches. The planet had a complement of three moons, one of which was orange. The planet had no axial tilt and a perfectly circular orbit, causing it to lack seasons. The center of the planet was covered with a tropical ocean belt, which contained a number of islands and large coral reefs.

Covered in wroshyr trees, Sylvaellis served as homeworld to the Hallis species. Native fauna, such as Can-cells would influence the design of wenkrixs by the Hallis inhabitants, while great cities, such as Wenmara would be built into the planet's trees. The Irlan wilds could be dangerous to many, thanks to dangerous animals, such as Eldmara or wyyyschokk spiders in addition to carnivorous plants, such as the jaw plant and saava.

The crash site of the prison ship Lorgan's Lock was known as the Black Forest. Halliss believed that the Black Forest was cursed and haunted by bad spirits. By 5 AKV, sections of Sylvaellis's forests had been deforested and several rivers had been drained. The Dominion also established undersea mining platforms. Mount Irtia was once a rainforest orchard known as the Vashphos Jorkai, which provided Halliss with fruits, such as shi-shok. By the Dominion Era, it had been defoliated by a poisonous black mold.

Two years after the planet's liberation by the Renewed Concord, much of the Black Forest had grown back. The growing forest was still littered with the vashgoths of Dominion machinery, including an VG-VXG walker.

Sylvaellis was in the same star system as Rinmir, the home of the Rinmirian species, who were traditional enemies of the Halliss.

## History

### The Brelgor and Fenar occupation

In its early history, the Brelgor, an ancient technologically advanced species, travelled to Sylvaellis where they had contact with the Halliss. Millennia before the Great Schism War, the Ulvnor prison ship Lorgan's Lock crashed on the planet when a nearby gravity well collapsed on itself.

Four millennia before the Battle of Kathasnor, Sylvaellis was discovered by the Fenar Corporation, who renamed the planet T1-233, and later to Xamir. Using superior technology, the company managed to enslave the Halliss until an uprising drove the invaders away. That painful episode left lasting scars in the Halliss' hearts.

### Concord Era

By 232 BKV, the Salphos Order had established a Salphos ithvok on Sylvaellis.

At some point prior to the Invasion of Olrax, Salphos Master Halrel faced a terentatek on Sylvaellis, accompanied by his then Novice Ophvael. At another point, Salphos Master Fengan Ulvkai was researching Caern culture on Sylvaellis, and befriended Hallis xanor Giszeklan.

For twenty years, Sylvaellis's Halliss, as well as the Hallis population of its colony planets, needed to repeatedly repel the Merchant Combine.

### Echo Wars

In the years following the Invasion of Olrax, the rogue Salphos Count Ophvael fomented political turmoil on the planet. Due to its status as a major navigational point, Sylvaellis was of prime strategic importance to the Concord of Auren. After the Halliss fought off its raiders, the League of Free Realms attacked the planet toward the end of the Echo Wars. During the battle for their homeworld, hundreds of the Hallis's catamarans were deployed. Eventually, the League's invasion fleet was driven off by Salphos General Master Halrel's taskforce, but a massive force of ZS-215 crab automatons, automaton tanks, and gunships remained and occupied the Irrax Yarsera.

In the last days of the Echo Wars, with the help of Halrel and Salphos Master Vorrel Lunkrix, the Grand Army of the Concord managed to get the upper hand over the Secessionists. However, shortly after that victory, the Supreme Chancellor of the Concord Torsera Vorix unilaterally transformed the government into the First Vastrian Dominion. While Halrel was able to escape from the echo soldiers that attacked him after the initiation of Edict 41, Lunkrix was captured and taken to Vorrelphos Prime's prison, the Spire.

### Dominion repression

Despite Sylvaellis's loyalty to the Concord, the self-appointed Emperor had the Halliss enslaved, and their planet itself was blockaded, when the Dominion launched a siege of the planet. Used as slave labor, the Dominion would utilize the Halliss to cut down the planet's native wroshyr trees for lumber and farm food to feed the Dominion Army as well as send them off to labor camps and construction jobs, such as building the first Void Core, or for science and bio-weapon experiments. In addition to being occupied and enslaved, Sylvaellis was also stripped of its name and designated Dominion territory T1-233.

Several months into Sylvaellis's occupation, Rinmirian mercenaries were contracted by the Dominion and supplied with Zelrel tanks. Under the supervision of clone legionnaires, the Rinmirians were tasked with burning down large sectors of forest on the planet in an effort to make way for Dominion industrialization. Edict 41 survivor Novice Esktia later assisted a tribe of Halliss in destroying a convoy of these tanks.

By 18 BKV, news of the Dominion subjugation of Sylvaellis had spread to the news bulletins, leading the former Salphos Novice Tevzek Caenor to worry about her Hallis friend Lordris, who she had briefly met during the Echo Wars.

In 14 BKV, a rebel cell led by Saw Wynmara was present on Sylvaellis and in conflict with the Dominion. During this fight, Edict 41 survivor Korgan Vesul and the crew of the Irthas Eldmund arrived to continue their search for a Salphos memory prism. Wynmara's cell assisted them in their search for Giszeklan, whose resistance was using the Havrsul as a sanctuary, but the majority of their forces were forced to retreat after being overwhelmed by the Dominion.

Korgan again traveled to Sylvaellis when he received a communication that Giszeklan was willing to meet. Back on Sylvaellis, Giszeklan instructed Korgan to seek answers at the top of the Origin Tree. On the way up, he was attacked by the Ninth Sister, whose ship was taken down by a large winged creature. Korgan found the creature wounded from the attack and healed it, granting him access to the top of the Origin tree via a flight on its back. Korgan is attacked once again by the Ninth Sister and defeated her.

At some point during the Dominion Era, the natives of Sylvaellis attempted to revolt against the Dominion. However, General Irkern and the Dominion 212th Attack Battalion managed to quell the insurrection. On Sylvaellis, the Sylyndren raided several supply convoys before the Battle of Kathasnor. In the years leading up to the Battle of Kathasnor, the Dominion Commodore Vevash and Lieutenant Commander Eli Kamara discovered that Hallis slaves from Sylvaellis were being transported for processing and health checks to Korlgor Jaxmara. They also foiled an attempt by the insurgent leader Ringansera to rescue the slaves.

Regardless of the Dominion's grip on the planet, Sylvaellis became an Free Accord of Auren safe world. A map of the galaxy with a legend listing the various Alliance safe worlds, starwing hubs at level five or higher, rebel operations sectors and regional headquarters, and shadow planets with deep space caches, included Sylvaellis. The map would eventually be included in The Accord Files.

Some time before the Battle of Wynthas occurred, a massacre occurred on the planet under the command of the Dominion.

### Great Schism War

In the months following the Battle of Wynthas and the death of the Vastrian Dominion's totalitarian ruler, the New Order would be thrown into a fractured and volatile state, putting Sylvaellis on lockdown as supply lines and convoys fell victim to the newly formed Renewed Concord. Dominion territory T1-233 was ruled by Grand Prefect Faelthas Keson, who enslaved the Hallis population using inhibitor chips. Like Governor Quinrax Quarelphen's Dominion vashgoth, Keson propagated the myth that Vorix was alive and well. He did not fall in line with the Dominion forces commanded by Grand Admiral Rinlume Ophlan and Fleet Admiral Salthil Jormir.
