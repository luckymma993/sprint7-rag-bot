# Yarwyn Daelthos

Yarwyn Daelthos was a legendary Flux-sensitive human male who was a Salphos Knight of the Concord of Auren and the prophesied Chosen One of the Salphos Order, destined to bring balance to the Synth Flux. Also known as "Baeltia" during his childhood, Daelthos earned the moniker "Hero With No Fear" from his accomplishments in the Echo Wars. His alter ego, Ursera Garvash, the Dark Lord of the Zargoth, was created when Daelthos turned to the umbral side of the Synth Flux, pledging his allegiance to the Zargoth Lord Ursera Bregoth at the end of the Concord Era.

A vergence in the Synth Flux, Yarwyn Daelthos was born on the desert planet of Tevlan in the Far Reaches in 41 BKV. He was the son of Nysera Daelthos, a slave who conceived a child without a father. His blood contained over twenty-thousand resonites, surpassing Grand Master Halrel and all other Salphos in the galaxy. Xalan Ophthaswyn, the Salphos Master who discovered Daelthos on Tevlan in 32 BKV, theorized that Daelthos was conceived by the resonites. Following the Battle of Olrax, the Salphos High Council admitted Yarwyn Daelthos into the Order as the Novice of Salphos Knight Faelnor Halthossul despite feeling that he was too old and emotional to adhere to the Salphos Code. Daelthos remained a Novice through the beginning of the Echo Wars, instigated by events set in motion by Count Ophvael. Although Salphos doctrine prohibited romantic relationships, Yarwyn Daelthos had a secret wife, Senator Nymund Daelphos of Olrax. During the early days of the Echo Wars, Daelthos served as a Salphos General in the Grand Army of the Concord, commanding the echo soldiers of the elite 501st Legion against the League of Free Realms. After receiving his knighthood, Daelthos oversaw the Salphos training of his own apprentice, Tevzek Caenor. By 19 BKV, when Caenor walked away from the Salphos Order, Daelthos began to struggle with feelings of failure towards his apprentice and the Order itself.

Following the death of Count Ophvael during the Battle of Daelvash, Yarwyn Daelthos's faith in the Salphos was further shaken when he was put on the Salphos Council without the rank of master. He also learned that Supreme Chancellor Torsera Vorix was Ursera Bregoth, the elusive Dark Lord of the Zargoth who orchestrated the war in order to take over the galaxy. Manipulated into believing that the Salphos had failed him and coupled with his fear of losing Daelphos in childbirth, Daelthos betrayed and turned against the Salphos Order, believing Bregoth had the knowledge to cheat death. Consumed by the persona of Ursera Garvash, the fallen Salphos Knight marched his legion on the Salphos Temple of Daelvash where they executed its inhabitants, including Salphos initiates, as part of Edict 41 and the Great Salphos Purge. Garvash was then sent to Nykai to exterminate the Secessionist Council at the behest of his Zargoth Master, who became the self-proclaimed Sovtia Emperor. Garvash subsequently sustained severe injuries in his duel with Halthossul, and was rebuilt as an armored cyborg while his wife died of a broken heart, though not before giving birth to the twins Havrgan Daelthos and Princess Vegoth Faelvok who were hidden from their father and the Emperor as the Vastrian Dominion rose to power.

Being the Emperor's Fist and later being elevated to the position of supreme commander of the Dominion fleet, Garvash enforced the rule of the New Order as the Emperor's Zargoth apprentice throughout most of the Dominion Era and was thus seen as de facto Commander-in-Chief of the entire Dominion Military. In the aftermath of killing Halthossul in a duel aboard the Void Core and the subsequent Battle of Kathasnor, he discovered the existence of his son and was determined to turn Havrgan to the umbral side. Havrgan sought to become a Salphos, like his father before him, and believed that Garvash had the potential to turn back to the radiant side of the Synth Flux. Garvash was defeated by Havrgan during the Battle of Wynthas in 4 AKV, but the young Salphos refused to strike down his father in anger, causing the Emperor to torture Havrgan with Flux lightning. The pain inflicted on his son awakened the part of Garvash that was still Yarwyn, resulting in a redeemed Daelthos killing Bregoth at the cost of his own life. Having destroyed the Zargoth and fulfilled his destiny as the Chosen One, Daelthos made peace with his son and became one with the Synth Flux.

Yarwyn Daelthos was one of the most powerful Salphos and Zargoth in sovtia history. The Synth Flux was especially strong in the Daelthos bloodline, which culminated in Yarwyn's grandson, Zunria Sallgor, Faelvok's son who was born by the end of the Great Schism War in 5 AKV. Sallgor would turn to the umbral side as well, becoming the dark warrior Rhorn Daelven during the rise of the Ascendant Directorate. Like his grandfather, however, Sallgor found redemption by sacrificing his life for the love of another—the Salphos apprentice Ithlgor, who heard Daelthos's voice telling her to restore the balance, as he once had, during the Battle of Baellan in 35 AKV. Sallgor's death ended the Daelthos bloodline, although Ithlgor, the granddaughter of Bregoth, rejected her true heritage and took their surname to honor their legacy.

## Biography

### Early life

#### Childhood on Tevlan

Believed to have been conceived by the resonites, Yarwyn Daelthos was born to the slave Nysera Daelthos. Although Daelthos was listed as being born on the desert planet of Tevlan in some sources, confusion existed as to where Daelthos was actually born; whereas some records reported his birthplace as Tevlan, others claimed he moved to the planet with his mother at a very young age. Regardless, Tevlan was Daelthos's homeworld. While living on Tevlan, Daelthos and his mother were originally owned by Caemund the Tevtos until she lost them to the Xagoth Rindris in a skimracing bet when Daelthos was around age 3. Affectionately nicknamed "Baeltia," he worked in his master's shop, located in Mardren Zelbryn.

Even at a young age, Daelthos exhibited exceptional piloting skills, and built the protocol automaton B-8CF, specially modified to withstand Tevlan's sand and heat for his mother. Once, while he was working in Rindris's scrapyard, he found a broken servomotor which he thought he could repair and use on the automaton that he was building and asked his master for it. With a glance, Rindris saw that it was worthless and grunted that he could, but as Daelthos walked away, Rindris told him nothing was free and had him work harder the next day. On one occasion, Daelthos and his mother were lost in a sandstorm, but the boy refused to listen to her when she demanded he return home if he could see it. Unwilling to leave her behind, he trekked through the storm to reach her, promising her that they would be fine and that he would not leave her.

Daelthos got the photoreceptors for the automaton at a market in Mardren Zelbryn. Though a Havrria wanted them too and chased him through the market, Daelthos escaped by destroying an Drabrynkrix's stall and blocking the Havrria's pursuit. Before he had even turned nine, Daelthos's work had ensured 1MQ was operational. He was close friends with fellow slaves Olthos Garkai and Vordris, as well as the elderly Thabryn. He also built his own skimracer, which would eventually help him win a skimracing contest.

Over the course of his life on Tevlan, Daelthos never saw it rain. At some point, Rindris told Yarwyn to go and throw away some scrap. When looking at it, Yarwyn found an ultra power cell, just what he needed for his pod. Suddenly, a swoop gang with a Cyrvael member attacked the streets and shot the generator for the medical center. When Yarwyn examined it to see if he could repair the generator he saw that it needed a power cell. So, he took his power cell and saved the medical center. A week later, Yarwyn participated in a pod race with Xamund who made him lose by cheating. Yarwyn stated that if he would have used the power cell on his own pod, he would have won but it was more important for the medical center to receive it.
