# Caephen

Caephen, born Eskdris jai Faelraxdris, was a Ulvix male warlord who served within the military forces of the League of Free Realms as a commanding officer during the Echo Wars in the final years of the Concord Era. In addition to his position as a general within the Secessionist Automaton Army, the Ulvix held the title of Supreme Martial Commander of the Secessionist Automaton Armies throughout the war, where he led countless engagements against the Concord's Grand Army. Recognized for his ruthless tactics and extensive cybernetic enhancements, Caephen utilized the Secessionist Automaton Army and his adept combat skills to instill fear throughout the galaxy as he traveled to and invaded planets while engaging numerous Knights of the Salphos Order, his sworn enemies.

A native of Olraxsul, a planet within Wild Space, Faelraxdris grew into a fearsome warrior and warlord of his people and developed exceptional skill in warfare and military strategy during his homeworld's war against the Rhophensera, an insectoid species from the invading neighboring planet Quathasnor in the star system with the same name near theirs. After demonstrating his military brilliance and earning a fearsome reputation on his homeworld, Eskdris jai Faelraxdris eventually adopted the name "Caephen," a name that inspired fear among his enemies. Caephen fought alongside the Dragan, Olraxsul's finest warriors, and organized them into his personal bodyguard, with each serving as a khan commanding their own troops. Caephen then waged war against the Rhophensera with increased intensity and determination while on the offensive. The Zargoth Lords Count Ophvael and Ursera Bregoth, his Zargoth master, recognized his exceptional potential as he led his people in their ruthless war against the Rhophensera. Under Caephen's leadership, the Ulvix successfully drove the Rhophensera back to their homeworld. Caephen then launched a series of campaigns on Quathasnor itself, and advanced across the Quathasnor worlds. However, the Concord of Auren and Salphos Order intervened in the conflict on the side of the Rhophensera, leading to punishment and famine for the Ulvix and generating an enduring grudge in Caephen. The Zargoth, who had significant plans for Caephen, offered him cybernetic enhancements, which the Ulvix accepted and took on over time. Sometime later, Ophvael conspired to mortally wound Caephen and sabotaged his shuttle, which resulted in a gruesome crash that left the man on the brink of death. After that, Caephen gave up almost everything that remained of his organic form—whether lost in the crash or by his own decision-making—to survive.

The reconstructed Caephen became a skilled plasmablade duelist under Ophvael's guidance, who trained the Ulvix in the arts of plasmablade combat and eventually welcomed him into a position as a warrior in the League. Shortly after the advent of the Echo Wars, Caephen was quickly granted the rank of general, as well as the title of Supreme Martial Commander of the Secessionist Automaton Armies, making him one of the most powerful people within the Secessionist Alliance, second only to his master Ophvael. Caephen wielded his skills as a brilliant military strategist and melee combatant during numerous campaigns against the Concord throughout the Echo Wars while also gaining a reputation for hunting and killing Salphos Knights as a Salphos hunter. He made a habit of claiming the plasmablades of defeated Salphos as trophies and weapons to be used against other opponents. His position and threat level led to his preeminence as one of the Concord's primary targets throughout the course of the war. As the war played out, Caephen found a personal nemesis in Salphos Master Faelnor Halthossul, whom he dueled on numerous occasions.

Days before the end of the war in 19 BKV, Caephen, under orders from Ursera Bregoth, launched a daring and unprecedented assault on the planet Daelvash, the capital world of the Concord. In his assault, the Ulvix managed to take the Concord's Supreme Chancellor, Torsera Vorix—in reality secretly Bregoth himself—captive aboard his flagship, the Providence-class Sylrel Invisible Hand. However, during the battle, the Chancellor was rescued from the general's grasp by Salphos Generals Faelnor Halthossul and Yarwyn Daelthos, an engagement that also resulted in Ophvael's death. Subsequently, Caephen became the de facto leader of the entire Secessionist Alliance and immediately withdrew of all remaining reinforcements and fled into the Far Reaches, prolonging the war. Following the Battle of Daelvash, Caephen, succeeding Ophvael as the League's Head of State, retreated to the planet Zelkern, where he addressed and relocated the members of the Executive Secessionist Council to the planet Nykai under Bregoth's orders. However, unknown to the general, Caephen's location on Zelkern was betrayed by Bregoth, resulting in a Concord invasion of the planet in an effort to bring a decisive conclusion to the war. Caephen was ultimately slain by his nemesis, General Halthossul, following a prolonged chase between the two, which resulted in the war's conclusion as well as the League's systematic collapse soon after.

## Biography

#### Early life

Eskdris jai Faelraxdris, the Ulvix male who would eventually become "Caephen," was born years before the Echo Wars into his family in the wilderness of the Wild Space planet Olraxsul, amid the ongoing war between his people, the Ulvix, and the Rhophensera, an insectoid species native to the desolate neighboring planet Quathasnor in the star system of the same name next to their own. The Ulvix were conquered by the Rhophensera, whom they had brutally conquered other planets in their sector, plundering them of their riches and enslaving their people. Though the Rhophensera inflicted great damage upon Faelraxdris's people, the Ulvix retained their zeal for conquering other societies during the conflict, and since childhood, Faelraxdris worshipped the warlords of Olraxsul as he watched them return home with spoils of war from those they conquered.

Faelraxdris was taught how to use a slugthrower, the Fenar Arms Outland rifle, by his father. By the age of eight, he was serving as a sniper and had already killed forty enemies. As he matured, Faelraxdris experienced a rapid rise in rank, becoming a feared warrior and warlord. Faelraxdris learned brutal and determined strategies, battle tactics, and the art of warfare during his homeworld's war against the Rhophensera. He also bored the capacity to instruct, encourage, and manage his fellow warriors with exceptional skill and competence. Among the Ulvix warriors, he wore fearsome masks. One was carved from the skull, bones, and teeth of his homeworld's deadliest predatory beast, the mumuu, while another was fashioned from the bones of an erkush. Before entering battle, his customary mask was smeared with patterns of karabbac-blood as warpaint.

An obsession with the glory gathered in war, he valued bravery in combat above all else throughout his life and began gathering weapons as trophies. Faelraxdris became an honorable, proud, and revered warrior and one of the greatest military leaders, and the Ulvix grew to regard him as a legendary figure. At that time, Faelraxdris also began to develop a deep hatred for the Concord of Auren. He soon came to the realization that the Salphos Knights of the Salphos Order, the protectors of the Concord, were nearly undefeatable in hand-to-hand combat through their possession of supernatural abilities originating from a mystical energy field known as the Synth Flux, and he despised them for their skill.

Faelraxdris had demonstrated his abilities and earned a bloody reputation as a warlord on his homeworld, with many adversaries, and eventually adopted the moniker "Caephen," whose name alone brought fear to his enemies. Over his years of battle, Caephen traveled and battled alongside Olraxsul's best warriors, the Dragan. He would assemble them into his elite bodyguards, with each a khan in command of troops of their own. Seemingly always alongside their warlord leader, the courageous Dragan shared in his victories and love of battle alike.

Accompanied by his Dragan, Caephen turned his attention to the stars to continue his conquests. Over the course of his exploits, Caephen came to topple nations, slay kings, and murder legends. At some point, he encountered a Hallis warrior and decapitated a member of the Vorvash species. Caephen then went to war against the Rhophensera with increased vigor and determination while on the offensive. Fortunately for Caephen, his reputation had drawn the attention of the Zargoth—Ursera Karel, also known as Count Ophvael, who discovered Caephen for the Zargoth, and his Zargoth Master, Ursera Bregoth, secretly the Concord politician Torsera Vorix—both of them saw his potential as he led his people in a brutal campaign against the Rhophensera. Legend claimed that they specifically were interested in his strength, power, and will to succeed. The Zargoth had been recruiting minions who possessed unique skills and a deep hatred of the Salphos, both of which Caephen had in abundance. As per the plan for the Echo Wars, the Zargoth needed to recruit someone to lead a automaton army. In particular, Caephen's hatred for the Salphos pushed him to near-madness and made him easily exploitable.
