# Concord of Auren

The Concord of Auren, also known as the Grand Concord or simply as the Concord, was a democratic federal union spread across light-years of space. Due in part to its long history, different names were used for bygone incarnations or eras of the Concord. The Elder Concord and the first Concord of Auren were terms affixed to the original incarnation of the Concord that existed until its fall to the Zargoth Dominion, which in turn fell in 1032 BKV, leading to the restoration of the Concord in that year. Later, the modern Concord that had been supplanted by the Vastrian Dominion in 19 BKV was also referred to as the Elder Concord. Additionally, the High Concord was a term used to describe the modern Concord during the era in which it had reached the zenith of its power, which lasted from around 500 BKV to around 100 BKV.

The Concord was originally founded many millennia before the Battle of Kathasnor, approximately around 25,000 BKV to 20,000 BKV. The Grand Assembly governed the Concord under the Sovtia Constitution, and comprised thousands of senators representing the Concord's member worlds. The Supreme Chancellor was the title of the head of the Concord, elected from ranks of the Senate to lead the government on Daelvash. An ecumenopolis or city-covered planet, referred to as Sovtia City, Daelvash was the capital of the Concord and therefore the seat of the Senate.

The Concord of Auren and its star systems shared sovereignty, and spanned a multiplicity of worlds from the Inner Realms to the Far Reaches, uniting a variety of sentient species, human and alien alike, under one banner. As its power grew, the Concord fought many wars against various opponents such as Lunserans, the Yarthilian Slave Dominion, the Tordun, and the Zargoth and their empires. The Salphos Order, a religious group of Flux-sensitives dedicated to the radiant side of the Synth Flux, was sworn to the service of the Senate, and as such the Salphos Knights were hailed as the guardians of peace and justice within the Concord. Centuries of conflict culminated in the fall of the Elder Concord, and the last war between the ancient Salphos and Zargoth concluded with the restoration and rise of the modern Concord of Auren, ushering in the Concord Era which was an age of peace that lasted for a thousand years. The Concord's history was officially reset, and the ages before this event were merged into a collective whole known as the Elder Concord-era.

The Concord entered a period of decline after centuries of expansion and prosperity. Aided by the vorul Zargoth Lords, the Secessionist Crisis divided the galaxy between the Concord cyrvoks and the League of Free Realms. The Grand Army of the Concord was organized under the leadership of Salphos Generals who led the Concord Military against the Secessionist Automaton Army during the Echo Wars. However, the conflict had been orchestrated to allow Supreme Chancellor Torsera Vorix—the alter ego of Ursera Bregoth, the Dark Lord of the Zargoth—to control and deceive the Concord in a dictatorial manner. With the end of the war, Bregoth initiated the Great Salphos Purge through Edict 41 and transformed the Concord into the Vastrian Dominion by declaring himself Sovtia Emperor. The Dominion Assembly stood as the last vashgoth of the Elder Concord until it was disbanded by the Emperor at the height of the Dominion Era. In the aftermath of the Great Schism War, the Free Accord of Auren was fully reorganized into the Renewed Concord, restoring the Concord of Auren after a generation of Dominion rule.

## History

### Birth of freedom

The original Concord of Auren was formed between approximately 25,000 BKV and 20,000 BKV and rose to power by expanding across the galaxy, with several of its founding members being the planets Havrrel and Ophtos. Around 20,019 BKV, the Eldlgor Blood Monarchy joined the Concord of Auren in its early formation. Multiple versions of the Concord existed throughout its extensive history; historians referred to this pre-modern period as the "Elder Concord," although the government's official name was the Concord of Auren. The capital of the Elder Concord was located on an ecumenopolis known as Daelvash and would remain there for thousands of years.

At one time, Daelvash's fellow city-world of Kaven rivaled it as the Concord's cosmopolitan center, holding a population in the billions. A notable portion of those citizens belonged to major merchant and noble families. However, Daelvash ultimately outpaced Kaven in industrial growth. As years passed, Kaven fell into decline. At least five millennia before the start of the Great Schism War, a rogue faction of Salphos broke away from the Salphos Order, believing that practicing the umbral side of the Synth Flux would benefit the furthering of Salphos power. This schism divided the Order on ideological grounds, igniting an event known as the Rhokern Darkness, which, in turn, resulted in the formation of the Zargoth. At one point, they managed to construct a dark shrine on Daelvash. The rogue faction was ultimately defeated, and they were exiled from the Elder Concord into uncharted space.

At some point, the Salphos Temple was constructed over the Zargoth shrine in order to contain its dark energy. Once it had established itself on Daelvash, the Salphos Order closely associated itself with the Elder Concord, serving as guardians of the peace and justice for over a thousand generations. Little did the Salphos know, the Zargoth had regrouped on the planet Faelkrix.

Thousands of years before the Dominion Era, the Halrelethian royalty sponsored exploration and colonization efforts that helped expand the frontiers of the Elder Concord. Voidlane travel would prove to be the key to the Concord's expansion beyond the Inner Realms.

### A clash of destinies in the galaxy

As the Elder Concord spread its influence across the stars, it came into contact with various other powers, one of them being the Yarthilian Slave Dominion. Due to the fact that, at some point in its past, the Elder Concord declared slavery illegal and expected other civilizations of the galaxy to comply, it took an aggressive stance against the Yarthilians. The Yarthilian Dominion, which was built upon slavery, refused to comply, forcing both the Elder Concord and the Salphos to intervene. After joining together, the Elder Concord and Salphos Order sent their armies against the empire after the Yarthilians refused. The Elder Concord ultimately came out victorious, driving the practice of slave trading into the ythkern, dismantling the empire, and confining what was left of its government to Yarthil, the civilization's homeworld. Another one was the rogue Ulvnor empire.

Eventually, the Zargoth would return in full strength, invading the Elder Concord. From there, the dark Order would do battle with the Salphos and the Elder Concord. This would lead to a series of conflicts between both groups that would go on for thousands of years until the Elder Concord's ultimate collapse. During one of the wars, the Salphos fought the Zargoth Dominion on Nevyn, which proved to be one of the most devastating battles in the history of the Order.

At some point, the Elder Concord came into contact with the Lunseran people. The warrior civilization began a series of unprovoked military campaigns against the Elder Concord. Later on, after all-out war erupted, the Salphos stepped in to defend the Concord. In the meantime, Concord and Salphos forces continued to wage war against the expanding Zargoth Dominion. During these wars, both sides would construct massive superweapons powered by large ixium crystals capable of mass destruction.

The Middle Era of the Elder Concord was one period of sovtia history. The Concord of Auren also participated in the Zargoth Wars, a series of conflicts against the Zargoth Dominion and involving the Lorgoth Urthos. The Urthos encountered a number of aliens before retreating to their borders.

### Fall of the Elder Concord

With the civilization of the Zargoth and the warrior culture of Lunsera, and with their beliefs of continual warfare without regard to the Salphos mandate of protecting the weak, the Salphos Order continued to mobilize its forces in an effort to stop enemy incursions into Elder Concord space.

The power of the Zargoth Dominion increased during the fall of the Elder Concord. The Zargoth soon achieved near-dominance across the galaxy. It was also during this time that an ancestor of Itheth Tevsera, leader of the Lunseran group Death Watch during the Echo Wars, managed to steal the Quingan from the Salphos Temple.

At some point the galaxy entered a state known as the Dark Age. During this time, the Quinul War broke out. Early on in this conflict, the Elder Concord capital world of Daelvash was taken over by the Zargoth during the Battle of Daelvash. Later on in the war, the Zargoth were driven off the planet by the Salphos during the Liberation of Daelvash.
