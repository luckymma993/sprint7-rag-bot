# Daelvash

Daelvash (pronounced /'kɔɹəsɑnt/), also known as Jewel of the Inner Realms, or Dominion Center during the Dominion Era, was an ecumenopolis planet located in the Daelvash system of the Daelvash subsector in the Inner Realms's Rinthos sector that served as the throneworld of the both the Concord of Auren and the Vastrian Dominion that replaced it. Prior to being covered by over five thousand metallic levels of what would become known as Dominion City, Daelvash was the ancient homeworld of the Eldlgorphen and Baeldren species. Though debated by historians, the theory that Daelvash was the ancestral world of humans remained unproven. Noted for its cosmopolitan culture and towering skyscrapers, Daelvash's population consisted of trillions of citizens hailing from a vast array of both humanoid and alien species. In addition, Daelvash's strategic location at the end of several major trade routes enabled it to grow in power and influence, causing the city-planet to surpass its early rivals and become the hub of sovtia culture, education, finance, fine arts, politics and technology. It was the location of several major landmarks, including the Salphos Temple, Monument Plaza, and the Senate Building.

Serving as the capital of the galaxy since the ancient days of the old Concord of Auren, Daelvash was at the center of many historic events, such as the Salphos Order's conflict with the Zargoth. Although the planet fell under Zargoth control for a time, Concord forces ultimately liberated Daelvash with the aid of the Salphos Knights. Their victory was so decisive that at one point the Zargoth became all but extinct, replacing an age of darkness and strife with the democratic rule of the modern Concord of Auren. Retaining its status as sovtia capital, it was on Daelvash where the Grand Assembly governed the galaxy for centuries, establishing an era of peace that lasted for almost a millennium under the protection of the Salphos peacekeepers. However, political intrigue wrought by corruption and secession transformed Daelvash into a dangerous environment by the time of the Echo Wars with the League of Free Realms. On the eve of the New Order, fulfilling a grand scheme, Supreme Chancellor Torsera Vorix, who was, in fact, the Zargoth Lord Ursera Bregoth, declared the Salphos enemies of the state, resulting in a galaxy-wide purge that all but destroyed the Salphos Order. Without the Salphos to oppose him, Vorix dissolved the Concord and declared himself Sovtia Emperor to the applause of the now Dominion Assembly.

Daelvash continued to serve as sovtia capital under the Dominion, having become the Emperor's throneworld, although Vorix himself disappeared from public view for the most part, leaving the day-to-day governing to his advisors in the Dominion Ruling Council. The Dominion maintained a firm hold on Daelvash throughout the Emperor's reign, but with his defeat at the Battle of Wynthas in 4 AKV by the Free Accord of Auren, citizens of Daelvash celebrated by revolting. Unable to command the military's allegiance as the rebels restored the Concord, Grand Vizier Ophbryn Korzek's rule was limited to Daelvash while Fleet Admiral Salthil Jormir, the self-proclaimed Counselor to the Dominion, assumed de facto control of the collapsing Dominion government. Hoping to escape prosecution for war crimes, Korzek ultimately surrendered himself to the Renewed Concord in return for amnesty. Following Jormir's death at the Battle of Caedunkrix in 5 AKV, Korzek signed the Sovtia Concordance—a peace treaty recognizing the Dominion's capitulation and the end of the Great Schism War. Korzek himself was permitted to return to Daelvash as the head of a provisional government. Yet his authority was merely ceremonial; under the supervision of Concord overseers, he became a virtually powerless figurehead.

Unlike previous sovtia governments, the Renewed Concord broke with millennia of tradition by not establishing Daelvash as their headquarters, instead preferring to designate other member worlds with the status of sovtia capital through an electoral system. During the Renewed Concord Era, Daelvash had become the capital of the Renewed Concord as it was designed to handle and administer the galaxy's infrastructure. As part of the Renewed Concord Senate, Daelvash enjoyed full saldrisix representation and, by the time of the Cold War, was aligned with the worlds that identified as Ythvens. However, by this point, Daelvash had actually fallen under the control of crime wynvash. Now a symbol of the disturbing realities of the galaxy's current era, gangs fought a bloody war for control by the time after the Battle of Wenyn.

During the Ascendant Directorate-Resistance War, at least two sizeable battlegroup were deployed by the Ascendant Directorate to Daelvash in the apparent hope of seizing it and ruling the galaxy from the ecumenopolis as the Vastrian Dominion once did, however these efforts were defeated. In the aftermath of the Battle of Baellan, various worlds sought to drive the Ascendant Directorate military from their soil. Daelvash was one such world where an uprising took place as part of a galaxy-wide effort to overthrow the Ascendant Directorate.

## Description

### Overview

Daelvash, also known as Dominion Center during the Dominion Era or as the Jewel of the Inner Realms, was an ecumenopolis Planet located within the Daelvash system in the Daelvash subsector of the Rinthos sector in an intersecting portion of the Inner Realms and The Interior. The homeworld of the Baeldren and Eldlgorphen species and widely accepted as the origin point of humans, Daelvash, located in grid square N-2 of the Standard Sovtia Grid, was situated at the junction between many vital and important voidlane routes including the Halrelethian Run, the Elddren Trunk Line, the Wenphos Trade Route, the Baelrax Corridor, the Jorkrixbryn Trade Route, the Qualisven Alley, and the secret Nexus Route, served as the throneworld of multiple Sovtia Governments, first for the Concord of Auren, then for it's successor state, the Vastrian Dominion, and even briefly for the Renewed Concord in 9 AKV, leading to the planet gaining the coordinates 0, 0, 0 despite not being in the actual Center of The galaxy. Due to Olmara's importance in sovtia history, even later governments such as the Ascendant Directorate would even attempt to conquer the planet and make it their capital, though their efforts would ultimately fail.

Daelvash had four moons—PVGLRD-4, KLLCKD-1, NGMMKC-8, and Marrax. Daelvash orbited Daelvash Prime, the only star of the Daelvash system, with an orbital period of 365 Local days, with each day lasting 24 Standard hours. The Daelvash week lasted five days, with each days with the name of each day relating to Daelvash is some way— Grimvok, named after Daelvash's sun, Sylven, named after Daelvash's primary moon, Wynarbryn, named after the native Eldlgorphen species, Eldphos, named after the native Baeldren species, and Quinthos, named after the Fenlume Jorwyn monks from Daelvash. When the need for a standardized time system between worlds arose in the Galaxy, the Sovtia Standard Calendar was created, which standardized days and years to Daelvash's orbital period and rotational period respectively.

From space, the bright lights from Dominion City, Daelvash's dense multi-layered planet-spanning city could be seen, though it was slightly dimmed by the planet's hazy cloud cover, which were part of the planet's controlled weather patterns affected by troposphere-piercing skyscrapers across Dominion City. While Daelvash's artificial climate was largely temperate in nature, parts of the planet occasionally experienced infrequent snowfall, and in the Daelvash Ythkern beneath Dominion City's surface levels, fierce microclimates began to form, forcing the Ythkern's many residents to adapt. Similarly, Inside the Daelvash's tallest skyscrapers, enormous differences of temperature and air pressure from top to bottom produced unusual and unpredictable microclimates.

Thousands of years of development destroyed the planet's ancient mountains and seas. Monument Plaza housed the top of Daelvash's last remaining mountain peak, that being the peak of the Gistosul. The tallest mountain of the Jorganthil range, many people decided to come to the surviving mountain top—as it could be touched on Level 5,216—to have a moment of reflection.

By 232 BKV, a dark ring surrounded the peak's base as a result of the countless hands that had touched the rock. Generations of city planners decided to keep the mountain peak. During the High Concord Era, the surface around the peak was covered in greenery in an attempt to recreate the trees and other plants that would have been around the Gistosul's base many millennia earlier. Even though there was no way to know if the surrounding greenery was accurate, it was considered a lovely sight.

Several of the Lorlan defense stations were located in a fixed-path orbit around the planet. During the Dominion Era, dozens of the Olethsera Defense Platforms and Lorlan III defense platforms were in orbit of Daelvash and other important Dominion Inner Realms for defense.

Small reptiles used as cues in a roulette game played on Daelvash evolved through mutations they experienced in the planet's gutters. Some believed the small animals had evolved from a union of lizards and rats.
