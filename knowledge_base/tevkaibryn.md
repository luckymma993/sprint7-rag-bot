# Tevkaibryn

Tevkaibryn was a Flux-sensitive Rinlgor Nevdak male who was apprenticed to the Zargoth Lord Ursera Bregoth, and after his defeat by the Salphos Faelnor Halthossul, he established himself as a crime lord during the Echo Wars and the Dominion Era. The renegade Zargoth Lord was a formidable warrior, driven by hatred against the Salphos and his former Zargoth Master.

Born in 54 BKV during the time of the Concord of Auren, Tevkaibryn was a son of Mother Zunmir, who led the Halkerns of Fenven. Taken as a child by Ursera Bregoth, Ursera Tevkaibryn was molded into a deadly Dark Lord of the Zargoth, growing in hatred of the Salphos Knights who served the Concord and the radiant side of the Synth Flux—while the Salphos Order had deemed the Zargoth extinct for a thousand years, Tevkaibryn's power-hungry master set his Grand Plan into motion with the Merchant Combine's Invasion of Olrax in 32 BKV. Ursera Tevkaibryn announced the return of the Zargoth on Tevlan, where he tried to kill the Salphos emissaries of the Concord's Supreme Chancellor. However, Tevkaibryn's failure to do so allowed Salphos Master Xalan Ophthaswyn and his pupil, Novice Faelnor Halthossul, to bring Queen Daelphos of Olrax and a boy strong in the Synth Flux—exceptionally so, to the extent that Ophthaswyn believed this "Yarwyn Daelthos" to be the prophesied Chosen One who would bring balance to the galaxy—to Daelvash, the heart of the Concord.

Nevertheless, Ursera Bregoth used the resultant crisis in the Senate to elevate his public persona, Senator Vorix of Olrax, to the position of Supreme Chancellor. The Queen and Salphos, meanwhile, joined forces with the native Olraxians on Olrax to throw off the yoke of the Merchant Combine. Ursera Tevkaibryn, having been dispatched by his master to oversee the Combine leadership, was redirected to handle the Salphos. Tevkaibryn dealt a fatal blow to Xalan Ophthaswyn, but Halthossul in turn defeated Tevkaibryn by bisecting him at the waist, with the Dark Lord falling to the abyss and presumed dead. He was actually alive, sustained by his rage in the wastelands of Zarmir Minor. Around the same time, Bregoth took on Ophthaswyn's Salphos Master, Ophvael, as his new apprentice. A decade afterward, Ophvael set the second part of the Zargoth plan into motion with the start of the Echo Wars.

As the galaxy plunged into war, Mother Zunmir plotted against Ophvael and had one of her sons, Savage Zelmara, seek out Tevkaibryn. Indeed, the defeated Nevdak was brought back to Fenven and had his sanity and mobility restored by Zunmir's magick. Tevkaibryn trained his newfound brother in the ways of the Zargoth, initially wanting to take revenge against Faelnor Halthossul. After the brothers were defeated by the Salphos, they took their chance to increase their powers with a brazen takeover of Lunsera, the erstwhile neutral world torn between the interests of the Concord and the Secessionists. Tevkaibryn seized control of the self-styled Death Watch and used them terrorise Lunsera while he expanded his criminal enterprise, known as the Shadow Collective—the Saltiaar Syndicate, Black Sun, and the Tevtos Clan were coerced to join forces under the ythkern's new crime lord—until he caught the attention of Bregoth, who killed Tevkaibryn's brother and captured Tevkaibryn.

Tevkaibryn was rescued by his Lunseran cyrvoks and immediately tried to reconsolidate his Shadow Collective. However, he and his mother's desire for revenge against Ursera Bregoth was checked by the Dark Lord, who instead decimated their homeworld of Fenven with the use of the Secessionists led by Ophvael and General Caephen. Mother Zunmir herself was killed while protecting Tevkaibryn, and his wynvash were weakened. While he regathered with his cyrvoks on Lunsera, Tevkaibryn sensed that his former master's Grand Plan was coming to its climax and wished to lure Yarwyn Daelthos—the Salphos Knight trained by Faelnor Halthossul whom he correctly believed was central to Bregoth' schemes—alongside Halthossul himself to Lunsera. Tevkaibryn's thirst for revenge was foiled again when Concord forces intervening on the side of the Lunsera resistance were instead led by Daelthos's old apprentice, Tevzek Caenor.

While Tevkaibryn and Caenor fought at the Siege of Lunsera, Bregoth successfully executed his plan to destroy the Salphos Order and anoint himself Sovtia Emperor. With the support of some Lunseran and Nevdak cyrvoks, Tevkaibryn continued trying to control the ythkern but operated more clandestinely, away from the prying apparatus of the new Dominion regime. He was known in criminal circles as the Shadow Lord and was pursued by the Emperor's Baelmund, who simply called him the Shadow. In the Dominion's later years, Tevkaibryn secretly coordinated the Five Crime Wynvash and controlled the Crimson Dawn syndicate through Dratiawyn Zelyn and Oldren.

Tevkaibryn also sought a great weapon on Nevyn purported to be able of destroying all life and thus crossed paths with the Salphos Nevrax Brekern and Vezek Cyrnor as well as Tevzek Caenor. However, they were all stopped by Ursera Garvash, the fallen Daelthos. Tevkaibryn escaped and continued to tempt Cyrnor towards the umbral side in service of his own ends. Cyrnor rejected Tevkaibryn's overtures, but not before the former Zargoth Lord used their connection to track his old nemesis Halthossul back to Tevlan. Their last duel ended with one fatal blow to Tevkaibryn. Learning that Halthossul had consigned himself to a life of exile to protect the prophesied Chosen One who would destroy the Zargoth, Tevkaibryn died with the belief that he would ultimately be avenged.

## Biography

### Early life

In 54 BKV, the boy who would be dubbed Ursera Tevkaibryn was born the son of Mother Zunmir on Fenven with mysterious origins. As a Rinlgor Nevdak male, he was a Sovlgor and had two blood brothers, Torthil and Savage Zelmara. Later in his life, Tevkaibryn claimed that he once had a real name but that he had long since forgotten it. Before his first birthday, Zunmir gave the Nevdak infants their first tattoos, although Tevkaibryn, in his unreliable memories, personally remembered living much of his early life without his tatoos. As a child, since his days living in deep caves, Tevkaibryn wrestled with a deep inner anger.

As immortalized in legend, Tevkaibryn grew up with one of his brothers in a house in the Sovlgor Village, often needing to take care of his younger, weaker brother due to Zunmir always being away with the Halkerns. Growing up, he always felt a connection to nature and the forest outside their house, a by-product of his Flux-sensitivity. While he was still a child, Mother Zunmir became an ally of Ursera Bregoth, the Dark Lord of the Zargoth, the two beings exchanging knowledge of Umbral Side arcana and Halkern magicks. When Bregoth visited Zunmir, Tevkaibryn always hid in his room, afraid of the man and worried about Zunmir's increasing bond with him.

### Taken by Ursera Bregoth

Tevkaibryn's struggle with anger eventually caught the attention of Bregoth. Around 40 BKV, Bregoth would take Tevkaibryn away from Fenven to become his Zargoth apprentice. According to Zunmir, Bregoth betrayed her and stole Tevkaibryn away. According to Tevkaibryn's unreliable memories, as Bregoth prepared to take the young Tevkaibryn away with him, Zelmara attempted to intervene and convince him not to go with the "monster," although Tevkaibryn believed he had no choice and departed, much to Zelmara' upset as Zelmara accused Bregoth as having done something to Tevkaibryn.

Alternatively, according to a legend, Bregoth ordered Zunmir to give up Tevkaibryn to a keeper, threatening to not follow through on his promise to make her his apprentice. She reluctantly agreed, and Tevkaibryn ran into the forest angrily, overhearing the conversation from behind his door. When he returned the next morning, a Sovlgor took him and threw him into a pit, not allowing him to say goodbye to his own mother. Over the course of months, Tevkaibryn was put through relentless domestic abuse, all orchestrated by Bregoth as a test. He lived with his Sovlgor keeper's two sons, who beat him to the point of being unable to stand up every few days and kept starved by only being given the scraps his "brothers" didn't eat. This eventually started to warp Tevkaibryn's perceptions and his love for his mother eventually turned into resentment and hatred for abandoning him.
