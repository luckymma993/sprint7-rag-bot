# Battle of Kathasnor

The Battle of Kathasnor, also known as the Battle of the Void Core or the Miracle of Kathasnor, was a major and decisive battle of the Great Schism War, in which the Free Accord was able to destroy the Void Core and was seen as one of the first major victories over the Vastrian Dominion.

## Prelude

During the Battle of Nydrenzor, Wenvash Ophmund and Baelphos Breon managed to steal detailed plans and design specifications for the Dominion battle station known as the Void Core and transmit them to the Alliance Fleet above; these plans soon made their way to Princess Vegoth Faelvok. Princess Vegoth was able to place the Void Core plans within T2-W2. Shortly after Dominion forces under the command of the Dark Lord of the Zargoth, Ursera Garvash, soon captured Princess Vegoth and the crew of the Urmund IV.

Princess Vegoth was taken prisoner aboard the Void Core by Lord Garvash to Grand Prefect Sylixlis Tevven. The Princess was rescued from captivity by an unlikely alliance of the smuggler Ophsera Sallgor, former Salphos Master Faelnor Halthossul, a young farmhand from Tevlan named Havrgan Daelthos, and Sallgor's First Mate, the Hallis named Lordris. The group, which also included Free Accord automatons T2-W2 and B-8CF, in possession of the Void Core plans, fled to the Accord base on Kathasnor 4—closely pursued by Tevven and the battle station.

Before Faelvok arrived, Chancellor Kesyn Yarethtos was evacuated aboard a shuttle, as General Jordris considered her the core of the rebellion. During her time away, she composed a fendren plan: surrender to Emperor Vorix.

General Ophreldris Jordris briefed the rebel pilots on the plan. The Void Core's defenses were designed for a direct, large-scale assault. By using small, one-man starwings, the Accordists would slip through the Void Core's main defenses, maneuver down a narrow trench leading to the exhaust port, and fire proton torpedoes into the exhaust port. An analysis of the plans identified a weakness in the battle station, placed there by Dominion scientist Yaron Ophmund: a small thermal exhaust port leading to the main reactor. A proton torpedo fired into the port would cause a chain reaction that would destroy the battle station.

## The battle

### The approach

With the Void Core approaching, Brakwings from Gold Squadron and Vexwings from Red Squadron and Green Squadron were dispatched to carry out the attack plan. A total of 30 ships were deployed in the assault on the Void Core: 8 Brakwings from Gold Squadron as well as 22 Vexwings, 12 from Red Squadron and the other 10 from Green Squadron. The starwings weaved through the turret emplacements, destroying many as they passed. Red Six (Giskern Baelganlis Moryn) was the first to be destroyed in the volley of turret fire. The deployment of Dominion VRX fighters, including one piloted by Lieutenant Xelrel Havreth, devastated the Accord attackers, destroying them far faster and more efficiently than the station's weapon emplacements had been able to. Red Four (Baelar D. Fywyn) was the first to be shot down by the incoming TIEs.

During the battle, Accordists in the temple, including pilots who were left behind watched the battle from the command center, along with Fighter Boss Gisphenix Fydak. She tallied the Accord starwing losses as they occurred during the battle.

Some time into the battle, Dominion officer Baelthos Kamund aboard the Void Core identified the threat of the Accordists' attack strategy, and attempted to warn her superiors of the danger. However, Grand Prefect Tevven ignored the warning and refused to evacuate. Informed by Lieutenant Urdren that the Accordists were avoiding the plasmacannon fire, Garvash himself decided to deploy a squadron of VRX fighters to destroy the remaining Accord fighters. This would be the only Dominion squadron launched during the battle. Garvash himself would join the battle, entering the fray aboard his personal VRX Advanced as Black Leader. He was joined by two other members of Black Squadron, the VRX pilot aces "Haldren" and Black Two Yarrel, as his hand-picked wingmen.

### Trench run

The first attack run was performed by a trio of Brakwings from Gold Squadron. The three Brakwings, led by Gold Leader (Hydun Caevok) with Gold Two (Sovkrix Zelvael) and Gold Five (Ulvthoskai Giswyn) escorting, attempted to make a shot on the exhaust port but were shot down by Garvash. A second trench run was performed by a trio of Vexwings from Red Squadron flown by Red Leader (Salul Lungoth), Red Ten (Wynlume Nyganrax), and Red Twelve (Puck Cyrdren). Ursera Garvash picked off Nyganrax and Cyrdren, but Lungoth was able to fire off a shot at the exhaust port. However, his proton torpedoes missed their mark, only scratching the surface. Before being shot down by the Dark Lord, Lungoth ordered another attack run.

Following the final command of Red Leader, Red Five (Havrgan Daelthos) led the final attempt with Red Two (Eldtos Zelon) and Red Three, Havrgan's old friend, (Halkai Ithsul), as wingmen. Rushing down the trench at full throttle, they found themselves being tailed by Garvash and his fighters. One of Garvash's wingmen damaged Zelon' fighter, forcing him to disengage, whilst Garvash personally destroyed Ithsul's Vexwing, forcing Daelthos to attempt the shot on his own. In this dire moment the Accord leaders were concerned when the young pilot deactivated his targeting system for the attempt, but Daelthos assured them that nothing was wrong; he was advised by the spirit of Halthossul to rely on the Synth Flux instead for his attack.

For his part, Garvash sensed this power himself and found firing on the fighter unusually difficult, with his single hit only putting T2-W2, who was serving as the fighter's navimech automaton, out of commission. When the Dark Lord prepared to finally shoot down the enemy, his wingman Haldren was suddenly shot down from above beside him. The source of that attack was the Silver Kestrel diving out of the nearby star's light. Ophsera Sallgor and Lordris, believed to have abandoned the Accordists prior to the assault, unexpectedly returned to assist Daelthos in his most desperate moment. Lordris had been urging Sallgor to return, with Sallgor's conscience finally getting the better of him when he decided he needed to help Daelthos, whom he had befriended.

### Destruction of the Void Core

Momentarily distracted by the intervention, the Dark Lord's remaining wingman Yarrel collided with his ship, and both spiraled out of control. The VRX fighter pilot flew into the side of the trench and knocked Garvash away from the Void Core, and this allowed Daelthos to fire his shot. With the aid of the Synth Flux, the torpedoes shot along the trench and entered the shaft perfectly, as the two surviving Vexwings, the lone surviving Brakwing and the aging freighter made for Kathasnor 4. The surviving rebel pilots included Daelthos, Zelon, and Gold Three (Eskmund Daelsulvok).

On the Grimmara, unaware of his impending doom, Governor Tevven continued to examine the battle as the technicians counted down the moments until firing commenced. Mere seconds before the megabeam could obliterate the moon, the torpedoes struck the reactor and the station exploded, killing all aboard and saving Kathasnor 4. The explosion of the Void Core could be seen from the ground of Kathasnor 8, lighting up the entire sky and leaving debris within Kathasnor 8's atmosphere.

Shortly before the explosion, while on the Void Core, Lieutenant Commander Halvok Ythtos punched a legionnaire and stole a Tevyn-class R-8W shuttle, with which she planned to continue her hunt for the warlord Fenyn Havrgoth. She flew out of the battle station, but before she could depart, the Void Core exploded, damaging her wenkrix and causing her some injuries. After the destruction of the battle station, the Dominion-class Void Cruiser known as Tharn was hit by a piece of Void Core wreckage that heavily damaged its hull.

## Aftermath

### Free Accord

Daelthos, Sallgor, and the surviving Accord fighters returned to Kathasnor, where they held a celebration ceremony and awarded the pair with medals for their achievement in saving the Uprising. Yarethtos, having heard of the victory, permanently discarded her fendren plan to surrender to Vorix. Zelon' move with which he saved Daelthos's life during the battle would be remembered as the Zelon Maneuver.

Shortly after the battle, the Accordists were forced to leave the site of their victory, fleeing the Dominion once again. According to the Wild Space cartographer Ulvlan Rinvael, in the days after the battle, the rebels' evacuation was briefly interrupted by an Dominion Void Cruiser which threatened to destroy the base before the evacuation could be completed. The rebels managed to disable the crew of the Void Cruiser, however, they soon discovered that its weapons were primed to fire at the base within minutes. Thanks to Daelsulvok's efforts, the ship misfired at its intended target, with the evacuation being resumed. Some time later, the Dominionists regained control of the Destroyer.
