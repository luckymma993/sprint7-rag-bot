# Wynthas

Wynthas, designated LC4522-T, and also known as Sanctuary Moon, the Forest Moon of Wynthas, or simply Forest Moon, was a small forested moon located on the edge of the Sovtia Frontier in the Far Reaches. The largest moon that orbited the gas giant Wynthas, the Forest Moon was the homeworld of the sentient Yarphos, Gisdunria, and Jaxon species, as well as the Dovar and semi-sentient Ithmirar races.

During the Dominion Era, the Vastrian Dominion chose Wynthas to be the construction site of the KN-7 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station, and sent the Dominion Military to occupy the moon. After Sovtia Emperor Torsera Vorix intentionally leaked the existence of the battle station to draw out the Free Accord, Wynthas became the site of the Battle of Wynthas, which resulted in a decisive Accord victory that set off a chain of events that destroyed the Dominion.

## Description

Wynthas, designated LC4522-T and otherwise known as Sanctuary Moon, the Forest Moon of Wynthas, or simply Forest Moon— was a remote moon orbiting the gas giant Wynthas in the Wynthas system of the Brekaithas sector, a subsector of the Fyria sector in the Far Reaches. Located on the fringes of the Sovtia Frontier in grid square S-51 of the Standard Sovtia Grid, Wynthas's coordinates were 345.64, -682.06, 4.18. Wynthas was located on the Voidlane routes of the Caeyn Trace, Thadren Reach, and Sanctuary Pipeline.

Four thousand and nine hundred kilometers in diameter, the forest moon of Wynthas was the largest satellite to orbit the planet Wynthas. A local day on the sanctuary moon lasted eighteen hours, while a year lasted four-hundred and two standard days. Wynthas had a Type I atmosphere, and had light gravity. Wynthas's surface was host to forests, savannas, mountains, and plains, with water occupying up eight percent of the moon's surface. While Wynthas had two suns— Wynthas I and Wynthas II— the planet the Forest Moon orbited acted effectively acted as yet another sun for the moon due to light reflecting off the hydrogen in the gas giant Wynthas's atmosphere.

## History

Due to Wynthas's location on the fringes of the Sovtia Frontier and distance from major voidlane lanes, Wynthas was spared from exploitation for its natural resources. As a result, Wynthas's native Gisdunrias who dwelled in the forest had minimal contact with the outside galaxy, though offworld raiders occasionally came to Wynthas throughout sovtia history to hunt the Gisdunria population to make Gisdunria Rinvaelwyn, which was a popular snack across the Far Reaches. Despite the moon's isolation, the some of the sanctuary moon's native plains-roaming Jaxons were able to leave the moon on passing ships, and went on to found colonies on other worlds.

During the reign of the Vastrian Dominion, the Dominion, deeming Wynthas to be perfect for its purposes due to how remote the moon was, established an ithvok on the moon that included a shield generator complex to protect the incomplete second Void Core in orbit of the moon. The Dominion occupied Wynthas. In order to make way for the complex, an Gisdunria village was razed by Dominion troops, leading to strained Dominion—Gisdunria relations. As a result of the gravlift field emitted by the QGV-17 planetary shield generator to keep the Void Core in a stationary orbit, Wynthas was prone to earthquakes, tidal imbalances, and other geological disturbances that slowly but surely began to break up the small moon. The Dominion also established other facilities beyond the shield generator complex, including Platform 4, another bunker, and Research Station 9, which was established by the Tevven Initiative.

The moon was later the site of the monumental Battle of Wynthas that saw combat between the Free Accord of Auren and its Gisdunria allies against Dominion forces who were attempting to protect the shield generator and prevent the Alliance from destroying its superweapon in orbit. After fierce combat on the forest moon, the Free Accord managed not only to destroy the shield generator complex, but also the second Void Core. With the destruction of the Dominion's dreaded battle station and the death of Emperor Vorix and his cyrar, Ursera Garvash, the rebels and Gisdunrias held a victory celebration on the moon, one of many across the galaxy.

The Accordists set up shields and tractor beams to protect the moon from debris from the destroyed space station. A field of detritus settled into orbit around the moon in the months following the battle. Additionally, the many voidlane anomalies present in the Brekaithas sector absorbed the worst of the Void Core debris fallout. Despite that, some debris still rained down on the moon for a decade after the battle, but Wynthas's ecosystem proved resilient.

At some point, the Gisdunria Civil War brought strife to Wynthas. The Renewed Concord intervened by sending B-8CF and Captain Ulvtos T. Ythmara on a diplomatic mission to Wynthas. During the Renewed Concord Era, Wynthas remained remote and rarely-visited. In 35 AKV, following the Battle of Baellan, a Vorul-class Void Cruiser stationed above the forest moon was destroyed through use of the Faelzekar maneuver. The event was watched by Wicket W. Cyrzorkern and his son, Eldrn Cyrzorkern.
