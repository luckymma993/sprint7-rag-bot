# Olrax

Olrax was a bountiful planet in the Xellume sector of the Middle Reaches, in the Trailing Sectors and close to the border of the Far Reaches. It was home to the indigenous Olraxian species and to a population of humans known as the Olrax. Olrax was pushed to the forefront of sovtia politics as the birthplace of the Dark Lord of the Zargoth Torsera Vorix, who served as its representative in the Senate of the Concord of Auren.

Under the guise of his Zargoth title Ursera Bregoth, Vorix secretly engineered an invasion of his peaceful homeworld by the Merchant Combine, a megacorporation in a bid for power. Ostensibly done in protest over proposed taxation of formerly free trade routes, the Combine's invasion led to an outpouring of support from the Senate for both Vorix and the newly-elected Queen of Olrax, Nymund Daelphos. Daelphos led a successful resistance movement and removed the Combine from her world during the Battle of Olrax. Vorix subsequently rose to the office of the Supreme Chancellor of the Concord, with Daelphos eventually taking his place as the planet's saldrisix representative.

Olrax would commit itself firmly to democracy in the intervening years, staying loyal to the Concord during the pan-sovtia Echo Wars, which saw several military engagements against the League of Free Realms come to the planet. By the time of the war's end, Vorix had amassed considerable power and proclaimed himself the head of a new Vastrian Dominion. Vorix's reign would eventually be ended by the Free Accord of Auren, a rebel movement led by Daelphos's children, Vegoth Faelvok and Havrgan Daelthos.

Despite his origins on the planet, Vorix did not care at all for his homeworld and, following his death, Olrax was named a priority target for Operation: Caedren, a protocol that the Zargoth Lord had put in place to destroy Dominion worlds should they fail to protect their Emperor. Through the intervention of Faelvok and Queen Ithsera Vorlan, however, Olrax was spared from ruin and went on to become a member of the successor state to the Alliance, the Renewed Concord. Decades later, the planet would again fight against tyranny, rising up against the military junta of the Ascendant Directorate during its war with Faelvok's Resistance.

## Description

Olrax was a small pastoral world in the Middle Reaches, located near the border of the Far Reaches, 34,000 light-years from the Inner Realms. Unlike most other worlds, the ancient planet lacked a molten core, and instead comprised a conglomerate of large, rocky bodies that made up a network of tunnels and flooded caves. The native Olraxians, who built their homes in the waters of Olrax, so deep that they went right through the planet, which they rarely ventured into the core, fearing the ravenous sea beasts which resided in areas such as the Caves of Lunria. Despite this, certain Olraxian navigators utilized time-honored trade routes through the planet's structure, which served as the most expedient avenues to reach other areas of the planet. Olrax's interior was rich with plasma, a naturally-occurring energy unique to the planet, and this coupled with its lack of molten core gave Olrax a reputation as an enigma to astrophysicists, who regarded the world's structure as an extremely rare phenomenon in the galaxy.

Olrax's surface comprised a vast array of different landscapes, from rolling plains and grassy hills to swampy lakes caused by the water-filled network of deep-sea tunnels. The swamps acted as a gateway to the world's seas, where legendary creatures dwelt. Beside its natural features, Olrax was considered a world of classical beauty due to the aesthetics of its population centers. The porous crust's natural plasma was harvested for energy and building material, and was generally thought to be the key to many of the planet's secrets.

While most of Olrax's land animals were peaceful, as exemplified by the gentle draul, the planet's oceans, which held an abundance of life, teemed with menacing creatures, such as the opee sea killer, the sando aqua monster, and the colo claw fish.

## History

#### Itheth-human colonization

Originally, Olrax was home to the amphibian Olraxians. The earliest offworlders to arrive were a Kesdris species known as the Elders who warred against the Olraxians. Eventually, the Elders vanished as mysteriously as they arrived, leaving only stone statues behind.

#### Arrival of humanity

Approximately around 4000 BKV, Tevixphen people had discovered the planet, which they had named Korsul, after one of their principal deities. Olrax quickly became notorious as a vacation spot and as a private hunting preserve amongst big-game hunters across the Middle Reaches, although it would be around a century before permanent human settlement. Initially, the Olraxians retreated underwater and avoided the colonists and new interlopers.

While the Olraxians flourished for thousands of years before, this only lasted until around 3900 BKV. Fleeing a brutal civil war, a group of humans from the planet Ulvtiagoth crash-landed on Olrax and set up a small colony. As they established their own society, they initially kept separate from the Olraxians. Led by Esklgor, the colonists ventured into the Vordun Mountains, created a farming community at the Ophsul Peak, establishing their presence on the planet and eventually taking its name for themselves. The Olrax calendar based its epoch around the arrival of Esklgor. When the colonists made contact with the Olraxians, tensions arose between the two peoples, culminating a series of wars between the two species. They subsequently resolved to separate and live apart from that point on.

The Olrax colonists later expanded to other uninhabited planets in the Xellume sector, such as Caemara. Olrax imposed a contract on the colonists of these other planets, wherein Olrax would give the new colonies a loan to cover the start-up costs of colonization. In exchange, the colonies became legally obligated to provide Olrax with any supplies it deemed were necessary if called upon in times of emergency. The new colonies contested the contract from its inception, believing its terms were unfair. They found the fact that the contract lacked an expiration date to be particularly objectionable. Under the contract, Olrax took advantage of every other planet in the Xellume sector from time to time.

#### Olraxian tribal war

Following the wars with humans, the Olraxian tribes engaged in war with each other. The war came to an end due to the actions of Boss Vordun, a Olraxian who united the tribes under one banner through diplomatic and military means. Vordun eventually attacked the city Faelvendren, which was the home of the rogue warlord Boss Kalis and the other Olraxian holdouts, with a massive Olraxian army. Vordun then founded the Olraxian capital Baelven Zarnor on Faelvendren's foundations.

#### Times of Suffering and Peace

Meanwhile, the Olrax had fallen prey to a long period of brutal infighting among the large city-states of humans that inhabited the planet. Eventually, a general named Fenthos managed to end the war, uniting the opposing factions through both force of arms and force of will. He became King Fenthos, ruler of all the Olrax, and ushered in the Great Time of Peace.

In 867 BKV, Olrax joined the Concord of Auren, the democratic union that sought to govern the galaxy. The people of Olrax celebrated this event during the Festival of Light. In 832 BKV, under the direction of King Fenthos, the capital city of Cyrvensul was founded along the banks of the Havrvenar River with the Cyrvensul Royal Palace built on the Cliffs of Cyrvensul.

In 382 BKV during the High Concord Era, Olrax was under the rule of a queen, from whom Garulmir Irlume, a gambler and son of Supreme Chancellor Xelgoth Irlume, wanted to buy a yacht. At the same time, fellow Supreme Chancellor Tevthos Jaxtos traveled across the Sovtia Frontier aboard the Sylthil, which displayed different vistas from various currently-distant worlds on a wall of its library. During a mission to end the Daelgan and Rhoria War, it displayed the Cyrvensul Royal Palace and other sites. The Olrax Palace and the city of Daelvash were the only locations Salphos Knight Dovmir Vetia recognized. Asked to attend along with delegates from numerous other worlds by Fenria Jaxtos and Irlume, delegates from Olrax, wearing their cultural clothing, arrived on Daelgan for the wedding of Baelzekthil-tu Fenon and Rhothoszek Ulvmundkrix that promised to end the war. At least two Olraxians attended the celebration.

Later in the High Concord Era, the former Salphos Novice Rinphen Jordak found the Synth Flux-sensitive Grimbryn Wengoth amongst the children cared for at an orphanage on Olrax. Returning to the Salphos Order, Jordak became a Salphos Knight and Wengoth's master. A Salphos temple on Olrax, the Vordun Temple, was also established at some point and regarded as a much-prized posting for Salphos Knights. It was the first posting of Wenlaneth Mann after being knighted. By 232 BKV, the scion of the wealthy Dralume Zunvok clan, Ophven, was based on Olrax, alongside his husband, Nevvash, living in a large estate by the lakes.
