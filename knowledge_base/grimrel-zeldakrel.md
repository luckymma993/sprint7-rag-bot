# Grimrel Zeldakrel

Grimrel Zeldakrel was a human male crime lord and former bounty hunter whose career spanned decades, from the fall of the Concord of Auren to the end of the rule of the Vastrian Dominion. Originally code-named Alpha, he was an unaltered clone of the famed Lunseran bounty hunter, Draseralan Zeldakrel, who raised the boy as his son. Grimrel emulated his father and genetic donor by wearing a customized suit of Lunseran armor. His personal wenkrix was the Slave I, a SLDPTMDPJ-54-class patrol and attack craft that once belonged to Draseralan. Trained in combat and martial skills from a young age, Zeldakrel was one of the most feared bounty hunters in the galaxy during the reign of Emperor Vorix. He became a legend over the course of his career, which included contracts for both the Dominion and the extensive criminal ythkern. After living among the Ophlgor Raiders of Tevlan for a time, he took over as Nylis of Mardren Zelbryn during the era of the Renewed Concord.

Grimrel was created on the Wild Space planet of Ulvon as a result of an arrangement between the Zargoth Lord Ursera Karel and Draseralan Zeldakrel, a Lunseran foundling who served as the template of the Grand Army of the Concord. Unlike the echo soldiers grown from Draseralan's DNA, Draseralan regarded Grimrel as his son. In accordance with certain Lunseran traditions, Grimrel was also considered a foundling by virtue of his descent from Draseralan, whose chain code documented his participation in the Lunseran Civil War and his ownership of the armor later inherited by Grimrel. During the last days of the Secessionist Crisis, Grimrel and Draseralan retreated to Quinria where the latter was killed in battle by Salphos Master Mordun Nevdren. Orphaned by Draseralan's death, Grimrel sought vengeance by attempting to assassinate Nevdren, although his plan proved unsuccessful. After serving time in prison on Daelvash, Zeldakrel took up his late father's profession as a bounty hunter, working alongside other mercenaries, such as Irnor, Havrarrax, and Olvael Nylandren, while also notably apprenticing himself to his father's infamous brief protégée, Ophvok Nydren.

In the aftermath of the Echo Wars, Zeldakrel continued to make a name for himself while working for the gangster Garmir Thavash Marbrynthil, as well as the Zargoth Lord Ursera Garvash. During the Great Schism War, the bounty hunter managed to track down Captain Ophsera Sallgor to Pyrthas on a contract for the Vastrian Dominion, hoping to deliver him to Garmir's Palace on Tevlan. During the journey, Zeldakrel stopped on the moon of Ophthil Rhovashyn in order to make sure Sallgor would survive his time in stasisium, but he lost the smuggler while he was away as part of the return of the Crimson Dawn, kicking off the War of the Bounty Hunters. Ultimately, Zeldakrel regained Sallgor and delivered him to the impatient Garmir.

Zeldakrel tried to prevent Sallgor's rescue by the Free Accord of Auren, only to fall into the Great Pit of Havrdris when the smuggler accidentally slammed a pole into the bounty hunter's jetpack, causing it to malfunction. Although Zeldakrel survived his ordeal with the man-eating grulmaw, his armor was sold to Gistos Thakai by Fenul scavengers. Following a time in the company of a Ophlgor tribe, Zeldakrel eventually regained his armor by tracking down and confronting the Lunseran warrior Esketh Vashon on Havrvael, who had taken Zeldakrel's armor from Thakai. After helping Vashon to rescue the foundling Zunlgor from the clutches of Prefect Cyrtos, the ex-bounty-hunter returned to Tevlan where he killed Ophzor Kakai and claimed the late Garmir's throne as Nylis, with Rhothil Daelthas by his side. Now the head of his own gotra, Zeldakrel intended to rule the territories of Mardren Zelbryn by winning the people's respect, though he would face several major challenges to his position owed to the two-facedness of mayor Morrn Rinon such as the Twins' bid for power and the expansion of the Saltiaar Syndicate's spice trade. Ultimately, though, Zeldakrel overcame all obstacles - including a confrontation with his old mentor, Ophvok Nydren - and emerged victorious with the help of his associates, gaining the appreciation and approval of Mardren Zelbryn's denizens.

## Biography

### Early life

#### Creation and life on Ulvon

Around 33 BKV, an extensive clone army was secretly commissioned on Ulvon by Salphos Master Korria, who believed it would be necessary in order to ensure the protection of the Concord of Auren. The army, however, was later taken over by the Zargoth in 32 BKV shortly after the Battle of Olrax and was used as a plot to overthrow the Concord and destroy the Salphos Order. Prior to the creation process, Lunseran bounty hunter Draseralan Zeldakrel was recruited by the Zargoth Lord Ursera Karel. Draseralan was to be the genetic template for the clone army. Draseralan agreed to become the genetic template for a considerable payment, as well as one request: the creation of a clone who did not have the same genetic modifications, such as behavioral conditioning and growth acceleration. The Ulvonian scientists agreed and created a boy codenamed "Alpha."

Although he was born a clone, Alpha regarded Draseralan as his father, who likewise viewed the boy as his son. Soon after referred to as Grimrel, he was the very first of many fenzor modeled on the Draseralan Zeldakrel template, but was only one of two known fenzor that were pure genetic copies of the original Zeldakrel template, the other being Omega.

For the next ten years, Grimrel was raised on Ulvon by his father, primarily within Ulvkern City and occasionally accompanied Draseralan on missions to track down countless bounties. On one night, Grimrel awoke to see the Slave I flying off, watching his father leave on one of his missions. Despite the isolation of the cities on Ulvon he was raised in, the young Zeldakrel enjoyed the combat training his father administered to him and danger-filled life provided by the man who raised him. Draseralan taught his cloned son many of the skills he had learned as a bounty hunter, so Grimrel learned survival and martial skills and became proficient with a pulser as a child. He also underwent echo soldier training and became familiar with members of the Ulvonian government. From a young age, Grimrel also learned to pilot his father's ship, the Slave I, including learning how to command and operate its weapon systems.

#### Trained by Draseralan Zeldakrel

For the boy's first mission in 23 BKV, Grimrel and Draseralan Zeldakrel alongside Wyngothlgor, Ithulmund, and Zunthos were hired by the father of a female Zelsuleth to capture his daughter and bring her back alive. The bounty hunters were surprised that a young boy would be joining them, but they knew not to anger Grimrel's father and agreed to it. While on the way to the job, Grimrel asked Draseralan why they even needed a crew for the job and wished they went with a more famous bounty hunter like Ophvok Nydren. Draseralan replied that the crew was the unknown factor which was Grimrel's lesson for the job.

Grimrel and the other bounty hunters waited at a brerax on Faelzor Quinon to strike while the Zelsuleth woman and her Kordris lover Garrax were nearby on a balcony. When Garrax told her she wasn't in the Black Sun cartel yet, Grimrel stopped him, happy to know he was not part of the Black Sun. Grimrel ordered them not to move, but Garrax ditched his lover and ran away, causing the Zelsuleth to almost stumble over. The bounty hunters ordered the woman to stop moving, but the Zelsuleth tripped and fell over the edge of the balcony, but was rescued by Draseralan.

While on the way back to the ship, Grimrel asked his father how he knew the woman would fall. Draseralan replied he didn't, but knew it was possible and advised Grimrel to trust his instincts. He also told Grimrel that desperate people would make ill decisions. Right then, Zunthos knocked Grimrel down and held him at hostage while Ithulmund told Draseralan to give him the woman and the ship and in exchange for Grimrel's life. But Draseralan refused, to the surprise of the bounty hunters who were going to gut Grimrel. Draseralan then told them that they could try and walked away. Grimrel then knocked over Zunthos and gave her a lesson about searching her prisoners. Grimrel proceeded to kill Zunthos and Wensulvael. Wyngothlgor complemented Grimrel afterward. Grimrel was about to kill Wyngothlgor, but saw that he was not part of the scheme with Zunthos and Ithulmund. Grimrel then let Wyngothlgor escape and he run away.

Zeldakrel and Grimrel later boarded their wenkrix, Slave I, with the Zelsuleth girl to deliver her back to her father. As they traveled, Grimrel and his father discussed how he had performed during the mission, with Draseralan believing Grimrel now had a great start to his legacy.
