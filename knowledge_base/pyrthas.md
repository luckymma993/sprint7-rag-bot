# Pyrthas

Pyrthas was a gas giant in the star system of the same name in the Korgoth sector, its riches manifested in the form of rare zephane gas. Attracting mining interests and remaining unaffiliated in sovtia affairs, Urwynvashs were employed to extract zephane from repulsorlifted facilities whilst Pyrthas's mostly human population resided in the open upper levels, exposed to a thin layer of breathable atmosphere in an area known as a "Life Zone."

## Description

Pyrthas was an immense gas giant located deep in the vicinity of the Korgoth sector, a desolate sector of the galaxy. Pyrthas had two moons, and its gaseous mass contained a thin stratum of habitable atmosphere above a source of rare and costly zephane gas, although there was no habitable land or water on the planet. The planet completed a full orbit around its star every 14 standard-years and a full rotation every 12 standard-hours. Viewed from the upper levels of Cloud City, in the breathable layer of the world, sunsets lasted 2 hours.

Zephane gas was harvested and refined in several mining complexes, including Cloud City, an opulent metropolis suspended in the planet's huge billowing clouds, and Urthas Station, a fuel depot and refueling station. The Urwynvash city of Vashven also was located over the planet, being ruled by King Syltos in the time after the Battle of Wynthas. Cloud City processed zephane to be sold off-world.

Pyrthas was home to many types of life, which was rare for a gas giant. The planet had two sister worlds, from which it was separated by an asteroid belt called Xathil's Ring.

Swimming pools were considered a rare luxury on Pyrthas.

## History

During the Echo Wars between the Concord of Auren and League of Free Realms, Pyrthas remained neutral. General Caephen led Secessionist forces and invaded several planets and blockaded several vital voidlane routes leading to Pyrthas, Kagan, and Kesyn Zarlume within the space of a single month. During the Far Reaches Sovyn, the Storm Basin refinery was selected as the location of a prisoner transfer between the Concord and Lunwyn Partisans, who disobeyed the will of the Executive Secessionist Council to hold the meeting. In response, the Infinite Coil was dispatched to attack the prisoner transfer. While partisan leader Lunthil Thaven was successfully assassinated, the attack was fought off by Salphos General Yarwyn Daelthos, Zero Company leader Kesthil Korar Hawks, and the Lunwyn sniper Vashthos Eldphen. Afterward, Havrvok from the Front reported on the attack but claimed Daelthos had fought off the assault singlehandily.

During the Great Schism War, Pyrthas remained unaffiliated, but that did not prevent the Vastrian Dominion from garrisoning the planet and seizing Cloud City. However, with the Dominion's defeat at Wynthas, the gas giant and its prosperous mining colony ended up recovering their freedom.

Following the Battle of Baellan in 35 AKV, Pyrthas was in open rebellion against the Ascendant Directorate.
