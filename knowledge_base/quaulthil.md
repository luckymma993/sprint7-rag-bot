# Quaulthil

Quaulthil was a remote, unexplored planet in the Quaulthil system, and one of the purest places in the galaxy within the Synth Flux. A remote world of swamps and forests, it served as a refuge for Salphos Grand Master Halrel during his exile after the destruction of the Salphos Order. Havrgan Daelthos received advanced training in the ways of the Synth Flux under Salphos Master Halrel on Quaulthil, and it was later the place of Halrel's death and transformation into the Synth Flux.

## Description

Quaulthil was a planet located in the Dovsulkrix sector of the Far Reaches. It was a hot and humid world that was a hotbed of life, with the Grimlisbryn species and numerous creatures—such as marshwings, dragonsnakes, butcherbugs, hunthounds, vine snakes, and swamp slugs—living on world. Salphos Master Halrel would also make a home for himself on Quaulthil during his exile. Quaulthil was a mysterious, mist-shrouded, swampy planet, mostly covered in swamps and bogs, interspersed with countless shallow lakes and zarzor and multiple living caves. The continental and oceanic crusts of Quaulthil were only vaguely defined, and there was little in the way of volcanic activity or earthquakes. Examples of flora included the lahdia plant, the yoghurt plant, twistoak, and yarum seed. The planet was devoid of any cities.

Among the purest locations in the entire galaxy within the energy field known as the Synth Flux, it shared a mysterious time-related quality with such locales as Fenrelthos and Lornor. The Cave of Evil was a vergence aligned with the umbral side of the Synth Flux on Quaulthil. The Cave was able to expand its influence across Quaulthil until Halrel's arrival, which created a powerful presence in the radiant side of the Synth Flux that kept the Cave in check. As such, during the Dominion Era, Quaulthil's ecosystem saw balance until Halrel's passing, which the Cave took advantage of to spread its darkness across Quaulthil once more.

Quaulthil had two seasons, a dry season and a wet season. During the peak of the long dry season (over half an orbit after the rains end) the uplands become too hot for most lifeforms to survive, necessitating any that live there to migrate to the lower lands. Beneath the rapidly leafing canopies of the twistoaks that emerge from the receding green and thick waters, the once-inundated lowlands revert back to a marshy swamp once again. As the air grows more humid, the gray overcast clouds return and before another quarter orbit passes the planet is far enough away from its sun that the dry season ends and the rains return, again flooding the lowlands and covering the trees in water, making the uplands habitable once more.

## History

### Concord Era

During the High Concord Era, an academic paper was written that described Quaulthil as a world strong in the Synth Flux.

During the Concord Era, Eskthas Pyrsera Alpha Team was dispatched to the planet Quaulthil in order to chart and map the extending borders of the Concord of Auren and learn more about it flora and fauna. After three months, Pyrsera and her Alpha Team were overwhelmed by diseases, dangerous wildlife, and fast-growing plants. No trace of the team was discovered.

Near the end of the Echo Wars, Salphos Grand Master Halrel traveled to the planet Quaulthil after hearing the voice of deceased Salphos Master Xalan Ophthaswyn. He ventured into the swamp, where he communed with Ophthaswyn, who appeared manifested as a group of fireflies. He told Halrel of how to communicate with the living after death through the connection between the Living Flux and the Cosmic Flux. Then, Halrel entered a cave, where he saw a vision of the Salphos fighting the fenzor and then the mysterious Zargoth Lord.

### Dominion Era

#### Halrel's exile

After his failure to defeat the Zargoth Lord Ursera Bregoth following the formation of the Vastrian Dominion, Halrel exiled himself to Quaulthil. Using an M3-standard wenkrix lifeboat, he began his exile, where he continued his studies of the Synth Flux. The lifeboat served as Halrel's initial shelter but it started to degrade within a year due to Quaulthil's swampy environment. As the first shelter was slowly consumed by the swamp, Halrel built a new home for himself. He positioned it near to the umbral side cave but out of range of its influence and would visit every few years. However, the Quaulthil wet season created a need for the Salphos Master to regularly relocate to the planet's highlands for the duration.

At some point, former Havrthil Kazek Sun arrived on Quaulthil in search of Halrel. After saving her from a monster, Halrel used the Synth Flux to lead Sun into the dark cave. While inside, she saw visions which dissuaded her from looking for him. She then left the planet as Halrel watched from afar.

#### Training ground of Havrgan Daelthos

Years later, Havrgan Daelthos of the Free Accord of Auren traveled to Quaulthil under the direction of late Salphos Master Faelnor Halthossul in hopes of finding Halrel and being trained in the ways of the Salphos Order. Arriving with his navimech T2-W2, Daelthos crashed into a bog upon landing and happened upon the Salphos, though he was unaware it was, in fact, Halrel at the time. The old master took Daelthos back to his hut for dinner, where he revealed his true identity to Daelthos. Initially reluctant to train Daelthos due to his age and lack of patience, Halrel was eventually convinced to teach him by the Synth Flux spirit of Halthossul.

One night during Daelthos's training, an Dominion probe automaton from Project Daelrax arrived on planet. The automaton's operator, Ythwyn, initially believed there was no reason to look further into the planet during the unit's patrol until she spotted Daelthos's Vexwing in the swamp. Although she realized the outline of what she saw resembled an Vexwing, she had realized the cruelty of the Dominion after witnessing the aftermath of the Battle of Havrmara, so she deleted the footage before moving on to her next automaton.

During his training, Daelthos entered the Cave of Evil and confronted a vision of Ursera Garvash, who he struck down, only to see his own face in Garvash's mask. Later, Daelthos ended his training early, however, once he sensed his friends Ophsera Sallgor and Vegoth Faelvok were in danger. Although Halrel and Halthossul attempted to reason with Daelthos to stay, the student left the planet, promising to return and finish his training.

He would keep his word, and after learning Garvash was his father during their duel on Cloud City, saving Vegoth and Lordris from Garvash on Cloud City, and rescuing Sallgor from Garmir the Tevtos on Tevlan, he would return to Quaulthil to complete his Salphos training. Upon arriving, he found Halrel sick and dying, who told him that his training was complete, but all he had to do was confront Garvash one last time, and the old master became one with the Synth Flux. The ghost of Halthossul then revealed to Daelthos the truth that Vegoth was, in fact, a Daelthos and Havrgan's sister before Havrgan left the planet to confront Garvash and the Emperor in hopes of turning Garvash back to the radiant side. The Cave of Evil, meanwhile, took advantage of Halrel's death to once again spread its power across Quaulthil.

### Renewed Concord Era

Sometime after the skirmish on the Kormara, Rhorn Daelven, nephew of Havrgan Daelthos, along with his master and Supreme Leader of the Ascendant Directorate, Lorrel, came to Quaulthil in order to face Daelven's darkest fears. In the Cave of Evil, he experienced a vision of his uncle Havrgan, whom he dueled and killed, and a vision of his parents Vegoth and Ophsera. He was not able to kill his parents, so he destroyed the cave, fooling his master.

By 34 AKV, herbs from Quaulthil had made their way to Ophphos's Brerax on Dralis, where they became a popular ingredient in the so-called Quaulthil Slug Breethmund alcoholic beverage. During or after 34 AKV, the freighter Silver Kestrel landed on Quaulthil. There, the Hallis crewmember Lordris attempted to take some time to himself to rest aboard the ship, only for the automatons MM-4 and T2-W2—the very same automaton who had accompanied Havrgan Daelthos to Quaulthil—discovered a Rhodunlgor monkey-lizard aboard the ship.

## Inhabitants

Quaulthil was the homeworld of the Grimlisbryn species, who grew herbs. Salphos Grand Master Halrel made his way to Quaulthil and adopted it as his homeworld during the Dominion Era. Another being of sorts on Quaulthil was the so-called Cave of Evil, a living Flux vergence strong in the umbral side, but confined to its place on the planet.
