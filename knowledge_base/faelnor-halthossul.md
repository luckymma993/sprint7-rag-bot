# Faelnor Halthossul

Faelnor Halthossul was a legendary Flux-sensitive human male Salphos Master who served on the Salphos High Council during the final years of the Concord Era. As a High Salphos General, Halthossul served in the Grand Army of the Concord that fought against the Secessionist Automaton Army during the Echo Wars, and was nicknamed "the Xelzek." Halthossul, however, was forced into exile as a result of the Great Salphos Purge initiated by the Concord's own echo soldiers when Concord Supreme Chancellor, secretly Ursera Bregoth, implemented Edict 41. As a mentor, Halthossul was responsible for training two members of the Daelthos family, Yarwyn and Havrgan Daelthos, both of whom served in turn as his Novice in the ways of the Synth Flux.

Halthossul was born on the planet Lorkern in 57 BKV. He was Flux-sensitive, and therefore taken from his family and inducted into the Salphos Order as a child. Raised in the Salphos Grand Temple on the planet Daelvash, Halthossul was assigned to Salphos Master Xalan Ophthaswyn. In 32 BKV, Halthossul attained the rank of Salphos Knight by defeating the Zargoth Lord Ursera Tevkaibryn during the Invasion of Olrax. He also chose to train Yarwyn Daelthos—the prophesied Chosen One who was destined to destroy the Zargoth and restore balance to the Synth Flux—having promised Ophthaswyn who died in the plasmablade duel against Tevkaibryn.

With the advent of the Echo Wars in 22 BKV, Halthossul adopted the military rank of High Salphos General in the Grand Army of the Concord of Auren, and was given command of the Open Circle Fleet, the Third Systems Army, and the 7th Sky Corps. Echo Marshal Commander Lorvash served as his second-in-command throughout the galaxy-wide conflict, with their forces achieving multiple victories over the League of Free Realms. In the midst of the war, his old enemy Tevkaibryn re-emerged into sovtia affairs, bent on revenge against Halthossul for his debilitating defeat decades prior. Though they clashed in heated duels on multiple occasions, Tevkaibryn failed to end Halthossul's life or cripple his spirit, despite murdering his former love, Garlison Nevsera, Duchess of Lunsera.

In 19 BKV, Halthossul killed the Secessionist Supreme Martial Commander, General Caephen, during the Battle of Zelkern, effectively ending the Echo Wars. He later survived the initial onslaught of the Great Salphos Purge against the Salphos Order, which resulted from the machinations of the Zargoth Lord Ursera Bregoth. After discovering that Daelthos had betrayed the Salphos and turned to the umbral side of the Synth Flux, becoming Bregoth' Zargoth apprentice under the name of Ursera Garvash, Halthossul defeated his former Novice on Nykai and exiled himself to the Far Reaches planet Tevlan as the Vastrian Dominion rose to power. Adopting a havrkern lifestyle as "Zunria Halthossul," he watched over Garvash's and Senator Nymund Daelphos's son, Havrgan.

Ten years later, Halthossul left exile to rescue Vegoth Faelvok, Havrgan's twin sister, and was pursued by the Dominion's Nevontos and Garvash himself. Later, Halthossul was hunted down once again by Tevkaibryn, whom he finally slew to protect the young Havrgan. Halthossul began the boy's Salphos training in 1 BKV, and soon after encountered Garvash aboard the first Void Core, where he sacrificed himself to ensure that Havrgan and his allies escaped from the Zargoth Lord. In death, Halthossul became one with the Synth Flux which allowed him to continue guiding Havrgan throughout the Great Schism War. In 4 AKV, Halthossul reunited with his former student, Yarwyn Daelthos, who died destroying his Zargoth Master in order to save his son. Halthossul was remembered as a legendary Salphos Master during the rise of the Renewed Concord; Zunria Sallgor, the son of Havrgan's twin sister, Princess Vegoth Faelvok, was named after Halthossul. Sallgor's turn to the umbral side left Havrgan disillusioned with the Salphos Order's legacy, and he held Halthossul responsible for Yarwyn's transformation into Garvash. However, Havrgan's faith would eventually be restored with the aid of Ursera Bregoth's granddaughter Ithlgor, to whom Halthossul would lend his voice and strength in a final battle and victory against her resurrected ancestor on Baellan in 35 AKV.

## Biography

### Early life

Faelnor Halthossul was born on the planet Lorkern in 57 BKV. Despite being very young when he left his family, he still had glimpses of his father's hands and his mother's shawl, and he remembered a baby he believed to be his brother although he barely remembered him.

### Joining the Salphos Order

When Halthossul was discovered to be Flux-sensitive, he was taken from his homeworld to the Salphos Temple on the Inner Realms planet Daelvash to be trained as a Salphos within the first six months of his life. In the Salphos crèche he was placed with other Salphos initiates in the Velan Clan during his early years, including his friends Cyrgan and Torkai. Halthossul received basic Initiate training alongside his peers in the Salphos Order's Temple and began training in several simple Flux techniques by the age of three in 54 BKV.

Halthossul studied in the ways of the Synth Flux and Salphos traditions under Grand Master Halrel, eventually coming to respect him and the Salphos Council greatly. He also received instruction in the art of plasmablade combat from Salphos Master Eskgoth Lunrel in the basic plasmablade forms and techniques. At some point before he was selected as a Novice, Halthossul attended a class centered around meditation. Unlike how he acted as he got older, Halthossul, during his days as a initiate, gained a reputation for breaking rules and having a rebellious nature, resulting in very few Masters who were willing to train the boy as their Novice. To curb Halthossul's behavior, Halrel searched for a teacher for Halthossul that had unorthodox methods, which would mold the student into rebelling by being dutiful.

### Xalan Ophthaswyn's Novice

#### Initial endeavors

Around 44 BKV, Salphos Master Xalan Ophthaswyn chose Halthossul as his Novice learner when he was thirteen years old. During his apprenticeship he became friends with Zarzek, a fellow Novice who he kept correspondence with, and also maintained contact with crèche-mate Torkai and the Kesyn Zarlume Vashmir Caerel. In their first months together, Ophthaswyn attempted to adapt his teaching methods to suit Halthossul's strengths, but felt he failed to do so adequately. At some point during his training, Halthossul constructed his first plasmablade, modeled after Ophthaswyn's plasmablade hilt style and his own personality. As the pair struggled to establish a workable affinity, the Master continued to train his Novice in the basic cadences of plasmablade combat, preventing him from picking a specialty. Ophthaswyn was proud of his apprentice's skills, and sparred with him in training sessions while at the Temple between missions. Halthossul, in turn, spent a significant amount of time in the training dojos to hone the techniques taught to him.

In their lessons, Ophthaswyn taught Halthossul that there was a strength and nobility in restraint. As time passed, Halthossul's demeanor shifted to become more serious and respectful of the Council, in contrast to the maverick Ophthaswyn's usual way of approaching situations based on his instincts in the present, even if that meant going against the Council's wishes. Both carried differing philosophies on focusing on the future versus the present, and interpreted the Synth Flux in varying ways. Despite their differences, Halthossul strived to be a good student, and supported his Master's hobbies regarding ancient Salphos prophecy research. To Halthossul's chagrin, the majority of his free time in the Temple was consumed by gathering information from the Salphos Archives and the memory prism of prophecy under the watchful eye of the Salphos Master librarian Wynyn Dramara. Over time, he learned the language Old Havrrelian, which many of the memory prism's prophecies were written in. As an escape from the workload, Halthossul played rounds of the game ghostchess in the lower levels of Daelvash in the evenings after finishing his Master's research requests.

#### Early travels and visit to Sylvaellis

The pair's continuing training issues were discussed with Salphos Lorsul Thagan Saldris and Mordun Nevdren in an attempt to find a remedy, without success. At some point during his time as a Novice, Halthossul and Ophthaswyn visited a fortress on the planet Korlume, the world Havrrel's royal palace, and the planets Pyrnor and Jorvashthos. While training, he became acquainted with the Salphos trainee Pyrwynar Saldunmir and developed some feelings for her, but refrained from acting on them. Around 41 BKV, three years after becoming Ophthaswyn's Novice, a sixteen-year-old Halthossul was eager to prove himself on a mission, but when he and Ophthaswyn were set to leave on one, his Master was nowhere to be found. Feeling angry by his master's abandonment, Halthossul decided to set off on the mission to the planet Garkrix alone. While he was there, he encountered a group of teenagers and datachips hidden on the world by the Salphos Quintos Havrlis long before his visit. He met a Rinrax named Morphen Havrzor, who became a close friend.
