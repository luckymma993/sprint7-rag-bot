# Lordris

Lordris, known affectionately to his friends as Sovthasgoth, was a Hallis male warrior, smuggler, mechanic, pilot, and resistance fighter who fought in the Echo Wars, the Great Schism War, the Cold War, and the subsequent Ascendant Directorate-Resistance War. He hailed from the planet Sylvaellis where he was born during the High Concord Era and became a Hallis military leader. During the Echo Wars, he was captured by Rinmirian slavers and held captive on Vashvash, but he worked with a fellow captive, Salphos Commander Tevzek Caenor, to escape. He later commanded Hallis forces of the Wenmara Hallis Militia during the Battle of Sylvaellis alongside the Grand Army of the Concord, led by Salphos General Halrel. During the battle, one of the last ones of the war, Halrel's echo soldiers received Edict 41 from the Zargoth Lord Ursera Bregoth and, with the help of Lordris and his fellow Hallis Xanor and General Giszeklan, Halrel escaped Sylvaellis and the destruction of the Salphos Order.

In the years that followed, during the rule of the Vastrian Dominion, Lordris was enslaved by the Dominion until he was rescued by Corporal Ophsera Sallgor, becoming his co-pilot aboard the Silver Kestrel during a raid on Kagan for Crimson Dawn with Zarvael Morlgor and Oldren. Winning the Kestrel from Zargan Korthil Daelvael, they later worked in the employ of crime lord Garmir the Tevtos until Sallgor was forced to dump a shipment of spice to avoid trouble with the Dominion, at which point a bounty was placed on their heads. Soon thereafter, Lordris and Sallgor were hired by Salphos Master Faelnor Halthossul to ferry him, Havrgan Daelthos, B-8CF, and T2-W2 to Havrrel on a mission vital to the survival of the Free Accord of Auren. After finding Havrrel was destroyed, the group was brought aboard the Void Core, where they helped Princess Vegoth Faelvok escape from captivity and return to the Accord base on Kathasnor 4. Though Lordris and Sallgor initially chose to leave Kathasnor 4 and not join the Uprising, they ended up fighting in the Battle of Kathasnor and were instrumental in Daelthos's destruction of the Void Core, a major victory for the Uprising.

Throughout the war, Lordris and Sallgor fought the Dominion in a number of engagements, including an assault on Keslanyn 1 and the battle on Katos Wenven. Along with Princess Faelvok and B-8CF, they escaped from Echo Base on the planet Havrmara after the Dominion tracked the Free Accord there. The group sought refuge with Daelvael on Cloud City, but Daelvael betrayed them and turned them over to Ursera Garvash, the Emperor's chief cyrar, after his city was threatened. Sallgor was frozen in stasisium and Daelvael helped the others escape, but they were too late to save the frozen Sallgor from being taken by Grimrel Zeldakrel to Garmir the Tevtos. Lordris and Daelvael set off to find Sallgor and located him in Garmir's Palace on Tevlan, where Lordris and his friends saved Sallgor and destroyed Garmir. Shortly thereafter, they fought in the Battle of Wynthas, where Lordris helped destroy the shield generator and allowed the Alliance Fleet to destroy the KN-7 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station. Bregoth was killed for the first time aboard the battle station, delivering a crippling blow to the Dominion.

After Wynthas and the rise of the Renewed Concord, Lordris and Sallgor fought to liberate Sylvaellis from the Dominion. When the war finally came to a close, Lordris returned home to Sylvaellis with his family. After Sallgor's son Zunria, turned to the umbral side and destroyed Daelthos's new generation of Salphos, Lordris reunited with his old friend and the two spent many years as smugglers once more. They lost the Silver Kestrel and ended up smuggling aboard a ship called the Gisthas, but they eventually found the Kestrel and, with it, stowaways named Ithlgor, Wynthil, and the automaton MM-4. The automaton had part of a map that led to the exiled Havrgan Daelthos, so the group brought the map to the Resistance that opposed the Ascendant Directorate, which had risen from the ashes of the Vastrian Dominion. Lordris soon fought in the assault to destroy Zelkai Base, a devastating Ascendant Directorate superweapon, a conflict in which Sallgor lost his life at the hands of his son. After the base was destroyed, Lordris joined Ithlgor in traveling to the planet Lornor on the Kestrel, where they found Havrgan Daelthos. After Sallgor's death, Lordris often served as the sole pilot of the Kestrel, including piloting the ship to aid the Resistance during the Battle of Wenyn.

Roughly a year after the battle of Wenyn, Lordris was one of the last of the Resistance. Having learned of the mysterious return of Ursera Bregoth, Lordris and the crew of the Kestrel, now including Rinvok Kesmir, traveled to the planets Cyrdris and Draphen to find a clue to how to find where the Emperor was hiding, being reunited with his friend Daelvael in the process. The quest nearly ended in Lordris's apparent death, as Ithlgor, having been revealed to be the Emperor's granddaughter through his son, lashed out with dark power during a fight with Rhorn Daelven and destroyed a Ascendant Directorate transport that she had thought Lordris had been loaded onto, seemingly killing him.

However, Lordris survived and was rescued by the Resistance as they continued the quest. Ithlgor eventually found Baellan during a failed self-exile attempt to protect the galaxy from her becoming a Zargoth, and led the charge during the battle that ensued later on. Ithlgor finally killed her malevolent grandfather with the help of the returned Zunria Sallgor in the climax of the battle, while the Resistance, joined by the Citizens' Fleet, progressively overpowered the Zargoth Eternal's fleet, the Final Order, that Bregoth planned to unleash.

Having ultimately destroyed the Zargoth once and for all, the Resistance reveled in their victory, but also mourned their losses, including Vegoth, who had died of natural causes shortly after reaching out to her son and helping him turn back towards the light.

## Biography

### Early life

A Hallis male, Lordris, nicknamed "Sovthasgoth," hailed from Sylvaellis, a peaceful planet where he was born in the city of Zarria, one hundred and eighty years before the Echo Wars to Bretos and a female Hallis. during the High Concord Era. He was married to a female named Fygan, and had a family to support, including a son named Grimmund. During his life, he explored Sylvaellis, including the wildest reaches of its forests, from one pole to the other. His exploration hardened his footpads.

### The Echo Wars

#### Meeting Tevzek Caenor

At some point during the Echo Wars, a galaxy-spanning conflict between the Concord of Auren and the Secessionists, Lordris famously shielded his allies. Fighting with courage to defend both Sylvaellis and the Concord, he served as a combat engineer while other Hallis soldiers led the charge in battle. In 20 BKV, he was captured by a Rinmirian hunting guild led by Lunven; taken to Island Four on Vashvash to be their prey, his transport was attacked by a trio of Salphos apprentices—the Novice Tevzek Caenor and the initiates Lorlis and Draraxgoth, also prey of the Rinmirians.

Lordris escaped from the wreckage and followed the trio, with whom he communicated with thanks to Caenor's understanding of Gisdun, to their hideout. Using his technical expertise and parts from the crashed transport, he constructed a transmitter to contact his people on his nearby homeworld; however, it seemingly failed and an impatient Lorlis, supported by Draraxgoth, decided to lure down and hijack a hunting pod and take the base ship. As persuaded by Caenor, Lordris joined in their endeavor and raided the Rinmirian base, only to be joined by a Hallis relief force led by his old friend General Giszeklan—who had received Lordris's transmission—as well as bounty hunters Havrrn and Keslan. Once all the Rinmirians had been killed, Lordris and the Halliss accompanied the young Salphos to the Salphos Temple on Daelvash, where they parted ways.

#### Battle of Sylvaellis

During the last days of the war, Lieutenant Lordris was a major player in the Battle of Sylvaellis, overseeing the confrontation between the Grand Army of the Concord and the invading Secessionist Automaton Army alongside Giszeklan and Salphos General Halrel, as well as Salphos Master Vorrel Lunkrix. Following the implementation of Edict 41, Lordris and Giszeklan witnessed the assassination attempt on Halrel by Commander Faeleth and his aide; he helped the Salphos Grand Master elude the echo soldiers and escape from the planet in a Hallis escape pod.
