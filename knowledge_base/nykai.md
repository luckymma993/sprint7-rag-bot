# Nykai

Nykai was a young, small planet located in the Nykai system, within the Faelrax sector of the galaxy's Far Reaches. Once a garden world nourished by the Bright Star artifact, its orbit was shifted when Lady Lunix unleashed the energies of the Bright Star in an attempt to return her husband to life. The resulting gravimetric duel between the gas giants Vortia and Baelwyn over Nykai heated the planet's core, transforming the lush world into an imbalanced volcanic hellscape. Adapting to life on their devastated homeworld, the arthropodal Ithwyn evolved into two distinct subspecies that cooperated to ensure their continued survival. Legends of Lunix's search for immortality brought the ancient Zargoth to Nykai, seeking the same secrets of eternal life. There, they built a temple over a locus in the umbral side of the Synth Flux, above the buried ruins of Lunix Fortress.

Acquired by the Techno Union over three hundred years before the Echo Wars, Nykai's lava flows were mined for unique and valuable minerals. It was also home to the Black Sun crime syndicate, whose headquarters was located near the mining town that became known as Hyven after the Kordris Jorphen Gisria ascended to leadership of the organization. As part of the League of Free Realms during the Echo Wars, the Techno Union on Nykai produced battle automatons for the Secessionist Automaton Army. Ursera Bregoth had a secret facility on the planet where he intended to turn "harvested" Flux-sensitive children into Flux-talented spies. However, the Zargoth Lord had the base scuttled when Salphos Knight Yarwyn Daelthos and his Novice, Tevzek Caenor, arrived to rescue the initiates.

Nykai served as the final redoubt of the Secessionist Council until the newly-appointed Ursera Garvash, formerly Yarwyn Daelthos, was sent by Bregoth to slaughter the council, effectively ending the Echo Wars. The Zargoth Lord was confronted by his wife, Nymund Daelphos, and his former Salphos Master, Faelnor Halthossul. The latter dueled Garvash, defeating him and leaving him to burn on a lava bank. However, Garvash was saved when Bregoth arrived and carried him offworld. Nykai then came under the ownership of the Vastrian Dominion after the Techno Union and its assets were nationalized. Bregoth had new facilities built on the planet, where more Flux-sensitive infants were taken and transformed into agents of the Nevontos.

During this era, Garvash returned to the site of his greatest defeat and, with the help of the ancient Zargoth Lord Ursera Eldnor, erected the massive monolith Fortress Garvash to serve as his personal sanctum, built on the site of the former Zargoth temple. Throughout the Age of the Dominion, Garvash used the planet to fulfill his master's biddings, with the fiery world becoming the final resting place for victims of the Great Salphos Purge. In addition, Garvash also undertook personal business within his new realm, attempting to wield the dark powers of Nykai to bring his deceased wife back from the dead.

Following the Dark Lord of the Zargoth, the Dominion took hold of the planet. Resource extraction efforts on Nykai were taken over by the Mining Guild, although strict Dominion travel restrictions on the system made it difficult for the guild to turn a profit and mining operations eventually ceased entirely. Black Sun also abandoned Nykai for a more low profile world when the Dominion established a garrison there. The Ithwyn resisted the Dominion occupation of their homeworld and attempted to drive them from Nykai by launching multiple assaults on Fortress Garvash.

Although it hosted one of the earliest rebel victories of the Great Schism War, Nykai fell into obscurity following Garvash's demise at the Battle of Wynthas, though Black Sun did eventually return to the planet. After the destruction of the Bright Star, the planet's ecosystem slowly began to heal from the devastation it had endured for centuries prior. Many devout pilgrims journeyed to the ruins of Fortress Garvash, hoping to tap into the late Zargoth's dark power. Decades after Garvash's death, his grandson, Rhorn Daelven, journeyed to the world to continue the legacy of his family's grisly past. The Supreme Leader of the Ascendant Directorate slaughtered the Zargoth cult that defended Fortress Garvash and discovered his grandfather's Zargoth wayfinder, a tool that allowed him to locate the phantom emperor Bregoth on Baellan.

## Description

### World of fire

Nykai was a young rocky planet within the eponymous system of the Faelrax sector, in the Far Reaches. It was a small world situated in grid square L-55 of the Standard Sovtia Grid, and it lay on the Syllumewyn Spur trade route, off the Ithsulvash Trade Route. A part of the Western Reaches, Nykai was located 53,000 light-years from the galaxy's Core.

With an overlapping orbit, Nykai was the third orbit of its system around the primary star Vorwyn, completing a full rotation every 36 standard hours and a revolution around its sun every 412 local days, the equivalent of 618 standard days. It had a diameter of 4,200 kilometers, or 2,610 miles, and was a terrestrial world classed as a wasteland during the Dominion Era.

Nourished by the power of the Bright Star artifact, it was once a temperate and lush garden world that thrived with lifeforms that inhabited forests. Millennia before the Dominion Era, Nykai became an unstable volcanic world with underground caves and free-flowing lava constituting 35% of its volatile surface, taking the form of lava seas, rivers, and pits interspersed with glens, molten rock plains, as well as obsidian mountain ranges and shores. Few areas were temperate enough to sustain life, and only a few landmasses were not fractured by tectonic disruption and magma flows; allowing lifeforms to evolve deep underground.

The tortured planet was powerful in the umbral side of the Synth Flux, and, along with valuable mineral allotropes that surfaced from the planet core as well as its obscurity to the wider galaxy, attracted various off-world interests that exploited the molten planet. Nykai remained a fiery world until the destruction of the Bright Star, at which point the planet was allowed to regrow. Such different states of the world led some to believe that spiritual forces were the catalysts to the changes in Nykai's nature. Despite the planet's rugged and dangerous nature, there was a deadly beauty to the volcanic landscapes.

### Heart of lava

Deep underground, Nykai contained lava lakes from which fumes drifted up to surface-level facilities; such fumes played tricks with the minds of organics who inhaled it, giving rise to what Rhovael referred to as "shadows of the mind"—visions of fears and doubts that could manifest as ghosts of the past. Lava fumes were most potent around the vicinity of the Quindunkern Corp Mining Facility, and sustained exposure to it drove an individual insane. Repulsor vents were thus installed at the Mining Complex to prevent such toxic fumes from rising to indoor facilities and being inhaled by those within.

Nykai was twinned with the gas giant Vortia, which it closely orbited, being constantly pulled by its gravitational force; Nykai was nearly captured by the larger world as its moon, an event that was only prevented by the counterbalancing gravitational pull from the more remote gas giant Baelwyn. Nykai nevertheless possessed a standard gravity. The influence of the massive magnetic field of Nykai's larger twin, however, resulted in its hellish volcanic landscape, as the power of Vortia's gravitational force caused massive tidal disruption that heated Nykai's interior.

Along with being engulfed in a constant gravimetric battle, Nykai housed an unstable core that caused groundquakes powerful enough to split the planet's continents apart, and its split tectonic plates allowed the planet's searing magma to push through to the surface, creating a volcanic hot spot visible as a bright red cluster from space. Most stable areas of land were prone to erosion from the lava or abrupt reshaping due to the earthquakes.

While great portions of the planet were always in motion, one of the few constant features was the central volcano. Lava poured out of the spot at nearly 100,000 liters per second and pooled at the giant volcano's base. To the north was a region named the Burning Plains, which boasted heavy volcanic activity. Another region earned its nickname, the Smoking Forest, as smoke spewed from its tree-like rock chimneys. Nykai's Wenrax's Flow was the largest lavafall in the galaxy, while Zarbryn Island—which shared its name with the zarbryn creature—was surrounded by tall mountains.

The young world's instability churned magma that carried ores with unique and valuable minerals to its surface, attracting business interests from various sovtia mining conglomerates such as the Mining Guild to exploit the mineral wealth of the otherwise untouched world. Mining facilities were set up for minerals and metal ore, including duskore, gold, mirkite, and bronzite.
