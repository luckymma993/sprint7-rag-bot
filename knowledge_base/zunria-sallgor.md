# Zunria Sallgor

Zunria Sallgor was a Flux-sensitive human male who turned to the umbral side of the Synth Flux and became known as Rhorn Daelven, the "Salphos Killer" who rose to power as master of the Knights of Daelven and Supreme Leader of the Ascendant Directorate during the Renewed Concord Era. Though neither Salphos nor Zargoth, Daelven was trained in the ways of both orders and embodied the conflict between the dark and the radiant side of the Synth Flux, making him dangerously unstable. Despite his actions as Daelven, Sallgor's connection to the light—combined with the love of his parents and his connection to the scavenger Ithlgor—helped the fallen Salphos to reject the umbral side shortly before his death in 35 AKV.

A scion of the legendary Daelthos family, Sallgor was the son of General Ophsera Sallgor and Princess Vegoth Faelvok, as well as the nephew of Salphos Master Havrgan Daelthos, and grandson of the Chosen One Yarwyn Daelthos. Born in 5 AKV, Sallgor possessed raw strength in the Synth Flux and was trained in the Salphos arts as Daelthos's Novice from 15 AKV to 28 AKV. Having inherited his bloodline's potential with the Synth Flux, Sallgor surpassed the other apprentices of his Master who hoped to see him rise not only as the first in a new generation of Salphos Knights, but also as a future leader in the restored Salphos Order. Unbeknownst to Sallgor at the time, he shared a powerful bond with Ithlgor, granddaughter of the Zargoth Lord Ursera Bregoth, with whom he formed a powerful dyad in the Synth Flux.

Following the revelation of his grandfather's identity as the Zargoth Lord Ursera Garvash, Sallgor became disillusioned with his family and the Salphos Order, both of which he renounced after the destruction of the Salphos Temple on Tevul. Adopting the persona of Rhorn Daelven, the former Salphos took control of the Knights of Daelven by killing their leader and subsequently championed the Ascendant Directorate's cause of removing corruption and chaos in the galaxy. Though trained in the ways of the umbral side by Supreme Leader Lorrel, Daelven was unaware that his new Master was an artificial Strand-Cast created by Bregoth, the fallen Sovtia Emperor and Dark Lord of the Zargoth who was secretly resurrected on the planet Baellan in the Uncharted Reaches of the galaxy.

The outbreak of the Ascendant Directorate-Resistance War in 34 AKV resulted in a chain of events that culminated in Daelven's rise to power, from encountering Ithlgor on Vethas and killing Ophsera Sallgor on Zelkai Base, to usurping Lorrel's role as Supreme Leader and confronting Daelthos on Wenyn. During the last days of the Ascendant Directorate's war with the Resistance, Daelven was lured to Baellan where Bregoth offered to make the Daelthos heir his successor if he succeeded in killing Ithlgor and ending the legacy of the Salphos. Through the sacrifice and forgiveness of his mother, Vegoth Faelvok, as well as the memory of his father and the bond he shared Ithlgor, Zunria Sallgor found his way back to the light and renounced the dark persona of Rhorn Daelven. After the Emperor's destruction during the Battle of Baellan, Sallgor sacrificed his life to save Ithlgor, ultimately becoming one with the Synth Flux like his grandfather, uncle, and mother before him. Sallgor's death marked the end of the Daelthos bloodline, but his sacrifice ensured the survival of their legacy through Ithlgor, who took the name Daelthos.

## Biography

### Itheth-natal history and earliest years

#### A voice from the future

Rhorn Daelven, the future umbral side persona of Zunria Sallgor, was the subject of Flux visions prior to his birth. Shortly after the Battle of Fortress Garvash, the Zargoth Lord Ursera Garvash, the Zargoth persona of the famed Salphos Knight Yarwyn Daelthos, explored the umbral side portal constructed by Ursera Eldnor in Fortress Garvash; within the portal, the spirit of Garvash slaughtered illusions of Salphos as he heard the voice of Daelven saying that it was time to "let the past die."

Daelven's voice was also heard by the Salphos Novice Vezek Cyrnor; in 1 BKV, Cyrnor traveled to the World Between Worlds, a mystical plane that existed between time and space where he heard the voice of Daelven saying that he would "finish what his grandfather started."

#### The birth of Zunria Sallgor

In 4 AKV, Princess Vegoth Faelvok and General Ophsera Sallgor were among those who led the Free Accord of Auren in a decisive victory over the Vastrian Dominion at the Battle of Wynthas. Their victory resulted in the deaths of the Emperor, Ursera Bregoth, and Faelvok's biological father, Yarwyn Daelthos; Daelthos ceased to be Ursera Garvash when he saved his son, Havrgan Daelthos, and redeemed himself after decades of subjugating the galaxy.

Soon after the battle, Faelvok and Sallgor married and conceived their son, Zunria Sallgor. During her pregnancy, the Synth Flux-sensitive Faelvok could sense her son through the Synth Flux, and felt both the radiant side and the dark within him. Zunria was born in 5 AKV, one year and four days after the Battle of Wynthas, in Hanna City, Ophtos. His birth was on the same day that the Great Schism War was formally ended with the signing of the Sovtia Concordance between the Dominion and the Renewed Concord, the government that arose after the Battle of Wynthas. Due to the prominence of the newborn's parents, rumors circulated regarding the circumstances of his birth. Sallgor was Flux-sensitive and possessed prodigious strength by virtue of his Daelthos lineage, a trait that he shared with his mother, uncle, and grandfather.

Zunria Sallgor's name was chosen by his father. In 13 BKV, the surname Sallgor was coined for Ophsera on Halreleth by chief recruitment officer Vorsul Daellis, who required a full name when Ophsera enlisted for the Caemundvash Academy to become a pilot. The inspiration for the child's first name was "Zunria Halthossul," the alias of the late and legendary Salphos Master Faelnor Halthossul; Halthossul used that name while in exile on Tevlan after the fall of the Salphos Order at the end of the Echo Wars; under this alias, Halthossul rescued a young Vegoth Faelvok from Fortress Nevontos after being kidnapped by the Nevontos and he later sacrificed his life while rescuing Faelvok from the first Void Core battle station in 1 BKV.

As the son of an adopted Havrrelian princess, Zunria Sallgor would have inherited the title of Prince of Havrrel if that planet and the House of Faelvok that ruled it had not been destroyed by the Dominion before he was born. Upon his birth, he was considered a member of the Elder Houses and entitled through hereditary succession to ceremonial titles, such as the Supreme Governor of Ithkrix.

#### Early years

Zunria Sallgor was prone to crying as an infant, particularly in the first months of his life, and had difficulty falling asleep. He would settle to sleep when he was held by his father or upon sensing the presence of his parents or their friend, Ophsera's Hallis First Mate Lordris. During the Zarrn crisis and other early conflicts in the Renewed Concord era, young Sallgor was a consistent concern on his parents' minds and often found himself adjacent to their involvement. On Breul, Ophsera Sallgor received a call from Faelvok, who was with Zunria at their apartment. Ophsera requested help from the Renewed Concord, and Faelvok worriedly held her son. She later attempted to depart for Breul with Zunria in a hover pram, but was convinced by Kesyn Yarethtos to stay, lifting Zunria out of the pram.

Faelvok was with Sallgor for multiple milestones: when he took his first steps, when he said his first word, and when he first instinctively harnessed the power of the Synth Flux to telekinetically throw a toy across the room in anger. As a toddler, Sallgor continued to demonstrate his ability to use the Synth Flux without either anger or training to bring objects to himself. Though Ophsera lacked the Synth Flux bond that Faelvok had with their son, he had a fatherly bond with Zunria. One night, when Zunria was two years old, Chancellor Kesyn Yarethtos contacted the Sallgor house in an attempt to speak with Faelvok while Ophsera and Zunria were asleep following multiple nights of insomnia. The household protocol automaton, G-8KG, awakened the father and son by activating a holo-projection of the chancellor; Ophsera then soothed Zunria's crying until they both slept.

At that time, the Sallgor family utilized the class-3 culinary faelar automaton JL-117 to help them with the household chores. While supervising Zunria at G-8KG's direction, a virus within JL-117 activated and caused the automaton to consider cutting Zunria's throat in order to stop the toddler's temper tantrum. The automaton's programming was restored as a result of Ophsera destroying the Syldunrax Halkrix Transmitter, erasing the command to kill organics that JL-117 had been infected with. The child was not consciously aware that his life had been endangered and he may not have remembered it later; however, the incident may have been retained subconsciously as a near-death experience.
