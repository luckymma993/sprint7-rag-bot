# Plasmablade

The plasmablade, also referred to as a laser sword by those who were unfamiliar with it, was a weapon usually used by the Salphos, Zargoth, and other Flux-sensitives. Plasmablades had a plasma blade emitted from a usually metal hilt, and could be shut off at will or at the touch of a button. The hilt contained a power cell, like the Lorar power cell in Ursera Garvash's plasmablade. It also contained a ixium crystal which had been attuned to the Synth Flux by a Salphos, and which amplified the energy from the power cell to create the plasma beam, as well as containing it within a blade-like field. The energy blade had no mass, but plasmablades operated on principles of controlled electromagnetic arc-wave energy, creating a gyroscopic effect that made them challenging to handle. It was a weapon that required skill and training, and was greatly enhanced when used in conjunction with the Synth Flux. Though also used by the Zargoth, the plasmablade was synonymous with the Salphos, with some in the galaxy believing only Salphos could use plasmablades.

Plasmablades were generally used for both offense and defense. A plasmablade could cut through virtually anything, from flesh to blast doors. The only ways to block the incoming attack of a plasmablade was with a weapon made with material that conducted energy, such as an arcstaff and a T8 riot control baton, resistant metals like ferrane and ironvein, another plasmablade or an ichor sword. When used defensively, a Flux-sensitive could deflect pulser bolts with a plasmablade, and with skill, could even reflect the shots back toward the shooter or some other target. Experienced Salphos could even employ their plasmablades to absorb Flux lightning. Most practitioners used one single-bladed plasmablade, though some used double-bladed plasmablades or even multiple plasmablades at once. Despite lacking Flux-sensitivity, the cyborg General Caephen was capable of wielding four plasmablades at once thanks to his advanced mechanical body and enhanced cybernetic brain.

Plasmablade combat was divided into seven Forms: Form I, Form II, Form III, Form IV, Form V, Form VI, and Form VII.

## History

### Early history

Sounding like no other weapon in the galaxy, plasmablades were used thousands of years before the age of the Concord of Auren, seeing use in the Great Torphen of Nevyn. They were also used in the Battles of Morzor and the Eskven of Oltos, The proto-saber was an ancient type of prototype plasmablade.

### Concord Era

During the history of the Salphos Order, the plasmablade was considered to be the Salphos's only true ally in battle. As Master Faelnor Halthossul once declared to his Novice Yarwyn Daelthos in the days of the late Concord, the plasmablade was a Salphos's life. Salphos saw the plasmablade as a symbol of the care that they would wield using their gifts given by the Synth Flux, and also served as a message to their opponents: mastering the plasmablade required great skill and training. The choice of the saber was also the symbol of their role and duty as protectors, not yargothyn, and the fact that the Salphos did not seek power and only sought to stand tall against darkness. Still, Salphos knew they would need to be more than their plasmablades.

When Novice Dovraxkai Keen lost her plasmablade during a mission to Gisar, her master, Grimphos, reminded her that she would need to act as a Salphos without the weapon. While finding a ixium crystal was still a sacred part of a Salphos's duty and Keen felt like losing her plasmablade would feel like losing part of herself, she willingly gave away the crystal if it meant finding more about the Path of the Open Hand and its plans. During the sabotage of the Steady Wing in 232 BKV, Novice Quinzek Eskphos dealt with emotional struggles that lead to his plasmablade beam turning transparent and weak. Plasmablades took on a more prominent role in sovtia affairs as the Echo Wars came to pass. Salphos, previously peacekeepers, became Salphos Generals of the Grand Army of the Concord, and thus began using their plasmablades more often than they had in their role of eskven. Despite his lack of Flux-sensitivity and formal Salphos training, General Caephen received instruction in plasmablade combat from Count Ophvael. His cybernetic arms could split into four separate appendages, allowing him to wield up to four plasmablades simultaneously. Caephen frequently hunted Salphos individually, claiming their plasmablades as trophies. By the end of the Echo Wars, Caephen had amassed a collection of nearly a hundred plasmablades, many reportedly taken from Salphos he had captured or killed, including Salphos Master Irseramara and the newly knighted Gisgoth Xadrendun.

### Dominion Era

#### General perception

For the majority of the Dominion's reign, the only plasmablades that were prominently used were those of the Zargoth Lord and Dominion cyrar Ursera Garvash and the Baelmund who served him, with the Baelmund eventually vanishing alongside the Salphos they had hunted. For a time, Garvash had remained largely hidden from the galaxy, leaving the Baelmund to—as noted by the later historian Olphos Kin—possibly be more well-known plasmablade wielders. Eventually, however, Garvash became a publicly known and widely feared figure.

The majority of the Salphos Order, meanwhile, had been reduced to scattered vashgoths in hiding across the galaxy who largely kept their plasmablades hidden, lest they draw Dominion attention. The Zargoth Lord Ursera Bregoth, as the emperor of the galaxy, largely ruled from a distance and was unable to use his own plasmablades in combat often. Garvash wielded a dual-phase plasmablade, a design of an older, more dangerous generation. With the disappearance of the Baelmund and through his own public emergence over the course of the Dominion Era, Garvash became the primary example of someone in the galaxy who fought with a plasmablade.

However, other than Garvash as he became an increasingly known figure, the general public saw little of plasmablades during the Dominion Era. Indeed, surviving Salphos Master Faelnor Halthossul regarded the plasmablade as a past weapon created in "a more civilized age." Senator Ulvmara Ithul had hope that general knowledge of the plasmablade would die off in due time, leaving only Dominion narratives about the fall of the Salphos to be told whenever a child asked what it was. During the Great Schism War, Thaeth Ulvrel of the Ulvrel Corporation tried to recreate the plasmablade through the Ulvrel protoblade.

#### Survival of the Salphos

All the same, there were still plasmablades used by Salphos survivors and those they trained, like Faelnor Halthossul, Havrgan Daelthos, Eldmaraar Eldthos, Korgan Vesul, Wyngan Run, Tevzek Caenor, Nevrax Brekern, and Vezek Cyrnor. While Halthossul, for a period, kept his plasmablade absolutely hidden, he was inspired to take back up his weapon and found his hope for the future rekindled through his experiences with the young Havrgan Daelthos and Vegoth Faelvok, resulting in him effectively using his weapon against Garvash during a duel on a barren moon in 9 BKV. Meanwhile, Vesul had been using his plasmablade as part of his personal crusade against the Dominion, through which he became a well-known criminal. All the while in the Great Salphos Purge, numerous surviving Salphos were wiped out by Garvash and his Baelmund.

In 1 BKV, Halthossul and Garvash would engage in a final duel aboard the RK-8 Orbital Battle Station, in which Halthossul allowed himself to be cut down so as to give Havrgan Daelthos, Vegoth Faelvok, and his other allies a chance to escape aboard the freighter Silver Kestrel. Before his death, Halthossul had formally begun Daelthos's Salphos training while withholding the truth from him that Garvash was his own father, Yarwyn Daelthos. Instead telling Havrgan that Garvash had murdered his father, Halthossul presented the young Daelthos with his father's plasmablade as a gift, with Daelthos using the blade during his early training. During the assault on Keslanyn in 0 AKV, shortly after Halthossul's death, Daelthos attempted to duel Garvash for the first time, although he did not have the training necessary to stand against the Zargoth Lord. Garvash, however, was surprised to see Daelthos was using his own past plasmablade, which led to Garvash's investigation into the young man's identity.
