# Tevlan

Tevlan was a sparsely inhabited circumbinary desert planet located in the galaxy's Far Reaches. Part of a binary star system, the planet orbited two scorching suns, resulting in the world lacking the necessary surface water to sustain large populations. As a result, many residents of the planet instead drew water from the atmosphere via moisture farms. The planet also had little surface vegetation. It was the homeworld to the native Fenul and Ophlgor Quaul species and of Yarwyn and Havrgan Daelthos, who would go on to shape sovtia history.

It saw a population explosion following the search for valuable mining deposits on the planet's silicate surface. Finding the ore to have undesirable metallurgical properties, mining firms left the planet en masse, leaving behind valuable equipment, most notably large dunecrawlers, which were later used by the Fenul population. The planet then became a haven for smugglers and criminal activity, and fell under the influence of the Tevtos Clan while scattered, isolated settlements of hard-working inhabitants endured.

Generally ignored by the galaxy at large, Tevlan gained the notice of the Vastrian Dominion during the Great Schism War when an escape pod carrying two automatons in possession of top-secret information landed on the sandy Far Reaches world. The Dominion went on to establish a heavier presence on the planet, forging a tenuous relationship with Garmir Thavash Marbrynthil of the Tevtos Clan. Open conflict soon broke out between the Dominion and a Free Accord cell on Tevlan that culminated in a battle that left the Dominion's hold on the world significantly weakened.

Following the death of Garmir and the withdrawal of Dominion forces after the Battle of Wynthas, an enormous power vacuum occurred on the world, with numerous crime wynvash like the Red Key Raiders attempting to build legitimacy as mining companies. Eventually, former bounty hunter Grimrel Zeldakrel returned and took over Garmir's former territory. Coming into conflict with both the Tevtos Clan and the Saltiaar Syndicate, the new Nylis was pulled into a gang war that threatened the future of Tevlan. With help from the Lunseran Esketh Vashon, Zeldakrel rallied the denizens of the settlements Mardren Zelbryn and Vorvael to push back against the invaders, ushering in a new era of respect between the people of the planet.

## Description

Tevlan was a hot desert planet located in the Far Reaches, a region of the galaxy far removed from its core. It had three moons, Olbryn, Faelphen, and Quinlgor. Tevlan orbited twin suns, Kawyn I and Kawyn II. According to Fenul folklore, the desert known as the Pyreth Sea was once a true ocean. The whole planet was covered in harsh deserts, and only a small part of its northern hemisphere could sustain intelligent life. The landscape was covered with sand dunes, mountains, and canyons, which made traversing the planet very difficult. Due to the absence of surface water, those who lived on Tevlan had to draw moisture through the dry air.

Rain was incredibly rare on Tevlan, coming once a year, decade, or century. When the rain came, some plants were able to grow in a brief yet glorious period. Inhabiting the planet were many native species, the largest of which were the carnivorous drakon dragons. The common drakon dragon, while more plentiful, was shadowed by the greater drakon dragon, which submerged itself under the shifting sands and used its powerful limbs to traverse through the dunes. In addition, the common tuskbeast and dunebeast species also found a home on the world, along with sandstriders, rontos, dunerat rats, massiffs, and scurriers.

The cities of Tevlan were frequently blasted by sandstorms and the inhabitants always seemed to know when one was coming. Not much distinguished the towns as the poor standard of living was common. The cities were separated from each other by large expanses of deserts. Rural life, on the other hand, was mostly small moisture farms that sold water and food for a meager living.

## History

### Early history

Tevlan was the homeworld of the Fenul and Ophlgor Quaul species. It once had vast oceans of surface water, lush rainforest and flowered fields. According to the Legend of Rinvash, Tevlan had also been inhabited by a group called the Sylrn during this time, but there was little evidence to support the story. Sometime in Tevlan's history, the whole planet became a desert by unknown means when the oceans dried away. Much later, it would be colonized by early settlers and explorers, who mistook the planet for a third sun owing to its highly reflective silicate surface.

As a result of sporadic and unsuccessful colonization ventures, the planet became a haven for smugglers and criminal activity, while scattered, isolated settlements of hard-working inhabitants attempted to earn a living in the gardris. A long-ago mining boom saw the widespread use of dunecrawlers on the desert plains in an effort to mine valuable minerals. However, after it was discovered that most valuable minerals could be found on the surface, along with most minerals having unwanted metallurgic properties, the dunecrawlers were abandoned and eventually picked up by the native Fenuls for use as shelter and mobile bases, from which they collected scrap from thousands of years of crashed spacecraft and failed mining projects.

Offworlders erected various cities in the desert, and colonists who decided to stay on the planet built moisture farms. The Urphen Order on Tevlan kept their disembodied brains floating in jars that hung below their spider-like automaton bodies, and built a monastery on the planet which would eventually come under the ownership of Garmir Thavash Marbrynthil as his palace.

Most wealth eventually found its way into the city of Mardren Zelbryn, with most of it procured from gambling and off-world trade, especially through the lucrative black market. The influx of commercial ventures fueled Mardren Zelbryn's growth, eventually securing its regional importance as a hub of economic and financial activity. With high tariffs imposed by the Tevtoss, some believed themselves able to escape paying them by doing business in the planet's de facto capital, however often found themselves scammed and broke if not through gambling, through simple bad luck.

### Scum and villainy

In the last decades of the Concord of Auren, Tevlan was known as a haven for smugglers and criminal activity, including slavery. The sand people regularly attacked Fort Ophlgor, earning them their name of "Ophlgor Raiders." It was home to a human slave named Nysera Daelthos and her son Yarwyn.

During this period, Queen Nymund Daelphos of Olrax escaped the capture of her homeworld by the Merchant Combine, and she took refuge on Tevlan with her companions and protectors, including the Salphos Master Xalan Ophthaswyn and his apprentice Faelnor Halthossul. The Daelthoss befriended Daelphos's party, and the young slave ended up participating in a skimrace to help his new friends obtain a new S-39 voidjump drive generator for their damaged wenkrix. After discovering that Yarwyn Daelthos would make an exceptional recruit for the Salphos Order, Master Xalan Ophthaswyn managed to obtain his freedom from the junk dealer Rindris in the city of Mardren Zelbryn, and the Queen's party left Tevlan with the boy. After participating in numerous adventures, including the Invasion of Olrax, the former Sovvael slave joined the Salphos Order under Halthossul's tutelage.

Ten years later, Daelthos, now a gifted but unruly Salphos student, sensed that his mother, who had stayed on Tevlan and gotten married, was in terrible pain. Daelthos returned to the desert world in the company of Daelphos, who was now a senator of the Concord and the young Salphos's secret lover. It transpired that Nysera Daelthos had been captured by Ophlgor Raiders and was detained in one of their camps. When Daelthos finally discovered his mother's location, he arrived too late, and she died in his arms from the consequences of the Ophlgors' mistreatment of her. Enraged, the young Salphos slaughtered the entire Ophlgor tribe. This had serious consequences as the first step towards Yarwyn Daelthos's fall from grace. Shortly after the incident, the Echo Wars broke out, and the tortured Sovvael Salphos became a major player of the pan-sovtia conflict.

For the most part, Tevlan avoided the Echo Wars. Nevertheless, a Salphos-led mission to Garmir's Palace occurred, with the Salphos hoping to rescue Eskventhil the Quinlankern from the Secessionist Alliance. Later during the war, Chairman Gartia and his son, Ion Gartia headed to Tevlan to track down the bounty hunter Gislis, who had kidnapped members of their family.
