# Rinvok Kesmir

Rinvok Kesmir was a human male who originally served as a member of the Spice Runners of Draphen, before becoming a pilot in the Renewed Concord and eventually joining the Resistance, rising to become acting General of the Resistance, during the Cold War and subsequent war with the Ascendant Directorate. The son of Lieutenant Fyvok Sylthas and Sergeant Daellume Kesmir of the Free Accord of Auren, Kesmir followed in his late mother's footsteps in becoming a pilot, serving the Renewed Concord Defense Fleet as the commander of Quarn Squadron, but grew disillusioned with the Concord's inaction to the Ascendant Directorate's violations of the Sovtia Concordance. Kesmir defected to the Resistance, where he became one of General Vegoth Faelvok's most trusted operatives. Kesmir flew under the callsign of Black Leader while piloting his specialized B-94 Vexwing starwing, Black One.

As the conflict with the Ascendant Directorate grew more desperate, Kesmir was sent to find Jaxrn Dralume Zunvok on Caedunkrix, who had a data chip containing part of a map to locate Faelvok's brother, Salphos Master Havrgan Daelthos. Though Kesmir entrusted the map to his navimech automaton MM-4, he was captured by the Ascendant Directorate and interrogated for the location of the map, eventually being forced to hand over the information. He escaped with the help of a defecting legionnaire, whom Kesmir nicknamed Wynthil, and returned to the Resistance base on Xelgan on his own. He aided Wynthil, Ithlgor, Ophsera Sallgor, and Lordris with air support during the Battle of Vethas, and he later flew during the battle to destroy the superweapon known as Zelkai Base. During the battle, after the base was damaged by Sallgor, Wynthil, Ithlgor, and Lordris, Kesmir fired the shots that destroyed the Zelkai weapon, saving the Resistance from certain destruction.

During the subsequent battle of Xelgan, Kesmir disobeyed orders and led an attack on the Ascendant Directorate Sylrel Fysul, but his reckless actions caused him to be demoted from Wing Commander to captain. As the Ascendant Directorate pursued the Resistance by tracking them through voidlane to the Wenyn system, Kesmir did not approve of the passive strategy of Vice Admiral Lunvok Faelzekar and worked with Wynthil and mechanic Rose Halyn to break into Supreme Leader Lorrel's flagship, the Supremacy, and disable the Ascendant Directorate's voidlane tracker before eventually staging a mutiny against Admiral Faelzekar in an effort to buy Wynthil and Rose time.

However, Kesmir and the mutineers had not realized that Faelzekar's plan was to discreetly transport the Resistance to an abandoned Accord base on the nearby planet Wenyn, and his group's actions caused the Ascendant Directorate to discover the Resistance's escape vessels and destroy many of them. Even so, General Faelvok gave Kesmir command after Faelzekar sacrificed herself, and he led a last-ditch counterattack against the Ascendant Directorate during the Battle of Wenyn. The counterattack was unsuccessful, but Kesmir was able to lead the surviving Resistance fighters while a Flux projection of Havrgan Daelthos distracted Rhorn Daelven and the Ascendant Directorate, where they found their way to Ithlgor and the Silver Kestrel, allowing them to escape.

After the escape, the group took the brief moment of peace to catch up on recent events, and as he left for the Battle of Urrax, the ace was promoted back to commander. After that, he reflected on what it truly meant to be a member of the Resistance, and how he believed it was about bringing light to dark and saving one's galaxy.

Serving the Resistance over the next year, Kesmir continued the fight against the Ascendant Directorate on countless worlds, soon obtaining knowledge that Rhorn Daelven had been in contact with the resurrected Ursera Bregoth. Bringing this knowledge back to the Resistance, Kesmir, Wynthil, Ithlgor, and Lordris departed from their newly established base on Urria Irvoksul and went to Cyrdris to the search for the Zargoth wayfinder. There, they found the retired general Wendak Daelvael, who directed the heroes towards the Zunlume Legacy, where his search went cold years back. Rinvok and his friends then encountered the Ascendant Directorate and Rhorn Daelven, and Lordris and the Kestrel were captured. Kesmir brought his friends to the Spice Runners' Den on Draphen to visit Quinsulkern Urnor to uncover newly read forbidden Zargoth translations from B-8CF's memory banks. Ithlgor, having sensed Lordris's presence on the Gisyn, alerted the others, and they boarded the destroyer to rescue their friend, finding the unexpected help from the traitorous Kesrel. The Resistance crew then traveled to Caeon Eldganthil, the ocean moon of Wynthas, to visit the wreckage of the second Void Core, while meeting Company 77, a group of Ascendant Directorate deserters.

Upon Ithlgor departing and returning to Urria Irvoksul, Kesmir received the news that General Faelvok had passed away. He took this very hard, burdened with the leadership role she passed to him. In a moment of vulnerability, Kesmir confessed his hopelessness in Faelvok's chamber, but was comforted by Wendak, who returned to help the Resistance. With this newfound hope, Kesmir promoted Wynthil to co-general and they planned their final assault on Baellan, using the course plotted by Ithlgor in Havrgan Daelthos's C-45M Vexwing starwing. The co-generals launched their attack, sending Wendak to the core worlds to gather support. The Resistance's initial attack was a bust, as the Zargoth Eternal picked off their pilots one-by-one. Kesmir lost hope and felt personally responsible for the losses.

Suddenly, Daelvael arrived at Baellan with citizens from all over the galaxy in tow in a massive Citizens' Fleet, and reminded Rinvok that he is never alone. Together, leading the people of the galaxy, Kesmir became the spark that lit the fire that would burn down the Ascendant Directorate. Civilian vessels began to take out the Zargoth Eternal's fleet of Fykrixtia-class Void Cruisers, the Final Order, while Ithlgor and Zunria Sallgor fought the resurrected Zargoth Lord on the ground. Having briefly restored his strength, Bregoth unleashed a storm of Flux lightning, disabling the fleet for a short time. Ithlgor managed to stop this however, and Kesmir was able to continue leading the citizens of the galaxy.

Victorious, Kesmir and the fleet returned to Urria Irvoksul to celebrate. Inspired by the Resistance, the rest of the galaxy begins to rise up against the rest of the Ascendant Directorate. Kesmir reunited with Wynthil, Ithlgor, and Tevrax Bliss, and embraced in their victory.

## Biography

### Itheth-birth

Three years prior to his birth, Rinvok Kesmir's voice was heard by Novice Vezek Cyrnor during his mission to the World Between Worlds, a realm between time and space.

### Early life

Born in 2 AKV on the tenth day of the fifth month as part of what would later be known as the victory kid generation, Rinvok Kesmir was the son of Fyvok Sylthas and Daellume Kesmir, who both served the Free Accord of Auren in the fight against the Vastrian Dominion during the Great Schism War. As an infant, Kesmir rarely saw his parents due to the Alliance's continued operations against the Dominionists before and after the Battle of Wynthas, as his mother worked as a Accord pilot for the Alliance's Green Squadron and his father was a member of the Pyrvaels, and Kesmir was thus left in the care of his maternal grandfather. Six months after the Battle of Wynthas, after the Dominion vashgoth put forward Operation: Caedren, Rinvok Kesmir's parents left Accord service to reunite with him, settle on Kathasnor 4 and build a house in a colony—close to which they planted a Flux-sensitive tree, a gift from Salphos Knight Havrgan Daelthos.

At age 6, Rinvok Kesmir began to learn how to fly in his mother's old WP-9 Zephwing interceptor, which was part of his mother's compensation package when she mustered out of the Uprising. She would take Kesmir up in it, and he would sit in her lap, learning how to control the ship from her. Tragedy struck his family when his mother unexpectedly died; at the time, Kesmir was only eight years old. Pyrrn Gisdak, Sylthas's fellow Accord pilot and friend, helped raise Kesmir, and was thus like family to him.

### The Spice Runners of Draphen

In 18 AKV, Kesmir flew his mother's Zephwing in space, but was being pursued by Kathasnor Four's Civilian Defense Flux. He was shot down and taken to a jail cell. He woke up and was distraught to find out that he had crashed his mother's Zephwing beyond repair. An upset Daellume met Rinvok at the police station, resulting in the father and son arguing about Rinvok always getting into trouble by taking joyrides on ships. Rinvok stormed off and ran into Pyrrn, who spoke to him about Fyvok and encouraged Rinvok to never give up flying.
