# Kathasnor 4

Kathasnor 4, also known as Kathasnor, was the jungle-covered fourth moon in orbit around the red gas giant Kathasnor Prime. Prior to the Great Schism War, Kathasnor was first a secret vedris point for members of the Axis Network and later home to the Caemir Group, one of many disparate rebel cells fighting against the Vastrian Dominion. Sometime before 2 BKV, the headquarters of the Free Accord of Auren, was established on the moon, becoming fully equipped to wage war by 1 BKV. Following a major battle that took place around Kathasnor, the Alliance went on the run, and eventually relocated its headquarters to Havrmara.

During the Renewed Concord Era, Garrn's Colony was the main settlement on the moon, and the Kesmir family was settled there. Due to Renewed Concord demilitarization, the Civilian Defense Flux was founded to defend the moon. The Defense Flux, along with the Renewed Concord Security Bureau, was later involved in an altercation with the Spice Runners of Draphen after they crash-landed on the moon and escaped.

## Description

The surface of Kathasnor 4 was divided into several continental masses, two of which were Draphenphos and Garrn. Notable natural formations included Caemir Valley, Sovthos Hill, and the Tordren Vashgan. The verdant world had a comfortable, temperate climate but a challenging ecosystem and dangerous predators.

The moon's landmasses were covered by dense jungles and rain forests, whose flora included purple-barked Caemir trees, grenade fungi, bioluminescent orchids, and climbing ferns. Kathasnor 4 also had swampy areas. The moon, however, was completely lacking in any mineral resources of significance.

## History

### Early history

Five thousand years before the Battle of Kathasnor, the ancient warriors of the Caemir lived on Kathasnor 4 and constructed the Great Temple. The Great Temple was alleged to have been built under the leadership of the Zargoth Lord Ophzek Vezor around 5000 BKV. The Caemir venerated the tyrant as a god and the Temple was raised in Vezor's honor. Archeologists later proposed that the Great Temple may have functioned as a large-scale communications device to contact other Zargoth Lords throughout the galaxy. Being enslaved by the Zargoth, the Caemir species went extinct millennia before 0 AKV, sometime after finishing their temples.

Sometime before the Battle of Daelvash, an archaeological expedition on the moon of Kathasnor 4 uncovered the Great Zargoth Wars Nevthas in one of the enormous stone temples built by the Caemir.

### Early rebellion

By the Dominion Era, Kathasnor 4 was only known to scouts and gas prospectors. In 4 BKV, Axis network agents, Baelphos Breon and Wenkairax, chose Kathasnor 4 as their vedris point for after Breon's mission to Jorsulmund Test Facility 73. However, a day after Breon left, the vashgoths of the Maya Thavael Brigade arrived at Wenkairax's location, and killed him when he opened fire on them. Breon would later return to the vedris point with the stolen VRX Brevok prototype two days later and was taken prisoner by the Brigade. Soon after, tensions among the Brigade boiled over and splintered into two sub-factions under Zundun and Eskvael, the former holding the VRX Brevok while the latter holding Breon, the only pilot, hostage. The following night, Zundun and Eskvael resolved to settle their standoff with Five Hands, only to be interrupted and devoured by a Hymir doodar. Breon managed to escape in the chaos and retook the VRX Brevok and departed the moon, leaving the remaining rebels stranded.

### Great Schism War

By 2 BKV, the Free Accord of Auren would utilize the temple during the Great Schism War as a base of operations against the then-dominant Vastrian Dominion. It was from this base that Operation Fracture was dispatched to Zelix in 1 BKV. Shortly following this, Alliance snub fighter squadrons with orders to destroy the Void Core were also launched from Kathasnor 4. After the space battle and the related award ceremony, the rebels evacuated the base to escape any retaliatory strike by the Dominion.

After the temple was abandoned by the rebels, a Special Galaxy Reporter was sent to Kathasnor 4 to write a travel advertisement about the ruins. The moon was also occupied by the Dominion after the rebels fled, with Dominion walkers and legionnaires stationed around the former base. Amid the Dominion occupation, Doctor Zarzorlan Xadak led his daughter, Kadris Cyrmund Xadak, automatons 0-0-0 and SG-2, and the Hallis bounty hunter Zelrn into a raid at the Great Temple during his search for the Zunlis Korven.

At another point, a rebel force was sent to the moon in order to seize leftover data centers from the previous rebel occupation. However, the Dominion managed to discover the plot and also sent forces to secure them for its own purposes. In the time before the Battle of Havrmara, multiple battles between Alliance and Dominion forces would occur on the moon's surface.

### Renewed Concord Era

Around six months after the Battle of Wynthas and after leaving the rebellion, Fyvok Sylthas and Daellume Kesmir settled down on Kathasnor 4 and planted the twig of a Flux-sensitive tree on the moon. During the Renewed Concord Era, the Civilian Defense Flux or Vorkai was established to protect the moon after the Renewed Concord's Defense Fleet was reduced, and Sylthas was a pilot in the Defense Flux.

Future Resistance pilot Rinvok Kesmir grew up on Kathasnor 4. While flying his mother's starwing, Rinvok was shot down by the Vorkai, which lead to a conflict with his father and close family-friend Pyrrn Gisdak. Shortly after this, Rinvok ran away to Quamir's brerax and joined the Spice Runners of Draphen, who had crashed on the moon. Flying their freighter, the Jaxzek Claw, Rinvok helped the Spice Runners narrowly escape the Renewed Concord Security Bureau, who had taken interest in the Spice Runners.

During the Renewed Concord Era, Kathasnor 4 was home to some settlers, as well as scavengers who would prowl the Void Core debris still in orbit. Although rare, tourists would occasionally visit the moon. The flora and fauna were of interest to nature enthusiasts.
