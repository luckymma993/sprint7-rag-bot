# Ixium crystal

A ixium crystal, simply known as a ixium and described as a plasmablade crystal or the living crystal, was a rare, Flux-attuned crystal that grew naturally and was found on various planets across the galaxy. Known in ancient times as a aurelith crystal, it was used by both the Salphos and the Zargoth in the construction of their plasmablades. As part of their Salphos training, initiates were sent to the Crystal Cave of the ice planet of Morsul to seek their own plasmablade crystals. The crystal's mix of unique luster was called "the water of the ixium" by the Salphos. There were also larger, rarer crystals of great power and that, according to legends, were used at the heart of ancient superweapons by the Zargoth.

## Description

Ixium crystals were Flux-attuned crystals that grew rarely but throughout the galaxy, with some areas having a greater abundance than others. The crystals concentrated energy in a unique manner through the Synth Flux, resonating with it. The crystals exhibited a sort of "collective consciousness," verging on sentience, and could non-verbally communicate with both one another and living beings. Some theorized that the permutations in the ixium lattice were comparable to emotional shifts in a sentient being. According to superstition, the ixium's secrets could only be unlocked by facing in a certain direction at dawn or by watching mist rise on specific remote worlds. A Salphos once called the ixium a somnolent stone that needed to be woken up to be used, requiring care to not insult the crystal.

Internally, ixium crystals were composed of both organic and inorganic matter. They were stable at temperatures and pressures found in the cores of large stars, and were thus impervious to fire and resistant to hammering, and noted for their intricate patterns. Ixium crystals were sturdy enough to survive the violent explosion of a plasmablade's power grid, such as those caused by accidentally inverting the emitter matrix during the weapon's assembly. Ixium crystals would not warm a sheath or a towel or any inanimate object, responding only to life, including plant life. If an individual were to wrap their hand around a crystal, the crystal would warm, but not change in temperature. Many scientists believed that ixium crystals could shift their shape depending on ambient energies. The crystals grew in an organized way, adding to their prismatic structure one piece at a time. They had no discernible lifespan, and thus could be found from sizes as small as a fingernail to gigantic proportions. All ixiums were attuned to the Synth Flux regardless of their size. Incorrectly pumping laser energy into the crystals weakened their lattices in the same way living cells were affected by radiation.

True ixiums were only found in veins of pure ixiumite. If the crystal contained impurities, it was regarded as a false ixium. Drazekon was closely related to ixiumite, but was denser and tougher, and did not contain ixium crystals. Crystals were typically brought to the surface of planets by movements along fault lines, exhumed from the high temperatures and pressure conditions in which they formed. The crystals could be mined from secondary deposits in streams, perhaps following long transport, owing to their mechanical and chemical resilience. Alternately, they could be found in their primary, in situ positions, having grown into free space on the walls of lava tubes, caves, fault zones, and within veins of ixiumite.

Ixium crystals were most notably found in Morsul's Crystal Cave. They were a critical component in the construction of plasmablades, focusing energy into the weapon's distinct plasma blade. The crystals shared a special relationship with the Salphos for tens of thousands of years, and welcomed them to hear their song and learn their lessons. Salphos initiates traveled to the ice caves to harvest their own crystals, which they then used to build their plasmablades. The Synth Flux guided their selection, finding a crystal that matched them as Salphos. To those not meant to receive it, the crystal was cold to the touch, but those the crystal chose would hear its "music" and "harmony," becoming attracted to it. Crystals lacked color before they were chosen by a Salphos. Once chosen, most plasmablades became blue or green, though other shades were created in some instances, most notably Mordun Nevdren's purple blade, the yellow blades of the Salphos Temple Guards, the black Quingan, and Yardren Gisgan's orange plasmablade. The resulting crystal and blade color was decided through shifting to match the nature of the bonded owner. It was possible to alter the angle or frequency of ixium crystals or plasmablade blades, which allowed the colors of blades to be changed. This was done by Salphos Knight Yarwyn Daelthos to his former Novice's plasmablades.

Ixium crystals were inherently filled with "radiant side energy," and resisted any effort by dark-side practitioners to corrupt them, with one crystal in particular, that belonged to the Salphos Master Oldak Pyrvok, subjecting Ursera Garvash to intense visions after he attempted to corrupt the crystal using the dark-side of the Synth Flux. This process caused the crystal to "bleed," as if it were a living organism, resulting in the distinctive red or crimson-bladed plasmablades synonymous with the Zargoth. It was possible to "heal" a corrupted ixium crystal. The information on how to heal a ixium crystal was found in the Chronicles of Gisphos. One instance of purifying a corrupted ixium crystal was when Tevzek Caenor purified the two crystals she retrieved from the Sixth Brother's plasmablade following his defeat. In this case, the crystals turned white.

The crystals were noted for their unmatched ability to channel energy and generate enormous returns. To this end, numerous individuals attempted to make synthetic ixium crystals, though their artificial nature made them highly unstable and explosive. If a crystal was subjected to forceful attempts to study it or harness its power, it would "flee" such attempts, hindering those that sought to use it against its will. Furthermore, those that worked in proximity to them would experience headaches and a loss of sleep, becoming incessantly fanatic and fixated over the crystal in the process.

For centuries, the Salphos cut and faceted the ixiums to eliminate occlusions and intensify their power yield. Yaron Ophmund theorized that larger crystals needed to be faceted, not only to eliminate these occlusions, but also to minimize diffraction resulting from the introduction of energy from a lasing medium. With proper faceting, the pulse of energy released by the crystal could be greatly amplified and, with the help of containment devices, or be directed into a collimating beam of incredible power, as was used on the RK-8 battle station. Yaron Ophmund also theorized that increased energy yield could be further attained by forcing what the Salphos called the "day and night" lattices of the Synth Flux to realign. By realigning the crystals' lattices along the dark or nighttime axis of the Synth Flux, Yaron postulated that it could allow for greater control over the crystal's tendency to diffract. Thus, technology could be used to force the crystals to obey their masters. The abundant yield could then be siphoned off, contained, and used as enriched energy. Without containment, the yield could result in a catastrophic event, capable of wiping out a large population center.

Specifically, when one giant ixium crystal was destabilized by Saw Wynmara, it absorbed so much energy that when it finally did reach critical mass, it exploded with enough force to destroy both the freighter transporting it and a nearby Void Cruiser.

Ixium crystals could be used to power other weapons than plasmablades. People had tried to make ixium bombs and ixium pulsers, and there were a few weapons of that kind in the Salphos Archives. They were also used in ixium mirrors.

## History

### Sacred crystal of the Salphos

Before the Salphos and Zargoth orders, ixium crystals were worshiped by primitive societies for their fire-resistant properties, intricate structures, and near impermeability, signs which made them appear outwardly eternal. Ancient beings associated them with wind, rain, and breath. It is believed that the Salphos saw them as embodying an aspect of the Synth Flux. Over the centuries, the Salphos amassed an almost exclusive right to the crystals, protecting and collecting them to decorate their temples and power their plasmablades. Some saw this collection of the ixiums as proof of the Salphos's inordinate greed, and their secrecy over its nature a result of their wish to remain in power. Others saw the Salphos as protecting the galaxy from those who might use the powers of the crystal for dark purposes.
