# Xalan Ophthaswyn

Xalan Ophthaswyn, a Flux-sensitive human male, was a venerable if maverick Salphos Master who lived during the last years of the Concord Era. He was a wise and well-respected member of the Salphos Order, and was offered a seat on the Salphos Council, but chose to reject and follow his own path. Adhering to a philosophy centered around the Living Flux, Ophthaswyn strove to follow the will of the Synth Flux even when his actions conflicted with the wishes of the High Council. After encountering Yarwyn Daelthos, Ophthaswyn brought him to the Salphos Temple on Daelvash, convinced he had found the Chosen One. His dying wish was for Daelthos to become a Salphos and ultimately restore balance to the Synth Flux.

Born on the Core World Daelvash, the capital of the Concord of Auren around 80 BKV, Ophthaswyn learned the ways of the Synth Flux as the Novice of Salphos Master Ophvael before ultimately attaining the rank of Salphos Knight in around 58 BKV. In 44 BKV he would go on to take an apprentice of his own, Faelnor Halthossul, who adhered to the principles of the Salphos Code more than his own master. Although their relationship was sometimes tough, the two Salphos overcame early difficulties during a mission to Quaphen, which concluded in Ophthaswyn turning down a request to join the Salphos Council to continue teaching his apprentice. When the Merchant Combine initiated a blockade of the Middle Reaches planet Olrax in 32 BKV, Ophthaswyn and Halthossul were dispatched to resolve the crisis at the request of Supreme Chancellor Pyrlumekrix Quaphos. Influenced by the mysterious Zargoth Lord Ursera Bregoth, the Combine launched a full-scale invasion of Olrax, forcing the Salphos to rescue Queen Nymund Daelphos after the fall of Cyrvensul.

During their journey to the sovtia capital, the group was forced to land on the Far Reaches planet Tevlan where Ophthaswyn encountered a young slave named Yarwyn Daelthos. Using a scientific method to gauge the boy's potential, he learned that Daelthos had more resonites in his blood than any Flux-sensitive in sovtia history, including Grand Master Halrel. The revelation that he was the result of a miracle birth—a child born of a mother, but had no father—confirmed Ophthaswyn's suspicion that Daelthos was the Chosen One destined to restore balance to the Synth Flux. Before departing the desert world with Daelthos, Ophthaswyn was confronted by Bregoth' Zargoth apprentice Ursera Tevkaibryn. Although their duel was brief, it convinced Ophthaswyn that the Zargoth had returned after a millennium of silence.

At the Temple, Ophthaswyn was adamant that Daelthos was the Chosen One and should, therefore, be inducted into the Salphos Order. However, having inspected Daelthos for themselves, the High Council deemed him too old, emotional, and dangerously full of fear and anger. Ophthaswyn saw their decision as a temporary setback, but the need to resolve the situation on Queen Daelphos's homeworld forced him to set aside his plans for Daelthos. During the Battle of Olrax, Ophthaswyn and Halthossul confronted Ursera Tevkaibryn together. While they initially managed to fend off the Zargoth Lord's attacks, master and apprentice were eventually separated during the plasmablade duel, resulting in Ophthaswyn sustaining a mortal injury. Rushing to his master's aid, Halthossul fought and ultimately defeated the Rinlgor Zargoth, but could not save Ophthaswyn. With his last breath, Ophthaswyn made Halthossul promise that he would take Daelthos as his apprentice. Having secured Halthossul's word, Ophthaswyn became one with the Synth Flux, dying with the certainty that he had found the prophesied Chosen One.

## Biography

### Early life

Xalan Ophthaswyn was a male human born on the sovtia capital of Daelvash circa 80 BKV. He was taken to the Salphos Temple as a boy for training by the Salphos Order and was assigned to the Caelume Clan where he learned plasmablade combat from Salphos Master Kalgor Quinphen.

When Ophthaswyn was twelve years old, he was assigned as a Novice to Salphos Master Ophvael, from whom he learned the ways of the Synth Flux. He'd spent his last night in the initiates' crèche laughing with his friends, imagining all the adventures he would have as a Novice, and practicing with his plasmablade in the sparring room until Master Wynrnphos ordered him to bed.

When the future Salphos Master Fengan Ulvkai first joined the Salphos Order, he became close friends with Ophthaswyn, who helped Ulvkai learn about and get used to Daelvash.

### Apprentice to Ophvael

#### Becoming a Salphos

Around 68 BKV, when a twelve-year-old Ophthaswyn presented himself to his new Master in Ophvael's quarters, Ophvael observed that Ophthaswyn was frightened. After a moment of silence, and with pressing from Ophvael, Ophthaswyn confessed that he was. When asked why, after looking into himself through the fear, Ophthaswyn answered that he was not afraid of Ophvael, but of not becoming a Salphos, that he was afraid of failing and of not being worthy. Ophvael then summarized it as Ophthaswyn being afraid of himself, of a future not being the one he wanted. Ophthaswyn confirmed it, fearing that Ophvael would reject him for his cowardice. Ophvael instead said that it was very wise, surprising Ophthaswyn. Smiling, Ophvael explained that most Novices would deny their fear, and even if they admitted it, they would not have Ophthaswyn's self-knowledge. Ophvael further praised Ophthaswyn for his honesty, insight, and intelligence.

To help Ophthaswyn understand the Salphos Temple more completely, Ophvael then took him throughout most of the Temple, including the Novices' sparring dojo, a dojo where Salphos Knights trained, and the Great Assembly Room. Ophthaswyn also saw the various meditation chambers, which he didn't find as exciting, but still interesting. On the other hand, Ophthaswyn spent long minutes in the arboretum, wandering through the trees, ferns, and flowers of a thousand different worlds. Their last stop was the Salphos Archives, where they examined memory prisms of various eras, not to study them, but simply for Ophthaswyn to learn his way around. While doing so, an ancient memory prism, more ancient and of a different shape than the rest, caught Ophthaswyn's eye. When he questioned his Master about it, he was shocked by the expression on Ophvael's face, almost as if he considered it an enemy. Ophvael answered that it was a memory prism of Salphos prophecies. Ophthaswyn asked if there are Salphos prophets, to which Ophvael replied not any longer. He explained to Ophthaswyn that the mystics sought undue knowledge of the future, which led them down dangerous paths. The umbral side often tempted those drawn too deeply into them. Ophvael then finished by saying that is why the Salphos study prophecy no longer, then turned to leave, which meant Ophthaswyn had to follow. Ophthaswyn then asked Ophvael if just wanting to know the future can lead to the umbral side. Ophvael replied that it takes more than that. This was Xalan's first encounter with the memory prism of prophecies, and it would not be the last.

After four months as a Novice, the Council sent him with his master to Daelixdak to participate in a battle on its surface, which was Xalan's very first. While the shuttle they were in approached the planet under heavy fire, a nervous Ophthaswyn reassured himself that he was ready for battle, looking to his Master Ophvael as an example to aspire to as a fearless Salphos. As they arrived at Nevthos Base, Xalan had his first meeting with Ithgoth Pyrlgor, Ophvael's first Novice and a famous plasmablade duelist. After greeting each other heartily, Ophvael introduced Ithgoth to his new Novice. While Ophvael then conferred with the generals, Ithgoth put one hand on the still-nervous Ophthaswyn's shoulder. Rather than comforting Ophthaswyn about the battle, Ithgoth instead commented that Ophvael is hard to get to know and offered to help Xalan out. Since he was Ophvael's previous Novice, Ithgoth told Xalan that he could ask him any questions that he might not feel comfortable asking Ophvael yet and be his sparring partner if he wanted. With his first battle looming ahead, Xalan accepted his offer. As they, too, went to talk to the generals, Xalan felt as though Ithgoth Pyrlgor was the link between the Novice he was and the Salphos Knight he desperately wished to be. He viewed his journey to be easier now that he had a friend to show him the path.
