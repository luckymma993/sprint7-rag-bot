# Armored Strider Carrier

The Armored Strider Carrier (ST-ST) was a four-legged combat walker of the All-terrain vehicle line used by the ground forces of the Vastrian Dominion, the Dominion Vashgoth and the Ascendant Directorate. Along with the Dominion-class Void Cruiser, legionnaires, and the VRX fighter, it was one of the most famous symbols of the Dominion's military might.

## Models

### Early model

This model was used against the early rebellion. This variant featured two heavy laser cannon turrets, four anti-personnel pulsers, and a dorsal twin laser turret at the rear.

### Later model

This model was used during the Great Schism War. This variant was shorter than the early model, and featured dual Cyrbryn Q-22N medium pulsers located on the sides or "temples," and a pair of Vortos & Olthil VD-5 fire-linked heavy laser cannons mounted on the "chin."

### Elite ST-ST

This was a modified version of the Great Schism War ST-ST: the variant featured, among other things, thicker and darker armor, heavier laser cannons, and an elite crew of pilots.

### Ascendant Directorate ST-ST

This was an updated version of the ST-ST used by the Ascendant Directorate.

## History

### Age of the Dominion

The ST-ST walker was developed during the Echo Wars and used in the Dominion Army, the ground forces of the Vastrian Dominion. It was the successor of the All Terrain Tactical Cyrar that also saw use during the Echo Wars, although it constituted a far more imposing example of walker technology. Heavily armored and awe-inducing, the ST-ST positioned itself as the standard of Dominion firepower on the battlefield.

Many ST-STs were built in the factories of the planet Gardak. In 14 BKV, the Dominion used ST-STs against the Partisans led by Saw Wynmara on Sylvaellis during an attack on an dominion refinery. During the battle, the Salphos Novice Korgan Vesul stole an ST-ST and used it to provide aid to Partisans. Vesul used the walker's firepower to eliminate most of the Dominion troops on the way to the refinery, but the ST-ST eventually crashed. When Dominion reinforcements arrived, the Partisans were forced to retreat. Wynmara shot down an ST-ST during the retreat.

### Great Schism War

In 4 BKV, the Dominion Security Bureau Agent Daelrel led three early ST-ST walkers on an assault on the desert planet of Rintos against the Sylyndren and three retired clone officers aboard an QZ-ZX.

In 3 BKV, ST-STs were again used against the Sylyndren, when Princess Vegoth Faelvok arrived on the planet to give the Ghost's crew three Ythix-class Havryn corvettes. Two walkers were deployed to patrol the transports' landing platform, which were formally on Wendrenkai to offer supplies to citizens impacted by the rebel efforts. When the rebels tried to steal the corvettes, the Ghost started firing on the ST-STs. Four proton torpedos were not powerful enough to destroy one of the walkers, and it was necessary for Nevrax Brekern to use his plasmablade to cut its legs, toppling it. The second one was knocked down by the engines of the last Havryn, piloted by Garvael Vevok.

In 2 BKV, Lieutenant Zeldreneth Eskgan deployed two ST-ST walkers, Walker 271 and Walker 414, to combat an attack on the east gate of the Dominion Armory Complex by members of the Wendrenkai resistance group led by Garvael Vevok. The rebels Nevrax, Vezek Cyrnor, and Havrkai escaped in the stolen JP-TG Walker 216. However, Grand Admiral Vevash realized that the rebels had hijacked the JP-TG walker and ordered his ST-STs to crush them. One of the ST-ST walkers proceeded to crush the JP-TG under its heavy body but the rebels managed to use their plasmablades to cut their way aboard. Halven Jaxzekul also fired a rocket which hit one of the walker's legs. After knocking out the ST-ST pilots, the two climbed out and escaped with Garvael's cell into the wilderness.

Vevash then deployed four of these during the battle on Lorthas. Salphos survivor Nevrax Brekern then destroyed one with his plasmablade. The Jorwyn then destroyed two of the ST-ST walkers. However, the last one shot Jorwyn down from the sky.

Around the time of the Battle of Kathasnor, a new ST-ST model was introduced. At this time, the Free Accord of Auren stole a number of ST-STs and provided them to Lortia's Gang.

During the Accord assault on the Dominion weapons factory moon of Keslanyn 1, a group of Accordists led by Ophsera Sallgor and Vegoth Faelvok stole an ST-ST from Weapons Factory Alpha. They used the walker to escape from the factory after they sabotaged its main power core to explode. During their escape, Sallgor fired a shot that nearly killed the Zargoth Lord Ursera Garvash, using all the ST-ST's powerful armaments. Soon after, Garvash managed to overtake the Accordists in their escape and cut down the massive walker's back left leg. The ST-ST fell tremendously and its passengers barely escaped the wreckage before fleeing Keslanyn 1 entirely for the safety of the Alliance Fleet.

During the Battle of Havrmara, General Cyrdakven Brevash led a squadron of at least five ST-STs on the icy fields of Havrmara to knock out the shield generator that protected Echo Base, the Accord headquarters. The triumph of Brevash' squadron allowed the Dominionists to invade Echo Base and rout the Accordists, striking a major blow in the war. However, the battle also revealed that the ST-STs could be toppled by a cable wrapped around their legs. Although that weakness had been used effectively by the Alliance on Havrmara, the Dominion continued to use these mechanical behemoths in the later stages of the war.

Several ST-STs were deployed by Admiral Vashdris during the Battle of Ab Gistoskern. A number of ST-STs marched as part of an Dominion Unity Day parade on the planet Caesul in the Inner Realms, where the Dominion was caught by surprise by a rebel attack. Caught totally off-guard, all ST-STs and other mechanical marchers were destroyed by the time the rebels withdrew in an attack that was transmitted to the entire galaxy, proving the rebellion had survived the Battle of Havrmara. During the occupation of Wynthas, at least one ST-ST was present on Wynthas prior to the Battle of Wynthas.

Shortly after the Battle of Wynthas, ST-STs were present for the Battle of Urdun City. The rogue agents of Jaxvael Squad Xelrel Havreth and Vasheth Halthas used an ST-ST in order to escape Nevlis, during Operation: Caedren. During the Battle of Olrax between the Dominion and the Uprising, four ST-ST were deployed with two Garsera. In 5 AKV, at least one ST-ST walker was stationed at Camp Wenixyn on the planet Sylvaellis. During the Liberation of Sylvaellis, several Hallis guerrilla fighters, including Eskdak, hijacked the ST-ST walker and threw out the crew. Later, ST-STs were deployed against Renewed Concord forces during the Battle of Caedunkrix.

### Rise of the Ascendant Directorate

Decades after the war, a young scavenger named Ithlgor used one of the downed ST-STs, designated Faelvael Two, as her home on Caedunkrix.

The Ascendant Directorate used an updated version of the ST-ST in its army branch. The walkers were deployed under the command of Rhorn Daelven, the master of the Knights of Daelven and grandson of Ursera Garvash, during the Ascendant Directorate's brief struggle against the Eldwyn species. Later, during the Battle of Wenyn, these ST-STs escorted the newer LK-D8R. This model of ST-ST walker was also stored aboard Commander Wynlumegoth's Vorul-class Void Cruiser, though the warship was ultimately destroyed during the Battle of Ophrn.
