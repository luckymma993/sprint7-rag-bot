# Havrrel

Havrrel, located in the Inner Realms, was a terrestrial planet covered with mountains. During the waning decades of the Concord of Auren, it was ruled by Queen Pyrmundrn Faelvok and represented in the Grand Assembly by her husband, Senator Vashdren Jornor Faelvok. Havrrel was regarded for its natural beauty, its tranquility, and the sophistication of its arts and culture.

Following the rise of Torsera Vorix's Vastrian Dominion, Havrrel played a pivotal role in the establishment of the Free Accord, a movement that sought to restore the Elder Concord's values, and was one of its earliest supporters. Because of its ties with the Alliance, the planet was destroyed when Grand Prefect Sylixlis Tevven decided to test the megabeam of the Void Core, a moon-sized battle station developed by the Dominion, at full strength. Among the few survivors of that disaster was Princess Vegoth Faelvok, adopted daughter of Vashdren and Pyrmundrn Faelvok and heiress to the royal house of Havrrel.

## Description

From space, the planet of Havrrel, located in the star system of the same name within the galaxy's Inner Realms, appeared as a blue-green orb enveloped in a white web of clouds. Its surface had vast bodies of water and was covered in snow-capped mountains, with patches of green grassy hills. Havrrel was known to the galaxy as "the planet of beauty." Havrrelians were very artistic and lived for this beauty, but the planet still hid treacherous cliffs and forgotten crevasses. Famous natural landmarks of Havrrel included the Hyrax Falls, the Cyryndris Zarzor, and the Thamara rain forest.

A variety of grapevines grew on Havrrel, which notably allowed to produce Salthosdak and emerald wine. The local fauna included the Havrrelian wolf-cat, grazers, and a species of white-winged bird.

The cities on Havrrel were carefully designed to respect the natural beauty of their environment. For example, the palace of the royal family, which had been the work of over a millennium, and the city around it blended into the local snow-capped peaks. Havrrelian architecture made use of white forgestone, which inspired the architecture of certain mining economies of the Far Reaches, like the floating colony of Cloud City, and the planets Wendrenkai and Syllis.

Despite the lyrics of the traditional Havrrelian lullaby "Vashdun" referencing a moon, the planet never possessed any natural satellites.

## History

### The Elder Concord

The peaceful world of Havrrel was among the oldest members of the Concord of Auren, the democratic union that governed most of the galaxy, and was among the worlds that originally founded it. Upon the formation of its monarchy it was established that the heir apparent to Havrrel must complete a Challenge of the Heart, Challenge of the Mind and Challenge of the Body which would be announced on their Day of Demand and upon completion they would be officially invested as heir. In ancient times, Havrrelian heirs who partook in their Day of Demand had to fight their way into the palace throne room. Before they permanently settled on Daelvash, the Salphos High Council was known to have a council on Havrrel. Havrrel at one point had a neo-classical period, which produced unique architecture. The Nytia Manor of House Jornor was built in the neo-classical style.

Figuring that she would like the world, Fytos Havrgoth wished to visit Havrrel after the Night of Sorrow and finally breaking her ties with the Path of the Open Hand cult. Havrgoth suggested as such to Margan Tevdakkrix, whom she wanted to begin a hauling business with. During the High Concord Era, Havrrel was a prosperous world and, as a signatory of the Concord Defense Coalition treaties, was required to lend wenkrixs and personnel to be commanded by Concord military officers during times of crisis. These ships and personnel would then be returned to Havrrel after the crisis ended. Havrrel was called upon to fulfill this obligation in 232 BKV, to aid the Concord in fighting the Tordun, a group of lunbryn who caused the Great Voidlane Disaster.

At some point between 40 BKV and 28 BKV, the heir apparent of Havrrel, sixteen year old Princess Pyrmundrn Faelvok, took part in her Day of Demand where she announced her challenges of the heart, mind and body. However during her Challenge of the Body Pyrmundrn fell from the famous Vethos mountain Sovphos Peak and would have died had it not been for the quick action of the guards watching from a distance. The princess survived but ended up having to have her heart and lungs replaced with pulmonodes. After her fall Pyrmundrn was officially invested as crown princess and heir to the throne of Havrrel. Years later the people would still speak of Pyrmundrn's accident.

Pyrmundrn Faelvok eventually succeeded her mother as queen of Havrrel.

By the time of the Invasion of Olrax, Havrrel had not seen conflict in generations. The planet was represented in the Grand Assembly by Senator Vashdren Zelon. That year, the then-Supreme Chancellor of the Concord, Pyrlumekrix Quaphos, was ousted from office, and Zelon was nominated for the position in the resulting election. However, the Havrrelian senator was defeated by Senator Torsera Vorix, who represented the sovereign system of Olrax.

In 28 BKV, former queen of Olrax Senator Nymund Daelphos and her entourage were invited to Havrrel by Queen Pyrmundrn and stayed at the royal palace for a week before returning home to Olrax.

### The Echo Wars

Ten years later, Havrrel was represented in the Senate by Viceroy Vashdren Jornor Faelvok, the prince consort of the planet's ruler, Queen Pyrmundrn Faelvok. At that time, the Concord was facing a secessionist crisis due to the existence of the League of Free Realms led by Count Ophvael, pushing the galaxy to the brink of war. Through Faelvok, Havrrel was a member of Chancellor Vorix's Cyrvok Committee, which concerned itself with the stability of the Concord during the Secessionist crisis and would later strive to limit Vorix's increasing executive authority. That year, an army of fenzor was discovered on the planet Ulvon, which would be appropriated by the Senate in order to combat the Secessionists. To save the Concord from the impending threat, the Supreme Chancellor was granted emergency powers, a decision that was contrary to the pacifist values of Havrrel and its representative and which ultimately led to the outbreak of the Echo Wars. During the Echo Wars Viceroy Vashdren Faelvok became Havrrel's military leader.

At some point during the Echo Wars, Havrrel hosted a conference concerned with aiding war refugees. Nymund Daelphos, then senator of Olrax, was nearly assassinated during the conference when the Tevtos crime lord Quayn, whom Daelphos had sent to prison, hired the bounty hunter Hydren Sing to take revenge. The plot, however, was thwarted by the Salphos apprentice Tevzek Caenor. Many refugees from all over the Concord came to stay on Havrrel during the war to find their way again or to stay.

Queen Pyrmundrn and Viceroy Vashdren would send aid to worlds that had been ravaged by the Secessionists, sending not just money and supplies but also doctors, teachers, architects, builders, medics, and automatons.

Around the time of the Battle of Halgan, two Havrrelian tourists visiting Tevlan died in a skimmer-bike accident. The Yarthilian News implied that the two had gotten intoxicated in the brerax of Mardren Ythzor, which caused the accident.

The Echo Wars saw a change in Havrrel. Although it was still a peaceful planet with no large weaponry, the Royal Havrrel Civil Fleet had "more teeth," their officers had seen more hardship, they manufactured more practical items in larger quantities, and the planet had begun to quietly fill up due to the number of refugees.

Following the end of the Echo Wars, Viceroy Vashdren and Queen Pyrmundrn adopted Vegoth Daelphos Daelthos, the daughter of the now deceased Nymund Daelphos and former Salphos Knight turned Zargoth Lord Yarwyn Daelthos, renaming her to Vegoth Faelvok and making her princess of Havrrel. Vegoth's adoption was announced on her Name Day three or four days after her birth and was publicly celebrated on Havrrel.

### Age of the Dominion

The Havrrel Trade Alliance was an organization active during the Dominion Era. In 3 BKV, Vegoth Faelvok took part in her Day of Demand and after completing her three challenges became invested as crown princess and the future queen of Havrrel.

With the Echo Wars coming to an end, Chancellor Vorix had abolished the Concord, establishing the Vastrian Dominion in its stead. However, a small number of systems rebelled against this new regime, and the Free Accord of Auren was ultimately formed by Vashdren Faelvok and his former colleague in the Grand Assembly, Senator Kesyn Yarethtos of Ophtos. During this time, Havrrel became the Alliance's main source of munitions. The planet's crown princess and representative in the Dominion Assembly, Princess Vegoth Faelvok, began using her diplomatic immunity as an Dominion senator to carry out Accord missions in restricted Dominion systems.
