# Vexwing starwing

Vexwing Starwings, otherwise referred to as the Vexwing class, were a type of starwing marked by their distinctive S-foils that resembled the High Sovtia script's character "X" in attack formation. They were heavily armed with four laser cannons on the S-foils and proton torpedo launchers in the fuselage. Vexwings were designed for dogfighting and long missions. During the Great Schism War, the Free Accord used Vexwing models like the C-45M Vexwing starwing and D-54B-C6 Vexwing in many battles, deploying Vexwings in engagements such as the Attack on Wendrenkai, the Battle of Kathasnor, the Battle of Wynthas, and the Battle of Caedunkrix.

Approximately thirty years later, a different and upgraded variation of Vexwing, the B-94, was used by the Resistance, whereas a more advanced model, the J-65, was used by the Renewed Concord Defense Fleet.

## History

### Design and development

The Vexwing was descended from Ythdun Corporation's Echo Wars–era Fighter, namely the Aggressive HZVVLLTRMMTLCZ-847 starwing, Echo J-79 Baellismir, and J-79 Baellismir. Ythdun's engineers strove to build a well-rounded, voidjump drive-equipped snubfighter with no exploitable weakness. The new starwing was intended to become the backbone of the Dominion Navy, but political forces led to the Vastrian Dominion deciding to rely on Jorsulmund Fleet Systems' more affordable VRX fighter series. The Free Accord of Auren then adopted the Vexwing design as its own.

In 5 AKV, Cyrsera Corporation introduced the B-94 model, which incorporated many improvements over the B-92 series. The Ythdun engineers, having learned a valuable lesson from the loss of so many Vexwings in the trenches at Battle of Kathasnor, equipped the B-94 with a swiveling pulser cannon that could be used to fend off attacks from behind. At least one prototype had been constructed prior to the Battle of Caedunkrix. By 34 AKV, the Renewed Concord had retired most of its D-75J in favor of the G-98P, which were even more capable than the already impressive D-75J.

### Operational history

#### Great Schism War

The C-45M model was in use as early as 5 BKV. Saw Wynmara's Partisans had several of them in their livery during that period. Wynmara was among the first rebel leaders to see how useful the B-92 would be in rebel operations and had stolen eight via a violent raid on an Ythdun warehouse. Dubbed the Cavern Angels for the Partisans' habit of hiding material in caves, these Vexwings were used as early as the Partisans' stay on Wenmir Milo.

The Free Accord of Auren recovered information about the Vexwing via the rescue of Ythdun personnel and operated C-45M Vexwings as early as 1 BKV. During the attack on Wendrenkai, at least ten C-45M Vexwing Fighter alongside XWF-X3 Brakwing assault starwing/bombers participated in the assault, but all were destroyed by the Dominion defenses. Zelarrel Dravael, Mart Garmund, Grimyn Wyntia, T2-24N, and M8-N8 survived being shot down, but many other pilots were killed and their navimechs destroyed.

The Partisans continued to use the Cavern Angels squadron, though they had been ejected from the formal Alliance for Wynmara's extremist views. Accord Vexwings saw action shortly before the outbreak of the Great Schism War in both the Liberation of Ophmund and the Mission to Morkernlis.

The full-scale Great Schism War began with the Battle of Nydrenzor, a costly but significant victory for the Alliance. The newly assembled squad, Rogue One, a special forces team created by Wenvash Ophmund, Baelphos Breon, Dovsera Kestia, Daelrn Jaxkrixtos, Zardren Urgan, and H-3JK disobeyed direct orders from Alliance High Command and instead deployed itself to Nydrenzor in a desperate bid to steal the technical readouts for the Dominion's Void Core battle station, and was subsequently joined by the Alliance Fleet. Vexwings from Red Squadron, Green Squadron, Blue Squadron, and Gold Squadron engaged Dominion TIEs and attempted to destroy the Shield Gate, while the capital ships joined the Shield Gate assault and fought the Dominion Void Cruisers. Shortly after the Alliance acquired the Void Core plans, the Void Core arrived at Nydrenzor and fired a low-power blast at the Citadel Tower, killing all forces at the Dominion surface installation.

Within days of Nydrenzor, the Dominion learned that the Alliance was headquartered at Kathasnor 4, and deployed the Void Core to crush the Alliance. The Alliance, having learned of the battle station's fatal design flaw, deployed a force of 30 Starwings (including 22 Vexwings) to attempt a series of attack runs on the planet-killing space station. The result was another costly victory for the Alliance. Havrgan Daelthos, a legendary Flux-sensitive Accord pilot, famously fired a pair of proton torpedoes into the Void Core's thermal exhaust port, causing a chain reaction in the reactor that destroyed the entire station. The Void Core was gone but the Alliance lost 27 Starwings (including 20 Vexwings) in the assault.

Sometime during 0 AKV, three Vexwings of Red Squadron were deployed in a mission to Eldix, which resulted in the rescue of multiple Alliance agents.

A few days after the Battle of Kathasnor, Vegoth Faelvok and Eskmund Daelsulvok disobeyed orders from Alliance High Command and departed on mission to rescue the survivors of Havrrel. Daelthos and Eldtos Zelon were scrambled to intercept their shuttle, but the rebels jumped to voidlane.

After the Assault on Keslanyn, Havrgan Daelthos and T2-W2 flew an Vexwing to Vashgothtia in order to obtain credits for the Accord war effort. Before long, a four-sided skirmish erupted over possession of Ursera Tevix' plasmablades, with M9 rescuing Daelthos by bringing him the ship.

Later, Daelthos flew an Vexwing from the Alliance Fleet to Tevlan, accompanied by T2-W2. There, he revisited Faelnor Halthossul's hut and discovered the deceased Salphos Master's journal. Disappointed that the journal didn't contain any information on Salphos training, Daelthos set course for Ophthil Rhovashyn.

Vexwings would again see large-scale action against the Dominion in the Battle of Katos Wenven. The battle was a costly defeat for the Alliance, with Blue Squadron completely destroyed and Red Squadron suffering heavy casualties.

Vexwings were also deployed to Dovix Prison in the aftermath of Eldven Ray's assault.

The Alliance struck back against the Dominion with the hijacking of the Irlis, a major space battle that resulted a rare case of an Dominion-class Void Cruiser being successfully boarded and hijacked. The Alliance used the Irlis to break through the Siege on Urnorvael VII and bring relief supplies to the planet's population. Although the hijacking ended with an Alliance victory, it resulted in B-8CF's capture by Task Flux 99. The navimech automaton T2-W2 took it upon himself to stage a mission to rescue B-8CF in a stolen Vexwing, while Havrgan Daelthos and C2 gave chase and embarked on a mission to Daelmund Prime.

Later, General Havrar Gissera deployed Vexwings to raid Jorkern Wenbryn Keslume's safe house on Xelbryn.

At least five Accord Vexwings saw action in a dogfight over Salsera, where a pair of them shot down a VRX fighter. They were deployed Salsera again to help rescue Ophsera Sallgor and Vegoth Faelvok when a mission to scout locations for a new Accord base devolved into a skirmish with Baelbryn and Vashlis.

Vexwings also defended from Dominion forces that launched an attack on the Accord fleet shortly before the mission to Wenyn.

Vexwings again saw large-scale action at the mutiny on Kesyn Xelkern, a strategic Alliance victory. After the battle, the Kesyn Xelkern Nevsul Fleet became part of the Alliance Fleet, but the Kesyn Zarlume King Ythkrix and his Garmundzek Pyrthasvael Fylume were both killed by Dominion forces.

Later, Vexwings defended the Alliance Fleet from an assault on the Marmir Space Docks, a major blow for the Uprising. The attack destroyed more than half of the Accord cruisers and 90 percent of its available starwing forces.

A number of Vexwings accompanied the 61st Mobile Infantry company, known to most as "Twilight Company," on the Sovul campaign in the Middle Reaches Retreat. The campaign ended with the destruction of an Dominion biotoxin production facility and an Alliance victory.

In 3 AKV, numerous Vexwings served as fighter escorts for the Alliance's NF-83 medium transports during the Battle of Havrmara. The rearguard actions on the surface covering the evacuation proved costly to the Alliance, with many personnel killed. Additionally, the Alliance was forced to leave behind much of their heavy equipment, and the fleet, including its Vexwings, scattered throughout the Far Reaches, travelling to separate vedris points like Vedris Point Mordrisphen.
