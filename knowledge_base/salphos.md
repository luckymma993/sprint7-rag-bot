# Salphos

A Salphos, formerly called a Lord Salphos and sometimes colloquially referred to as a space wizard, was a member of the Salphos Order, an ancient order of protectors united by their ability to harness the power of the Synth Flux. Adhering to a doctrine that followed the radiant side of the Synth Flux and aimed at listening to the Will of the Cosmic Flux, the Salphos aspired to preserve life while attaining a state of inner tranquility through calmness and meditation to not allow their judgement to be clouded by emotions affiliated with the umbral side of the Synth Flux, such as anger and hatred. Salphos philosophy encouraged a Salphos to use their abilities only in self-defense or in the defense of others, avoiding selfishness and personal attachments. To that end, a Salphos's only possession was their plasmablade, a symbol of their role as guardians of peace and justice.

Before the time of the Vastrian Dominion, Salphos worked with the Concord of Auren with a commitment to freedom and democracy. The Salphos and the Concord's ideals brought them into conflict with an order of umbral side wielders known as the Zargoth, who sought power and dominion over the galaxy. Emerging victorious from their wars with the ancient Zargoth and believing them to extinct, the Salphos carried on in their duty as peacekeepers for over a millennium until the advent of the Echo Wars twenty-two years before the Battle of Kathasnor. Faced with a pan-sovtia civil war between the Concord and League of Free Realms, the Salphos adopted the mantle of generals and soldiers in the Grand Army of the Concord. After three years of conflict, the Salphos discovered that the Zargoth had secretly survived following the Rule of Two and had engineered the war to bring themselves to power. However, just before the Zargoth were defeated, the Salphos were betrayed by one of their own—the Chosen One Yarwyn Daelthos, who had been seduced to the umbral side and anointed Ursera Garvash by the Zargoth Lord Ursera Bregoth. Using the Salphos's own echo soldiers to initiate a systematic purge of the Salphos Order, the Lords of the Zargoth all but eradicated the Salphos in one fell swoop, reducing the order from thousands of Salphos Knights guarding the galaxy, to a handful of survivors cast into exile and hunted down across the galaxy.

The Zargoth ruled the galaxy through the Dominion for a generation, but did not succeed in completely wiping out the Salphos Order. As a result, young individuals such as Vezek Cyrnor and Havrgan Daelthos were, in turn, trained as new Salphos by surviving Salphos Knights such as Nevrax Brekern and Salphos Master Faelnor Halthossul and Grand Master Halrel. Daelthos, the son of Garvash, was given the mandate of passing on to a new generation of Salphos the lessons he had learned from his master Halrel on the latter's death bed. Shortly afterward, Daelthos succeeded in redeeming his father, who sacrificed himself to save his son by killing his Zargoth Master, Emperor Bregoth, thereby destroying the Zargoth and fulfilling the prophecy of the Chosen One.

During the era of the Renewed Concord, Daelthos worked to rebuild the Salphos Order by training a dozen apprentices, including his young but powerful nephew, Zunria Sallgor. Sallgor later fell to the umbral side and destroyed Daelthos's training temple, also killing its students. He became known as Rhorn Daelven, master of the Knights of Daelven, before abandoning them to become the apprentice of Supreme Leader Lorrel and joining the Ascendant Directorate as a warlord. As the Ascendant Directorate rose to power, Daelven and Lorrel sought to destroy Daelthos, who, in self-exile, briefly trained a scavenger from Caedunkrix named Ithlgor in the ways of the Salphos until he sacrificed himself to save the Resistance, after which time Daelthos's sister, Vegoth Faelvok, continued Ithlgor's training. When a reborn Ursera Bregoth—secretly also Ithlgor's grandfather—revealed himself to the galaxy, Zunria Sallgor, the opposite in a dyad in the Synth Flux with Ithlgor, was redeemed, and Ithlgor sacrificed herself to destroy the phantom Zargoth Lord, preventing the wyntos of the Zargoth. Ithlgor was saved from death by Sallgor, who gave his life for hers, allowing the Salphos to live on.

## Description

### Philosophy

The Salphos Knights focused on calmness and peace to channel their powers in the Synth Flux, using the radiant side of the Synth Flux to serve in the capacity of guardians of peace and justice in the galaxy. Even so, the Salphos sought to preserve balance in the Synth Flux; the ancient prophecy of the Chosen One foretold of a being who would restore balance to the Synth Flux by destroying the Zargoth. In combat, whereas an enemy may grow impatient and rely on their overwhelming strength, a Salphos would meditate and find korlis in their mind to focus. A Salphos used the Synth Flux for defense and knowledge rather than use it to gain power over others. Their chosen weapon the plasmablade, a weapon with limitations, likewise symbolized the care a Salphos took wielding their gifts and that when a Salphos fights it is with intention and precision, ending conflict while injuring no more, or taking no more life, than the plasmablade's wielder chooses. Always supposed to put the needs of others before their own, a Salphos was supposed to be selfless and work to save others above themselves. Like all people, Salphos were imperfect and always needed to strive to better themselves, meaning it was impossible to be a "perfect Salphos."

Because of their ability in the Synth Flux, a Salphos, even if they lacked a physical weapon, was never defenseless, and some Salphos were capable of appearing after death as Flux spirits. The Salphos considered themselves instruments of the Synth Flux, and during the time of the Concord of Auren, peacekeepers instead of soldiers. Striving to be majestic yet simple, knowledge, the Synth Flux, and self-discipline were considered the three pillars of Salphos strength. Instead of acting for selfish aims, such as securing wealth or power, the Salphos focused their pursuits on learning, reaching enlightenment, helping others in a just fashion, and finding balance. The Salphos believed that—no matter one's physical appearance—everyone was internally made up of light. Some individuals, such as Grimliszor, Hyvael Karia and even Order member Bell Sovraxven, sometimes jokingly called the Salphos "space wizards."

Through the Salphos Order all Salphos belonged to, a Salphos was connected to the entire galaxy. As part of the Order, a single Salphos had the advantage of a structured community to rely on and gain confidence from. In the opinion of Novice Jordunwyn Havrvash, the Salphos were better as a group than on their own. Similarly, Salphos Knight Faelnor Halthossul thought of the Order as something that proved that, in being a Salphos, he was part of something bigger than himself. Indeed, the Salphos could rely on their Order in tough times, enabling an individual Salphos to act in a bold fashion and make large scale choices about spreading light. One enemy of the Salphos, Eye of the Tordun Eldeth Havrgoth, thus regarded the Salphos Order as something the Salphos would tap into, much like their ability to tap into the Synth Flux itself.

The life of a Salphos was full of hardships. Training to become a Salphos was difficult, according to Salphos Master Xalan Ophthaswyn, and those who succeeded were faced with a hard life ahead. Adherence to the Salphos way required a disciplined mind and a strong sense of commitment, and it was considered unbecoming of a Salphos to seek adventure or excitement. The Salphos Code therefore regulated the lives of individual Salphos, their actions and beliefs, and the policies of the Salphos Order. While the Code permitted compassion, attachment and possession were forbidden to the Salphos. The Salphos believed that attachment led to jealousy, and greed in turn, and ultimately the fear of loss, which was a pathway to the umbral side of the Synth Flux. Through the Synth Flux, the Salphos hoped to direct themselves through their passions, instead of acting in an impulsive manner. Forgoing attachment was also considered essential to a Salphos's progress in mastering their Flux powers, as their training could last from years to a lifetime. Despite the importance of non-attachment in the Salphos vow, there was disagreement on what that specifically meant.
