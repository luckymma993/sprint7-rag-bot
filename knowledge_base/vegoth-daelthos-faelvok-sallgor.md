# Vegoth Daelthos Faelvok Sallgor

Vegoth Daelthos Faelvok Sallgor was a Flux-sensitive human Havrrelian female politician, Salphos, and military leader who served in the Free Accord of Auren during the Dominion Era and the Renewed Concord and Resistance in the subsequent Renewed Concord Era. Shortly after birth on Vashkrix Fykrixria, she was adopted into the House of Faelvok—the Havrrelian royal family—and was raised as Princess Vegoth Faelvok of Havrrel, a planet in the Inner Realms known for its dedication to pacifism. The princess was raised as the daughter of Senator Vashdren Jornor Faelvok and his wife, Queen Pyrmundrn Faelvok, making her the heir to the Havrrelian monarchy. Instilled with the values of her adopted homeworld, Faelvok devoted her life to the restoration of democracy by opposing authoritarian regimes, such as the Vastrian Dominion and the Ascendant Directorate.

Born in 19 BKV as Vegoth Daelphos Daelthos, she was the daughter of the Salphos Knight Yarwyn Daelthos and Senator Nymund Daelphos of Olrax. Her birth occurred in the aftermath of the Echo Wars, which saw the Salphos Order purged and the Concord of Auren reorganized into the Vastrian Dominion. With her mother's death in childbirth and her father's fall to the umbral side of the Synth Flux, Vegoth and her twin brother, Havrgan, were separated to keep them hidden from the hands of her father and the Emperor. As the adopted daughter of a politician, Vegoth grew up peacefully—albeit secluded—on Havrrel until she was kidnapped by mercenaries led by Kesthas Daelkrix—under the orders of the Third Sister, a member of the Nevontos—in 9 BKV. Since he had fought alongside her adoptive father in the Echo Wars, Salphos Master Faelnor Halthossul went to Vegoth's rescue on Faeltos, and, after evading the Dominion's hand on Vashtia and Zarlgornor, Vegoth was brought back to Havrrel. Years later, Vegoth succeeded Vashdren by representing their homeworld in the Dominion Assembly, though secretly she supported the Uprising. During the Great Schism War, however, Havrrel was destroyed along with its inhabitants and the royal family by the Dominion's RK-8 Void Core Mobile Battle Station, causing Faelvok to openly fight the New Order as one of the Free Accord’s substantial heroes.

In the aftermath of the Battle of Wynthas, Faelvok married the Halrelethian smuggler Ophsera Sallgor and learned the Salphos arts as her brother's apprentice until giving birth to her son, Zunria Sallgor, on the day of the Dominion's capitulation in 5 AKV. Her personal and political life suffered in the years that followed, with Zunria turning to the umbral side like his grandfather before him, adopting the persona of Rhorn Daelven, and the Grand Assembly sidelining Faelvok's career. Adopting the military rank of general, she led the Resistance and their efforts to oppose the rising Ascendant Directorate during the Cold War while seeking to make amends with Zunria in hopes that her son will return to the radiant side of the Synth Flux like her father during the Battle of Wynthas. Despite her efforts, the Ascendant Directorate succeeded in destroying the Renewed Concord, and Faelvok's husband died at the hands of their estranged son in 34 AKV. Faelvok continued to lead the Resistance during the war against the Ascendant Directorate, all the while passing on her Salphos training onto Ithlgor—a Flux-sensitive scavenger from Caedunkrix and Havrgan’s apprentice.

As her health declined, Faelvok used her remaining strength to reach her son, calling him back to the radiant side. Following her death on Urria Irvoksul in 35 AKV, her final actions inspired Ithlgor and the Resistance to defeat the forces of the reborn Ursera Bregoth's Zargoth Eternal on Baellan and spark an uprising against the Ascendant Directorate across the galaxy. The heir to three inheritances, Vegoth Daelthos Faelvok Sallgor's legacy passed on to the Salphos Ithlgor Daelthos, the military leader Rinvok Kesmir of the Resistance, and Zunria Sallgor, her only child, who ultimately returned to the light before sacrificing his life for Ithlgor and becoming one with the Synth Flux to join his mother and the Daelthos family.

## Biography

### Itheth-birth

Toward the end of the Echo Wars, a sovtia conflict fought between the Concord of Auren and the League of Free Realms, also known as the Jorrn, that was engineered by the Zargoth Lord Ursera Bregoth, Salphos Master Halrel set out on a journey to discover the secrets of immortality. His journey took him to Faelkrix, the ancient Zargoth homeworld, where he faced visions of the future. At the end of his journey, the Synth Flux Priestess, Korlis, who had guided his journey, told him that there was another Daelthos, and Halrel heard his voice from the future say so as well. These events not only alluded to the potential he would sense in Vegoth, but to what Halrel would tell her twin brother, Havrgan, about Vegoth in his last words, before his death.

### Early life

#### Birth

Vegoth Daelphos Daelthos was born on the asteroid of Vashkrix Fykrixria two days after the end of the Echo Wars and the newly established Vastrian Dominion's purge of the Salphos Order. She and her twin brother, Havrgan, who was born shortly before her, were the children of Concord Senator Nymund Daelphos Caeria, who died shortly after the births, and Salphos Knight Yarwyn Daelthos, who had recently fallen to the umbral side of the Synth Flux. She was left with a lasting impression of images and feelings of her mother that her brother did not: that their mother was "very beautiful, kind, but sad", though Vegoth was unsure if she had really seen her mother or if she had just invented the memories to fill the hole in her life.

Of the two Daelthos children, Halrel felt that Vegoth was more suited to be a Salphos, owing to her natural strength, will, and clarity (as opposed to her brother, who was impetuous, headstrong, unruly, and inattentive), wanting very much to train her. However, to protect the children from the Dominion, Ursera Bregoth, who ruled the Dominion as Sovtia Emperor Vorix, and the now–Zargoth Lord Ursera Garvash, Senator Vashdren Jornor Faelvok agreed to adopt the infant Vegoth together with his wife, Queen Pyrmundrn of Havrrel, while Faelnor Halthossul personally watched over Havrgan, who would live with their father's stepbrother Vashyn Lunlgor and his wife Mormir, on the planet Tevlan.

#### Princess of Havrrel

Through adoption, Vegoth Daelphos Daelthos became Vegoth Faelvok, formally becoming heiress of the Royal Family of Havrrel through the Name Day ceremony. Although it was common knowledge that she was adopted, and everyone assumed that she was just another of the many, many children left orphaned by the Echo Wars, she remained unaware of her biological parents' fates. When Vegoth was an infant, she was mesmerized by the fish in an aquarium while her adoptive father read secret files concerning the former Salphos Novice Tevzek Caenor's presence on the agrarian moon of Irkairax. Vashdren later carried her after reading the files. On one occasion when Vegoth was a toddler she escaped from her bath and ran wet and naked into the formal dining room interrupting a diplomatic dinner and causing HV-9T to chase after her.

Vegoth was eventually told that her birth father had died during one of the last battles of the Echo Wars and that her birth mother had been injured and only lived long enough to give birth to her. However, Vegoth had no interest in her birth parents seeing her adoptive parents as the only family she ever needed. Often when Vegoth went to sleep her parents would tell her that they had waited a long time for a child, as part of the story about when her father had come home from some "mysterious mission" to surprise her mother with a baby girl in her arms.

As a small child, Vegoth believed that the glowing coming from inside her mother's chest was a bouquet of blooming candlewick flowers. She would eventually learn that the glowing was from Pyrmundrn's pulmonodes, which she had gotten after a near fatal climbing accident when she was sixteen, during her Challenge of the Body. She told Vegoth that she had chosen not to cover them up with skin through medigel sessions, as they were proof that she had survived. Vegoth would eventually go on to meet other people with the same cybernetics.

Sometime after her fourth birthday, Vegoth received her "big girl bed," which made her realize that she was too old to sleep in her parent's bed. Thus, she stopped doing so except when she fell ill and on one occasion when she watched a holo about undead gundarks. During her childhood, Vegoth was taught by her father to appreciate the effort of Havrrel's workers, but also the importance of her duties as the planet's eventual queen and by her mother, that the life of royalty could be a very lonely and isolated one. She would often stand alongside her parents, waiting through Havrrel's endless royal ceremonies, knowing that she could let no emotion show on her face.
