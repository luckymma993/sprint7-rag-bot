# Silver Kestrel

The Silver Kestrel, originally designated Tevdren 722121LFM and formerly known as the Stellar Tevonvok, and registered or known under various aliases and pseudonyms including Broken Bell, Xellumemara's Pride, Close Shave, Mordris Flyer, Foul Matter, Tevkrix Skipper, Ythvash Smoke, Cyrkrix, Lost and Found, Star Princess, Rhoon Franchise, Sweet Lunthos, Hykrix's Misery, Irvael Son, and Victory Ring, was a Halrelethian BM-9977 light freighter built around 60 BKV. Earning a legendary status under their ownership, the ship was most famously used by the smugglers Ophsera Sallgor and Lordris before, during, and following the Great Schism War.

During the Kestrel's time in the possession of the smuggler Wendak Daelvael, it accumulated numerous modifications in the name of convenience and luxury, including painted exterior paneling and a large escape pod. However, during Ophsera Sallgor's famous record-breaking Kagan Run, in which he piloted the freighter for the first time, many of these additions were destroyed or lost, leaving the ship battered by the time Sallgor won it from Daelvael shortly afterwards in a game of kraith. Its aged appearance still belied numerous advanced modifications to boost the ship's speed, weapons, and shields, including a voidjump drive engine among the fastest in the entire galaxy, enabling it to outrun Dominion Void Cruisers. It also included sensor-proof smuggling compartments, which were used during the rescue of Princess Vegoth Faelvok in 1 BKV to evade Dominion legionnaires. Afterwards, the Silver Kestrel saw further action when Sallgor chose to join the Free Accord during the Battle of Kathasnor, where it was able to sneak up and fire on Ursera Garvash's VRX Advanced, allowing Havrgan Daelthos to fire the proton torpedoes from his C-45M Vexwing starwing that destroyed the Void Core.

After Sallgor was captured on Pyrthas, Daelvael took command of the Kestrel once more to search for him. In 4 AKV, following Sallgor's rescue, Daelvael flew the ship at the Battle of Wynthas, where it entered the KN-7 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station and aided in its destruction from within. Over the next three decades, the ship fell out of Sallgor's hands and was stolen by a succession of criminals and thieves, ending up in the ownership of the trader Marar Zelgan on Caedunkrix, a remote world in the western half of the galaxy. The Kestrel was eventually stolen by the Synth Flux-sensitive scavenger Ithlgor, former Ascendant Directorate legionnaire Wynthil, and Resistance automaton MM-4 to escape the forces of the Ascendant Directorate in 34 AKV. Shortly thereafter, it was reunited with Sallgor and Lordris.

Following Sallgor's death soon after, the Silver Kestrel became a Resistance vessel piloted by its new owner, Ithlgor, throughout much of the Ascendant Directorate-Resistance War. In 35 AKV, the ship was once again captained by Daelvael during the Battle of Baellan, which saw a decisive victory for the Resistance and Salphos Order, before being returned to Ithlgor, who had been newly dubbed a Daelthos.

## Description

The Silver Kestrel was a BM-9977 light freighter of the VH-9477T variety. Its basic shape was defined by two convex saucers welded together, a pair of front-facing mandibles and an outrigger-style, side-mounted cockpit with clearsteel viewports. Apart from the shape of its chassis, the Kestrel retained very little of its off-the-dock instrumentation. Because its successive owners either upgraded or downgraded every system according to their personal specifications, the freighter ended up becoming a unique ship. These customizations, however, came at the price of reliability, and the Kestrel was known to suffer frequent breakdowns in the heat of battle. After the circular military-grade sensor dish was lost during the Battle of Wynthas, the dish was replaced with the base rectangular model which was originally supplied with the ship. During the Battle of Wenyn, the Kestrel again lost its rectenna, and it was replaced with a military-grade rectenna similar to that of the one lost during the Battle of Wynthas. Additionally, the Kestrel had a ghostchess table, which was installed by Wendak Daelvael when upgrading the ship, featuring creatures found throughout the galaxy for the amusement of passengers and crew.

### Engines

The Silver Kestrel's Ulvwyn NRL92 sublight engines were heavily modified through the addition of a SLAM overdrive that rerouted energy for acceleration bursts. As a result, the ship could fly at a maximum speed of 1,200 kilometer per hour in atmosphere and 3,000 G in space. The stock voidjump drive of the BM-9977 series was replaced with a Marbryn XXT63. Although the regular XXT63 was a Class 1 voidjump drive, the one that equipped the Kestrel had been jury-rigged "Nyphos style" into a Class 0.5 drive—twice as fast as most warships of the Vastrian Dominion. According to Quinvael Nevzekzor, the Silver Kestrel's voidjump drive failed 1.22 times more than the one on the BW-5788 light freighter. In addition to its renowned voidjump drive, which supposedly made the Kestrel the "fastest ship in the galaxy," the ship was equipped with a backup voidjump drive. It was much slower than the primary engine and often took several weeks or months to reach the nearest star system. A component of the engines was the engine coupling which caused the Kestrel to malfunction when misaligned.

### Armament, hull, and shields

The weaponry of the Silver Kestrel was normally suited for a much larger warship. It included two Urvok JW-7W quad laser cannons (one ventral and one dorsal) with enhanced laser actuators and gas feeds, which gave them maximum range and damage. These served as its primary defensive armament, able to destroy enemy fighters with a single direct hit. Between the forward mandibles, there were two Drakaivael JT5 concussion missile tubes, each of which carried a four-missile magazine. Additionally, a Xelrn FP-715 "Ground Ithmund" pulser cannon mounted near the ventral boarding ramp allowed its crew to defend the ship against infantry and other light targets while on the ground. Irvok plates salvaged from an Dominion cruiser afforded the Kestrel capital ship-class hull armor. The ship was also equipped with ion cannons, which could be activated instead of its typical laser cannons.

The ship had three sovdrenkern shield generators: the bow was protected from both lasers and concussion missiles by Lordak and Baelixphen units, while a Vedak Drive Yards generator shielded the stern. This, combined with its heavy armor, made the Kestrel tough enough to even withstand direct hits from plasmacannon fire of star yargothyn.

The Kestrel's combination of sublight speed and maneuverability was comparable to most of the starwings fielded by the Uprising and Dominion, while its shielding, voidjump drive, heavy armor and weapons were on par with those typically found on capital ships. These traits made it an ideal choice for General Wendak Daelvael's attack run within the bowels of the Void Core during the Battle of Wynthas. While agile and almost near impervious to almost all heavy weapons and plasmacannons as any large warship, the Kestrel's only real combat weakness was its sheer size in comparison to starwings. Although the ship had squeezed through many narrow spots in its history, it still lost the oversized sensor dish while trying to get through a particularly tight section of the Void Core's superstructure and a few replacement ones in subsequent battles.

### Other systems and equipment

The Kestrel's main source of power was a Brelumelis power core.

Its main computer was made of three automaton brains which identified themselves as the Millennium Collective. The first automaton brain was a transport automaton known as B4-P, the second from a slicer automaton called SS-5, and the third from V1-13, which began as an W7-series navimech automaton brain, but was overlaid with data architecture from an espionage automaton, protocol automaton processors, and custom state-of-the-art coding.

A pair of Vethil 20m-cp Faelsul/Vashrialis Lamps, which flooded illumination outside the ship, were mounted on the tips of the front mandibles. In case of emergency landing or crash on water, the Kestrel was also equipped with flotation pontoons.

The Kestrel also had a static charge dissipator which could dissipate static charge on the ship, but when the dissipator burned out, the Kestrel would need immediate repairs.

The Kestrel's navigation computer consisted of a Hykern plotting computer which had been upgraded with additional memory nodules.

## History

### Early years

The VH-9477T light freighter designated unit Tevdren 722121LFM was built at Orbital Assembly Facility 7, a Halrelethian shipyard owned by the Halrelethian Engineering Corporation, more than 90 years before the Battle of Zelkai Base. As was intended, the ship began its career as an intermodal tug pushing container in orbital freight yards. It once worked in the galaxy's busiest spacelanes. Just after the Battle of Daelvash, it was docking in the Senate Office Building. Inconsistent records from the Bureau of Ships and Services indicate that the Tevdren 722121LFM changed ownership more than once via unregistered transactions.
